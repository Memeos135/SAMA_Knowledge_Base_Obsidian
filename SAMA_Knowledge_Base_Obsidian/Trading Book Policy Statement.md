# Trading Book Policy Statement

## Connections

### [[Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's market-risk positions are correctly captured, start from the Trading Book Policy Statement (TPS): the Market Risk framework mandates that every bank with market-risk exposures maintain a TPS and defined trading desks, because book boundary and desk structure drive whether instruments and internal risk transfers receive trading-book versus banking-book treatment. The internal-risk-transfer rules in section 5 turn on how positions are assigned to the regulatory books, which the TPS governs. Conclude that TPS documentation and desk definitions are a prerequisite to defending any market-risk capital calculation and to the recognition of internal hedges.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 24 / 5.22):** "both the trading book leg of the internal risk transfer and the external hedge must be included in the market risk capital requirements."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / 3.11):** "All banks with market risk exposures are required to have a Trading Book Policy Statement (TPS)."

### [[Trading Desk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the boundary of market-risk obligations, read the Trading Book Policy Statement and the trading desk definition as one requirement set: the TPS is where the bank must document its trading-desk structure, and the desk definition in [4.7] supplies the attributes SAMA will check. Para 4.1 mandates every bank with market-risk exposures to hold a TPS, and IMA eligibility under [10.4] turns on SAMA accepting both the organisational infrastructure (including desk definition and structure) and the desks nominated for model approval. You would conclude that a defective or undocumented desk definition undermines both the TPS obligation and any IMA approval that depends on it.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12 / 4.1):** "All banks with market risk exposures are required to have a Trading Book Policy Statement (TPS)."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 14 / 4.7):** "A trading desk for the purposes of the regulatory capital charge is an unambiguously defined group of traders or trading accounts."
