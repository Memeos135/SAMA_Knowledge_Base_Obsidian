# Finance and Refinance Activities Licensing Guidelines

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a finance/refinance licence application, treat the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations as the binding source of both defined terms and substantive obligations that this guidance merely operationalizes. The guidelines state that terms carry the meanings in the Law/Regulations, that supplying false or omitted material information is a violation of the Law (specifically Article 7) risking rejection or cancellation, and that fees follow Article 24 of the Implementing Regulations. You would therefore ground any licensing-compliance conclusion in the Law itself and not rely on the guidance alone for definitions or enforcement consequences.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / Instruction 3):** "The terms and phrases mentioned in the license application shall have the same meanings stated in the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Instruction 4):** "...shall be deemed a violation of the Finance Companies Control Law, in particular Article 7 thereof, and may lead to the rejection of the license application or cancellation of the license."

### [[Finance Support Activities Licensing Guidelines]] — `semantically_similar_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are mapping SAMA's non-bank licensing regimes, these two Guidelines appear to be parallel but distinct products — one for finance/refinance activities under the Finance Companies Control Law, the other for finance support activities under the separate Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities. Although their procedural texts closely track each other (LEI requirement, Fit and Proper forms, referral to the Ministry of Commerce), the governing instruments and timelines differ (e.g. 60 business days vs. 30 business days for the initial decision). Verify which regime governs the specific activity before relying on either document, because the wrong Guidelines would point you to the wrong controlling law and deadlines.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / Page 4):** "The Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations set forth the provisions regulating licensing... initial approval or reasoned rejection within 60 business days."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Page 4):** "The Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities set forth the provisions... preliminary approval or justified rejection within (30) business days."
- **Caveat:** Relationship is INFERRED from structural similarity; the two Guidelines govern different regimes under different instruments — confirm the applicable law before relying on shared text.

### [[Fit and Proper Form]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When preparing a finance-company licence application, the Fit and Proper Form is a mandatory attachment whose scope you must map carefully: it is required not only for each founding member and each board/sub-committee candidate, but also — where a founding member is an entity — for every natural or legal person owning 5% or more of that entity. The guidelines make submission and proper sign-off/approval of these forms a documentary precondition for a complete application. You would therefore identify all in-scope individuals (including 5%+ upstream owners) and ensure a completed, signed and approved form for each before treating the application as submittable.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Documents item 5):** "SAMA's Fit and Proper Form for each founding member after being completed and signed by each founding member, and approved by the person authorized to submit the license application."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Documents item 6):** "In the event where a founding member is an entity, a Fit and Proper Form shall be submitted for every person (natural or legal) owning 5% or more of this entity."

### [[Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)  GLEIF|Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) / GLEIF]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a finance/refinance license applicant on what must be in place before SAMA grants the license, treat obtaining an LEI as a mandatory pre-license gating item, not an optional formality. These Guidelines expressly require the company to prove it has secured an LEI from the GLEIF-accredited Saudi Operating Unit as a step in the licensing procedures, sequenced after incorporation and before SAMA's on-site verification and license decision. You would conclude that failure to produce the LEI stalls license issuance regardless of other requirements being met.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4, Licensing Procedures item 5):** "The company must provide SAMA with proof that it has obtained the Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4, item 5):** "from the Saudi Operating Unit accredited by the Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation (GLEIF)."
