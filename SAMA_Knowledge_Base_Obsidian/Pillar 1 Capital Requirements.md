# Pillar 1 Capital Requirements

## Connections

### [[Basel II Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining a bank's minimum capital floor under SAMA's Basel II adoption, treat Pillar 1 as the fixed, rules-based baseline: it fixes minimum capital for credit, market and operational risks by explicit calculation rules, not supervisory judgement. The Basel II framework document expressly structures itself around Pillar 1 as the starting point that Pillar 2 supplements. For a capital-adequacy decision this means you first compute the Pillar 1 minimum, then assess whether Pillar 2 add-ons apply — do not treat the Pillar 1 number as the bank's total required capital.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4):** "minimum capital requirements are calculated based on explicit calculation rules in respect of credit, market and operational risks"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Introduction and Overview):** "Basel II's structure is built upon three pillars. Under Pillar 1, minimum capital requirements are calculated based on explicit calculation rules in respect of credit, market and operational risks."

### [[Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Plan (ICAAP)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the ICAAP, do not treat it as a substitute for the Pillar 1 minimum capital calculation — the document places ICAAP under Pillar 2 and describes it as supplementing, not replacing, the credit, market and operational risk charges computed under explicit Pillar 1 rules. This matters because a bank may base its ICAAP on Pillar 1 results plus additional risks, or model all risks internally, but the Pillar 1 minimum remains the floor. You should confirm the ICAAP demonstrates capital commensurate with risk in addition to satisfying Pillar 1, not merely re-derives the Pillar 1 number.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4):** "Under Pillar 1, minimum capital requirements are calculated based on explicit calculation rules in respect of credit, market and operational risks"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 17):** "banks may choose to base their ICAAP on the results of Pillar 1 risks calculation with additional risks ... assessed separately and added to Pillar 1"
