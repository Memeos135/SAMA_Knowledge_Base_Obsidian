# Risk Factor Modellability

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

Market-risk capital methodology concept governing whether a risk factor is 'modellable' — a threshold determining eligibility for internal-model capital treatment versus stressed-capital add-ons.

## How members connect

- Feeds the IMA regime (community 249): modellability outcomes shape what IMA banks must disclose and how capital is computed.
- A defined technical criterion subordinate to the internal-models market-risk framework rather than a disclosure obligation itself.
