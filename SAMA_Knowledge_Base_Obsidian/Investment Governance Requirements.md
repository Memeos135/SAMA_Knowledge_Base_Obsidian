# Investment Governance Requirements

## Connections

### [[Banks Investment Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping who is accountable for investment decisions, do not read the Investment Rules as merely portfolio-level guidance — Section 6 imposes concrete governance obligations, making the Board ultimately responsible for oversight and for approving the Investment Policy and Strategy, with a management-level committee accountable for approvals, limits and risk assessment. The governance requirements are an integral, mandatory component of the parent Rules, not optional best practice. In a compliance review you would check for a documented board-approved Investment Policy, a functioning investment committee, and (for Foreign Bank Branches) that head/regional office assumes the board's oversight role.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Sec 6.2):** "The Board of Directors of a bank is ultimately responsible for the oversight of the bank's investments. The Board or its delegated authority is responsible for approving the internal Investment Policy and Strategy."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Sec 2.2):** "ensure the alignment with sound risk management and governance practices"

### [[Investment Policy]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When testing governance compliance, treat the Investment Policy as the mandatory instrument through which the governance requirements are operationalised — Section 6.4 prescribes its minimum contents, including governance controls and delegation, investment eligibility criteria, prudential exposure limits tied to SAMA capital/liquidity/reserve requirements, counterparty criteria and data-keeping. The two are not separable: a bank cannot demonstrate governance compliance without a Board-approved Policy containing these elements. In a review you would check that the Policy exists, is Board-approved, and actually covers each enumerated item rather than treating governance as a general expectation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Sec 6.4):** "Banks' Investment Policy should include, but not limited to, the following: Governance requirements including setting controls, responsibilities, and delegation of authority."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6-7 / Sec 6.4):** "Prudential exposure limits taking into consideration minimum capital, liquidity and reserve requirements set by SAMA."
