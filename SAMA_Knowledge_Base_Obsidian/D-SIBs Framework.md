# D-SIBs Framework

## Connections

### [[D-SIB Assessment Methodology]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether a bank falls within the D-SIB regime, treat the Assessment Methodology not as guidance but as the operative mechanism the Framework enacts: the covering circular states SAMA 'has developed the enclosed assessment methodology' and mandates its implementation from 1 January 2016. The Framework is the instrument that gives the methodology binding force, so the indicators (size, interconnectedness, substitutability, complexity) are the exclusive criteria for designation. For a compliance decision, you cannot separate the two — designation risk turns entirely on the methodology the Framework incorporates, and its effective date is the trigger for HLA obligations.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1 (covering circular)):** "SAMA has developed the enclosed assessment methodology providing for an indicator-based measurement approach ... implemented effective from 1 January 2016"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4, para 3):** "SAMA has developed an assessment methodology based on an indicator-based measurement approach for assessing and designating D-SIBs in Saudi Arabia"

### [[Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the capital consequences of D-SIB designation, read the Framework as directly imposing the HLA obligation: banks designated as D-SIBs 'will be required to meet additional Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA) capital requirements as prescribed in the enclosed methodology.' The Framework establishes the obligation and the HLA provisions specify its content — met fully by CET1, calibrated to the bank's bucket. For a compliance decision, once a bank is designated the HLA charge is not discretionary as to existence, only as to magnitude (bucket-based), and it stacks on top of the target CAR SAMA sets for the bank's risk profile.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1 (covering circular)):** "banks designated as D-SIBs will be required to meet additional Higher Loss Absorbency(HLA) capital requirements as prescribed in the enclosed methodology"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6, paras 11-12):** "The HLA requirement ... should be met fully by common equity Tier 1 (CET1) ... will be in addition to the target CAR determined by SAMA"
