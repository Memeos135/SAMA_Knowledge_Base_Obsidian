# Default Risk Capital

**Cohesion:** 0.21 - loosely connected
**Members:** 14 nodes

## Why this community

Basel-aligned market-risk capital rules under the SAMA standardised approach (SMAR), specifically the Default Risk Capital charge and its treatment across securitisation and non-securitisation exposures.

## How members connect

- The Standardised Approach (Market Risk) is the umbrella methodology; the DRC Requirement and Residual Risk Add-On are its component charges feeding RWA for Market Risk under SMAR minimum capital requirements.
- The DRC Requirement decomposes into DRC for Non-Securitisations, DRC for Securitisations (CTP) and (non-CTP), each computed using the Jump-to-Default risk measure and the Hedge Benefit Ratio.
- Correlation Trading Portfolio is the defined scope for the CTP-specific DRC treatment, distinguishing it from ordinary securitisation exposures.
