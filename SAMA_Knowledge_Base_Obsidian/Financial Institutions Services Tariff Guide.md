# Financial Institutions Services Tariff Guide

## Connections

### [[Administrative Fees Regulatory Provisions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a finance provider may charge, read the Administrative Fees provisions and the Services Tariff Guide as one issuance: the same circular attaches the Tariff Guide and, on the same authority approval, amends the underlying regulatory texts governing administrative fees. The Guide is binding on SAMA-supervised institutions when imposing fees for services and products, and it supersedes any conflicting prior provisions. For a fee-compliance decision, conclude that the amended fee articles (finance controls Art. 4, Consumer Protection conduct rule, and the model consumer finance contract clause) must be applied together with the Guide's tariff caps — the Guide does not stand alone but is anchored to the amended statutory texts.
