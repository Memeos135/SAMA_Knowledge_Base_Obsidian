# Fit and Proper Criteria

## Connections

### [[Fit and Proper Forms Reference]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are scoping a FinTech's Sandbox obligations, treat the 'Fit and Proper forms and approvals for management' listed in the Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria as pointing back to the substantive fit-and-proper regime in the Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions — the Sandbox document appears to reference the forms/criteria rather than create its own standard. The connection is inferential: the Sandbox guidance lists 'Fit and Proper Forms' among applicable guidelines but does not reproduce the criteria, so the actual fitness/propriety and SAMA non-objection tests live in the 8294 Requirements. Verify against the current fit-and-proper forms on SAMA's website before assuming which management roles need approval, since the Sandbox AC is periodically updated and the applicant may not yet be a 'Financial Institution' under 8294's defined scope.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Ch.2):** "set out the minimum fit and proper criteria that the Financial Institution has to adopt to assess the fitness and propriety of candidates"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11):** "The AC requirements consists of assessment and compliance requirements across: i. Fit and Proper forms and approvals for management"
- **Caveat:** INFERRED: the Sandbox guidance references fit-and-proper forms/guidelines generically; the link to the 8294 Requirements is not stated verbatim — confirm against the fit-and-proper forms currently published by SAMA.

### [[Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating a senior appointment at a SAMA-supervised entity, understand that the fit-and-proper criteria are the substantive test the Requirements impose, not an optional benchmark: the document expressly obliges the Financial Institution to establish policies ensuring candidates 'meet and continue to meet the fitness and propriety criteria' throughout their tenure. The criteria (integrity, competence and capability, financial capability, independence) are defined within the same instrument, so they bind both positions requiring SAMA non-objection and positions that do not. You should conclude that a firm's own assessment must apply these minimum criteria continuously, and that SAMA can cancel a non-objection if a holder later fails them.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7):** "Competence and capability: The candidate must have the necessary academic qualification, or adequate experience, skills to perform his/her role effectively."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / Chapter 3 clause 12):** "establish policies, standards and procedures for nomination to Senior Positions to ensure that all candidates ... meet and continue to meet the fitness and propriety criteria."
