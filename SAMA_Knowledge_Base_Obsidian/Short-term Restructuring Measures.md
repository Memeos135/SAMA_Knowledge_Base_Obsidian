# Short-term Restructuring Measures

## Connections

### [[RestructuringWorkout Options|Restructuring/Workout Options]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a workout, treat short-term measures as a distinct, time-limited sub-category of the restructuring-options framework with its own eligibility test. The Guidelines classify reduced payments, interest-only and moratorium as short-term measures that provide only temporary relief, require formal documented evidence of expected cash-flow recovery, and 'should not exceed a tenor of 24 months.' You should conclude that using these tools beyond that tenor, or without documented evidence of recovery, falls outside the permitted short-term category and would need to be reframed as a long-term restructuring.
