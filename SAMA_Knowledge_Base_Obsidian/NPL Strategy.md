# NPL Strategy

## Connections

### [[Portfolio Segmentation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's compliance with SAMA's Guidelines on Management of Problem Loans, treat portfolio segmentation as an operative component of the NPL strategy rather than a separate exercise: the design phase requires targets set 'by main portfolios', and segmentation is the mechanism that produces those homogeneous portfolios. Both provisions sit in the same guidance (SAMA_EN_3575), and segmentation is described as 'the essential first step in developing a cost-effective and efficient approach to NPL resolution.' You would check that a bank's documented NPL strategy actually maps reduction targets onto its segmentation criteria (nature of business, arrears buckets, loan balance), not merely lists them abstractly.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 28):** "by main portfolios (e.g. retail mortgage, retail consumer, retail small bus[iness])"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 21):** "It is the essential first step in developing a cost-effective and efficient approach to NPL resolution."
