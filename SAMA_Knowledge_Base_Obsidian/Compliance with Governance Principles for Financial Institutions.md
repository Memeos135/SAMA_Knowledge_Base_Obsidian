# Compliance with Governance Principles for Financial Institutions

## Connections

### [[Business Risk Assessment Guide for MLTFPF|Business Risk Assessment Guide for ML/TF/PF]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are scoping obligations for a finance company or payment services provider, note these documents appear to intersect but sit in different regimes and should not be conflated: 10910 mandates compliance with SAMA's governance principles for supervised financial institutions (with a defined roadmap, board approval, and one-year deadline), while 10911 is an AML/CTF/PF business-risk-assessment guide whose Chapter Two also addresses 'governance and responsibility of the financial institution.' The apparent link is the shared governance theme, but 10910 is an enforceable governance mandate whereas 10911 is a non-binding methodological guide that expressly does not restrict statutory AML obligations. For a compliance decision, treat governance duties under 10910 as directly enforceable and use 10911 only as risk-assessment guidance; do not assume compliance with one satisfies the other.

### [[Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping governance obligations for a finance company, note that this governance circular and the Implementing Regulations appear to overlap because both bind finance companies under SAMA supervision and the circular mandates compliance with SAMA's Governance Principles for supervised financial institutions. The circular directs all finance companies and payment-service providers to assess their governance, adopt a board-approved roadmap, and achieve compliance within one year. Treat the governance-role definitions in the Implementing Regulations (e.g. senior management, risk/audit/compliance functions) as reinforced by this circular's binding governance requirements, but verify the Governance Principles document itself for the substantive obligations rather than inferring them from the Implementing Regulations.
