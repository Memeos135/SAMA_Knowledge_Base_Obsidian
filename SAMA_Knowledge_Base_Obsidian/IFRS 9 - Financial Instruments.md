# IFRS 9 - Financial Instruments

## Connections

### [[Circular on IFRS 9 Financial Instruments Implementation Plan]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a finance company's accounting-standard obligations, treat this circular as the enforcement hook that makes IFRS 9 mandatory rather than optional. The circular grounds itself in Article 71 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law, which requires finance companies to follow international accounting standards, and directs all licensed finance companies to begin IFRS 9 readiness immediately and file quarterly progress reports. You would conclude that IFRS 9's forward-looking impairment approach (classification/measurement, impairment, hedge accounting) is a binding supervisory expectation for finance companies, and check whether the entity is a licensed finance company within scope and whether the reporting cadence was met.
