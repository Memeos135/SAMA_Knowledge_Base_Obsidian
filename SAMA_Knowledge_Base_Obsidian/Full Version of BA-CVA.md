# Full Version of BA-CVA

## Connections

### [[Basic Approach for CVA (BA-CVA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying BA-CVA, decide up front which version applies: the full version recognises counterparty spread hedges and is intended for banks that hedge CVA, while the reduced version (which forms part of the full calculation) removes hedging recognition and suits banks that do not hedge. §11.12–11.14 establish that the reduced BA-CVA is embedded within the full BA-CVA as a conservative limit on hedging recognition. For a capital assessment you would conclude that a hedging bank cannot ignore the reduced computation, since it feeds and caps the full-version result.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 639 / §11.13-11.14):** "the reduced BA-CVA is also part of the full BA-CVA capital calculations as a conservative means to limit hedging recognition."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 636 / §11.7):** "Two approaches are available for calculating CVA capital: the standardized approach (SA-CVA) and the basic approach (BA-CVA)."

### [[Eligible CVA Hedges]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank may reduce its CVA charge through hedging, note that hedge recognition is only available under the full BA-CVA, and only for hedges meeting the eligibility criteria set in 11.17-11.19. The framework confines eligible external CVA hedges to specified treatment (excluded from market risk capital, capitalized in the CVA portfolio if internal and eligible), and the reduced version recognizes no hedges at all. Conclude that a bank claiming hedge offsets must both elect the full BA-CVA and demonstrate each hedge satisfies the eligibility tests; ineligible hedges default to trading-book capital treatment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 639 / 11.13):** "The full version recognizes counterparty spread hedges and is intended for banks that hedge CVA risk."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 637 / 11.10):** "Eligibility criteria for CVA hedges are specified in 11.17 to 11.19 for the BA-CVA and in 11.37 to 11.39 for the SA-CVA."

### [[Reduced Version of BA-CVA]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When choosing between the two BA-CVA variants, understand they are nested, not independent: the reduced version is a component of the full version's calculation, used 'as a conservative means to limit hedging recognition.' The full version recognizes counterparty spread hedges and suits banks that hedge CVA, while the reduced version zeroes out hedge recognition for non-hedging banks. Conclude that a bank claiming hedge benefits must run the full BA-CVA and its more demanding inputs, and that the reduced figure acts as a floor on how much hedging benefit the full version can deliver.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 639 / 11.13):** "The full version recognizes counterparty spread hedges and is intended for banks that hedge CVA risk."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 639):** "the reduced BA-CVA is also part of the full BA-CVA capital calculations as a conservative means to limit hedging recognition."
