**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected

## Why this community

Standardization of the SME classification used across SAMA-supervised sectors. The circular fixes what counts as a micro/small/medium enterprise, which drives thresholds, reporting, and prudential/consumer treatments that turn on firm size.

## How members connect

- Single-member community: the circular is the authoritative source of the SME definition itself.
- Functions as a definitional anchor other rules rely on when they scope obligations by enterprise size; no internal edges, so treat linkages to size-based provisions as external cross-references.
