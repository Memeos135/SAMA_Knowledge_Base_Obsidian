# Calculation of Expected Losses

## Connections

### [[Expected Loss for SL under Slotting]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining EL for specialized lending exposures under the IRB approach, do not apply the general PD×LGD formula in chapter 15; the general rule explicitly diverts you to the slotting method. Chapter 15 (para 15.3) states that for exposures subject to the supervisory slotting criteria, EL is calculated as described in the slotting chapter (paras 13.8–13.12), and chapter 13 confirms it sets out both RWA and EL for SL. Conclude that for PF/OF/CF/IPRE slotting exposures you must use the supervisory-category EL treatment in chapter 13, then feed the result into the chapter 15 provision/EL difference mechanism.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 174 / para 15.3):** "For exposures subject to the supervisory slotting criteria EL is calculated as described in the chapter on the supervisory slotting approach (paragraphs 13.8 to 13.12)."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 138 / para 13.1):** "This chapter sets out the calculation of risk weighted assets and expected losses for specialized lending (SL) exposures subject to the supervisory slotting approach."

### [[Treatment of Expected Losses and Provisions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how expected losses affect a bank's regulatory capital under the IRB approach, treat 'Calculation of Expected Losses' as a sub-step of the broader chapter on EL and provisions, not a standalone rule. Chapter 15 both defines how EL is computed (PD x LGD x EAD, with supervisory or best-estimate values for defaulted exposures) and directs that the difference between EL and total eligible provisions is then included in or deducted from capital per the Basel III capital rules (Circular 341000015689). For a capital-adequacy assessment you should read the calculation and the provisions-comparison together, and exclude securitization EL, which is handled separately at paragraph 18.36.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 174 / para 15.2):** "A bank must sum the EL amount (defined as EL multiplied by exposure at default) associated with its exposures to which the IRB approach is applied...to obtain a total EL amount."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 174 / para 15.1):** "the method by which the difference between provisions...and EL may be included in or must be deducted from regulatory capital"
