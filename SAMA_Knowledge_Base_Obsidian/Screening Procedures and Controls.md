# Screening Procedures and Controls

## Connections

### [[Sanctions List Management]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When operating a sanctions program under these Rules, understand that 'Screening Procedures and Controls' (Chapter Three) and 'Sanctions List Management' (Chapter Four) are sequential, interdependent obligations within the same TFS regime: the lists maintained under Chapter Four supply the reference data against which the screening required by Chapter Three is performed. Both derive their binding force from the same TFS Rules and share the defined terms Sanctions Lists and Designated Persons. For a compliance decision, do not assess screening adequacy without confirming that list-management (currency and completeness of the applicable UNSC-derived lists) is also satisfied — a defect in one chapter undermines the obligation in the other.

### [[Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping screening obligations, read 'Screening Procedures and Controls' (Chapter Three) as an integral, mandatory part of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules rather than optional guidance. The parent Rules impose binding minimum TFS obligations on all SAMA-supervised institutions and organize the screening duty into a dedicated chapter, so the screening obligations carry the same compulsory force and rely on the Rules' defined terms and governance allocation. For a compliance decision, conclude that screening is a required control whose standard is fixed by the TFS Rules — verify the chapter text and the definitions in Chapter One before assessing adequacy.
