# ABCP Conduit

## Connections

### [[Internal Assessment Approach (SEC-IAA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If a bank holds unrated exposures (liquidity facilities, credit enhancements) to an ABCP programme, the SEC-IAA is the approach that lets it use internal assessments — but only for these conduit-related exposures and only with SAMA approval. Paragraph 21.1 ties SEC-IAA specifically to 'securitization exposures extended to ABCP programmes', requiring at least one approved IRB model and mapping of internal assessments to ECAI equivalents that then feed the SEC-ERBA risk weights. You would conclude that SEC-IAA is not a free-standing approach but a defined route conditioned on the ABCP context; check that the ABCP itself is externally rated (21.2(1)) and that SAMA approval has been obtained before relying on internal assessments.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 269 / 18.96):** "ABCP conduit/conduit – ABCP conduit, being the special purpose vehicle which can issue commercial paper"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 306 / 21.1):** "a bank may use its internal assessments of the credit quality of its securitization exposures extended to ABCP programmes"

### [[STC Short-Term (ABCP) Securitization Criteria]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating STC compliance for an ABCP conduit, recognize that the definition of the conduit itself is embedded in the short-term criteria block (18.96) and that compliance is tested at two levels: paragraph 18.97 requires conduit-level exposures (e.g. investing in the conduit's commercial paper) to satisfy the criteria at both conduit and transaction levels, while 18.98 lets transaction-level exposures qualify if the relevant transaction-level criteria are met. This means an ABCP conduit is not a single STC unit — the sponsor must give representations and warranties per criterion. Conclude that STC relief for a conduit exposure depends on identifying which level your exposure sits at and confirming the correspondingly applicable criteria, not on a global conduit assessment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 270 / Art 18.97):** "compliance with the short-term STC capital criteria is only achieved if the criteria are satisfied at both the conduit and transaction levels"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 269 / Art 18.96):** "ABCP conduit/conduit – ABCP conduit, being the special purpose vehicle which can issue commercial paper"
