# AML/CTF Risk Assessment

**Cohesion:** 0.29 - loosely connected
**Members:** 7 nodes

## Why this community

AML/CTF risk-assessment regime: the enterprise-wide obligation to identify and assess ML/TF risk and apply a risk-based approach, anchored in the primary AML and terrorism-financing statutes and interpreted through SAMA's AML/CTF Guide against FATF standards.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Anti-Money Laundering Law and Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing are the enforceable primary sources; the AML/CTF Guide is subordinate guidance that cites both and operationalizes them.
- FATF is cited as the external standard-setter informing how the Guide reads the RBA and risk-assessment duties — persuasive/benchmark, not primary law.
- Obligation chain: the Risk-Based Approach and ML/TF Risk Assessment are the core substantive duties; Board of Directors Responsibilities links to ML/TF Risk Assessment, placing ultimate accountability for the assessment at board level.
