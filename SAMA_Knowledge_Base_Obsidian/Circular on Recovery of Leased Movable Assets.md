# Circular on Recovery of Leased Movable Assets

## Connections

### [[Finance Lease Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a licensed lessor on repossession of leased movable assets, treat this circular as subordinate to the Finance Lease Law and read the two together: the circular derives its authority from paragraph (7) of Article (25) of the Finance Lease Law, which permits the lessor to recover movable assets only where the contract grants that right and only under supervision of firms licensed by the Ministry of Justice per the Enforcement Law. The circular adds an interim constraint — pending Ministry of Justice licensing, lessors must comply with Ministry of Interior instructions barring self-help repossession of vehicles. The consequence: a lessor cannot rely on the contractual repossession right alone; it must use competent authorities, and staff who repossess without them are exposed to prosecution.
