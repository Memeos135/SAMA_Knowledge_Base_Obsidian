# Shariah Governance Framework

**Cohesion:** 0.38 - loosely connected
**Members:** 7 nodes

## Why this community

Shariah governance regime for Islamic financial institutions: the framework mandating board-level Shariah oversight, an independent Shariah Committee, dedicated compliance and audit functions, and controlled product development.

## How members connect

- The Shariah Governance Framework is the governing instrument; it references and allocates roles across Board, Executive Management, Shariah Committee, Shariah Compliance Function, and Internal Shariah Audit.
- Obligation chain: the Board carries ultimate responsibility and references the Shariah Committee, whose rulings bind the institution and gate the Development Process of Shariah-Compliant Products.
- Three-lines structure implied: Shariah Compliance Function (ongoing) and Internal Shariah Audit (independent assurance) are distinct control functions subordinate to the Framework.
