# Risk Management

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulation of Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a finance company's ongoing obligations, treat risk management as a mandated internal function rather than good practice, because the Implementing Regulation's own definitions embed it. The Regulation's definition of 'senior management' expressly includes the persons responsible for risk management, internal audit and compliance functions, tying these control functions to the governance obligations imposed under the Finance Companies Control Law. You should conclude that a licensed finance company must maintain a distinct risk-management function and that its heads fall within the accountable senior-management perimeter for supervisory purposes.
