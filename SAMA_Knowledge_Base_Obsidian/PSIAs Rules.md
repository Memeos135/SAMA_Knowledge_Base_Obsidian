# PSIAs Rules

## Connections

### [[Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the risk obligations of a bank offering profit-sharing investment accounts, treat the RMF as importing the PSIAs Rules by reference rather than as a self-contained standard: clause 6.5 of the RMF expressly directs PSIA-offering banks to comply with 'the risk management requirements set out in the PSIAs Rules (September 2022) and any subsequent updates.' Because the RMF and PSIAs Rules are both issued under the same Banking Control Law authority, the PSIA-specific requirements are enforceable in their own right and are not fully reproduced in the RMF. You should therefore consult the current PSIAs Rules directly for PSIA risk/allocation obligations, and confirm you are using the latest version rather than relying only on the RMF summary.
