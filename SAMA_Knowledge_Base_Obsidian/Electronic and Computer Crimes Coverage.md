# Electronic and Computer Crimes Coverage

### [[Bankers Blanket Bond  Financial Institution Bond|Bankers Blanket Bond / Financial Institution Bond]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing a bank's crime/fidelity insurance program, do not treat ECC coverage as freely purchasable on its own: the guide states 'ECC coverage may not be written without a BBB/FIB being present,' making the Bankers Blanket Bond/Financial Institution Bond a precondition for ECC. Both are listed among the six primary coverage types under Regular Insurance, and ECC is described as a 'companion piece' generally purchased in the same limit as the BBB/FIB. In assessing coverage completeness you would confirm a BBB/FIB is in force before crediting the bank with ECC protection and would expect their limits to be aligned.

- **What this link tells you:** When mapping insurance coverage to the electronic-crime risk category, note that ECC coverage is the guide's designated risk-transfer response to third-party fraud affecting computer and telecommunications systems. Treat this as guidance describing market coverage structure rather than a SAMA mandate to buy either.
