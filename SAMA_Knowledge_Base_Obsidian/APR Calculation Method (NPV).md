# APR Calculation Method (NPV)

## Connections

### [[Rules Governing Calculation of APR]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how a finance provider must compute APR for retail lending disclosures, treat Article 6's net-present-value method as an operative requirement of the parent Rules, not a standalone concept. The APR Calculation Method is the substantive core of the Rules Governing Calculation of APR, and both bind all finance providers engaging in retail lending under Article 2's scope and must be read with the Finance Companies Control Law, Regulations for Consumer Financing and Circular No. 381000095091. Conclude that any APR shown in advertising, offers, contracts, and periodic statements must trace to this NPV formula and SAMA's Excel calculator; a provider cannot rely on an alternative computation method to satisfy the Rules.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / Art 6):** "The APR should be calculated based on the net present value method using the following formula"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13 / Art 2-3):** "These Rules shall be applicable on all finance providers engaging in retail lending... to standardize the Annual Percentage Rate (APR) calculation"
