# Contingency Funding Plan (CFP)

## Connections

### [[Early Warning Indicators]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's Contingency Funding Plan meets SAMA's ILAAP expectations, do not treat early warning indicators as a separate optional checklist — the CFP guidance expressly folds them into the invocation and escalation architecture. The CFP must include 'early warning signals using benchmark indicators' with defined triggers (e.g. 30% or 50% decline in collections for three continuous months), and Section B independently lists the indicators (concentrations, currency mismatches, limit breaches, rating downgrade, rising funding costs, deposit outflows). A reviewer should therefore verify that the same benchmark indicators feed the CFP's escalation triggers, and treat an absence of quantified triggers as a compliance gap in the CFP itself, not merely in monitoring.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 26 / Section C (CFP)):** "Early warning signals using benchmark indicators i.e. Availability of credit lines, collection efficiency... These signals should have triggers based on 30% or 50% decline in collections for continuous three months."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 25 / Section B):** "An important component of liquidity risk management and the contingency funding plan is the early warning indicators including: Growing concentrations in assets or liabilities."

### [[Liquidity Stress-testing]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When preparing a Contingency Funding Plan, do not design it in isolation from stress-testing: the guidelines require CFP invocation triggers, funding-source assessments and early-warning signals to be calibrated to and tested against liquidity stress scenarios, and both sit together in Annexure (1). The CFP must be a standalone document attached to the ILAAP, and its testing explicitly includes liquidity stress-tests and quantitative decline triggers. For a compliance decision, conclude that CFP assumptions (amount and speed of contingency funding, ability to sell assets under stress) must be consistent with the stress-testing outputs; a CFP whose triggers or funding estimates ignore the stress results would be internally inconsistent with SAMA's requirements.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 26 / Section C.i–ii):** "it should be prepared as a standalone document and attached to the ILAAP document... Regularly tested and updated... - Liquidity stress-test[ing]"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 22 / Annexure (1) A.1):** "A Bank must conduct on a regular basis appropriate stress-tests so as to: a) Identify sources of potential liquidity strain"
