The obligations (direct practice through the Bank, qualified unit director, IFCE-certified Authorised Employees, SAMA non-objection/notification, no dealings with non-Clients) all attach to the marketing-and-distribution activity as defined. ### [[Insurance Company Obligations in Bancassurance]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]

Conclude that obligations attach to distinct parties under one contractual framework, and confirm from the specific article which duties bind the Company versus the Bank before allocating compliance responsibility.

### [[Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what conduct the Rules actually regulate, anchor on the defined term 'Bancassurance Activities,' because scope and every downstream obligation turn on this definition. Conclude that activities outside marketing/distribution, or dealings with persons who are not the Bank's Clients, fall outside this regime — note in particular that the Bank must not conduct such activities with other than its Clients.
