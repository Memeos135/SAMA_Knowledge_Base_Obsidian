# Circular on Outsourcing Debt Collection Operations

## Connections

### [[Cancellation of Debt Collection Licensing Requirement]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a bank or finance company on outsourcing debt collection, treat these two circulars as a single evolving regime rather than independent instruments: the earlier circular (10866) removed the requirement that the outsourced collector be SAMA-licensed (allowing contracting with a non-licensed collector after a SAMA no-objection letter), and the later circular (10908) formally records SAMA's decision to cancel the licensing requirement for financing-entity debt-collection activity. Both rest on the same authority chain — the SAMA Law (Royal Decree M/36) and the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51) — plus the Governor-approved amendments to the Finance Companies Implementing Regulation and the Support Activities Licensing Rules. For a compliance decision you would conclude that licensing is no longer the gating condition, but verify the current no-objection/no-impediment obligations and that previously licensed collectors update their commercial-register activity as 10908 requires.

### [[Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are assessing whether a bank or finance company may outsource debt collection, read this circular against the amended Implementing Regulations, because the circular expressly issues in reference to the Governor's approval of the amendments to the Implementing Regulations and to the Finance Support Activities Licensing Rules. The circular changes the position by allowing outsourcing to a debt-collection entity 'without requiring that it be licensed by SAMA,' subject to relevant rules. Conclude that the licensing regime referenced in the Implementing Regulations no longer conditions permissible collection outsourcing, but the outsourcing must still comply with related instructions — verify current requirements before relying on the relaxation.

### [[Rules for Licensing Finance Support Activities]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping whether a debt-collection outsourcing arrangement must use a SAMA-licensed collection entity, note that the finance-support licensing rules expressly exclude debt collection from 'finance support activities,' and this circular is the instrument that removes the prior requirement that banks/finance companies only outsource collection to SAMA-licensed collection entities. The circular cites the amendments to both the Finance Companies Control Law Implementing Regulations and these support-activity licensing rules as its legal basis, cancelling the earlier restrictive circulars. For a compliance decision: outsourced collection now needs only a SAMA non-objection letter and compliance with relevant rules — you should not treat 'licensed collection entity' as a mandatory precondition, but must still verify the non-objection and outsourcing requirements.
