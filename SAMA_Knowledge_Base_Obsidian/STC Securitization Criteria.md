# STC Securitization Criteria

## Connections

### [[ABCP Conduit  Programme|ABCP Conduit / Programme]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether an ABCP-related exposure can qualify for the more favourable STC (simple, transparent, comparable) capital treatment, don't test the conduit alone: the STC scope provision (18.66-18.67) brings ABCP conduits and the transactions they finance into the STC framework, and 18.97 requires the short-term STC criteria to be met at BOTH the conduit and transaction levels for conduit-level exposures. The defined term 'ABCP conduit' in 18.96 is the anchor that ties the conduit-specific criteria (18.99 onward) into the broader STC criteria set (18.96-18.165). Conclude that STC alternative capital treatment for a conduit exposure fails unless dual-level compliance is demonstrated; a transaction-level pass is only sufficient for exposures at the transaction level.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 260 / 18.67):** "Exposures to ABCP conduits and/or transactions financed by ABCP conduits, where the conduit and/or transactions financed by it meet the criteria in 18.96 to 18.165."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 277 / 18.97):** "compliance with the short-term STC capital criteria is only achieved if the criteria are satisfied at both the conduit and transaction levels."

### [[Traditional Securitization]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a securitization exposure qualifies for the favorable STC capital treatment, first confirm the transaction is a traditional securitization, because the STC framework's scope is expressly limited to that structure. The standard defines a traditional securitization as a tranched structure serviced by an underlying pool's cash flows, and 18.66 restricts STC eligibility to such transactions (including ABCP conduits) that meet the detailed criteria in 18.72–18.165. Conclude that a synthetic securitization can never be STC-compliant, and that STC status further requires ongoing satisfaction of the criteria plus originator/sponsor disclosure enabling the investor's own assessment before alternative treatment is applied.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 260 / 18.66):** "Only traditional securitizations including exposures to ABCP conduits and exposures to transactions financed by ABCP conduits fall within the scope of the simple, transparent and comparable (STC) framework"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 237 / 18.2):** "A traditional securitization is a structure where the cash flow from an underlying pool of exposures is used to service at least two different stratified risk positions or tranches"
