# Customer Collection Controls

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Consumer-protection/conduct controls on debt collection practices, with specific application to individual borrowers and real estate financiers.

## How members connect

- The collection-controls circular is conceptually related to the real estate financier obligations circular — collection conduct duties overlap where real estate finance is extended to individuals.
- Both operate in the finance-company/consumer regime, constraining how financiers may pursue individual customers.
- Decision consequence: a real estate financier must satisfy both its sector-specific obligations and the general individual-customer collection controls when recovering from individual borrowers.
