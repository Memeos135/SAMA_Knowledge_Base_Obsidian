# Commodity Risk

## Connections

### [[Sensitivities-Based Method]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When computing standardised market risk capital, treat commodity risk not as a stand-alone charge but as one of the defined risk classes fed into the sensitivities-based method. The framework lists commodity risk among the market risk classes and defines commodity delta risk factors along delivery-location and time-to-maturity dimensions, which are then run through the delta/vega/curvature sensitivities-based method and aggregated with prescribed risk weights and correlations. For a capital decision this means commodity positions must be capitalised by mapping their sensitivities into the SBM buckets, so you should confirm commodity exposures are captured under the SBM rather than treated separately.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 398 / 7.13):** "the commodity delta risk factors are all the commodity spot prices... Commodity delta risk factors are defined along two dimensions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 383):** "the risk-weighted sensitivities are aggregated using specified correlation parameters to recognise diversification benefits between risk factors"
