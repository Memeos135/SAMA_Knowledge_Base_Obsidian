# Key Covenants

## Connections

### [[Glossary of Technical Terms]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing restructuring documentation, use the guidance's definition of covenants to understand what the bank can enforce, since 'Key Covenants' is a technical term explained within the same SAMA restructuring guidance. The text defines covenants as undertakings whose breach gives the bank the right to call the loan, charge fees, or apply higher interest, and states that all restructuring agreements should contain them at a minimum. Conclude that covenant provisions are the enforceable trigger points a compliance reviewer should confirm are present (financial statements, tax payment, no sale without approval), and that a covenant default does not always translate into practical acceleration where the loan is performing.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 87):** "Violation of any covenant gives the banks right to call the loan, charge fees, or collect interest at a higher rate... All restructuring agreements should contain covenants."
- **Grounding — related node (Glossary of Technical Terms):** "Covenants are undertakings (or promises) given by a borrower as part of a loan agreement."

### [[Restructuring Agreement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When drafting or reviewing a restructuring agreement, treat covenants as a mandatory component rather than optional add-ons, because the guideline states all restructuring agreements should contain covenants and specifies a minimum set. Covenants function as the bank's early-warning and enforcement lever — their violation gives the bank the right to call the loan, charge fees or raise the rate — so they operationalise the monitoring purpose of the restructuring agreement. For a compliance review, confirm that any restructuring agreement includes at least the stated minimum covenants (financial statement submission, tax payment, restriction on sale of the company without prior bank approval).
- **Grounding — this node (Page 87):** "All restructuring agreements should contain covenants. At a minimum, they should include provisions to submit financial statements; pay taxes as they become due; prohibit sale of company... without prior approval of bank."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 85):** "The restructuring agreement is the main document that regulates all the details of the workout."
