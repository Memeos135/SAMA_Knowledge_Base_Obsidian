# Finance Companies Control Law Implementing Regulation

## Connections

### [[Finance Contract Summary Form]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining which finance contracts must carry the summary form, read this circular as a subordinate instrument executing higher regulatory articles. The circular issues the 'Finance Contract Summary Form' expressly under Article 79 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law and paragraph (1) of Article 5 of the updated Consumer Finance Regulations, and states the form must head all finance contracts subject to those instruments. The consequence: the form's scope is fixed by those parent provisions — it applies to contracts governed by the Finance Companies Control Law and updated consumer-finance rules, and the summary itself does not waive or replace the underlying contractual obligations (as the form's own notice states).
