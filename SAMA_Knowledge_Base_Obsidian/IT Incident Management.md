# IT Incident Management

## Connections

### [[SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping incident-handling obligations, treat IT Incident Management (3.3.8) as connected to business continuity because both address service disruption and this subdomain carries a mandatory SAMA-reporting duty. The subdomain requires timely identification, response and handling of incidents impacting information assets and 'to report relevant incidents to SAMA, according to a defined communication protocol,' complementing the continuity objectives the BCM Framework governs. For a compliance decision you should conclude that incident response and external SAMA notification are enforceable Control Requirements, and that continuity planning does not substitute for the incident-reporting obligation. The specific reporting protocol/thresholds are not detailed in the provided text and should be verified in the primary instruments.
