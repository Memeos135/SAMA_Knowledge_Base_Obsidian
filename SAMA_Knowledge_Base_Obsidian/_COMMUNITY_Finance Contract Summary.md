# Finance Contract Summary

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Consumer-facing disclosure obligations for finance companies, tying a mandated contract summary document to the governing implementing regulation under the Finance Companies Control Law.

## How members connect

- The Finance Contract Summary Form is the prescribed disclosure instrument required under the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law.
- Hierarchy: Law -> Implementing Regulation -> mandated Form; the Regulation is the enforceable source, the Form the compliance artifact.
- Decision-usefulness: finance companies must issue the summary to borrowers per the Regulation's terms; the Form defines the minimum disclosure content.
