# Financial Investigations Unit

## Connections

### [[Exchange Houses Operating Procedures Guide]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising an exchange house on its reporting obligations, note that the Guide defines the Financial Investigations/Intelligence Unit (FIU) as the competent authority to receive and analyse suspicious-activity reports across all financial and non-financial institutions, and directs exchange houses to report to it. This link tells you the FIU is the mandatory recipient of suspicion-based reports and the trigger point at which an exchange house may enhance, restrict or terminate a relationship, grounded in the Guide's definition and its instruction to report suspected ML/TF to the unit. Conclude that reporting to the FIU is a distinct statutory duty under the AML regime (not a SAMA supervisory report) and verify the exact reporting channel and thresholds against the AML Law and its Implementing Regulation, which the Guide supplements.

- **Caveat:** Both sides are OCR-garbled Arabic; excerpts are paraphrases. Confirm the FIU's role, name and reporting mechanics against the primary AML Law/Bylaws text before relying.

### [[Suspicious Transaction Reporting]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining where an exchanger's suspicious-activity report must go, treat these two nodes as a single obligation chain within the same Exchange Business Procedures Manual: the Manual's definition of the Financial Investigations Unit (FIU) fixes it as the authority empowered to receive and analyse reports of activity suspected of money laundering and terrorism financing, and the Manual's 'Reporting' (البلاغ) section imposes the duty to report. The consequence is that a suspicious-transaction obligation is discharged by reporting to the FIU as defined here, not to SAMA generally or to an authority the firm selects; confirm the FIU as the statutory recipient before concluding a report is properly filed. Because both nodes derive from the same document, the definitional linkage is internally consistent.
