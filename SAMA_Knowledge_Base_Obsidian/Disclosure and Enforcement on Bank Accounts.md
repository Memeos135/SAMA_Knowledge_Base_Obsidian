# Disclosure and Enforcement on Bank Accounts

## Connections

### [[Chapter II Supervisory Rules and Controls|Chapter II: Supervisory Rules and Controls]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's obligations to disclose or enforce on customer accounts under SAMA request, read the disclosure/enforcement provisions as part of Chapter II's supervisory rules and controls, not as a separate procedural regime. The disclosure and enforcement duties (blocking, compulsory deduction, comprehensive relationship search, confidentiality) are grounded in the same supervisory chapter that also mandates the electronic record and account controls, so they share the same enforceable status and apply across all customer and account categories catalogued there. You should conclude that when SAMA issues a disclosure/enforcement request, the required search spans every relationship type the Chapter II controls capture, and that these requests may be received only by SAMA except where SAMA's instructions state otherwise.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 22 / 10.1):** "Such requests shall be received only by SAMA except for cases stated in SAMA's instructions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / TOC):** "Chapter II Supervisory Rules and Controls 9"

### [[SAMA (Saudi Central Bank)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a bank on how to respond to a request to freeze, deduct from, or disclose a customer's accounts, treat SAMA as the exclusive and controlling channel — the Rules for Bank Accounts vest disclosure and enforcement authority in SAMA and require banks to act only in the form, manner and period SAMA specifies. Requests may be received 'only by SAMA except for cases stated in SAMA's instructions,' so the link tells you a bank cannot lawfully action a third party's freeze/disclosure demand outside that channel. Conclude that any enforcement instruction must be validated against SAMA's authority and confidentiality requirements before execution.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 22 / section 10.1):** "Such requests shall be received only by SAMA except for cases stated in SAMA's instructions. Banks shall carry out requests for disclosure and enforcement according to the form, manner and period specified by SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1):** "Rules for Bank Accounts ... Please refer to SAMA's website (www.sama.gov.sa) for the last updated and amended version of the Rules."
