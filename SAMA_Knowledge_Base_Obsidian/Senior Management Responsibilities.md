# Senior Management Responsibilities

## Connections

### [[AMLCTF Compliance Function|AML/CTF Compliance Function]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping accountability for AML/CTF, do not treat the compliance function and senior management as interchangeable: the guide requires the AML/CTF compliance officer to be appointed at senior-management level yet to report independently to the board, and separately places continuous implementation and monitoring of compliance on senior management. The link matters because the defined term 'senior management' expressly includes the compliance and AML/CFT department heads, so the officer sits within senior management while also being answerable above it. Conclude that responsibility runs on two tracks — senior management owns ongoing implementation, the compliance officer owns the operating program with independent board reporting — and check that the institution has preserved the officer's independence rather than collapsing it into ordinary senior-management line control.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Definitions):** "Senior management: It includes the managing director, the chief executive officer... and directors of key departments in charge of functions of risk management, internal audit, compliance, AML/CFT"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 56 / para 9.1):** "Ensuring that the officer responsible for the AML/CTF compliance function works independently and administratively and technically reports to the board of directors and its committees."
