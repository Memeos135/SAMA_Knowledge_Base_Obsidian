### [[Outsourcing Rules for Finance Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what an outsourcing arrangement legally requires of a licensed finance company, treat this concept as the subject matter of the Outsourcing Rules document itself — the document IS the source of the concept's defined terms and obligations. The Rules define 'outsourcing to an external service provider' as any contract/agreement under which an external provider delivers functions to the finance company, and expressly make the finance company non-exempt from liability if the provider breaches applicable rules. You would conclude that outsourcing does not transfer regulatory responsibility: the finance company must have a board-approved policy, conduct risk review at contracting and renewal, and remain liable for the outsourced functions.

### [[Real Estate Refinance Companies Regulation Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing outsourcing arrangements of a real estate refinance company, read the Rules for Outsourcing to External Service Providers as the applicable operative regime — the Refinance Companies Regulation Rules contain a dedicated chapter on 'outsourcing tasks to external service providers,' and the Outsourcing Rules impose the enforceable substance (board-approved written policy, non-delegable responsibility, due diligence on the provider's licences, and SAMA/auditor access rights). The refinance company remains liable even where the external provider fails to comply with applicable rules. For compliance decisions, treat the Outsourcing Rules as the detailed standard governing that chapter, and verify that refinance-company outsourcing meets its material/non-material task classification and retained-liability requirements.
