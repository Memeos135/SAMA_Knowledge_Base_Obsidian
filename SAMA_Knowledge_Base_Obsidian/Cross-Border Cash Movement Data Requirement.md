# Cross-Border Cash Movement Data Requirement

## Connections

### [[Cross-Border Cash Transport]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are mapping cross-border cash reporting duties for licensed money changers, treat these two items as sitting in different obligation buckets rather than one regime. The 5565 guide describes 'cross-border cash and negotiable-instrument transport' as a business activity governed by the exchange-business rules and AML/CTF rules, whereas 5645 is a distinct SAMA instruction requiring all licensees to submit monthly data on inbound/outbound cash movements through land, air and sea border crossings under Articles 15 and 17 of the exchange-business regulations. They appear conceptually linked (both concern currency crossing the border) but the connection is thematic, not a defined cross-reference, so verify the primary texts before assuming the guide's transport section discharges the 5645 monthly reporting requirement.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1):** "provide the Agency with data on inbound and outbound cash movements to/from the Kingdom through border crossings (land, air, sea) on a monthly basis (paraphrase)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 (table of contents)):** "cross-border transport of cash and negotiable financial instruments (paraphrase of the guide's activity heading)"
- **Caveat:** INFERRED link; both sides' Arabic is OCR-garbled, so excerpts are paraphrased. No explicit cross-reference between the two documents is present — confirm against primary texts.

### [[Rules Regulating Exchange Business (Decision 1730)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a licensed exchanger's data-submission duties, read the Rules Regulating Exchange Business (Minister of Finance Decision 1730) as the enabling authority and this circular as its exercise: node B expressly invokes Article 15(a) (duty to provide SAMA with all data it requests at any time) and 15(c) (SAMA may issue any instructions it deems necessary for supervision), plus Article 17 on record-keeping, to impose a specific monthly requirement to report cross-border cash movements through land, air and sea ports. The consequence: this cross-border cash-data obligation is not a standalone rule but a delegated instruction under the Exchange Rules, so it binds category (A) and (B) licensees and its legal force flows from Decision 1730 — check the current version of the Rules to confirm the delegation still stands.
