# Debt Instrument Issuance via SPVs

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are advising a finance company on issuing debt instruments through an SPV, note that the permission and its conditions flow directly from the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51) and its Implementing Regulation, not merely from CMA rules. The circular cites Article 14 of the Law (permitting issuance of securities/sukuk under the Capital Market Law, proportionate to assets and financial position) and Article 66 of the Implementing Regulation (issuance allowed only after a SAMA no-objection letter). You should conclude that dual clearance is mandatory: apply to the CMA to establish the SPV under its SPV rules, and obtain SAMA's prior written no-objection before issuing any debt instrument.
