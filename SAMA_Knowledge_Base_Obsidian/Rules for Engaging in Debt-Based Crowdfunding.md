# Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding

## Connections

### [[Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the duty-holder for every obligation in these Rules, note that the Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company is the defined licensed entity on whom substantially all requirements fall. Article 1 defines it as a joint-stock company licensed to engage in the activity, and operative articles (segregation of Collection Account funds, disclosure, cooperation with SAMA inspections) address that entity by name. The reader should therefore attribute compliance liability — including the express warning that refusal to provide information to SAMA is a violation of the Rules and the Law — to the licensed Company, not to Participants or Institutional Beneficiaries.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3, Art 1.2.3):** "Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company: A joint-stock company licensed to engage in Debt-Based Crowdfunding activity"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 21):** "The Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company and its staff shall not conceal ... any information, documents, violations"

### [[Fit and Proper Form]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing who must clear fit-and-proper vetting, cross-read the Fit and Proper Form against the Rules' governance provisions rather than treating it as a standalone licensing checklist item. The Rules define the licensed entity and impose senior-management eligibility criteria — professional eligibility, KSA residency, qualification/experience, and a clean record under the Banking Control, insurance, finance, and Capital Market laws — which are the substantive standards the Form operationalizes for founders and board/committee candidates. The reader should therefore verify each vetted individual meets the Rules' Article 7 senior-management conditions, since the Form is the evidentiary vehicle for those substantive tests.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6, Art 7):** "All candidates for supervisory and executive positions ... must meet the professional eligibility requirements set by SAMA"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3, item 6):** "SAMA's Fit and Proper Form for each candidate member in the board and its sub-committees ... signed by the candidate"
- **Caveat:** Link between the Form and Article 7 criteria is a reasoned cross-reference; the Guidelines do not expressly cite Article 7 as the Form's basis, so confirm against the primary text.

### [[Guidelines for Applying for Debt-Based Crowdfunding Licensing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what governs a debt-based crowdfunding applicant, read the Guidelines as procedural implementation subordinate to the substantive Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding (and the Finance Companies Control Law above them). The Guidelines expressly borrow their defined terms from, and condition application completion on fulfilling the requirements set in, those Rules; they do not create standalone obligations. Consequently, the reader should resolve any definitional or substantive question by consulting the Rules and the Law, and treat the Guidelines only as the licensing-process layer.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3, Art 1):** "the following terms and phrases ... shall have the same meanings stated in the Finance Companies Control Law"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Page 4):** "terms and phrases ... shall have the same meanings stated in the Finance Companies Control Law and the Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding"
