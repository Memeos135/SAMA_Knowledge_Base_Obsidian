# Basel Committee Problem Assets Guidance

## Connections

### [[Prudential Treatment of Problem Assets]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what standard governs classification of non-performing and forbearance-treated loans for banks in KSA, read SAMA's circular as adopting the Basel Committee's problem-assets guidance by reference rather than restating it: the SAMA circular expressly directs banks to comply with the guidance issued by the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision and points to the BIS publication (d403). The cited Basel document therefore supplies the substantive criteria (scope of assessment, application level, disclosure under Pillar 3, IRB classification) that the SAMA instrument makes binding on all banks operating in the Kingdom. A compliance reader should treat the external Basel text as the operative content and check the linked BIS publication for the actual definitions and thresholds, since the SAMA circular itself does not reproduce them.
