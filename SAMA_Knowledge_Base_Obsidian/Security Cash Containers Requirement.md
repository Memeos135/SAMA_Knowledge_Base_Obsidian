# Security Cash Containers Requirement

## Connections

### [[Security-Inked Damaged Cash Compensation Mechanism]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's or money-transport firm's liability for security-inked damaged cash, treat the security-container requirement as a precondition, not a separate topic: the compensation mechanism explicitly conditions eligibility on the beneficiary having used containers matching the technical specifications approved under the earlier SAMA circular on mandatory secure cash-transport containers. The document itself opens by stating it supplements those container instructions and requires beneficiaries to comply with them. Practically, a compliance reviewer evaluating a compensation claim should verify container compliance first, because negligence in handling the security bags shifts the operational cost of compensation onto the requesting bank per each note.
