# Secondary Market of Real Estate Finance

## Connections

### [[Real Estate Finance Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When analysing rights to trade or refinance real estate finance receivables, read 'Secondary Market' as a term defined in Article 1 of this Law — the trading of a finance entity's rights arising from primary-market contracts — and tie it to SAMA's licensing of real estate refinance companies and its mandate to facilitate data flow between primary and secondary markets. This tells you that trading of lender rights is a distinct, regulated layer separate from the borrower-facing primary contract, and that SAMA-licensed refinance vehicles (in which PIF or licensed finance entities may co-own) are the intended channel. Conclude that secondary-market activity is governed here and, where securities are involved, also engages the Capital Market Law; confirm the licensing and disclosure route before treating a receivables transfer as freely tradeable.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Art 1):** "Secondary Market: Trading of finance entity's rights arising from primary market contracts."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Art 2):** "License a joint stock company (or more) for real estate refinance ... facilitate the flow of data between primary and secondary markets"
