## Connections

### [[Compliance Principles for Banks]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are mapping which conduct/compliance obligations reach exchange (money-changing) businesses, note that these two instruments appear to sit in different but adjacent regimes: 4878 sets the compliance-function principles for banks, while 4939 extends the separate 'Code of Conduct and Work Ethics in Financial Institutions' regime to exchange companies. The link is thematic (institutional governance/integrity), not a direct cross-reference — 4939 explicitly builds on a prior conduct circular, not on the bank compliance principles. Treat this as a lead only: verify each instrument's own defined scope, because the bank compliance principles are addressed to banks and do not automatically bind exchange centers, whereas the conduct principles were expressly widened to cover them.
