# Remediation Plan (RP)

## Connections

### [[Blue Team Report (BTR)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what evidence must underpin a Remediation Plan, note that the Blue Team Report is a co-equal mandatory input alongside the RTER — the RP is expressly to be based on both. The White Team must draft the RP from the Red Teaming Evaluation Report and the Blue Team Report, incorporating agreed recommendations from both Red and Blue Teams. A reviewer should therefore not accept a Remediation Plan that omits Blue Team findings, and should verify both source reports exist before treating the RP as complete.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 24 / Remediation Plan (RP)):** "The White Team should draft a Remediation Plan, which should be based on the Red Teaming Evaluation Report and the Blue Team Report."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 24 / Conclusions):** "The conclusions regarding the required and suggested improvements (from both the Blue and Red Team)"

### [[Red Team Evaluation Report (RTER)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When checking closure of a red teaming exercise, treat the Remediation Plan as directly derived from the Red Team Evaluation Report — it cannot be assessed in isolation. The framework requires the White Team to draft the Remediation Plan based on the Red Teaming Evaluation Report (and the Blue Team Report), setting improvement areas, priorities and a roadmap. A reviewer should confirm that RP recommendations trace back to RTER findings and that progress is periodically reported to the Cyber Security Committee and the Green Team, since that reporting obligation is what makes the exercise's outcome supervisable.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 24 / Remediation Plan (RP)):** "The White Team should draft a Remediation Plan, which should be based on the Red Teaming Evaluation Report and the Blue Team Report."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 15 / 5.4 Reporting):** "These observations and findings provide the basis of evaluating the detection and response capabilities of the Blue Team."

### [[White Team]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying who owns post-exercise follow-up, the Remediation Plan is a White Team deliverable, not a vendor product — the White Team must draft it based on the Red Teaming Evaluation Report and the Blue Team Report, and monitor and report its progress to the institution's Cyber Security Committee and the Green Team. This anchors accountability for closing identified gaps (prevention, detection, response, recover) inside the Member Organization. For a compliance decision, check that the White Team both produced the RP and maintained periodic status reporting to the Cyber Security Committee and SAMA's Green Team, since remediation follow-through — not just the test — is what the Framework expects.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 24 / Remediation Plan (RP)):** "The White Team should draft a Remediation Plan ... status and progress ... is monitored and periodically reported to the Cyber Security Committee ... as well as the Green Team"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 18 / Section 6.4):** "The White Team should create a remediation plan based on the recommendations provided by the Red Team and Blue Team"
