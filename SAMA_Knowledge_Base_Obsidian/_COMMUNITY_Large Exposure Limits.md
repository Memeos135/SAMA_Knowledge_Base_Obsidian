# Large Exposure Limits

**Cohesion:** 0.05 - loosely connected
**Members:** 46 nodes

## Why this community

Bank prudential regime under the Banking Control Law and Basel III: concentration-risk (large exposures), capital adequacy (ICAAP/Pillar 1&2) and liquidity (LCR/HQLA) rules that bind licensed banks.

## How members connect

- The LEX Rules cite the Banking Control Law and Basel III capital framework as their legal/standard-setting basis, then reference operative concepts — Maximum Exposure Limits, Group of Connected Counterparties, exposure-value definition, SA-CCR, LTA, QCCP treatment.
- LCR guidance (SAMA General/Specific Guidance) cites the Basel III LCR standards and cascades into HQLA tiers (Level 1/2/2B), inflow/outflow run-off categories and monitoring tools.
- ICAAP/Pillar 2 links Basel II supervisory-review and stress-testing obligations to the capital adequacy assessment banks must perform.
- Cross-regime overlap: Covered Bonds and Level 2 assets appear in both the LEX and LCR contexts, and 'Treatment for Shariah' flags Shariah-compliant adaptation of these prudential measures.
