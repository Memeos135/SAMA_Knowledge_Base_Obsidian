# SMAR - Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk

## Connections

### [[DRC for Securitisations (CTP)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the capital treatment of securitisation default risk for a bank's trading book, read the DRC-for-securitisations (CTP) provisions as a component of, not a standalone rule from, the SMAR Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk framework. The DRC bucketing, JTD and hedging rules (paras 8.27–8.40) operate inside SMAR's overall market-risk capital regime, and SMAR disclosures explicitly enumerate DRC as one of the regulatory models (alongside ES and SES). You would therefore conclude that any DRC-for-securitisations figure must be aggregated and reported within the SMAR capital requirement rather than assessed in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 843 / Table MRB):** "the percentage of capital requirements covered by the models described for each of the regulatory models (expected shortfall (ES), default risk capital (DRC) requirement and stressed expected shortfall (SES)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 439 / Art 8.35):** "the total capital requirement for default risk securitisations must be calculated as a simple sum of the bucket-level capital requirements."

### [[Default Risk Capital (DRC) Requirement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When locating the DRC requirement in the rulebook, treat it as a component chapter of SMAR (Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk), not a standalone regime — DRC is the standardised-approach default-risk charge sitting alongside SMAR's sensitivities-based, residual-risk and internal-models measures. The SMAR document supplies the defined terms and cross-referenced paragraphs (e.g. expected shortfall under SMAR13) that frame where DRC contributes to the total market-risk capital charge. For scoping you would conclude that DRC obligations must be read together with the broader SMAR framework and cannot be assessed in isolation from the overall market-risk capital calculation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 845):** "Unconstrained expected shortfall: Expected shortfall (ES) as defined in SMAR13.1 to SMAR13.12"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 429 / 8):** "8- Standardised approach: default risk capital requirement"

### [[RWA for Market Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When computing the market-risk RWA component, the SMAR standard supplies the governing methodology — including the standardised approach (chapters 6–9) and the internal models approach with its expected-shortfall definitions (SMAR13.1–13.17) and non-modellable risk factor charges. Market-risk RWA also captures FX and commodities risk arising on banking-book positions, not just trading-book instruments. For a compliance decision, verify which SMAR approach applies, that banking-book FX/commodity exposures are included, and that any IMA use rests on the SMAR ES and NMRF provisions before relying on the market-risk figure.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 845 / row 1):** "Unconstrained expected shortfall: Expected shortfall (ES) as defined in SMAR13.1 to SMAR13.12, calculated without supervisory constraints"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 732 / 5.5):** "RWA for market risk for instruments in the trading book and for foreign exchange risk and commodities risk for exposures in the banking book"
