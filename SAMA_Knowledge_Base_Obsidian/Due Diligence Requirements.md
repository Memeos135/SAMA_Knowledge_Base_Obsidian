# Due Diligence Requirements

## Connections

### [[Exposures to Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When risk-weighting exposures to banks, do not treat rating-based bucketing as sufficient on its own: the due-diligence obligation in chapter 6 conditions and can override the base risk weight. Both provisions sit in the same SAMA credit-risk framework, and paragraph 7.16 (within Exposures to Banks) expressly ties back to the general duty in 6.1 to understand counterparties 'at origination and thereafter on a regular basis (at least annually).' Conclude that a bank must be able to demonstrate to SAMA that its due diligence supports the applied risk weight, and that due diligence can force a higher — never a lower — weight than the external rating implies.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10 / para 6.1):** "Banks must perform due diligence to ensure that they have an adequate understanding, at origination and thereafter on a regular basis (at least annually), of the risk profile ... of their counterparties."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 17 / para 7.16):** "Due diligence analysis must never result in the application of a lower risk weight than that determined by the external rating."
