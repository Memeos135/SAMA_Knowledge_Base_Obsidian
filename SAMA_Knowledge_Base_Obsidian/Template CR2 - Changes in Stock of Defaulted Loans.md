# Template CR2 - Changes in Stock of Defaulted Loans

## Connections

### [[Template CR1 - Credit Quality of Assets]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining Pillar 3 credit-quality disclosures, treat CR1 and CR2 as a linked pair rather than standalone templates, because both fall under section 19.2.1 'General information about credit risk' and share a common defined term. CR1 gives the point-in-time stock (gross carrying values, defaulted vs non-defaulted, allowances), while CR2 reconciles the movement in that defaulted stock over the period; the instructions expressly tie the two, stating that for a standardised-approach bank the defaulted exposures 'in Templates CR1 and CR2 should correspond' to exposures past due more than 90 days. A reader should check that the defaulted-exposure figure disclosed in CR1 is consistent with the closing balance derived in CR2, and use the same default definition and off-balance-sheet treatment across both.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 798):** "the default exposures in Templates CR1 and CR2 should correspond to exposures that are "past due for more than 90 days", as stated in SCRE7.96"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 797):** "Template CR1: Credit quality of assets ... Provide a comprehensive picture of the credit quality of a bank's (on- and off-balance sheet) assets."
