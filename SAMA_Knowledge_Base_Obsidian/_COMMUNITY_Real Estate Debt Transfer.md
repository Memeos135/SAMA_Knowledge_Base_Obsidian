# Real Estate Debt Transfer

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Real estate financing regulation governing the transfer/assignment of mortgage debts and the contractual terms subjecting such financing to SAMA rules.

## How members connect

- The Time Periods Circular references the Contract Subjection Circular, imposing deadlines/timeframes on the debt-transfer process for contracts already scoped under SAMA supervision.
- Hierarchy: the subjection circular fixes which financing contracts fall within scope; the time-periods circular then imposes procedural obligations on transferring those in-scope debts.
- Decision-useful for finance companies/banks assessing timing obligations when assigning real estate financing portfolios.
