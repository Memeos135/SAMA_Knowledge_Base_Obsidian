# Basel III Post-Crisis Reforms

### [[Basel III Post-Crisis Reforms]] — `semantically_similar_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping SAMA's capital regime, recognize that the operational-risk framework (SAMA_EN_4041) and the Pillar 3 disclosure framework (SAMA_EN_4234) are two components of a single, shared adoption of the Basel III post-crisis final reforms, not independent rulebooks. Both documents expressly derive from the December 2017 Basel III finalization package, share the same December 2022 issuance/version (2.1), and use consistent scope carve-outs (domestic banks consolidated and standalone; foreign bank branches excluded). For a compliance decision you should read them together — the standardized approach output computed under 4041 is disclosed under the 4234 templates — and confirm effective dates align (disclosure requirements effective 01 January 2023).

Conclude that to interpret a disclosure requirement you must trace it to the corresponding SAMA Basel III capital standard; the disclosure numbers are outputs of those calculation frameworks, and the Framework supersedes prior SAMA Pillar 3 instructions.
