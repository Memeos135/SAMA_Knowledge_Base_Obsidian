# Correspondence Relationship

## Connections

### [[Non-resident Commercial Bank Correspondent Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing correspondent-banking obligations, read the Account Opening Rules (300.2.5) alongside Section 13 of the AML/CTF Rules, because the latter supplies the enforceable AML/CTF regime that the account-opening permission presupposes — including senior-management approval, understanding each party's responsibilities, ongoing monitoring, and the absolute prohibition on dealing with shell banks. Section 13 grounds these duties in Article (9) of the AML Law and Article (68) of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crime and Financing, so they are statutory, not merely procedural. Conclude that permission to open a correspondent account under 300.2.5 does not discharge the separate Section 13 obligations, and both must be satisfied before and throughout the relationship.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 62 / Section 13):** "Article (9) of the Anti-Money Laundering Law and Article (68)... set forth the obligations that must be fulfilled by the financial institution before entering into a correspondence relationship"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 96 / Rule 300.2.5):** "Correspondent accounts may be opened for non-resident commercial banks (including GCC banks)"
