# Additional Licensing Guidelines for Digital-Only Banks

## Connections

### [[Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a digital-only bank applicant, do not treat the digital-only guidelines as a self-contained rulebook; they are additive to the general banking licensing criteria. Document 1897 expressly states its requirements 'must be considered as additional requirements to be met along with the Banking Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria,' and it points back to specific sections (e.g., section D on risk management, section E on compliance/AML-CFT) of document 1868. The consequence: a compliance review must confirm the applicant satisfies both the full conventional licensing baseline and the digital-specific overlays, and cannot rely on the digital guidelines alone to establish licensability.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3, Purpose and Scope para 2):** "They must be considered as additional requirements to be met along with the Banking Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3, Objective/Overview):** "These Licensing Guidelines and minimum criteria set out the... Licensing process for banks... under the Banking Control Law"

### [[Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing governance obligations for a digital-only bank, apply the Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks in full — the digital-only guidelines create no lighter regime. Document 1897 states digital-only banks 'are required to follow the same SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia as with conventional banks,' the same instrument the general licensing criteria invoke for internal audit adequacy. The practical conclusion: a digital-only applicant's governance and internal audit arrangements are measured against the identical corporate governance standard as a conventional bank, so a reviewer should not accept a reduced governance framework on grounds of a purely digital operating model.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4, Governance):** "Digital-only Banks are required to follow the same SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia as with conventional banks."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7, para 26):** "internal audit arrangements and requirements as set out in SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia."

### [[SAMA Cyber Security Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating a digital-only bank's technology risk posture, treat the SAMA Cyber Security Framework as a mandatory, non-negotiable baseline rather than one option among many. The general licensing criteria require IT/accounting systems adequacy assessed 'as per SAMA's Cyber Security Framework and BCM framework,' and the digital-only guidelines reinforce this by requiring compliance with 'all relevant requirements, such as (but not limited to) SAMA's Cybersecurity Framework and BCM Framework.' For a decision: confirm the applicant demonstrates full conformity with the Cyber Security Framework — being fully digital raises, not lowers, the bar, and other regulators' rules (e.g., NCA) are additive on top.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4, Technology and Cybersecurity Risks para 2):** "SAMA requires compliance with all relevant requirements, such as (but not limited to) SAMA's Cybersecurity Framework and BCM Framework."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7, para 23):** "SAMA will have regard to the integrity and security of the systems and arrangements for Business Continuity Management... as per SAMA's SAMA Cyber Security Framework and BCM framework."
