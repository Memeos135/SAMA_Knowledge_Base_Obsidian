# Operational Risk

## Connections

### [[Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the RMF's coverage, note that Operational Risk is a defined term and enumerated category the framework requires banks to manage, distinct from credit, market and liquidity risk. The introduction lists Operational Risk among Shari'ah-banking exposures and Section 4 defines it as 'the risk of losses resulting from inadequacy or failure of internal processes, people and systems, or from external events.' You would conclude that operational-risk obligations fall within the RMF's overarching Section 6 duty to identify, measure, mitigate, monitor and report all risks, integrated with the bank's overall risk management, rather than being an optional add-on.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Section 4 (Definitions)):** "Operational Risk: The risk of losses resulting from inadequacy or failure of internal processes, people and systems, or from external events."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Introduction):** "various types of risks including, but not limited to... Operational Risk"
- **Caveat:** The provided context for the Operational Risk node does not include a dedicated operational-risk section; the link rests on the definition and the introduction's enumeration. Verify whether a standalone operational-risk principle exists in the full text.

### [[Shari'ah Non-compliance Risk (SNCR)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When classifying Shari'ah non-compliance risk for capital and control purposes, treat it as a sub-category of operational risk, not a stand-alone regime: clause 9.3 requires banks to 'consider Shari'ah non-compliance risk (SNCR)... as part of the bank's management of operational risk,' and the definitions confirm operational risk covers losses from 'inadequacy or failure of internal processes, people and systems.' The consequence is that SNCR must be measured, mitigated and monitored within the operational-risk framework (clause 9.4), and the framework further instructs banks to prioritise Shari'ah compliance above other identified risks. You should therefore verify SNCR is captured in operational-risk policies and the ICAAP assessment rather than managed separately.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Definitions):** "Operational Risk: The risk of losses resulting from inadequacy or failure of internal processes, people and systems, or from external events."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13 / 9.3):** "Banks must consider Shari'ah non-compliance risk (SNCR) and failure in performing their fiduciary responsibilities as part of the bank's management of operational risk."
