# Simple Approach (Collateral)

## Connections

### [[Credit Risk Mitigation (CRM)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how collateral reduces credit-risk RWA under the standardised approach, treat the 'simple approach' as one of the two prescribed CRM methods, not a separate regime. The framework states that when calculating credit risk mitigation, banks must use either the simple approach or the comprehensive approach with standard supervisory haircuts; under the simple approach the counterparty's risk weight is replaced by the collateral instrument's risk weight. Conclude that eligibility conditions for the simple approach (e.g. collateral pledged for at least the life of the exposure and revalued at least every six months) are the operative CRM constraints, and check that any collateral relied on meets those tests before recognising mitigation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 74 / 9.32):** "Under the simple approach, the risk weight of the counterparty is replaced by the risk weight of the collateral instrument collateralizing... the exposure."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 755):** "When calculating the degree of credit risk mitigation, banks must use the simple approach or the comprehensive approach with standard supervisory haircuts."
