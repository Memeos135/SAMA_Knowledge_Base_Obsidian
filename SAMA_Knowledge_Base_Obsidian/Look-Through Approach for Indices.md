# Look-Through Approach for Indices

## Connections

### [[Equity Investments in Funds]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how to capitalise a bank's fund holdings under SAMA's minimum capital framework, treat the look-through approach for indices as the primary method the 'equity investments in funds' rules point to. Para 7.35 requires banks to apply a look-through and treat the fund's underlying positions as if held directly, and cross-references the index look-through criteria at 7.31/7.34 for funds holding index instruments. Conclude that you cannot assess a fund's capital charge in isolation — you must first test whether the underlying/index positions can be looked through, since that determines whether direct-holding treatment (and permitted netting) applies before any mandate-based or fall-back method is considered.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 406 / 7.35):** "banks must apply a look-through approach and treat the underlying positions of the fund as if the positions were held directly by the bank"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13 (contents) / Ch.24):** "24. Equity investments in funds ... The look-through approach 325"

### [[Sensitivities-Based Method]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a bank holds index instruments or multi-underlying positions, the look-through approach determines what risk factors and sensitivities feed the sensitivities-based method — either decomposing to constituents (which may net with single-name sensitivities) or mapping to an index bucket. The look-through rules govern how sensitivities are attributed before SbM aggregation, and mandate consistency through time and across identical instruments. Conclude that index treatment is a precondition to the SbM calculation; check whether the index meets the criteria in [7.31] to decide between look-through and index-bucket treatment, and apply it consistently.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 406):** "A look-through approach must always be used for indices that do not meet the criteria set out in [7.31] ... sensitivities to constituent risk factors ... allowed to net with sensitivities to single-name instruments."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 385):** "Risk position: the portion of the risk of an instrument that relates to a risk factor. Methodologies to calculate risk positions for delta, vega and curvature risks."
