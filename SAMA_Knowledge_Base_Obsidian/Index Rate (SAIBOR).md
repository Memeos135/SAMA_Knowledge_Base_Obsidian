- **What this link tells you:** When assessing how a variable-rate Ijarah mortgage payment is set under SAMA's mandated standard contract, treat the index rate and the variable-payment calculation method as a single dependency, not two separate clauses: the calculation article uses the reference index to derive each period's rental amount, and the contract also fixes floor and ceiling limits tied to index movements. The document's own index of articles lists the variable-payment method (Article 15/Yo) alongside schedules for payments 'if the index rises to the ceiling' and 'if the index falls to the floor,' which only make sense read together. For a compliance review of a lender's individual real-estate finance contracts, verify the variable-payment article actually anchors to the stated index and carries the min/max caps required by the model form before treating the pricing terms as conforming.
