# Circular on Cash Movement Compliance

## Connections

### [[Banking Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating exposure for irregular cash-transport by money changers, note that the enforcement teeth come from the Banking Control Law, not the cash-transport circular itself: the circular states that anyone proven to move funds irregularly, or to receive irregularly-moved cash from other exchange firms, is subject to the statutory penalties set out in the Banking Control Law (Royal Decree). You would conclude that the Banking Control Law supplies the sanction regime backing these cash-movement instructions, so any penalty analysis must reference that law's provisions.

- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the substantive rules governing cash movement by exchange firms, this circular points you to the Cash and Precious Metals Transport Law as the underlying regime: the circular reinforces compliance with the law on transporting money and precious metals issued by Royal Decree, plus the attached security-safety guide. You would conclude that the circular is a supervisory reminder subordinate to that transport law and its security guide, and that the operative technical/security requirements sit in those instruments, not the circular.
