# Electronic Record System

## Connections

### [[Chapter II Supervisory Rules and Controls|Chapter II: Supervisory Rules and Controls]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a bank's account-data obligations, treat the electronic record requirement as a core mandatory control within Chapter II, applying to every customer category the Rules cover. Section 1 of Chapter II requires all banks to establish an electronic registration system per Appendix (C), and it expressly ties into the detailed requirements in Chapters III and IV as the basis for opening, operating and following up accounts — meaning the record is not optional bookkeeping but a supervisory precondition. You should conclude that the minimum data fields (name, ID, expiry, address, employer/CR) must be maintained for each customer type before and throughout the relationship, and that this record underpins the on-going monitoring and disclosure duties elsewhere in the chapter.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9 / Ch II §1):** "all banks shall establish an electronic registration system in accordance with the classification set forth in Appendix (C)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / TOC):** "Chapter II Supervisory Rules and Controls 9"

### [[Freezing of Bank Accounts]] — `shares_data_with` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are assessing how a bank must respond to a SAMA disclosure or enforcement (freezing) request, treat the electronic record as the source that makes that response possible: the identification and account data captured under the electronic-record rules is the same dataset the bank must search and act upon when SAMA orders blocking, compulsory deduction or transfer. The link appears to reflect that the unified electronic database of accounts underpins the Compliance Department's ability to 'timely access the customer identification data' and to locate 'all relationships between the bank and the customer' for enforcement, but the two provisions sit in different chapters and the relationship is inferred rather than stated as a cross-reference. Conclude that adequacy of the electronic record is a precondition for lawful, complete freezing/disclosure responses — but verify the primary text before treating this as a formal linkage.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9 / Ch. II sec. 1):** "all banks shall establish an electronic registration system ... This system serves as an electronic record ... as a basis for opening, operating, and following up bank accounts."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 22 / sec. 10.1):** "banks shall search for all relationships between the bank and the customer, including all active; closed and suspended accounts, inoperative accounts, deposits, valuable"
