# Recognition of Financial Receivables

## Connections

### [[Supervisory LGD and EAD Estimates]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining LGD for a corporate exposure secured by financial receivables, check the receivables-eligibility conditions before applying any supervisory or reduced LGD, because the two provisions operate as a gate-and-value chain within the same IRB framework. Paragraph 16.61–16.62 route banks that lack own-estimate approval to supervisory LGD/EAD (per 16.127–16.144), and the receivables-recognition rules (16.132 onwards) sit in that same range and define which receivables count as eligible collateral in the first place. The practical conclusion: a favourable supervisory LGD tied to receivables collateral is only available if the receivables meet the maturity, legal-certainty and enforceability tests of 16.132–16.136 — verify eligibility first, then apply the LGD treatment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 230 / 16.132):** "Eligible financial receivables are claims with an original maturity of less than or equal to one year..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 204 / 16.62):** "banks that do not meet the requirements for own-estimates of EAD or LGD... must use the supervisory estimates of these parameters."
