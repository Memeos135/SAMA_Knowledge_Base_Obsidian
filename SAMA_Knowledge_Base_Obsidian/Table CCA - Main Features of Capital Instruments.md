## Connections

### [[SACAP - Capital Reforms Guidance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining which Pillar 3 disclosure templates a bank must complete on capital instruments and distributions, treat the SACAP capital-reform guidance as the substantive basis and the disclosure templates (Table CCA / Template CDC) as the reporting mechanism that operationalizes it. The link is textually supported because the template content — CET1 minimums, capital conservation buffer, G-SIB surcharge, countercyclical buffer and Pillar 2 add-ons that trigger distribution constraints — draws directly on the capital definitions and deductions specified across SACAP (e.g. SACAP4.1, SACAP4.2). Conclude that the mandatory disclosure figures cannot be assessed in isolation from the SACAP capital-composition rules, and any change in SACAP treatment flows through to what these templates report.
