# Real Estate Valuation Standards

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 3 nodes

## Why this community

Real estate valuation standards applicable to SAMA-supervised lenders, anchored in the Accredited Valuers Law and its regulator. Concerns obligations on financial institutions when commissioning collateral/property valuations.

## How members connect

- The Circular on Real Estate Valuation Client Obligations references the Accredited Valuers Law: the circular operationalises statutory valuation requirements for SAMA-supervised clients (law -> circular hierarchy).
- The circular references the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers, the body accrediting and overseeing valuers — establishing which valuers institutions may rely on.
- Decision consequence: valuations used for lending/collateral must be sourced from accredited valuers per the Law, with client-side obligations set by the circular.
