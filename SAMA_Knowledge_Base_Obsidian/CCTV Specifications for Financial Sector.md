# CCTV Specifications for Financial Sector

## Connections

### [[CCTV Surveillance Objectives (IdentificationRecognitionDetection)|CCTV Surveillance Objectives (Identification/Recognition/Detection)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's CCTV deployment meets SAMA's minimum security standard, treat the three surveillance objectives (Identification, Recognition, Detection) as the definitional yardstick that fixes the mandatory camera performance for each area. The framework document sets numeric thresholds (e.g. Identification = 250 Pixels/m, 40 pixels face width, Full HD with WDR) and then maps each functional area (entrances, cash counters, ATM customer face = Identification; corridors, perimeter = Detection) to one of those objectives. So a compliance review should conclude that a camera in an Identification-classified area which cannot meet the Identification pixel-density spec is non-compliant regardless of its general image quality — the objective, not the hardware label, controls.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3):** "The following specifications shall be used to define surveillance objectives for CCTV equipment at branches: Identification ... Recognition ... Detection"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4):** "Full coverage of cash counters — Identification; Full coverage of parking areas — Detection; Cull coverage of customer face at ATM/CCDM — Identification"
