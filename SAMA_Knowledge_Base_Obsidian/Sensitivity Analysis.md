# Sensitivity Analysis

## Connections

### [[Stress Testing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing a bank's methodology mix, Sensitivity Analysis is the second named technique under Section 5 — measuring the effect of shocking a single or small number of related risk factors without evaluating their inter-relationships. The Rules allow banks to use sensitivity analysis, scenario analysis, or a combination, so this link tells you the two are alternative or complementary tools within one framework rather than mutually exclusive requirements. Conclude that a bank's stress-testing process should document its choice between sensitivity and scenario analysis, and that SAMA can assess the adequacy of that methodological choice on review.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 17 / Section 5.1):** "Sensitivity Analysis measures the change in the value of portfolio for shocks of various degrees to a single risk factor or a small number of closely related risk factors"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 15 / Section 4.2):** "a choice to either use a sensitivity analysis or a scenario analysis or a combination of both"
