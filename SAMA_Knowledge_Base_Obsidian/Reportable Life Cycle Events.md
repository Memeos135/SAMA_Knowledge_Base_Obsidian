# Reportable Life Cycle Events

## Connections

### [[Section A Trade Reporting Requirements|Section A: Trade Reporting Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping what a reporting bank must actually submit under Section A, read the trade reporting obligation together with the life cycle events list, because the reporting duty is not one-off. Section A requires reportable transactions and 'any subsequent business events' to be reported on a T+1 timeline using a life cycle approach, and Appendix B enumerates the specific event types (New, Modify, Error, Early Termination, Correction, Valuation update, Compression) that trigger fresh reports. Conclude that compliance with Section A means capturing each defined life cycle action for the life of a contract, not merely reporting the original trade.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 53 / Appendix B):** "List of reportable life cycle events for OTC derivative transactions: New (N)... Modify (M)... Early Termination (C)... Valuation update (V)... Compression (Z)."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / 3.1.4):** "reports to the SAMA authorised TR reportable transactions (including where appropriate any subsequent business events) before 23:59:59 of the next business day (T+1)."
