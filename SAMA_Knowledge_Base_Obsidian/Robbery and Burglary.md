# Robbery and Burglary

- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the operational-risk scope of this SAMA guide, treat 'Robbery and Burglary' as one enumerated sub-category of the broader 'Criminal Risk' element (section 2.1), not as a stand-alone regime. The guide lists Fraud, Forgery, Counterfeit Currency, Robbery and Burglary, and Electronic Crime as the components of criminal risk, so a reader can conclude the robbery/burglary discussion is scoped and prioritised within that taxonomy.
