### [[Due Diligence Measures (CTF)]] — `semantically_similar_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** These two due diligence regimes appear closely parallel but govern distinct predicate offences — one targets terrorist financing (CTF), the other money laundering (AML) — so do not assume compliance with one automatically satisfies the other. Both impose risk-based CDD, permit postponed verification for low risk, require refusal/termination plus reporting where DD is not feasible, and allow reliance on third parties, but each rests on its own Law and Implementing Regulation with separate reporting channels. Because this link is inferred from conceptual similarity, verify the specific triggers, thresholds and enhanced/simplified DD rules in each primary text before treating obligations as interchangeable, particularly the tipping-off and third-party reliance conditions that differ in wording.

### [[Implementing Regulation to the AML Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the source of AML customer due diligence duties, anchor them in the Implementing Regulation to the AML Law, which operationalises Article 7 of the Law rather than creating freestanding obligations. Sections 7/2–7/14 set the risk-based CDD framework: enhanced measures for higher risk, simplified for lower (barred where suspicion exists), conditions for third-party reliance (7/10–7/11), and mandatory refusal/termination plus STR filing where DD cannot be completed. You would rely on these sections to determine the required depth of CDD for a given customer and confirm that any third-party reliance meets the 7/11 conditions (immediate access to ID data, supervised relied-upon party).
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / 7/14):** "shall determine the extent and depth of application of due diligence measures under Article 7 of the Law based on the types and levels of risk"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / 7/10):** "A financial institution... may rely on another financial institution or designated non-financial business and profession to perform identification and verification of the customer"
