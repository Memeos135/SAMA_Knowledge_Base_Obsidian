# Bankruptcy Law

## Connections

### [[Bank Accounts for Liquidation and Financial Restructuring]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When onboarding or freezing accounts tied to insolvency, treat the Bankruptcy Law as the statutory trigger that defines both the freeze obligation and the permitted liquidation-account regime — the rules require the bank to stop dealings and freeze balances upon official notice of liquidation/administrative liquidation under the Bankruptcy Law, and separately permit a dedicated liquidation account operated by the court-appointed officeholder. This link tells you the account rules are downstream of a court order and statutory procedure, not the bank's discretion. You would conclude the bank must verify the court order, the officeholder's appointment/powers, and the statutory validity period before operating the account.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 24 / 10.2.1):** "Starting any liquidation or administrative liquidation procedures under the Bankruptcy Law for the account holder"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 89-90 / 300.1.7):** "Commencement of any liquidation procedures under the provisions of Bankruptcy Law ... The bank shall receive the court order ... Appointing one or more bankruptcy officeholder"
