# Professional Certificates Requirement

## Connections

### [[Credit Advisor Professional Certificate]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying which staff must hold the Credit Advisor professional certificate, read it as one specific limb of the broader professional-certificates obligation, with its own targeted population and stated carve-outs. The circular requires bank, exchange and finance-company employees (including third-party contracted staff) directly involved in granting credit to individuals, plus branch managers, to obtain the credit advisor certificate, while excepting finance-lease registration companies, the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company, and support-activity companies. A compliance reader should conclude that credit-granting staff fall within scope regardless of employer type among covered entities, but should check the enumerated exclusions before applying the certificate requirement to a given firm.

### [[Customer Care Department (Bank)]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing staffing of a bank's Customer Care Department, read the certification circular as supplying the enforceable qualification standard for that department's employees rather than treating competence as discretionary. The Customer Care instrument requires department managers and staff to hold, as a minimum, the professional certificate in retail banking fundamentals and the credit advisor certificate, while the separate certification circular expressly names 'موظفي إدارة العناية بالعميل' among front-line staff obligated to obtain the retail banking fundamentals certificate within one year of joining. A compliance reader should verify the primary text of both, but can treat certification of customer-care staff as a cross-referenced, mandatory obligation, extending to third-party contracted personnel.
