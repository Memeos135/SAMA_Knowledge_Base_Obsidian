# Abandoned Accounts

## Connections

### [[Inoperative Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When classifying an account's status, read 'abandoned' as a later stage within the single lifecycle of inoperative accounts, not a separate regime: an account becomes abandoned only 'after completing the periods specified' following unclaimed status, which is itself the terminal end of the dormant/unclaimed progression governed by this Rule. This matters because the escalating obligations (double supervision, separate filing, balance treatment as liabilities, eventual transfer to a suspense account) attach in sequence, and government-entity accounts are expressly excluded from the abandoned phase only. Conclude that you must track the timing chain that moves an account into the abandoned category and confirm whether the specific customer type is carved out of that final stage.
