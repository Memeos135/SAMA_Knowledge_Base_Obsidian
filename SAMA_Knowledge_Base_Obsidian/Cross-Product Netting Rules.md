# Cross-Product Netting Rules

## Connections

### [[Internal Models Method (IMM)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank may net across SFTs and OTC derivatives for capital purposes, note that cross-product netting is available only within IMM, not as a general entitlement. The rules state expressly that the Cross-Product Netting Rules (7.61–7.71) apply to netting across SFTs, or across SFTs and OTC derivatives, for regulatory capital computation under IMM, and require a written bilateral arrangement plus reasoned legal opinions of enforceability across all relevant jurisdictions. You would therefore confirm the bank holds IMM approval and the requisite legal opinions before accepting any cross-product netting benefit in its capital figures.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 67 / 7.61):** "The Cross-Product Netting Rules apply specifically to netting across SFTs, or to netting across both SFTs and OTC derivatives, for purposes of regulatory capital computation under IMM."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 48 / 7.2):** "A bank may also choose to adopt an internal models method to measure counterparty credit risk (CCR)... to only OTC derivatives, to only SFTs, or to both, subject to the appropriate recognition of netting specified in 7.61 to 7.71."
