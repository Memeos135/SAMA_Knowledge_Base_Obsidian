# Contractual Maturity Mismatch

## Connections

### [[Liquidity Monitoring Tools]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying reporting obligations under Part 2, treat contractual maturity mismatch as the first of the five prescribed monitoring metrics, carrying specific data assumptions that a bank cannot restate at its discretion. Paragraph 176 lists it (item I), and paragraphs 179–183 fix the assumptions — no rollover of liabilities, no new asset contracts, separate reporting of open-maturity instruments, contingent liabilities and rehypothecated collateral. Conclude that the mismatch report must follow these prescribed contractual assumptions and at minimum cover the LCR categories; verify open-maturity and contingent-trigger items are reported separately.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46 / para 177):** "The contractual maturity mismatch profile identifies the gaps between the contractual inflows and outflows of liquidity for defined time bands."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 46 / para 176):** "The metrics discussed in this section include the following: I. Contractual maturity mismatch"

### [[Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk Management and Supervision]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** This link appears to connect the contractual maturity mismatch monitoring tool with the Basel Committee's 'Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk Management and Supervision', and the text supports that connection: the LCR standard states it must be supplemented by supervisory assessment 'in line with the Sound Principles' and by the Part 2 monitoring tools, of which maturity mismatch is the first. Practically, you should not treat LCR compliance as sufficient on its own — the mismatch metric feeds a broader supervisory judgement under the Sound Principles, and supervisors may impose more stringent parameters based on that assessment. Verify how SAMA operationalises the Sound Principles alongside the monitoring tools before relying on LCR figures alone.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46 / para 175-176):** "Examples of actions that supervisors can take are outlined in the Committee's Sound Principles (paragraphs 141-143)... I. Contractual maturity mismatch"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 10 / para 15):** "the LCR... must be supplemented by detailed supervisory assessments of other aspects of the bank's liquidity risk management framework in line with the Sound Principles"
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED/conceptual; the mismatch tool and Sound Principles are linked as parts of one framework rather than by a direct cross-reference between them — confirm in the primary text.
