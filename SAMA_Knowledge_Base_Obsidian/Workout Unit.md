# Workout Unit

## Connections

### [[Loan Life Coverage Ratio (LLCR)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing how a bank decides whether to restructure a distressed loan, note that the LLCR is the analytical tool assigned to Workout teams for viability assessment of medium- to long-term and project-finance debt. This link tells you the Workout Unit's approval of a restructuring plan should be grounded in defined viability metrics (LLCR alongside DSCR/Debt-EBITDA), with the Guidelines indicating an indicative 1.4 LLCR threshold for multi-year loans. Check that WU viability assessments actually apply LLCR to long-horizon exposures rather than relying on single-point ratios.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46 / 5.2.1):** "the Workout Unit must choose from a variety of options to tailor a restructuring plan that meets the needs of specific borrower"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 82 / Appendix 2):** "Loan Life Coverage Ratio (LLCR) should be used by Workout teams to assess the viability of a given amount of debt"

### [[Watch List]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing escalation controls, read the watch list and Workout Unit as sequential stages of the same mandatory pathway: a loan may remain on the watch list only temporarily, and once the bank's specified time expires it must be either returned to the originating unit (if resolved) or transferred to the Workout Unit. The Guidelines require a documented, time-bound policy with clear criteria and approvals for the mandatory transfer to the WU. Check that the bank has defined the maximum watch-list tenure and the objective transfer triggers, because leaving stressed loans in the originating unit is expressly flagged as raising NPLs and lowering recovery.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / B):** "Banks shall establish and document a policy with clear and objective time-bound criteria for the mandatory transfer of loans ... to the Workout Unit"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Monitoring):** "After that period, loan must be either returned to originating unit or transferred to Workout Unit"

### [[Workout Plan]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping who is accountable for a restructuring outcome, treat the workout plan as the product the Workout Unit is required to design and negotiate: the WU tailors the restructuring option to the borrower, and the bank may only approve a plan where projected cash flows suffice and the borrower is cooperative. The link confirms the WU's segregation from Loan Originating Units and its reporting to Risk Management, so plan approval sits within an independent decision chain. Check that plan approval authority and pre-conditions are documented and that conflicts of interest with originating officers are excluded.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 32 / 4):** "Workout Unit (WU) should be established as a permanent unit ... reporting directly to the Risk Management function rather than the Business / Loan Originating Units"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 46 / 5.2.1):** "the Workout Unit must choose from a variety of options to tailor a restructuring plan ... borrower must meet two essential pre-conditions"
