# Labor Law

## Connections

### [[Circular on Employing Nationals and Recruitment Contracting]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether a bank or exchange house may hire non-Saudis or Saudize positions, read this SAMA circular together with the Labor Law it cross-references: the circular's localization mandate operates 'with regard to' the Labor Law issued by Royal Decree, so the Labor Law supplies the underlying employment-law framework while SAMA adds sector-specific restrictions (cash, transfer, AML/CFT and information-security roles reserved for Saudis). The circular does not override the Labor Law; it layers supervisory conditions on top of it. The reader should conclude that compliance requires satisfying both regimes simultaneously — Labor Law contracting rules plus SAMA's stricter Saudization limits for sensitive banking functions.
