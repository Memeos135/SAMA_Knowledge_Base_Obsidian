**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected

## Why this community

Capital adequacy regime — the countercyclical capital buffer as a component of the Basel III capital stack applied to KSA banks.

## How members connect

- The CCyB provision cites Basel III Capital Standards as its parent framework, situating it as a subordinate, regime-specific buffer within the broader capital standards.
- Compliance consequence: the CCyB requirement must be read together with the base Basel III minimum ratios and other buffers as SAMA calibrates them; the buffer is variable and set by supervisory judgment.
