# Bank (Agent Banking Principal)

## Connections

### [[Banking Agent]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When allocating liability in an agent-banking arrangement, do not treat the Agent as an independent third party absorbing its own regulatory risk — the Bank remains the responsible principal. The Regulation makes the board of each Bank responsible for compliance, holds the Bank solely responsible for selecting Agents and answerable for the Agent's acts or omissions, while retaining overall responsibility for the agency relationship. You should conclude that supervisory and enforcement exposure for Agent conduct sits with the licensed Bank, and that contractual delegation to the Agent does not shift that regulatory accountability.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Art 3):** "Banks shall be solely responsible for the selection of Agents ... The Bank is responsible for all actions or omissions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Art 1):** "Exclusive Agent: an Agent that entered into an agency agreement with one Bank to exclusively provide banking services and/or products on behalf of the contracting Bank"

### [[Fit & Proper Assessment]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When onboarding an Agent, the Bank cannot rely on a light-touch commercial check — it must conduct and evidence a formal fit-and-proper assessment as a licensing prerequisite. Article 13's assessment tests the moral, business and professional suitability of the Agent and its proprietors/managers, screens for negative credit-bureau data and ML/TF, fraud or integrity-related criminal records, and adequacy of funding sources; Article 4 further requires the CEO or a designated senior officer to sign a declaration confirming the assessment was performed. You should conclude that a documented, senior-attested fit-and-proper file is a condition of SAMA non-objection, and its absence is itself a compliance failure by the Bank.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / Art 4):** "signed declaration by its Chief Executive Officer or a duly designated senior officer, confirming that the Bank has carried out the proper and fit assessment"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / Art 13):** "carry out a 'fit and proper' assessment ... ensuring that the Agent, its proprietors, and persons involved in the management ... are sufficiently competent, 'fit and proper'"

### [[Regulation of Agent Banking in KSA]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When allocating liability in an agent-banking model, the Bank is the principal that bears sole responsibility for selecting Agents and is answerable for all their actions or omissions, with board-level accountability for compliance with the Regulation. The Regulation defines 'Bank' by reference to the Banking Control Law and makes the Bank–Agent relationship direct and contract-governed. You should conclude that the Bank cannot contract away its regulatory responsibility, and check that the Bank's board oversight, Agent-selection controls, and the CEO fit-and-proper declaration are documented before treating the arrangement as compliant.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Article 3):** "The Bank is responsible for all actions or omissions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Article 3 Responsibility):** "It is the responsibility of the board of directors of each Bank to ensure compliance with this Regulation. Banks shall be solely responsible for the selection of Agents."
