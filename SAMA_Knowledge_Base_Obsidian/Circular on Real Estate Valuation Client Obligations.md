# Circular on Real Estate Valuation Client Obligations

## Connections

### [[Accredited Valuers Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a bank, finance company or insurer commissions a real-estate valuation, treat this circular's client-obligations as the SAMA-supervised layer sitting on top of a separate profession law: the circular is expressly issued 'with reference to' the Accredited Valuers Law (Royal Decree) and its Implementing Regulation – Real Estate Branch (Ministerial Decision), and it designates these SAMA-licensed entities as 'clients' of real-estate valuation. It then imposes concrete duties — not dealing with valuers lacking primary membership in the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers, governance separating credit/underwriting staff from the valuation process, and non-interference with valuer independence. Conclude that valuer eligibility and professional conduct are governed by the referenced Valuers Law/Regulation, while the client-side independence and governance duties are enforceable by SAMA under the Banking Control, Cooperative Insurance and Finance Companies Laws cited as the circular's authority; check both regimes together.

### [[Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank, real-estate finance company or insurer can lawfully engage a valuer, treat membership in the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers (Taqeem) as a hard eligibility gate, not a preference: the circular expressly forbids dealing with valuation practitioners who do not hold a primary-member card of the Authority. This obligation derives from the circular's stated legal basis in the Banking Control Law, the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law, the Finance Companies Control Law and the Accredited Valuers Law and its real-estate branch implementing regulation, which together designate these licensees as 'clients' of real-estate valuation. The reader should conclude that verifying Authority membership is a precondition to instructing any valuer, and a valuation obtained from a non-member is non-compliant regardless of its quality.
