# SAMA Circular No. 381000095091

## Connections

### [[Rules Governing Calculation of APR]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When interpreting APR obligations under the Regulation for Consumer Financing, do not read that Regulation in isolation — Article 2 of the APR Rules directs that they be read together with Circular No. 381000095091, which clarifies Articles (10) and (21) and the APR calculation mechanism in that Regulation. This makes the circular an interpretive gloss forming part of the applicable body of rules on how APR is derived and disclosed under consumer financing. You should conclude that resolving an APR-disclosure question requires consulting the circular's clarification alongside the Regulation and the APR Rules, and should not rely on the Regulation's articles alone where the circular modifies their reading.
