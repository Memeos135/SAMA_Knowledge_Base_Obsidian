# Equity Exposure Phase-in

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

Transitional (phase-in) arrangements for the capital treatment of equity exposures, providing a time-limited path to full risk-weighting requirements.

## How members connect

- Single-node community: sets the transitional timeline and interim risk weights for equity exposures.
- Subordinate/temporal qualifier to the standing equity and capital-instruments treatment (see community 262).
- Decision relevance: dictates which risk weight applies during the transition window, so timing determines the capital charge a bank must currently hold.
