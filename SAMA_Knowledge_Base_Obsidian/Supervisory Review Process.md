# Supervisory Review Process

## Connections

### [[Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Plan (ICAAP)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When planning ICAAP timing and documentation, recognise that the ICAAP is the instrument SAMA uses to exercise its Supervisory Review responsibility, so the document must be verifiable and follow-up-able: SAMA sets the frequency and nature of review, requires at least annual board-level review, and mandates re-submission on significant change. This makes documentation quality and reporting to the board (and onward to SAMA) an enforceable requirement, not internal practice. You should confirm the ICAAP is board-reviewed at least annually, re-submitted when material changes occur, and traceable in business plans, board minutes and policy documents so SAMA can verify it.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10):** "it must be verifiable and it is possible for both the banks and SAMA to do a follow-up ... SAMA requires that this must take place at least once a year"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5):** "an important purpose of the ICAAP document is for senior management to inform the Board of Directors and subsequently SAMA on the ongoing assessment of the bank's risk profile"
