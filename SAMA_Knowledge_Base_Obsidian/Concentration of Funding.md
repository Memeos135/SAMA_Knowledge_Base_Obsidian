# Concentration of Funding

## Connections

### [[Liquidity Monitoring Tools]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining a bank's LCR-related reporting obligations, recognise that 'Concentration of Funding' is not a standalone requirement but one of the Part 2 monitoring-tool metrics that sit alongside — and are distinct in status from — the binding LCR standard in Part 1. As a monitoring metric it defines specific thresholds (a significant counterparty/product exceeding 1% of the balance sheet; a significant currency being 5% or more of total liabilities) that supervisors use to detect negative trends, rather than a pass/fail ratio. You would conclude that funding-concentration figures are supervisory-monitoring inputs, and check the defined significance thresholds when scoping what must be reported under the monitoring-tools regime.
