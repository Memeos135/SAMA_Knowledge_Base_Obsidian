# High-Volatility Commercial Real Estate (HVCRE)

## Connections

### [[Supervisory Slotting Approach for Specialized Lending]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When classifying a commercial real-estate exposure as HVCRE, recognise that this definition feeds directly into the supervisory slotting approach — HVCRE is a specialized-lending sub-type with its own slotting risk weights, distinct from the PF/OF/CF/IPRE table. The link matters because HVCRE (higher loss-rate volatility, including ADC financing) is carved out for a separate slotting treatment, so correct identification of an exposure as HVCRE changes which supervisory-category risk weights apply. Confirm whether an ADC or high-volatility CRE exposure meets the HVCRE definition in 10.15 before applying the slotting weights, since HVCRE and other SL exposures are slotted under different tables.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 95 / Para 10.15):** "HVCRE lending is the financing of commercial real estate that exhibits higher loss rate volatility (i.e. higher asset correlation) compared to other types of SL."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Chapter 13 contents):** "Risk weights for specialized lending (HVCRE) 139"

### [[Supervisory Slotting Criteria for IPRE and HVCRE]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When slotting high-volatility commercial real estate (HVCRE) exposures, do not look for a separate HVCRE criteria table: paragraph 13.5 states the slotting criteria for HVCRE are the same as those for IPRE (para 13.14), even though HVCRE carries its own risk-weight table (Table 21) reflecting higher asset correlation. So the assessment factors are shared with IPRE while the resulting risk weights differ. Conclude that for HVCRE you apply the IPRE criteria table to place exposures into categories, then read across to the HVCRE-specific risk weights — and confirm the exposure meets the HVCRE definition (para 10.15) rather than being ordinary IPRE, since that classification drives which risk-weight table applies.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 95 / Art 10.15):** "HVCRE lending is the financing of commercial real estate that exhibits higher loss rate volatility (i.e. higher asset correlation) compared to other types of SL"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 139 / Art 13.5):** "The slotting criteria on which this mapping must be based are the same as those for IPRE, as provided in paragraph 13.14"
