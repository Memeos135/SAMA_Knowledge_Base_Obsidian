# Anti-Money Laundering Law

**Cohesion:** 0.06 - loosely connected
**Members:** 43 nodes

## Why this community

AML/CTF preventive-measures regime plus the fit-and-proper gatekeeping and adjacent prudential rules for finance companies/DTFCs; covers who must apply CDD, report to the FIU, and hold approved senior positions.

## How members connect

- The AML Law is the parent statute; its Implementing Regulation, Preventive Measures for FIs/DNFBPs, Due Diligence Measures and the FIU/GDFI enforcement bodies are subordinate references defining obligations and offences (e.g. Article 2 ML offence).
- The CFT Law parallels the AML Law with its own preventive measures (adding NPOs) and the Permanent Committee — a linked but distinct sanctions/TFS-adjacent regime.
- Fit-and-proper chain: Requirements for Senior Appointments -> Fit and Proper Criteria -> positions needing SAMA non-objection, including the AML/CTF Director, tying governance approval to the AML function.
- KYC is treated as conceptually equivalent to Due Diligence Measures; the DTFC/Finance Companies rules and IFRS 9 provisioning cluster here because account-opening/KYC and juristic-person verification bridge AML CDD and prudential asset-quality duties.
