# Rules for Regulating BNPL Companies

## Connections

### [[BNPL Activity]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping whether an entity needs a SAMA license, anchor on this defined term: the Rules define 'BNPL Activity' as 'a type of financing that allows a consumer to purchase goods or services without a term cost payable by the consumer,' and Article 3(2) prohibits carrying out that activity without a SAMA license under the Finance Companies Control Law. The document is the source that both defines the term and imposes the licensing obligation on it. For a compliance decision you would test a product against this definition — no term cost to the consumer, financing to purchase goods/services — to determine whether the BNPL licensing regime (and its capital, management, consumer-protection and financial-crime requirements) is triggered.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Art 3):** "BNPL activity shall not be carried out except after obtaining a license from SAMA in accordance with the provisions of the Law"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Art 1):** "BNPL Activity: A type of financing that allows a consumer to purchase goods or services without a term cost payable by the consumer."

### [[BNPL Licensing Provisions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether an entity may lawfully conduct BNPL activity, rely on this link: the Rules make licensing a hard precondition (no BNPL activity without a SAMA license) and set out the full licensing chain — application, verification visits, five-year term, renewal, revocation, and fees in Articles 3, 7-12. The licensing provisions are subordinate to the Finance Companies Control Law, and the license is activity-specific (no other activity without prior SAMA non-objection). You would conclude that unlicensed BNPL activity is prohibited outright, and that scope creep beyond the licensed activity independently requires SAMA approval.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Art 3(2)):** "BNPL activity shall not be carried out except after obtaining a license from SAMA in accordance with the provisions of the Law"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / Art 9(3)):** "The BNPL company shall not engage in any activity other than the activity for which the license has been issued unless it obtains a prior written non-objection from SAMA."

### [[Consumer Protection & Data Confidentiality]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a BNPL company's consumer-protection obligations, treat them as spanning both Article 17 (complaint-handling function and confidentiality of consumer data) and the wider consumer safeguards in Articles 20-22 (fee prohibitions, CDD, KYC, data privacy, credit limits), because the parent Rules link these provisions under one duty regime that also cross-refers out to AML/CTF regulations, the Electronic Transactions Law, and the Responsible Lending Principles. Note the term boundary: the BNPL 'Consumer' (beneficiary of the activity) is the protected subject, distinct from the AML 'Customer', even though Article 21 imports CDD/KYC concepts. You should therefore verify BNPL consumer-protection compliance across all these articles jointly, and not read Article 17 as the sole locus of the obligation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Art 17):** "The BNPL company, including all of its employees, shall maintain the confidentiality of consumers’ data and transactions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 10 / Art 21):** "The BNPL company shall develop a CDD program and comply with the AML/CTF regulations and instructions... a. Know Your Customer (KYC)... c. Data privacy and confidentiality."

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping obligations for a BNPL company, treat the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51) as the governing parent statute — the BNPL Rules expressly adopt the Law's defined terms and state they shall not prejudice the Law's provisions. This means BNPL definitions and requirements must be read consistently with the Law, and where the Rules are silent the Law's provisions still apply. You would conclude that a BNPL 'Consumer' and licensing/supervisory obligations sit within the M/51 framework, and check the Law before relying on the Rules alone.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_6523 Page 3 / Art 1 & Art 3):** "The terms ... shall have the same meanings stated in the Finance Companies Control Law ... These Rules shall not prejudice ... The Finance Companies Control Law issued by Royal Decree No. M/51"
- **Grounding — related node (SAMA_EN_6324 Page 4):** "نظام مراقبة شركات التمويل الصادر بالمرسوم الملكي رقم (م/51)"
