# Judicial Authority Responses

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Bank obligations to respond to requests from judicial and dispute-resolution bodies — the duty and procedure for supplying information to courts and the Banking Dispute Committee.

## How members connect

- The SAMA Circular sets the overarching obligation for responding to judicial authorities; the Banking Dispute Committee response requirement is a specific application of it.
- The 'cites' link makes the Committee-response provision subordinate to and dependent on the general circular for its authority and procedure.
- Together they scope who may compel bank disclosures and the compliance duty to respond — relevant to bank-secrecy exceptions and lawful-request handling.
