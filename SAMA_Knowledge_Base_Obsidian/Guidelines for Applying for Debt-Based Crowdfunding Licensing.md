# Guidelines for Applying for Debt-Based Crowdfunding Licensing

## Connections

### [[Fit and Proper Form]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on a debt-based crowdfunding license application, treat SAMA's Fit and Proper Form as a mandatory, non-optional deliverable, not a courtesy attachment. The Guidelines list it as a required document for every founding member and every board and sub-committee candidate, each to be completed, signed, and approved by the authorized submitter. Because inaccurate or omitted material information is expressly deemed a violation of the Finance Companies Control Law (Art. 7) and can trigger rejection or license cancellation, the reader should verify each named individual's fit-and-proper submission is complete and truthful before filing.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3, item 5-6):** "SAMA's Fit and Proper Form for each founding member ... and for each candidate member in the board and its sub-committees"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3, item 5):** "after being completed and signed by each founding member, and approved by the person authorized to submit the license application"

### [[Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what governs a debt-based crowdfunding applicant, read the Guidelines as procedural implementation subordinate to the substantive Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding (and the Finance Companies Control Law above them). The Guidelines expressly borrow their defined terms from, and condition application completion on fulfilling the requirements set in, those Rules; they do not create standalone obligations. Consequently, the reader should resolve any definitional or substantive question by consulting the Rules and the Law, and treat the Guidelines only as the licensing-process layer.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / Page 4):** "terms and phrases ... shall have the same meanings stated in the Finance Companies Control Law and the Rules for Engaging in Debt-Based Crowdfunding"
