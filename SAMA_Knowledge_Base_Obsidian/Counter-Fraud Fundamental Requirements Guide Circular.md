# Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements Guide Circular

## Connections

### [[Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements]] — `semantically_similar_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** Treat these two as a single enforceable package rather than two similar documents: the 10529 circular is the cover instruction that adopts and mandates compliance with the Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements (10530), which is the actual control body. The circular imposes the enforceable obligations — self-assessment, board-approved roadmap, quarterly reporting and a full-compliance deadline — on finance companies and payment service providers, while 10530 supplies the domains and control requirements they must meet. When scoping obligations, rely on the circular for who is bound and the timelines, and on 10530 for the substantive controls; do not read the Requirements document as merely advisory guidance.
