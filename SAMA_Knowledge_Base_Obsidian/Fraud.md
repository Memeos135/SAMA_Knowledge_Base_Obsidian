# Fraud

## Connections

### [[Bankers Blanket Bond  Financial Institution Bond|Bankers Blanket Bond / Financial Institution Bond]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When considering how this guide expects fraud loss to be treated, note that the Bankers Blanket Bond/Financial Institution Bond is presented as the risk-transfer response to fraud, sitting alongside training, prevention/detection programmes and internal audit. The guide expressly names BBB/FIB as one control for the incidence of fraud, and the BBB/FIB's insuring agreements (employee dishonesty, etc.) map directly onto fraud exposures. A reader can conclude the two concepts are linked as risk-and-transfer, but this is guidance framing rather than a binding purchase mandate, so verify whether any separate SAMA instrument makes such cover compulsory.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / section 2.1.1):** "Fraud currently represents the single largest area of operational loss within the Kingdom's banking system."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / section 1.2):** "The incidence of fraud may be controlled through ... internal auditing and, finally, through the Bankers Blanket Bond (BBB) and Financial Institution Bond (FIB)."
- **Caveat:** Relation is 'conceptually_related_to'; the guide describes BBB/FIB as one option among controls, not a mandated requirement — confirm against any binding SAMA rule before relying on it.

### [[Criminal Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Fraud is the first and largest enumerated criminal-risk element (section 2.1.1) in this guide, so it should be scoped inside the operational/criminal-risk framework rather than treated as an isolated AML matter. The guide states criminal activity is the single largest operational-risk area and identifies fraud (predominantly employee-perpetrated, plus card, ATM and POS fraud) as the leading operational loss. For a compliance reader, this locates fraud-loss controls within the Board-approved Operational Risk Management Plan and signals particular attention to internal (employee) fraud and payment-card exposure.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Section 2.1.1):** "Fraud currently represents the single largest area of operational loss within the Kingdom's banking system"
