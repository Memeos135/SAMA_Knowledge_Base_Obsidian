# Foundation and Advanced IRB Approaches

## Connections

### [[IRB Risk Components (PD, LGD, EAD, M)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a bank may estimate itself versus what it must take from supervisory values, read the F-IRB/A-IRB approach choice and the risk-components definition together, because the approach dictates ownership of each parameter. Para 10.30 lists risk components (PD, LGD, EAD, M) as one of the three IRB elements, and 10.31 assigns them: under F-IRB banks estimate PD and rely on supervisory estimates for other components, while under A-IRB banks provide their own PD, LGD, EAD and M. Conclude that the permissible source of each risk component is not free-standing — it is set by which approach SAMA has approved for the asset class, and the A-IRB prohibitions (e.g. large corporates, banks/financial institutions) further constrain when own-LGD/EAD/M estimates may be used.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 109 / para 10.31):** "Under the advanced approach (A-IRB approach), banks provide their own estimates of PD, LGD and EAD, and their own calculation of M, subject to meeting minimum standards."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 109 / para 10.30):** "Risk components: estimates of risk parameters provided by banks, some of which are supervisory estimates."
