# Credit Record

## Connections

### [[Credit Information Company]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a licensed credit bureau's obligations under the Credit Information Law, treat 'Credit Record' as the defined output that 'Companies' are legally required to produce, maintain and exchange — the two terms are locked together by Article 1's definitions and Article 4(3). This means a credit information company's core statutory duties (accuracy, first-time consumer consent under Article 9, error correction, retention under Article 10) all attach to the credit record it issues; you cannot scope the company's liability without reference to the record. Conclude that any compliance review of the company must test the record's lifecycle — consent, accuracy, correction, retention and confidentiality — as inseparable duties.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / Art 1 (Definitions)):** "Credit Record: A report issued by companies containing consumer credit information."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Art 1 (Definitions)):** "Companies: Credit information companies licensed to collect and maintain credit information on consumers and provide the same to members upon request."

### [[Credit Information Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on consumer rights over their credit data, anchor them in the Law's defined 'Credit Record' — a report issued by companies containing consumer credit information — because that definition triggers the Article 9 rights (consent to establish, free copy on decline, right to annotate, right to correct errors and escalate to the committee). The Law also imposes maintenance duties on companies under Article 10. Conclude that any dispute over record accuracy, access fees, or unlawful requests is governed by these statutory rights and the committee mechanism, not by contract alone.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Art 9):** "A consumer credit record may not be established with companies for the first time except with the written consent of the consumer"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Art 1):** "Credit Record: A report issued by companies containing consumer credit information"
