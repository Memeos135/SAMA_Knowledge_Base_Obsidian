# Banks Remuneration Rules

## Connections

### [[Board of Directors Responsibilities (Remuneration)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assigning accountability for remuneration governance in a bank, treat the Board as the non-delegable owner: the Rules make the Board responsible for the overall design and oversight of the remuneration system and expressly bar delegation of that responsibility to senior management. The Board must also approve senior-management remuneration on NRC recommendation and ensure an independent annual compliance review. A compliance reviewer should therefore verify Board-level (not management-level) sign-off on remuneration design, senior-management pay, and the annual review, since defects at Board level are the enforceable failure point.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / §2.1 item 5):** "The Board shall review and, if satisfied, approve the remunerations of the senior management based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / §2.1 item 2):** "The Board ... shall be responsible for the overall design and oversight of the remuneration system ... and accordingly shall not delegate this responsibility to senior management."

### [[Nomination and Remuneration Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's remuneration governance, treat the Nomination and Remuneration Committee as a mandatory organ created and constrained by the Banks Remuneration Rules, not an optional structure. The Rules require the committee (min. three members), tie its composition and responsibilities to SAMA's Key Principles of Governance, and route Board approval of senior-management remuneration through its recommendations; the terms of reference must be submitted to SAMA with the Compliance Report each cycle. Conclude that remuneration decisions and policy documents must show the committee's role and interaction with Risk Management/CRO, and that absence of a compliant NRC is itself a breach of these Rules.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / 1.3):** "These rules shall apply to banks... The objective of these rules is to set the minimum requirements... on remuneration"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / 2.2):** "Banks shall have a Nomination and Remuneration Committee comprising of at least three members... consistent with SAMA's Key Principles of Governance"

### [[Remuneration Adjustment (MalusClawback)|Remuneration Adjustment (Malus/Clawback)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's variable-pay arrangements meet SAMA requirements, note the Rules require the remuneration system to include mechanisms — in-year adjustment, malus and clawback — that can reduce or recover variable pay after award or payment, documented in the bank's policies. These are triggered by poor financial performance, employee misconduct, material error, or risk-management failure, and reductions must be justified to SAMA with the Compliance Report. A reviewer should confirm that malus/clawback provisions actually exist in policy with defined triggers and approval authority, since their absence is a substantive compliance gap, not a discretionary design choice.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9 / §2.5):** "linking remuneration with performance and alignment of remuneration with risk taking ... identifying, monitoring and reporting misconduct."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13 / §4.3 item 43):** "Remuneration system should provide for mechanisms to adjust variable remuneration, including ... malus or clawback arrangements ... Such mechanisms must be documented in the Bank's policies and procedures."

### [[Remuneration Compliance Report]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When planning SAMA reporting obligations, note that the Rules impose a concrete, recurring filing duty: a semiannual Compliance Report assessing alignment with the Rules, plus gap action plans and specified remuneration data (Appendices I–III, top-12 employees), on fixed deadlines (before March 31 and August 31). This is the primary mechanism by which SAMA supervises remuneration practices, and non-alignment must be disclosed with remediation timelines. A reviewer should confirm both timeliness and completeness of these filings, because material deficiencies expose the bank to rectification directives, increased capital/liquidity requirements, or penalties.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9 / §2.5):** "Banks shall have a written Remuneration Policy ... The Remuneration Policy shall ensures the achievement of prudent management of the risks associated with remuneration."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 16 / §5.2 items 56, 58):** "Banks are required to submit a semiannual Compliance Report to SAMA ... assuring full compliance, or highlighting gaps along with an action plan."
