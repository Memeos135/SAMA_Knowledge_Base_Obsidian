# G-SIB Indicators Disclosure

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

Systemic-importance transparency: disclosure of the indicators used to assess and score global systemically important banks (G-SIBs).

## How members connect

- Single member: Template GSIB1 mandates disclosure of the quantitative indicators feeding the G-SIB assessment methodology.
- Scope is limited to banks in the G-SIB assessment population; the template standardises data for comparability across jurisdictions.
- Subordinate to the Basel G-SIB framework as adopted by SAMA, and connected to higher loss-absorbency/buffer requirements that flow from the scoring.
