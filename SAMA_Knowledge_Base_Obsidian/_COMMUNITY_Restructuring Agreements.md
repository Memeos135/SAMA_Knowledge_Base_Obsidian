# Restructuring Agreements

**Cohesion:** 0.29 - loosely connected
**Members:** 7 nodes

## Why this community

Consensual debt-restructuring framework for distressed corporate borrowers — the principles, standstill, term sheet, covenants and agreement that govern out-of-court workouts among creditors.

## How members connect

- 'Restructuring Principles' set the governing rules; the 'Restructuring Agreement' is the operative instrument that implements them and references the term sheet and Key Covenants that bind the parties.
- The 'Standstill Agreement' is a distinct, prior-stage instrument (creditor forbearance) conceptually feeding into the eventual Restructuring Agreement.
- 'Early Warning Signals' trigger the process; the Glossary defines these and the Key Covenants, so defined-term meaning flows from the Glossary into the operative documents.
- This is a governance/credit-risk regime, not an enforcement statute — obligations are contractual among consenting creditors rather than imposed by SAMA.
