# IRB Credit Derivatives Effect

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

Pillar 3 disclosure of how credit derivatives used as credit risk mitigation (CRM) affect risk-weighted assets under the Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) approach. Covers the quantitative disclosure obligation for banks recognising credit-protection instruments.

## How members connect

- Single-member community: Template CR7 imposes a standardized disclosure requirement for the RWA impact of credit derivatives recognised as CRM under IRB.
- Sits within SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure framework; conceptually linked to broader IRB credit-risk and CRM disclosure templates/tables (e.g., CR8, CRC).
- Scope limited to credit derivatives used as protection — not general counterparty exposures.
