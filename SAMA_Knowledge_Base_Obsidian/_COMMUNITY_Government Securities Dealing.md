# Government Securities Dealing

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Framework governing primary dealers in the Saudi government securities market and their interaction with the sovereign debt issuer.

## How members connect

- The Primary Dealer Guidelines set the eligibility and conduct obligations for dealers and reference the DMO as the issuing/managing authority.
- Institutional linkage: the DMO is the counterparty/authority the Guidelines operationalize around.
- Decision-usefulness: a market participant reads the Guidelines to understand primary dealer duties and the DMO's role in the issuance framework.
