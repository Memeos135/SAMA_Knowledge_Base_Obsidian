# White Land Fees Law

## Connections

### [[Circular on Registering White Land Fee Lands]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on real estate held or financed by banks and finance companies, note that this circular ties SAMA's supervisory powers to the White Land Fees Law (Royal Decree M/4) and its Implementing Regulation, directing financiers to register lands subject to the fees within the prescribed period so real owners avoid financial penalties. The relationship establishes that white-land fee-registration obligations reach financiers holding such lands, not only ultimate owners. You should check whether any land in a financier's portfolio falls within the fee regime and ensure timely registration to avoid penalty exposure.
