# Financial Crime Insurance

**Cohesion:** 0.25 - loosely connected
**Members:** 9 nodes

## Why this community

Operational/criminal risk and its transfer through insurance — the categories of financial crime a bank faces and the bond/coverage products that mitigate residual loss. This is a risk-management and insurance-adequacy space, distinct from AML/CTF criminal-conduct obligations.

## How members connect

- Criminal Risk sits under Operational Risk and enumerates the covered perils (Fraud, Forgery, Counterfeit Currency, Robbery/Burglary, Electronic Crime) — a definitional taxonomy of insurable crime exposures.
- Insurance instruments map to those perils: the Bankers Blanket Bond addresses fraud-type losses and Electronic/Computer Crimes Coverage addresses electronic crime, showing the risk-to-coverage correspondence a firm assesses for adequacy.
- Links are 'conceptually related' rather than obligation-imposing — these describe risk-transfer product scope, not enforceable mandates.
