# SOPE Operational Risk Standard

## Connections

### [[Template OR1 Historical losses|Template OR1: Historical losses]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining which banks must complete the operational-loss disclosure (Template OR1), read it as an implementation of SAMA's operational risk (SOPE) capital standard rather than a stand-alone reporting duty: OR1 exists to disclose the historical loss data that feeds the internal loss multiplier (ILM) and business indicator (BI) buckets defined by that standard. The template's scope hooks directly on the operational risk framework — mandatory for banks in the second/third BI bucket and for first-bucket banks with SAMA approval to use internal loss data. Conclude that OR1 obligations are triggered by the bank's status under the operational risk standard, so verify the bank's BI bucket and any SAMA ILM/loss-data approvals before treating the template as applicable.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 856 (OR1 scope)):** "mandatory for: (i) all banks that are in the second or third business indicator (BI) bucket... and (ii) all banks in the first BI bucket which have received SAMA approval to include internal loss data"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 856):** "To disclose aggregate operational losses incurred over the past 10 years... This disclosure informs the operational risk capital calculation."

### [[Template OR3 Minimum required operational risk capital|Template OR3: Minimum required operational risk capital]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's disclosed minimum operational risk capital (OR3) is correct, read OR3 as the reporting output of the SOPE operational risk standard's methodology, not a free-standing figure: the standard defines the BI buckets, the ILM and the loss-event thresholds (e.g. 44,600 / 446,000 SAR) that OR3 must reflect. The template therefore inherits its calculation rules and scope from that standard. Conclude that any review of OR3 must trace back to the operational risk standard's parameters — check the applicable ILM treatment and threshold before accepting the disclosed capital requirement.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 857):** "Loss event threshold: 44,600 SAR or 446,000 SAR for the operational risk capital calculation if applicable"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 751):** "Minimum capital requirement T: Pillar 1 capital requirements at the reporting date. This will normally be RWA * 8%..."
- **Caveat:** Node B is a concept label and the OR3 source context shown is capital-metrics/CDC text rather than OR3 body; the link to the operational risk standard is inferred — confirm the standard's article numbers in the primary text.
