# Circular on Land Financing under Real Estate Finance Regulation

## Connections

### [[Real Estate Finance Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether land financing to individuals is subject to Article 12 of the Implementing Regulation, treat this circular as SAMA's authoritative interpretation issued under the Real Estate Finance Law (Royal Decree M/50) and the Finance Companies Control Law. The circular confirms that land financing to individuals by all real estate financiers (banks and finance companies) falls under Article 12 of the Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law. You should conclude that the parent-law Article 12 requirements apply to individual land financing across all financier types, and not treat land financing as carved out.
