# Board of Directors Responsibilities (Remuneration)

## Connections

### [[Banks Remuneration Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assigning accountability for remuneration governance in a bank, treat the Board as the non-delegable owner: the Rules make the Board responsible for the overall design and oversight of the remuneration system and expressly bar delegation of that responsibility to senior management. The Board must also approve senior-management remuneration on NRC recommendation and ensure an independent annual compliance review. A compliance reviewer should therefore verify Board-level (not management-level) sign-off on remuneration design, senior-management pay, and the annual review, since defects at Board level are the enforceable failure point.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / §2.1 item 2):** "The Board ... shall be responsible for the overall design and oversight of the remuneration system ... and accordingly shall not delegate this responsibility to senior management."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / §2.1 item 5):** "The Board shall review and, if satisfied, approve the remunerations of the senior management based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee."

### [[Nomination and Remuneration Committee]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping who decides remuneration, read the Board and the NRC as linked but distinct roles rather than interchangeable: the NRC recommends and the Board reviews and approves, and remuneration-adjustment decisions must be noticed to the Board or the NRC. The Board retains non-delegable overall responsibility, so the NRC's advisory function does not displace Board accountability. A reviewer should check that recommendation-then-approval sequencing is respected and that the NRC's role is not treated as final authority, since supervisory scrutiny attaches ultimately to the Board.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / §2.1 item 5):** "The Board shall review and, if satisfied, approve the remunerations of the senior management based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 15 / §4.3 item 53(e)):** "communicating in writing to all affected individuals the value of remuneration adjustments ... That includes noticing the Board or Nomination and Remuneration Committee."
