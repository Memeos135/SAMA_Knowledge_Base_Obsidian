# BNPL Licensing

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

BNPL licensing authority derives from the Finance Companies Control Law; this cluster covers who may lawfully offer BNPL and the entry requirements for authorization.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Finance Companies Control Law is the primary statute; the BNPL License Application Guidelines are subordinate SAMA guidance operationalizing entry into the regulated finance-company perimeter.
- The Guidelines cite the Law as the source of SAMA's licensing power — a BNPL provider must satisfy statutory finance-company conditions plus the application criteria.
- Decision consequence: unlicensed BNPL activity is a licensing breach under the Law, not merely non-conformance with a guide.
