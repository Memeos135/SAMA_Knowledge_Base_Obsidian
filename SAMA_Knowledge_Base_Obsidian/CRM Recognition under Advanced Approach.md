# CRM Recognition under Advanced Approach

## Connections

### [[Treatment of Guarantees and Credit Derivatives (CRM)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining capital relief from guarantees or credit derivatives under the IRB advanced approach, read the advanced-approach recognition rules together with the general CRM treatment of guarantees and credit derivatives, because the former builds on and constrains the latter. Paragraph 12.22 fixes a floor — recognized CRM cannot produce a risk weight below a comparable direct exposure to the protection provider and no CRM may raise the capital requirement — mirroring the standardized-approach rule that protected portions take the protection provider's risk weight. Conclude that eligibility limits on credit derivatives (e.g. nth-to-default instruments give no relief) carry into the advanced approach, and check the guarantee/credit-derivative eligibility conditions before assuming any A-IRB capital benefit.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 123 / Para 12.22):** "the adjusted risk weight will not be less than that of a comparable direct exposure to the protection provider... banks may choose not to recognize credit protection if doing so would result in a higher capital requirement."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 88 / Para 9.78):** "The protected portion is assigned the risk weight of the protection provider. The uncovered portion of the exposure is assigned the risk weight of the underlying counterparty."
