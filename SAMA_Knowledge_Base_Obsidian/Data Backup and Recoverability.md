# Data Backup and Recoverability

## Connections

### [[SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a Member Organization's data backup and recovery controls, do not read the Data Backup and Recoverability subdomain (3.3.10) in isolation, because it expressly requires alignment with a separate SAMA instrument. Control Requirement 3.3.10(2)(a) mandates that the data backup management policy be aligned with the 'SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework,' and the subdomain's RPO/RTO obligations tie recovery capability to BCM objectives. For a compliance decision you should conclude that backup adequacy must be tested against the BCM Framework's continuity requirements, and that satisfying backup controls alone does not evidence BCM alignment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 26 / 3.3.10):** "RTOs for critical services such as payment systems, customer related services... adequately defined considering the high availability."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 25 / 3.3.10):** "The data backup management policy should consider the following... a. alignment with SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework."
