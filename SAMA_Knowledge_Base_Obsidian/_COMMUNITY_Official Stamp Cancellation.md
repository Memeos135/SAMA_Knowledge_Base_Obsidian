# Official Stamp Cancellation

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

Administrative modernization of documentary formalities. This circular removes the official-stamp requirement, affecting the validity/formality standard for accepted documents and correspondence.

## How members connect

- Single-member community: an amending/relaxing instrument that supersedes any prior requirement mandating an official stamp.
- Legal consequence is that absence of a stamp can no longer be a basis to reject an otherwise valid document; check for other provisions that previously imposed the stamp.
