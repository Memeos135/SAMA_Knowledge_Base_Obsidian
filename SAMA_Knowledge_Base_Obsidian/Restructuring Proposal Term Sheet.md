# Restructuring Proposal Term Sheet

## Connections

### [[Restructuring Agreement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a bank on how a workout should be documented, treat the term sheet and the restructuring agreement as sequential stages of one process rather than interchangeable documents. Both concepts sit in the same SAMA credit-restructuring guidance (SAMA_EN_3575): the term sheet records the main terms agreed during negotiation before lawyers prepare the legal documents, while the restructuring agreement is the formalized instrument that ultimately regulates all details of the workout. For compliance purposes conclude that a signed term sheet is a negotiating/pre-contractual step and does not by itself replace or amend the original loan agreement — the binding position depends on the restructuring agreement (or amendatory loan agreement) that follows.
