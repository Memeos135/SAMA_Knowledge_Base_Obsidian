# Private Foundations Accounts

## Connections

### [[Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what documents a bank must obtain before opening an account for a private association or foundation, treat the MHRSD license as the gating credential — the account-opening rules for private foundations expressly condition acceptance on a license issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development plus a board decision approving the business and the authorized signatories. This link tells you the eligibility test is not internal to the bank but tied to an external licensing authority. You would conclude that absent a valid MHRSD license (and board minutes on signatories), the account cannot be opened under this section.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 61 / Rule 300.1.5.2):** "private associations licensed by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) to carry out different activities, including their branches"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 46 / Rule 300.1.5.2):** "a copy of the license issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, a copy of the decision of the board of directors of the society/foundation ... approving the establishment of the business"

### [[Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping obligations for a private foundation's account, read it as a sub-type within the juristic-persons rulebook, meaning general juristic-person controls (identity verification of authorized signatories, dormant/abandoned account handling, SAMA disclosure and enforcement) operate alongside the foundation-specific licensing evidence. The corpus treats private societies/foundations as juristic persons requiring an MHRSD licence and board-approval documentation before opening an account. Conclude that foundation onboarding cannot be assessed against the foundation-specific paragraphs alone; verify the applicable 300-series juristic-person controls also apply.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16 / 5.2.2):** "the person authorized to operate the account if the account is for a juristic person"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 46):** "a copy of the decision of the board of directors of the society/foundation (meeting minutes) approving the establishment ... (for private societies/foundations ...)"
