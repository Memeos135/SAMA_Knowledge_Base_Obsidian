# IRB Default & Provisions

**Cohesion:** 0.06 - loosely connected
**Members:** 32 nodes

## Why this community

Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) credit-risk capital regime: the minimum qualifying requirements, rating-system and risk-quantification standards, default/expected-loss and provisions treatment, and collateral recognition banks must satisfy to use internal estimates.

## How members connect

- 'Minimum Requirements to Use IRB Approach' is the gatekeeping parent referencing all qualifying conditions: rating system design, use of ratings, risk quantification, governance/control unit, validation and disclosure — permission to use IRB is conditional on all being met.
- Risk Quantification chains into the estimation obligations (Definition of Default, PD/Own-LGD/Own-EAD estimation, supervisory LGD/EAD fallbacks) and the recognition of guarantees, credit derivatives and eligible collateral.
- Expected-loss/provisions branch links Treatment of EL and Provisions to Calculation of EL, Total Eligible Provisions and the specialized-lending slotting EL, connecting capital treatment to accounting provisions.
- Supervisory Slotting parent scopes specialized-lending sub-criteria (project, object, commodities, IPRE/HVCRE finance) as a defined alternative to own estimates.
