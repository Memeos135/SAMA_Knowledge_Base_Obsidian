# Overview on Operational Risk

## Connections

### [[Criminal Risk]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping this SAMA operational-risk guidance, treat criminal risk as a sub-category of the broader operational-risk concept rather than a separate regime. The overview defines operational risk as 'pure risk' with only loss/no-loss outcomes and expressly names 'losses due to criminal activity (fraud, counterfeiting, forgery, etc.)' as examples, so criminal risk falls inside the operational-risk framework the guide addresses. For a compliance reader this means criminal-risk controls are part of the Operational Risk Management Plan approved by the Board, not an independent AML obligation, and should be assessed within that governance structure.
