# Digital ID Acceptance

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

CDD/identity-verification guidance permitting reliance on government digital credentials (Absher/Tawakkalna) for customer and family-status verification within KYC obligations.

## How members connect

- Both instruments authorize acceptance of state-issued digital documents as valid identity evidence; the Family Record item references the Digital ID item as a companion verification source.
- Together they define acceptable means of satisfying identification/verification requirements without requiring physical documents.
- Scope note: reliance is on official government-issued digital IDs, distinct from third-party CDD reliance regimes.
