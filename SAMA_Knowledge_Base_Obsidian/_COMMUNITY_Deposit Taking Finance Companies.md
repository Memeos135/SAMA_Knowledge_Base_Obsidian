# Deposit Taking Finance Companies

**Cohesion:** 0.40 - moderately connected
**Members:** 5 nodes

## Why this community

Prudential and governance regime for deposit-taking finance companies, subordinate to the Finance Companies Control Law and specifying capital, liquidity, and governance obligations.

## How members connect

- Finance Companies Control Law is the primary statute; Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations is the implementing instrument that derives its authority from it.
- The Regulations reference Capital, Liquidity, and Corporate Governance/Risk Management as the substantive prudential requirement pillars binding these licensees.
- Read as law -> regulation -> requirement components: the licensee's obligations flow down from the Control Law through the Regulations to each specific prudential standard.
