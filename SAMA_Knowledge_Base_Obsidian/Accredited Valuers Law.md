# Accredited Valuers Law

## Connections

### [[Circular on Real Estate Valuation Client Obligations]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a bank, finance company or insurer commissions a real-estate valuation, treat this circular's client-obligations as the SAMA-supervised layer sitting on top of a separate profession law: the circular is expressly issued 'with reference to' the Accredited Valuers Law (Royal Decree) and its Implementing Regulation – Real Estate Branch (Ministerial Decision), and it designates these SAMA-licensed entities as 'clients' of real-estate valuation. It then imposes concrete duties — not dealing with valuers lacking primary membership in the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers, governance separating credit/underwriting staff from the valuation process, and non-interference with valuer independence. Conclude that valuer eligibility and professional conduct are governed by the referenced Valuers Law/Regulation, while the client-side independence and governance duties are enforceable by SAMA under the Banking Control, Cooperative Insurance and Finance Companies Laws cited as the circular's authority; check both regimes together.
