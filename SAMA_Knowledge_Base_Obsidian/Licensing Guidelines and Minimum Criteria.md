# Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria

## Connections

### [[Additional Licensing Guidelines for Digital-Only Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a digital-only bank applicant, do not treat the digital-only guidelines as a self-contained rulebook; they are additive to the general banking licensing criteria. Document 1897 expressly states its requirements 'must be considered as additional requirements to be met along with the Banking Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria,' and it points back to specific sections (e.g., section D on risk management, section E on compliance/AML-CFT) of document 1868. The consequence: a compliance review must confirm the applicant satisfies both the full conventional licensing baseline and the digital-specific overlays, and cannot rely on the digital guidelines alone to establish licensability.

### [[Banking Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the legal force of these Licensing Guidelines, read them as an implementing instrument subordinate to the Banking Control Law, not as an independent source of authority. The Guidelines expressly state they set out SAMA's licensing process 'under the Banking Control Law' and are issued pursuant to SAMA's Charter and the BCL (Royal Decree No. M/5), so substantive prohibitions and eligibility rules — such as who may carry on banking business and the restricted use of the word 'Bank' — trace back to the BCL itself. For a licensing decision you would rely on the BCL for the binding statutory requirements and treat the Guidelines as the procedural and minimum-criteria overlay, checking the BCL text where a legal obligation must be established.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Objective):** "These guidelines and minimum licensing criteria are issued in accordance with the authority vested in SAMA under the Charter... and the Banking Control Law – issued via Royal Decree No. M/5"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Restricted words):** "The Banking Control Law prohibits the use of the word 'Bank, or its synonyms, or any similar term in any language on papers or printed matter'"

### [[Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a license applicant on internal-control expectations, do not treat the Licensing Guidelines' audit criterion as self-contained — it cross-refers to a separate governance instrument that supplies the substantive standard. The Guidelines require applicants to satisfy SAMA on internal audit arrangements 'as set out in SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia,' making that document the operative source for the audit adequacy test. For a compliance decision you would consult the Corporate Governance Principles for the actual internal-audit requirements rather than relying on the Guidelines' brief reference.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / Section G):** "Applicants are required to satisfy SAMA on the adequacy of proposed internal audit arrangements... as set out in SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / Section G):** "SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia."

### [[Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping who must clear SAMA before a bank starts operating, treat the banking licensing criteria and the senior-appointments requirements as a single obligation set, not two separate tracks. The Licensing Guidelines require applicants to satisfy SAMA on the fitness of individuals managing the bank and expressly cross-reference the Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions, which impose the standing rule that board nominees and senior management may not assume roles without SAMA's written non-objection. For a decision, verify that every senior/board appointment planned in a license application already meets the fitness-and-propriety and non-objection process in document 1997 — SAMA can consult other regulators on candidate suitability, so the licensing sign-off is contingent on that separate approval regime.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 (Section on Board/senior positions)):** "Appointments to Senior Positions in Financial Institutions Supervised by the Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority (SAMA)."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6, para 8):** "Candidates for Senior Positions shall not assume their responsibilities... prior to obtaining SAMA's written non-objection."

### [[SAMA Cyber Security Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating an applicant's IT and systems adequacy for licensing, note that the Guidelines defer the security and continuity standard to a separate SAMA framework rather than specifying it internally. The Guidelines state SAMA will assess systems integrity and security 'as per SAMA's SAMA Cyber Security Framework and BCM framework,' so those documents supply the binding technical benchmarks against which IT arrangements are judged. For a compliance decision you would treat the Cyber Security Framework (and BCM framework) as the controlling source for the systems-security criterion and verify conformity there, not from the Guidelines' summary reference.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / Section F):** "SAMA will have regard to the integrity and security of the systems and arrangements for Business Continuity Management (BCM) and Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP) as per SAMA's SAMA Cyber Security Framework"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / Section F):** "as per SAMA's SAMA Cyber Security Framework and BCM framework."
