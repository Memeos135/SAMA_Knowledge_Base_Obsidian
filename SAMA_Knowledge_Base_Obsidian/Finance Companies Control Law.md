# Finance Companies Control Law

## Connections

### [[BNPL License Application Guidelines]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a BNPL applicant, treat these BNPL licensing guidelines as subordinate procedural instruments that derive their authority from the Finance Companies Control Law and the Rules Regulating BNPL Companies — the guidelines expressly state defined terms carry the same meaning as in that Law and Rules. Critically, the guidelines make providing false or omitted material information a 'violation of the Finance Companies Control Law, in particular Article 7 thereof,' so licensing consequences flow from the Law, not merely the guidance. Conclude that the enforceable obligations and definitions sit in the Law/Rules, and the guidelines only operationalize the application process; verify Article 7 in the primary Law before relying on the sanction framing.
