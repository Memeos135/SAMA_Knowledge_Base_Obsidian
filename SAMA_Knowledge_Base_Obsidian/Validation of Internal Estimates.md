# Validation of Internal Estimates

## Connections

### [[Minimum Requirements to Use IRB Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When judging whether a bank's IRB estimates can be relied upon, treat validation (Section 8) as a mandatory minimum requirement, not optional model assurance — it is listed within Chapter 16 and paragraph 16.121 requires banks to 'have a robust system in place to validate the accuracy and consistency of rating systems.' The concrete standards include regular back-testing of realized versus estimated PDs (and LGD/EAD for advanced IRB) using long data histories, updated at least annually. Conclude that IRB eligibility depends on demonstrable, documented validation, and that absence or weakness of validation undermines the whole IRB permission regardless of how well the models are otherwise designed.
