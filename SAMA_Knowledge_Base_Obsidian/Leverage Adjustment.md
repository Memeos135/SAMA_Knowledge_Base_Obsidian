# Leverage Adjustment

## Connections

### [[Look-Through Approach (LTA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the leverage ratio exposure measure for equity investments in funds, treat the Leverage Adjustment as coupled to the Look-Through Approach: SAMA's table of contents lists 'Leverage adjustment' and 'Application of the LTA and MBA to banks using the IRB approach' consecutively under 'Equity investments in funds,' meaning the leverage inherent in a fund's structure feeds the LTA computation. For a compliance decision, conclude that where a bank looks through to underlying fund positions, it must carry the fund's leverage into the exposure measure rather than reporting only its equity stake; verify the specific SLEV/fund-treatment article numbers before relying on the calculation mechanics.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 871):** "As these adjustments reduce the total leverage ratio exposure measure, they shall be reported as a negative amount."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13):** "The look-through approach ... Leverage adjustment ... Application of the LTA and MBA to banks using the IRB approach"

### [[Mandate-Based Approach (MBA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a bank cannot fully look through a fund but knows its investment mandate, the Mandate-Based Approach applies, and the same Leverage Adjustment sits alongside it in the framework's structure ('The mandate-based approach ... Leverage adjustment ... Application of the LTA and MBA'). This tells you the leverage-adjustment step is not exclusive to full look-through: an MBA calculation of fund exposures must likewise reflect leverage in the fund structure. For your decision, do not assume MBA lets a bank ignore embedded fund leverage; check the specific fund-treatment provisions to confirm the adjustment applies under MBA as it does under LTA.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 871):** "these adjustments reduce the total leverage ratio exposure measure, they shall be reported as a negative amount."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13):** "The mandate-based approach ... Leverage adjustment ... Application of the LTA and MBA to banks using the IRB approach"
