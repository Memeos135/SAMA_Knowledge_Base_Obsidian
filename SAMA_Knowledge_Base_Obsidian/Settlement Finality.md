# Settlement Finality

## Connections

### [[Systemically Important Payment System (SIPS)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on the irrevocability of a payment order, note that Settlement Finality is legally scoped to SIPS rules — it is not a free-standing protection for any payment system. The definition makes finality binding, enforceable and non-reversible 'in accordance with SIPS rules', and Article 10 requires the SIPS-related rules to fix the point of finality (including multi-system execution) and to protect clearing arrangements on member/operator default. Therefore, whether a payment order enjoys finality/enforceability protection depends on whether the system is SIPS-classified and on the specific rules SAMA has set for it; do not assume finality applies to non-SIPS systems.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5):** "Settlement Finality: The point at which a payment order is deemed to be duly completed... and may not be reversed, revoked, or modified... in accordance with SIPS rules."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11 / Art 10):** "The point at which a payment order made by a member becomes final, and the point at which such order becomes subject to settlement finality"
