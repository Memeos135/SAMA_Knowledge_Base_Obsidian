# IT Risk Management

## Connections

### [[Information Technology Governance Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a Member Organization's IT risk obligations, read section 3.2 as binding Control Requirements under the Framework rather than as guidance. The Framework mandates that the IT risk management process 'should be defined, approved, implemented and communicated' and 'aligned with the Member Organization's enterprise risk management process', including third-party relationships and associated risks. For a compliance decision, conclude that IT risk identification, treatment, reporting and profiling must be evidenced and are subject to SAMA self-assessment and audit, and that outsourced/third-party IT risks fall squarely within this obligation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 17):** "IT risk management process should clearly address the following sub processes... risk identification, analysis and classification; risk treatment; risk reporting; and risk monitoring and profiling."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6):** "To ensure IT risks are properly managed throughout the Member Organizations."
