# Remuneration Compliance Report

## Connections

### [[Banks Remuneration Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When planning SAMA reporting obligations, note that the Rules impose a concrete, recurring filing duty: a semiannual Compliance Report assessing alignment with the Rules, plus gap action plans and specified remuneration data (Appendices I–III, top-12 employees), on fixed deadlines (before March 31 and August 31). This is the primary mechanism by which SAMA supervises remuneration practices, and non-alignment must be disclosed with remediation timelines. A reviewer should confirm both timeliness and completeness of these filings, because material deficiencies expose the bank to rectification directives, increased capital/liquidity requirements, or penalties.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16 / §5.2 items 56, 58):** "Banks are required to submit a semiannual Compliance Report to SAMA ... assuring full compliance, or highlighting gaps along with an action plan."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9 / §2.5):** "Banks shall have a written Remuneration Policy ... The Remuneration Policy shall ensures the achievement of prudent management of the risks associated with remuneration."
