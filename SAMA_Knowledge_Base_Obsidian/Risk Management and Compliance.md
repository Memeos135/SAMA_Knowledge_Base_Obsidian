# Risk Management and Compliance

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulation for Payments Law (SAMA 1430)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a payment service provider's governance obligations, treat 'Risk Management and Compliance' as an enforceable chapter of the Implementing Regulation, not a standalone policy topic. The Regulation's table of contents lists it under Part Four (licensee obligations), Chapter One, alongside outsourcing and review rules, and SAMA's cover letter confirms that all licensees and payment system operators subject to the Law and Regulation must comply with every provision. You would conclude that risk management and compliance duties are mandatory licensee obligations flowing from the Payments and Services Law, so check the specific articles in that chapter rather than relying on the concept label alone.
