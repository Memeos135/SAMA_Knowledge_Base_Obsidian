# Supervisory Authority

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulation to the AML Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing who a financial institution or DNFBP must answer to under the AML regime, treat the 'supervisory authority' as the enforcement and instruction-issuing node, not merely a monitoring body. The Implementing Regulation makes compliance with the supervisor's instruments mandatory (Art 24/6) and empowers the supervisor to impose additional controls on foreign branches/subsidiaries or force closure (Art 24/5). Conclude that any supervisory instruction, rule or guideline carries the same binding force as the Regulation itself, and check that your obligation inventory captures supervisor-issued instruments, not just the text of the Regulation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18 / Art 24/6):** "shall comply with any instructions, rules, guidelines or any other instruments issued by a supervisory authority"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / Art 14/3):** "apply measures consistent with the requirements under the Law and this Implementing Regulation"
