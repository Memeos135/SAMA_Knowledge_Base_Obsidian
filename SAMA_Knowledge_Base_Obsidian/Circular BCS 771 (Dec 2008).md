# Circular BCS 771 (Dec 2008)

## Connections

### [[Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the level at which a bank must manage liquidity for NSFR purposes, do not read the NSFR scope-of-application section in isolation: the NSFR document expressly imports Principle 6 from Circular BCS 771 (5 Dec 2008) as an overlay. This means that irrespective of whether NSFR is applied on a consolidated basis, the bank still owes a standing obligation to monitor and control liquidity risk and funding needs at the level of individual legal entities, foreign branches, subsidiaries and the group, accounting for limits on liquidity transferability. Conclude that BCS 771 remains live governing guidance and check its Sound Principles when assessing group-level funding-risk obligations rather than relying on the NSFR consolidated scope alone.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5):** "in line with Principle 6 as outlined in Circular #BCS 771 dated 5 December 2008, a bank should actively monitor and control liquidity risk exposures and funding needs at the level of individual legal entities"
