# Guidelines to Apply for Payment Service Providers License

## Connections

### [[Business Risk Assessment Guide for MLTFPF|Business Risk Assessment Guide for ML/TF/PF]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** These documents appear related only because both address SAMA-supervised financial institutions, but they belong to distinct regimes and serve different decisions: 10911 is an AML/CTF/PF business-risk-assessment guide applying to all supervised financial institutions, while 10951 is a procedural licensing guide for payment service provider applicants under the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments and Payment Services. A PSP applicant is caught by both — it must satisfy the licensing requirements in 10951 and, once operating as a financial institution, apply the AML risk-assessment expectations in 10911. For a compliance decision, do not treat AML obligations as part of the licensing checklist or vice versa; confirm the PSP's AML duties from the AML Law and its implementing regulation rather than from either guide, since both are non-binding aids.

### [[Payment Systems Oversight Framework]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping the payments regime, treat these two as different functions of the same SAMA framework rather than interchangeable: the Oversight Framework governs SAMA's supervision/oversight of payment *systems* and their operators (self-assessment, supervisory assessment, PFMI compliance), while the Licensing Guidelines govern how a firm becomes an authorised *payment service provider* (EMI/PI) under the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments. Both appear to sit under the same Law of Payments and Payment Services and its Implementing Regulation, but they address distinct obligation-holders and lifecycle stages. The link is INFERRED — verify the primary texts before treating oversight obligations (on system operators) as applying to a PSP licence applicant, since scope and defined roles ('member', 'client', 'operator') differ.
