# Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law

## Connections

### [[Amendment to Finance Support Activities Licensing Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping which rules bind a digital brokerage or other finance-support entity, treat these two instruments as one hierarchy: the Finance Support Activities Licensing Rules expressly derive their defined terms from, and operate subordinate to, the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations. The Rules state that undefined terms carry the meaning given in the Control Law and its Implementing Regulations, and apply 'without prejudice' to them. Conclude that support-activity licensees remain bound by the Implementing Regulations' definitions and general provisions; do not read the licensing Rules as a self-contained regime.

### [[Cancellation of Debt Collection Licensing Requirement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** To decide whether a debt-collection entity for finance-provider debts still needs a SAMA licence, read this circular together with the amended Implementing Regulations it references: SAMA cancels the licensing requirement for practising the debt-collection activity, issuing this decision in reference to the Governor's approval of the amendments to the Implementing Regulations and the Finance Support Activities Licensing Rules. Conclude the licensing prerequisite is removed, but affected entities carry a transitional compliance obligation to align their registrations.

### [[Finance Lease Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the governing framework for a finance lease offered by a licensed finance company, read these together: the Finance Lease Law (node A) supplies the substantive contract regime and defined terms (Lessor, Lessee, Leased asset, Contract register), while the Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law (node B) expressly incorporate the Finance Lease Law within its 'أنظمة التمويل' (Finance Laws) definition and govern the licensing/supervision of the entity offering the lease. So a finance-lease transaction is subject to both the Law's contract rules and the Regulations' company-level prudential and conduct obligations. Check both layers — contract validity and registration under the Lease Law, and licensing/exposure/governance requirements under the Regulations — before treating either as complete.
