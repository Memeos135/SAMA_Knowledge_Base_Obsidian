### [[AML Due Diligence Measures]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how far to verify an Awqaf beneficial owner, read the Awqaf circular together with the AML Implementing Regulation's risk-based due-diligence framework rather than as a stand-alone data step. ### [[Beneficial Owner Identification]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a financial institution onboards or monitors an Awqaf (endowment) relationship, treat the Awqaf-data circular as an operational channel for satisfying the standing beneficial-owner identification duty, not a substitute for it. The AML rules require verifying the identity of the beneficial owner — the person owning or controlling 25% or more, or otherwise exercising ultimate control — while the circular directs institutions to use the General Authority for Awqaf's channels to verify beneficial-owner data and to notify discrepancies without prejudice to STR obligations. Conclude that Awqaf verification results feed the CDD determination but the institution retains the full beneficial-owner obligation, including reporting where data diverges.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1):** "the General Authority for Awqaf has made available a number of channels enabling financial institutions to access beneficial-owner data of endowments (paraphrase; source Arabic is OCR-garbled)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Art (CDD)):** "verifying the identity of the beneficial owner ... the person who owns or controls (25%) or more of the legal person."
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED; node A Arabic is OCR-garbled so its text is paraphrased. Confirm the circular's exact obligations against a clean copy.
