# SCCR - Minimum Capital Requirements for CCR and CVA

## Connections

### [[RWA for Credit Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the credit-risk RWA line, note that it explicitly EXCLUDES counterparty credit risk and credit valuation adjustment, which are governed instead by the SCCR standard (chapters SCCR3–SCCR11). The disclosure instructions carve out CCR and CVA exposures to separate rows precisely because they are computed under SCCR, not the general credit-risk framework. For a compliance decision this tells you not to capture derivative/SFT counterparty and CVA charges in the standard credit-risk calculation — route them to SCCR and check the boundary so exposures are neither double-counted nor omitted.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 751 / rows 6-10):** "Counterparty credit risk: RWA and capital charges according to the counterparty credit risk chapters of the Basel framework (SCCR3 to SCCR10)."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 751 / row 1):** "Credit risk (excluding counterparty credit risk): RWA and capital requirements according to the credit risk standard of the Basel framework (SCRE)"
