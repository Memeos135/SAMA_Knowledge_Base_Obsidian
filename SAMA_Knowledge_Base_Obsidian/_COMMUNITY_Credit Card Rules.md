# Credit Card Rules

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA rules governing issuance and operation of credit cards by supervised issuers, covering consumer-protection terms, fees, disclosure, and operational conduct.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Credit Card Issuance and Operation Rules are the binding instrument; the FAQ is interpretive guidance clarifying application, not creating new obligations.
- The FAQ references the Rules to resolve ambiguity; where they conflict, the Rules prevail as the enforceable source.
- Decision use: rely on the Rules for the legal requirement and treat FAQ answers as SAMA's interpretive position on scope and application.
