# Credit Risk Management

## Connections

### [[Expected Credit Loss Provisioning]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a finance company's obligations, do not treat ECL provisioning as a standalone accounting exercise — the Rules embed it within the credit risk management framework, requiring documented ECL methodologies reviewed at least annually, quarterly board reporting on ECL adequacy, and independent annual internal audit evaluation. Both derive from Article 13 of the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 62 of its Implementing Regulation, which mandate provisioning per international accounting standards and permit SAMA to require additional provisions. For compliance, conclude that provisioning adequacy, governance oversight, and write-off/recovery discipline are components of a single enforceable framework, not separable duties.
