# Products and Services Risk Assessment

## Connections

### [[New Banking Products and Services Regulation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a bank must do before launching a new product, treat the risk-assessment requirements as an integral component of the overarching New Products Regulation, not a separate exercise. The Regulation states risks from new/modified products 'must be identified, assessed, monitored and managed appropriately by the banks,' and Section 6.3 operationalizes this by mandating a full risk assessment that 'form[s] the basis on whether or not to introduce them to the market.' A compliance reader should conclude that failing to complete and document the 6.3 risk classification and lifecycle review is itself a breach of the Regulation's core obligation, and that the risk assessment output is a prerequisite to any notification or non-objection decision.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Sec 6.3.2):** "Banks must conduct a full risk assessment of new products and services which form the basis on whether or not to introduce them to the market"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Sec 1 Introduction):** "the risks posed by the introduction and/or modification of products and services must be identified, assessed, monitored and managed appropriately by the banks"
