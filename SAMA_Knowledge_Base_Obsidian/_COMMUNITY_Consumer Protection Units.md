# Consumer Protection Units

**Cohesion:** 0.29 - loosely connected
**Members:** 7 nodes

## Why this community

Consumer protection organizational regime: the mandated internal units and staff-competency requirements banks must maintain to deliver fair treatment, complaint resolution, and financial education under SAMA's consumer protection principles.

## How members connect

- The Customer Care Department is the anchor function referencing subordinate units — Complaints Handling, Quality and Performance Analysis, and Financial Awareness and Education — as the operational structure required by the Financial Consumer Protection Principles.
- The Principles set the substantive standards these units exist to fulfil (fair treatment, redress, awareness).
- Competency overlay: the Professional Certificates Requirement imposes qualification duties on staff (e.g., the Credit Advisor certificate) and links to the Customer Care Department, conditioning who may perform these consumer-facing roles.
