# Protection of Safeguarded Funds

## Connections

### [[Electronic Money]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating client-money exposure for an e-money issuer, connect electronic money to the safeguarding regime: funds received against issued e-money are the 'safeguarded funds' the Regulation requires to be protected. Part Six, Chapter Two covers issuance and redemption of electronic money while Part Seven separately governs protection and safeguarding of protected funds, both within the same Regulation. You would conclude that issuing e-money triggers the safeguarding obligations in Part Seven — relevant to insolvency and customer-priority questions — so check those articles rather than assuming issued balances are ordinary company funds.
