# Conflict of Interest and Confidentiality

## Connections

### [[Code of Conduct and Work Ethics for Financial Institutions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing governance obligations on individuals within a SAMA-supervised institution, treat confidentiality and information-handling as enforceable conduct duties: the Code lists 'safeguarding confidentiality and mechanisms for information disclosure,' misuse and leaking of inside information for market manipulation, and gifts/hospitality among its mandatory principles. These bind all personnel (directors, executives, employees, consultants and third-party staff) as a minimum standard that the board must adopt into a firm-specific policy. The reader should conclude that conflict-of-interest and confidentiality breaches by individuals are disciplinable under the Code's consequences section and should be checked against, not substituted for, related governance and insider-information rules.
