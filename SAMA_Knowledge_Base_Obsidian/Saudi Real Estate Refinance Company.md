# Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are assessing who may conduct real estate refinance activity, note that this Regulation both creates and constrains the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company: it mandates PIF (or a wholly-owned entity) to incorporate the named joint stock company, sets a minimum capital of five billion riyals, restricts the licence to refinance activity, and subjects it to SAMA supervision. The entity is therefore a statutorily-defined vehicle, not an ordinary market participant, and its share-ownership limits (PIF ≥51%, finance entities ≤30%) are hard-coded. Conclude that any transaction, share acquisition, or product by this Company must be checked against Articles 16–20 and the required SAMA no-objection letters.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9-10 / Art 16-17):** "The capital of the real estate refinance company be not less than five billion riyals... shares of PIF...shall not, at any time, be less than 51 percent."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9 / Art 16-17):** "PIF or any entity wholly owned by it shall...incorporate a joint stock company to be named 'The Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company'"

### [[Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you need the operative rulebook for the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company, the Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies appear to be the detailed regime for exactly the vehicle the Implementing Regulation establishes: both use the same defined 'Real Estate Refinance Company,' both subject it to SAMA supervision under the Real Estate Finance Law and Finance Companies Control Law, and the Rules expressly state they are read in conjunction with those Laws and their Implementing Regulations. The connection is strong on shared definitions and hierarchy but is tagged inferred here. Conclude that operational duties (capital adequacy, prohibited activities, no-objection triggers) sit in the Rules, and check the Rules directly rather than the Regulation for day-to-day obligations of the Company.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9 / Art 16-17):** "the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company... shall be subject to the supervision of SAMA in accordance with the Law, this Regulation..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 21 / Art 51):** "These Rules shall be read in conjunction with the Finance Companies Control Law... the Real Estate Finance Law... and it's Implementing Regulations."
- **Caveat:** Edge is INFERRED; the Rules govern the same entity by shared definition and hierarchy, but verify scope in the Rules' primary text.
