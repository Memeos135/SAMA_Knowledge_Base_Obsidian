### [[Section B Risk Mitigation Requirements|Section B: Risk Mitigation Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping obligations for non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives, note Section B is the risk-mitigation limb of the same parent Requirements and applies on a different basis than Section A: it binds any bank that is a contracting party to non-centrally cleared OTC derivatives 'irrespective of the bank's outstanding notional amount... or whether or not the transaction is executed for hedging purposes.' Critically, centrally cleared OTC derivatives (directly or indirectly) are carved out of Section B. For a compliance decision you should first classify each transaction as centrally cleared or not — that classification determines whether the Section B risk-mitigation requirements (e.g. confirmation, clearing status reporting) apply at all.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Introduction para 2):** "OTC derivatives, which are centrally cleared, either directly or indirectly, are not subject to the risk mitigation requirements."
