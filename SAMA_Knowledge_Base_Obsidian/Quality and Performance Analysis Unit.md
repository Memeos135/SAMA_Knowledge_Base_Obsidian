# Quality and Performance Analysis Unit

## Connections

### [[Customer Care Department (Bank)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the mandatory structure of a bank's Customer Care Department, note that the Quality and Performance Analysis Unit is one of three units the department must contain at minimum, not an optional add-on. The instrument states the department must comprise at least a complaints-handling unit, a quality and performance analysis unit, and a financial awareness/education unit, with the bank sizing each unit to its business volume. A compliance reader should conclude that absence of a functioning quality unit — including root-cause analysis of recurring complaints — is itself a shortfall against these binding minimum controls.
