# Saudi Arabia Financial Investigation Unit (SAFIU)

## Connections

### [[Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a remittance center forms reasonable grounds to suspect ML/TF, SAFIU is the mandated recipient of the report, so identify it as the reporting endpoint for suspicious-activity obligations under this Guide. The Guide directs centers to report to SAFIU both where funds/transactions appear to be criminal proceeds or ML/TF-related and where a correspondent fails to respond on an incomplete-originator wire, and to retain related decisions and records for ten years under SAMA's AML/CFT rules. A reader should conclude that reporting cannot be discharged internally — SAFIU filing, plus documented rationale and ten-year retention, is the required response.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18 / item 26):** "When there are reasonable grounds to suspect that customers' funds, operations and transactions represent proceeds of criminal activity ... they must be reported to SAFIU."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / Section 3 Definitions):** "SAFIU: The authority authorized to receive and analyze reports of activities suspected of being related to money laundering and terrorist financing"
