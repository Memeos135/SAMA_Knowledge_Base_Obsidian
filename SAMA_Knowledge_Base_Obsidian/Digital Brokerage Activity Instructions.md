# Digital Brokerage Activity Instructions

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the legal authority behind the Digital Brokerage Activity Instructions, treat the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51) as the enabling parent statute — the Instructions expressly state SAMA issued them under the powers granted by that Law, and licensing/scope flows from the finance-supporting-activities licensing rules subordinate to it. This makes the Instructions a subordinate instrument: if the Law or its Implementing Regulation changes, the Instructions must be read consistently with it. For compliance decisions, a digital brokerage entity should verify licensing and conduct obligations against the Law and its Implementing Regulation, not the Instructions in isolation, since the Instructions state they do not derogate from related systems including the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation.

### [[Financial Institution Customer Protection]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping conduct obligations of a licensed digital-brokerage support entity, note it is not exempt from SAMA's customer-protection regime: the Digital Brokerage Instructions expressly state they do not derogate from related instructions, and list SAMA's Principles and Rules for Protecting Financial Institutions' Customers among the texts to be read alongside them. Both flow from SAMA's statutory mandate to issue instructions protecting customers. For a compliance decision you would apply the customer-protection principles cumulatively to the brokerage's dealings with its 'client' (العميل), in addition to the sector-specific technical, disclosure and conflict-of-interest duties in the Instructions.
