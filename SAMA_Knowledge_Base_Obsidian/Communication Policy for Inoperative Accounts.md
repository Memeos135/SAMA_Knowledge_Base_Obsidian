### [[Inoperative Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** In determining what a bank must do before reclassifying or closing an inoperative account, the customer-communication obligations are integral to the inoperative-account regime rather than an optional add-on: the Rule requires banks to contact customers, inform them of the intended action, and prompt reactivation before status change or closure. This is grounded in the express duties to notify customers 'before completing five years,' to give a month's notice before closing small balances, and to inform embassies/diplomats 'a year before changing the account status from dormant to unclaimed.' Conclude that failure to complete the prescribed communications is a compliance gap that can invalidate a status change or closure, so verify the notice steps for the specific stage in question.
