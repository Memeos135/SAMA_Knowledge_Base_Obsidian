# Leased Asset Acceptance Certificate

## Connections

### [[Ijara Financing Lease Contract]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating an Ijarah real estate financing, treat the Leased Asset Acceptance Certificate as an integral annex of the Ijarah contract, not a separate optional document — it is one of the enumerated appendices (شهادة القبول بالأصل المؤجر) to the standardized Ijarah form. Its function is to evidence the lessee's acceptance of the leased asset, which triggers rent-payment and possession obligations under the Ijarah contract's substantive articles. You would conclude that a compliant Ijarah file must include this certificate as executed in the mandated form, and that the certificate's legal effect flows from and is governed by the parent Ijarah contract.
