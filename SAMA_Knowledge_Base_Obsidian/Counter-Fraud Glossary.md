- **What this link tells you:** When interpreting any control requirement in the CFF, read the defined terms in Appendix A as controlling — the glossary supplies the operative meaning of terms like 'Counter-Fraud Programme', 'Employee', and 'External Fraud' used throughout the control requirements. Because both are parts of the same instrument (SAMA_EN_10530), a compliance reader should resolve scope questions (e.g. whether contractors and board members count as 'Employee') by reference to Appendix A rather than ordinary usage. Check the glossary before concluding whether a person or activity falls inside a given obligation.
