# SAMA Mandatory Instructions on Retail Real Estate Finance

## Connections

### [[Credit Advisor]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a bank or real estate finance company's pre-contract obligations for retail mortgages, note that this SAMA mandatory circular makes the credit advisor a hard gating requirement, not a courtesy. The instructions require the financier, sufficiently before the offer expires, to assign a qualified credit advisor to explain the nature, risks, terms and repricing mechanism of the proposed financing, and expressly prohibit that advisor from being the same employee who dealt with or presented the offer to the customer; documenting this contact is 'an essential requirement for completing the contract'. Conclude that no retail real estate finance contract may be concluded unless the credit advisor has given the explanation, answered all questions and documented it — a segregation-of-duties and disclosure precondition to a valid contract.

### [[Real Estate Finance Offer]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on retail mortgage sales conduct, treat the Real Estate Finance Offer as a regulated instrument with mandatory minimum terms set by this SAMA circular, not a freely-drafted commercial document. The circular requires the financier to give the customer an offer valid for not less than fifteen business days, containing clear printed copies of all documents in the same form that would be signed at contract, and to allow the customer to take those documents away for independent advice; it also imposes a cooling/review period of not less than five business days from receipt. Conclude that offer validity duration, document completeness and the review window are compliance-mandatory features whose breach, per paragraph 6, bars conclusion of any retail real estate finance contract.
