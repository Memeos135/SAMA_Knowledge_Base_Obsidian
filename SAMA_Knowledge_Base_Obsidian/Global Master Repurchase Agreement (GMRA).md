# Global Master Repurchase Agreement (GMRA)

## Connections

### [[Master Repo Agreement (MRA)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When deciding which master agreement governs a KSA repo, distinguish the two: the SAMA-approved MRA (2020 version) is mandatory for repo transactions in Saudi Arabia and is subject to Saudi law, whereas the GMRA is only tolerated for pre-existing cross-border transactions with a foreign-currency leg, subject to compliance with Saudi rules. Both are functionally equivalent industry master agreements for sale-and-repurchase of securities, which is why the guideline treats them in parallel. You would conclude that new SAR-denominated repo activity must use the MRA and cannot default to the GMRA, and that reliance on the GMRA is a narrow carve-out to verify against section 5.3.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / 5.3):** "there is no prohibition on bank's existing repo transaction... governed by the GMRA, subject to compliance to the relevant and applicable rules and regulations in Saudi Arabia."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / 7.1):** "repo transactions in Saudi Arabia must be governed by the standard Master Repurchase Agreement (MRA)... (2020 version)... approved by SAMA."
