# Bank Periodic Data Reporting

## Connections

### [[Implementation Rules for Banking Control Law (SAMA 1429)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's ongoing reporting duties, treat the periodic-data obligations as a direct, enforceable output of Article (17) of the Banking Control Law as operationalised by these Implementation Rules — not optional practice. The Rules specify the exact statements and frequencies (monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual) banks must submit to SAMA, plus auditor-provided data on request. You should therefore scope reporting obligations by reference to the frequency schedule in these Rules and treat late, incomplete, or withheld submissions as a compliance breach exposed to Article (22) penalties.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5-6):** "(7) Any other data or information requested in the form and at the time determined by SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1):** "Article (17) regarding the organization and specification of the periodic data that must be provided to SAMA for supervisory and statistical purposes"
