# IRB Risk-Weight Functions

## Connections

### [[IRB Risk Components (PD, LGD, EAD, M)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining RWA under the IRB approach, recognise that the risk components (PD, LGD, EAD, M) are the inputs that drive the risk-weight functions — the two are sequential steps in the same calculation, not separate regimes. The corpus structures IRB into chapter 11 (Risk Weight Functions) and chapter 12 (Risk components), with functions producing risk-weighted assets for defaulted and non-defaulted exposures from those component estimates. A reader checking capital adequacy should confirm that component estimates comply with the quantification standards before relying on any RWA output, since errors in components propagate directly into the risk-weight functions.
