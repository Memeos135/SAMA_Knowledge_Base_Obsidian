# Portfolio Segmentation

## Connections

### [[Initial Viability Assessment]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing how a bank triages NPLs, read the initial viability assessment as the second, embedded stage of the segmentation process, not a standalone step. The guidance sequences them explicitly: 'Stage two — initial viability assessment' follows the Stage one segmentation, and both are to be done 'immediately after the non-performing loan is transferred to the Workout Unit.' You would verify that viable, marginally-viable and non-viable classifications flow directly from the segmentation exercise and drive resolution routing (workout vs. legal/foreclosure), since the guidance says non-viable loans should be 'promptly referred to legal unit.'
- **Grounding — this node (Page 22):** "Stage two — initial viability assessment. Following the initial segmentation, NPLs which are currently not in legal procedure should be further screened"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 23):** "determining viable, marginally-viable and non-viable borrowers"

### [[NPL Strategy]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's compliance with SAMA's Guidelines on Management of Problem Loans, treat portfolio segmentation as an operative component of the NPL strategy rather than a separate exercise: the design phase requires targets set 'by main portfolios', and segmentation is the mechanism that produces those homogeneous portfolios. Both provisions sit in the same guidance (SAMA_EN_3575), and segmentation is described as 'the essential first step in developing a cost-effective and efficient approach to NPL resolution.' You would check that a bank's documented NPL strategy actually maps reduction targets onto its segmentation criteria (nature of business, arrears buckets, loan balance), not merely lists them abstractly.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 21):** "It is the essential first step in developing a cost-effective and efficient approach to NPL resolution."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 28):** "by main portfolios (e.g. retail mortgage, retail consumer, retail small bus[iness])"
