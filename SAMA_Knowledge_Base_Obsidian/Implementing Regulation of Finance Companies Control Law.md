# Implementing Regulation of Finance Companies Control Law

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the legal basis of any finance-company obligation, treat the Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree M/51) as the primary statute and its Implementing Regulation (Governor's decision) as the subordinate instrument that operationalises it. The Shariah Governance Instructions themselves are issued under authority derived from both the Law and its Implementing Regulation, and defined terms flow from Law to Regulation to Instructions. You would conclude that to resolve a requirement you must read the Law first, then the Implementing Regulation, and not rely on the Regulation in isolation.

### [[Guideline for Calculating Early Repayment Amount]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining a borrower's early-repayment entitlement and the maximum compensation a financier may charge, read the Guideline as a subordinate explanatory instrument built directly on the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law: the Guideline expressly states it was prepared based on Articles 82 and 84 of that Regulation (alongside the Finance Lease Regulation and Consumer Finance rules). Because the Guideline is illustrative, the binding early-repayment limits — declining-balance method and compensation capped at the term cost of the three following months — derive from the cited Regulation articles, not from the Guideline itself. You should treat the Guideline's worked examples as interpretive aid but anchor any compliance decision on the primary Regulation text it cites.

### [[Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When interpreting undefined terms or scope in the Shariah Governance Instructions, do not read them in isolation — Article Two directs that words carry the meanings given in the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. The Instructions are anchored to both the Law and its Implementing Regulation (issued by Governor decision), which supply the definitional backbone. Conclude that any compliance analysis of the Instructions requires cross-checking the Implementing Regulation for the operative definitions and licensing conditions, since those instruments govern gaps in the Instructions.
