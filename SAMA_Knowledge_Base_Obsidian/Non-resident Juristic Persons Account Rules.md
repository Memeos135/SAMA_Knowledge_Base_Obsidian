- **What this link tells you:** When establishing relationships with non-resident financial institutions — particularly non-resident/GCC banks under the 300.2 rules — the completed AML/CFT form is a mandatory correspondent-banking control: the bank must obtain it from each correspondent bank confirming commitment to AML/CFT policies and that the bank does not allow shell banks to use its accounts. This link connects the enhanced due diligence expected for cross-border institutional counterparties to the specific onboarding documentation required. You would conclude that for non-resident banking counterparties an AML/CFT attestation plus assessment of internal controls is a condition of the relationship, not optional.
