# Outsourcing Rules

**Cohesion:** 0.29 - loosely connected
**Members:** 7 nodes

## Why this community

Outsourcing governance regime: SAMA's controls on delegating functions to third parties, distinguishing material from non-material arrangements and imposing prior-approval and continuity safeguards.

## How members connect

- Rules on Outsourcing is the governing instrument; the DBC Outsourcing Requirement references it, and it in turn scopes Material Outsourcing, Third-Party Service Provider, and the special Foreign Bank Branch case.
- Trigger/consequence: classification as Material Outsourcing activates the SAMA No Objection Requirement (prior regulatory clearance) and Business Continuity Management obligations.
- Note scope limit: Foreign Bank Branch Outsourcing is treated as a distinct sub-case under the same Rules, signalling differentiated application by entity type.
