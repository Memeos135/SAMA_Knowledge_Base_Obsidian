# Bank Account Supervision

**Cohesion:** 0.40 - moderately connected
**Members:** 6 nodes

## Why this community

Supervisory rules for opening, operating, freezing and disclosing bank accounts, integrating account-integrity controls with KYC/AML obligations under SAMA supervision.

## How members connect

- 'Chapter II: Supervisory Rules and Controls' is the governing provision that references account freezing, disclosure/enforcement, the electronic record system, and the KYC/AML requirements as component obligations.
- The KYC Principle and AML/CFT link ties account supervision to the AML regime — customer identification/monitoring conditions the opening and maintenance of accounts.
- Account-freezing measures share data with the electronic record system, indicating freeze actions rely on and update centralised records for enforcement.
- SAMA is the supervisory authority referenced for disclosure and enforcement, confirming these are regulator-imposed obligations on account-holding institutions.
