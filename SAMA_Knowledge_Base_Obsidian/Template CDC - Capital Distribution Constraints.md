- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's disclosures on capital adequacy are complete, note that KM1 (key prudential metrics, quarterly, mandatory for all banks) and CDC (capital distribution constraints, annual, mandatory for banks) both concern the capital-ratio/buffer stack but serve distinct functions — KM1 reports actual ratios over time while CDC discloses the CET1 ratio below which distribution constraints are triggered, taking into account conservation buffer, G-SIB surcharge, countercyclical buffer and Pillar 2. The link is one of a shared Pillar 3 capital-ratio framework rather than a direct computational cross-reference. Conclude that both must be produced (at their respective frequencies) and that CDC provides the threshold context for interpreting the CET1/buffer figures reported in KM1.
