# Violation Topic Classification Guide

## Connections

### [[Instructions for Publishing Banking Penalties]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a bank prepares its annual-report disclosure of SAMA penalties, it cannot label violations freely: the instructions require that violation topics be determined 'only in accordance with the attached Violation Topic Classification Guide,' which maps each specific penal-decision topic to one of five published categories. This makes the Guide a binding taxonomy, not illustrative guidance — the annexed table (Pages 3-4) converts granular decision wording (e.g., AML/CFT, consumer protection, ATM/POS performance) into fixed public headings. The reader should conclude that disclosure wording must be reconciled against this Guide, and any topic not listed falls to the residual 'other' category rather than a self-chosen label.
