# Remote Customer Service

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 3 nodes

## Why this community

Consumer-protection expectations for customer service delivered remotely, situated within SAMA's remote-work supervisory framework. Concerns maintaining service quality and accessible communication channels when staff operate off-site.

## How members connect

- Remote Work Compliance in Customer Service and Providing Customer Communication Channels are both conceptually tied to the Remote Work Initiative — the initiative sets the context in which these service obligations apply.
- Links are thematic rather than a strict law->regulation hierarchy; the obligation reader should treat customer communication-channel provision as a continuing duty regardless of remote-working arrangements.
- Cross-regime touchpoint: remote-work operational arrangements do not relax consumer-protection service standards.
