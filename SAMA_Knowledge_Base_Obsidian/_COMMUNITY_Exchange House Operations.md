# Exchange House Operations

**Cohesion:** 0.14 - loosely connected
**Members:** 17 nodes

## Why this community

AML/CTF and operating-conduct regime for exchange houses and money-service operations: KYC/CDD duties, suspicious-transaction and cross-border reporting, and account/secrecy rules anchored in the AML Law and FATF standards.

## How members connect

- The Exchange Houses Operating Procedures Guide is the central conduct instrument, referencing the AML Law, CDD, KYC, correspondent banking, cross-border cash transport, STR, and FATF as its obligation sources.
- CDD and KYC are treated as linked defined concepts; STR feeds the Financial Investigations Unit, establishing the reporting obligation chain from detection to authority.
- Rules Regulating Exchange Business (Decision 1730) is the licensing/operating framework, updated by the Cross-Border Cash Movement Data Requirement and Work Procedures updates — treat these as amendments to operating duties.
- Account-related instruments (Current Account Template, Banking Accounts Rules, Data on Bank Accounts) reference the AML Law and Banking Secrecy, showing account onboarding and data-disclosure obligations sit within the same AML/secrecy tension.
