# CCR in the Trading Book

## Connections

### [[Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When capitalising internal risk transfers of banking-book credit exposure hedged through the trading book, recognise that the trading-book (CCR/market-risk) treatment and the banking-book credit-risk treatment are interdependent: a banking-book credit exposure is deemed hedged for capital purposes only if the trading book enters an exactly-matching external hedge with an eligible third-party protection provider. This cross-links the CCR/trading-book rules with the Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk (SCRE), under which banking-book credit RWA are computed. Conclude that failing the matching conditions means the banking-book exposure remains fully capitalised under credit-risk rules while the external hedge is captured in market risk — so you cannot claim capital relief on the credit-risk leg without satisfying the trading-book hedging conditions.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 379, s.5.21):** "The credit exposure in the banking book is deemed to be hedged for capital requirement purposes if and only if: The trading book enters into an external hedge with an eligible third-party protection provider that exactly matches the internal risk transfer."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 751, rows 1-5):** "Credit risk (excluding counterparty credit risk): RWA and capital requirements according to the credit risk standard of the Basel framework (SCRE)."
