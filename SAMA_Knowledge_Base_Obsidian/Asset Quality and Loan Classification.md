# Asset Quality and Loan Classification

## Connections

### [[Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) Regulations 2020]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a DTFC's credit and provisioning obligations, read the Asset Quality chapter as mandatory prudential rules of the parent Regulations, not as accounting discretion — every DTFC must review, classify and provision its loan portfolio at least quarterly using the classification criteria and the fixed minimum provisioning percentages (Normal 1% through Loss 100%). The rules also impose contagion classification (one non-performing loan taints all loans to that borrower) and profit-suspension on non-performing exposures. Conclude that provisioning cannot simply follow IFRS where these Regulations require more: where regulatory provisions exceed IFRS impairment, the higher regulatory figure and statutory loan loss reserve treatment governs.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 11-12 / paras 35-45):** "Every DTFC shall review, classify and appropriately make provisions for its loan portfolio at least once every three months... For loans classified 'Loss', 100%"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / para 3):** "DTFCs are also required to comply with the Finance Companies Control Law, SAMA regulations for Finance Companies (FCs), and other relevant laws and regulations"

### [[Stage 123 Credit Risk Classification|Stage 1/2/3 Credit Risk Classification]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you work across both finance-company regimes, note these two classification systems appear to address the same credit-quality problem but are NOT interchangeable — the ECL Rules use an IFRS 9 Stage 1/2/3 (with 2A/2B, 3A/3B) framework, while the DTFC regulations use a distinct Normal/Watch/Substandard/Doubtful/Loss ladder with fixed provisioning percentages (1%/5%/25%/75%/100%). Both operate under the Finance Companies Control Law and both drive provisioning, but the DTFC scheme is prescriptive and category-based whereas the ECL Rules are model-based. Because this link is inferred, verify the primary texts before mapping one to the other: a compliance reviewer should treat a DTFC as potentially subject to both the DTFC Appendix C classification and the ECL staging rules, and confirm which return/framework applies to a given portfolio rather than assuming Stage 3 equals 'Loss'.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_8383 Page 12 / Chapter 6):** "For loans classified 'Normal', 1%; 'Watch', 5%; 'Substandard', 25%; 'Doubtful', 75%; and 'Loss', 100%."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / Page 12):** "Stage 2A ... Stage 2B ... any exposure which is assessed as impaired or otherwise is in default as determined in Section 8."
- **Caveat:** Relationship is INFERRED — the two classification/provisioning schemes are conceptually parallel but structurally different; confirm in the primary texts which framework binds a given entity before treating them as equivalent.
