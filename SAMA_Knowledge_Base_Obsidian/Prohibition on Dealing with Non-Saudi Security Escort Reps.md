# Prohibition on Dealing with Non-Saudi Security Escort Reps

## Connections

### [[Law on Transport of Money, Precious Metals & Valuable Documents]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising banks on engaging security-escort or cash-transport providers, read the SAMA prohibition circular as an enforcement instruction operating under the Law on Transport of Money, Precious Metals & Valuable Documents (and the Private Civil Security Guarding Law) — the circular expressly reaffirms compliance with that law and its Implementing Regulation while barring dealings with non-Saudi representatives of such firms. The consequence: banks must not negotiate with any non-Saudi representative of these companies and must report them to regional police, and this duty sits atop the licensing/eligibility requirements the parent law places on cash-transport activity. Check counterparty representatives' status and licensing under the transport law before contracting.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_6493 Page 1):** "عدم التعامل والتفاوض مع أي مندوب غير سعودي ... التقيد التام ... نظام نقل النقود والمعادن الثمينة والمستندات ذات القيمة ولائحته التنفيذية"
- **Grounding — related node (SAMA_EN_7278 Page 5, Art 2):** "لا يجوز الترخيص بمزاولة نقل النقود والمعادن الثمينة والمستندات ذات القيمة إلا للجهات المشار إليها ... بشرط توافر إدارة خاصة بالأمن"

### [[Law on Transport of Money, Precious Metals & Valuable Documents (Royal Decree)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are advising a bank on whether it may engage a cash-transport or security-escort provider, treat these two instruments as one compliance picture: the circular (6493) directs banks not to deal or negotiate with any non-Saudi representative of private civil security-guard and cash-transport firms and to report them, while the Royal Decree Law (7278) sets the statutory licensing regime for the transport of money, precious metals and valuable documents that those firms operate under. The circular itself references compliance with 'نظام نقل النقود والمعادن الثمينة والمستندات ذات القيمة ولائحته التنفيذية,' so the link is a subordinate circular reinforcing a parent law. This connection appears sound but is inferred from thematic overlap; before relying on it, verify in the primary Law text which entities may be licensed (banks, exchange houses, licensed private civil-guard companies) and confirm the circular's non-dealing/reporting duty against the current licensing provisions.
