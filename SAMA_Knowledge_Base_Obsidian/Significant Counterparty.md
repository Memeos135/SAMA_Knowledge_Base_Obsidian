# Significant Counterparty

## Connections

### [[Concentration of Funding Metric]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying the concentration-of-funding metric, the trigger for inclusion is the defined term 'significant counterparty' — you cannot compute the numerator without it. The metric aggregates all liabilities and direct borrowings to a single counterparty or group of connected/affiliated counterparties, and para 191 fixes the threshold at more than 1% of the bank's total balance sheet (with room for other defining characteristics). A compliance reader should conclude that scoping this reporting line depends first on correctly identifying and grouping connected counterparties against the 1% test, not on individual exposures viewed in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 53 / para 191):** "A "significant counterparty" is defined as a single counterparty or group of connected or affiliated counterparties accounting in aggregate for more than 1% of the bank's total balance sheet."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 53 / para 190):** "The numerator for counterparties is calculated by aggregating the total of all types of liabilities to a single counterparty or group of connected or affiliated counterparties..."
