# SAMA Supervision and Inspection

## Connections

### [[Banking Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** This link tells you SAMA's supervisory and inspection powers are not free-standing but grounded in and bounded by the Banking Control Law — specifically Articles (18) inspection and (22) penalties. When challenging or scoping a supervisory action, you should check that it traces to a specific enabling article of the Law, because SAMA's authority to inspect, demand data, and sanction flows from those provisions. The practical consequence is that both the bank's cooperation duty and SAMA's enforcement reach are defined by the Law's articles, not by SAMA's discretion alone.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6):** "In implementation of Article (22)... if SAMA obtains knowledge that a bank has violated the provisions of the Banking Control Law and its Implementation Rules"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1):** "Article (22) regarding the procedures and penalties SAMA is authorized to apply under this Resolution in implementation of this Article"

### [[Implementation Rules for Banking Control Law (SAMA 1429)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When preparing for a SAMA inspection, treat cooperation as a legally mandated, non-discretionary duty: these Implementation Rules operationalise Article (18) by expressly prohibiting refusal of access to records, hiding information, or ignoring inspection recommendations. Non-cooperation is itself an enumerated violation, and Article (22) (implemented in the same Rules) authorises SAMA to apply procedures and penalties. You should therefore conclude that obstruction or concealment during inspection is independently sanctionable, separate from any underlying substantive breach.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6):** "banks shall fully cooperate with SAMA's inspection team when visiting the bank. To achieve this, the bank's staff shall not exercise any of the following acts"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1):** "Article (18) regarding banking inspections conducted by SAMA, the behavior of the bank staff, and compliance with the recommendations and instructions issued by SAMA"
