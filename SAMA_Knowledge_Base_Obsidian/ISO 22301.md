# ISO 22301

## Connections

### [[Business Continuity Management Framework]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether meeting ISO 22301 alone discharges a Member Organization's obligations, note that the BCM Framework treats ISO 22301 only as a source input, not the binding standard. The framework states its requirements 'are based on SAMA requirements, industry practices and international standards, such as ISO 22301,' and mandates compliance with the SAMA document itself. You should conclude that the enforceable obligation is the SAMA framework as written; ISO 22301 conformance is supportive evidence but does not substitute for the framework's specific control considerations.
