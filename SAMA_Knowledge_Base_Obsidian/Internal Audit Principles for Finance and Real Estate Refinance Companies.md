# Internal Audit Principles for Finance and Real Estate Refinance Companies

## Connections

### [[Compliance Principles for Finance and Real Estate Refinance Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping the governance/control obligations of a finance or real estate refinance company, treat the Internal Audit Principles and Compliance Principles as a linked control framework rather than isolated documents. Both share the same defined subjects ('Company', 'Board', 'Executive Management', 'Audit Committee') and the same scope (binding on finance and real estate refinance companies, advisory-only for finance support and lease-registration companies), and the Compliance Principles list SAMA instructions as a body the Compliance Unit must monitor — including internal-audit-adjacent duties. Practically, you should reconcile the two 'second-line'/'third-line' functions when scoping responsibilities: the Compliance Unit reports to the Audit Committee and reviews specific departments, while Internal Audit provides independent assurance over the whole control system, so an obligation satisfied for one does not discharge the other.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Scope of Application 3.1):** "The provisions of these Principles shall apply to finance companies and real estate refinance companies."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Scope of Application (a)):** "The provisions of these Principles shall apply to finance and real estate refinance companies."

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining who is bound by the Internal Audit Principles and on what legal footing, read the Principles as subordinate to the Finance Companies Control Law: the Principles expressly define 'Law' as the Finance Companies Control Law and 'Company' as finance and real estate refinance companies licensed by SAMA. This means the Principles are implementing instructions under the Law's supervisory mandate, not free-standing rules. Consequently, for a licensed finance company the internal audit obligations are mandatory, while finance support companies and lease-registration companies receive them only as a guide unless SAMA elects to enforce them.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Section One):** "Law: The Finance Companies Control Law... Company: The finance companies and the real estate refinance companies licensed by SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Art 1):** "Law: Finance Companies Control Law... Finance company: A joint stock company licensed to engage in finance activities."
