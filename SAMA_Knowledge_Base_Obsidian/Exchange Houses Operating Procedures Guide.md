### [[Suspicious Transaction Reporting]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping an exchange house's AML duties, treat suspicious-transaction reporting as a mandatory, standalone obligation the Guide imposes: it requires reporting any operation where there are reasonable grounds to suspect a link to criminal or irregular activity to the competent authority (the FIU).
