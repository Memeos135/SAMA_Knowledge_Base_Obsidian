# Negotiating Strategy

### [[SWOT Analysis]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When preparing to negotiate a rehabilitation plan, treat SWOT analysis as the analytical input feeding the negotiating strategy rather than a separate exercise. The Guidelines direct that the bank should identify strengths and weaknesses in the account and 'build the plan into negotiating strategy,' with SWOT considering all internal and external factors before borrower negotiations begin. You should use this link to conclude that a documented account assessment (SWOT plus root-cause analysis) is expected groundwork before finalising negotiation objectives, though these are advisory best-practice steps rather than hard prescriptive requirements.

### [[Workout Plan]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When examining how a workout plan was finalized with the borrower, treat the negotiating strategy (including SWOT analysis of the account) as the guideline-expected preparatory step that shapes the plan's terms rather than an informal tactic. Conclude that a workout plan reached without a documented negotiating strategy lacks the analytical basis SAMA expects, so check that the plan's terms trace back to a pre-negotiation assessment of the account's strengths and weaknesses.
