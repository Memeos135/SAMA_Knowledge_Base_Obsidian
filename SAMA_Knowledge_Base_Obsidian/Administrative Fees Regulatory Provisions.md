# Administrative Fees Regulatory Provisions

## Connections

### [[Consumer Finance Controls]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying administrative-fee limits, note that this issuance directly amends Article 4 of the Consumer Finance (Implementation of Finance) Controls to require banks and finance companies to comply with SAMA's administrative-fee instructions and to bar deducting such fees before contract signing (with the real-estate valuation exception). The Consumer Finance Controls are therefore the substantive home of the fee-cap obligation, and the fee provisions here operate as an amendment to that regime. For a compliance decision, check the current amended text of Controls Art. 4 alongside the Tariff Guide, since the fee ceiling and timing rule now live in the amended Controls rather than in a standalone tariff instrument.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / amended text):** "لا يجوز حسم الرسوم الإدارية إلا بعد توقيع العقد باستثناء تكاليف التقييم العقاري"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / reference to Art. 4):** "المادة (4) من ضوابط التمويل الاستهلاكي المحدثة بموجب التعميم رقم ..."
- **Caveat:** Source Arabic is OCR-garbled in places; article number (4) is drawn from the cover circular's stated amendments, not full clause text.

### [[Financial Institutions Services Tariff Guide]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a finance provider may charge, read the Administrative Fees provisions and the Services Tariff Guide as one issuance: the same circular attaches the Tariff Guide and, on the same authority approval, amends the underlying regulatory texts governing administrative fees. The Guide is binding on SAMA-supervised institutions when imposing fees for services and products, and it supersedes any conflicting prior provisions. For a fee-compliance decision, conclude that the amended fee articles (finance controls Art. 4, Consumer Protection conduct rule, and the model consumer finance contract clause) must be applied together with the Guide's tariff caps — the Guide does not stand alone but is anchored to the amended statutory texts.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / amended provisions):** "يجب على البنك أو شركة التمويل الالتزام بتعليمات البنك المركزي الصادرة في شأن الرسوم الإدارية"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1 / cover circular):** "مرافق لكم دليل تعرفة خدمات المؤسسات المالية ... الالتزام ... عند فرض رسوم مقابل الخدمات والمنتجات المقدمة للعملاء"
