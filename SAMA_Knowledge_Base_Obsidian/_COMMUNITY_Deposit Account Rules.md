# Deposit Account Rules

**Cohesion:** 0.10 - loosely connected
**Members:** 22 nodes

## Why this community

Deposit account operating rules intersecting with AML/CTF customer due diligence and prudential reporting for deposit-taking institutions, including deposit-taking finance companies. Covers account types, KYC/beneficial-owner verification, dormancy/freezing, and asset-quality reporting.

## How members connect

- Accounts Operating Rules define and govern General, Deposit, and Term Deposit accounts and the sub-regimes for juristic persons, inactive/dormant accounts, and freezing/updating — all keyed to the General Account definition.
- KYC links to CTF Due Diligence Measures and AML Due Diligence Measures; beneficial-owner identification/verification (incl. Awqaf) ties account onboarding to the AML and CTF Implementing Regulations under the Supervisory Authority.
- Deposit Taking Finance Company (DTFC) is subject to prudential returns — Appendix A (capital to RWA), Appendix B (liquidity) — connecting account rules to reporting obligations.
- Loan Review / Classification and Provisioning with Appendix C asset-quality classification sit alongside as SAMA-supervised credit-quality reporting for the same institutions.
