# Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book (IRRBB)

## Connections

### [[Basel IRRBB Standard (2016)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what standard governs a domestic bank's IRRBB management, treat the Basel IRRBB standard as the substantive content that SAMA has adopted and made binding through this 2017 circular. The circular explicitly transmits the Basel Committee's revised interest rate risk principles and its stricter outlier threshold (15% of Tier 1 capital) as SAMA's own regulatory expectation for identification, measurement, monitoring and control of IRRBB. Note the scope limit: the cover directs this to 'All Domestic Banks only and not applicable to Foreign Bank Branches' — so before applying the standard, confirm the entity is an in-scope domestic bank.
