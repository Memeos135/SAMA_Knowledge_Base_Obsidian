# Registered Mortgage Law

## Connections

### [[Mortgage Registration Procedural Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank or finance company's mortgage documentation is enforceable, read these procedural requirements as SAMA's implementation of the Registered Mortgage Law (Royal Decree No. M/49), which the circular expressly cites as its statutory reference. The circular does not create the mortgage regime; it operationalizes registration for financiers with notaries and mandates use of the SAMA/Ministry of Justice forms, so compliance turns on both the substantive law and these procedural steps (mortgagee must be a licensed bank/finance company, mortgaged property must be owned by the mortgagor, Shariah-committee product approval, etc.). Confirm each procedural condition is met and that the underlying registration complies with the Registered Mortgage Law before relying on the mortgage as valid security.
