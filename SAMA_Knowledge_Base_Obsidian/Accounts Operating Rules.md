# Accounts Operating Rules

## Connections

### [[DTFC General Account Opening Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how a DTFC must respond to official disclosure or blocking demands, read the operating rules together with the opening data-capture requirements, because the search obligation depends on data gathered at opening. Chapter 14 requires that all communications on disclosure/blocking pass through SAMA only, and that any search cover every category of account (active, closed, suspense, inactive, dormant), while the opening chapter fixes what customer and authorized-person data must be recorded. You should conclude that a DTFC cannot respond directly to a court or other authority — only via SAMA — and that its disclosure reply must confirm all account categories were searched.

### [[Term Deposit Account Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a Deposit-Taking Finance Company (DTFC) on account operations, treat the General Account and Term Deposit Account rules as one integrated regime rather than separate products, because the operating rules make the General Account exist solely to service Term Deposits and hard-wire funding flows between them. Under the linkage rules a Term Deposit may only be credited from the General Account to which it was tied at initiation, must share that account's name, cannot be de-linked/re-linked, and on maturity principal and profit route back to the originating General Account. For compliance decisions, conclude that you cannot assess Term Deposit obligations (funding source matching, disclosure/blocking on SAMA request, freezing on ID expiry) in isolation — the linked General Account inherits the same customer identity and is captured in any SAMA disclosure or blocking search.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 55 / Para 195):** "As stated in Section 1.1, the sole purpose of the General Account is to facilitate operations of Term Deposit Accounts."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 46 / Para 120):** "DTFC must linked Term Deposit Account to a General Account upon its initiation. (Term Deposit Account will only receive credits from the same General Account to which it is linked...)"
