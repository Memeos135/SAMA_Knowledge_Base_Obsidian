# Restructuring KPIs

## Connections

### [[Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning Rules]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating how a restructured/forborne exposure should be classified and provisioned, read the credit-risk classification material together with the restructuring principles and early-warning indicators, since they are elements of the same SAMA guideline and share the concept of 'Forborne Exposures' and negative-trend triggers. The early-warning signs listed (arrears, negative rating trends, forborne status) feed both restructuring decisions and how the exposure is graded and provisioned. For a decision this means a restructuring does not neutralise classification/provisioning obligations — a forborne or early-arrears exposure remains subject to the risk-grading treatment set out in the guideline.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 83 / Appendix 3):** "If banks are effective in identifying early warning signs, addressing the issues and engaging in early restructuring solutions..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 80):** "Forborne Exposures... Negative trend in probability of default and/or internal rating... Early arrears (e.g. 5-30 days of past due...)"

### [[Monitoring the Restructuring Plan]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's restructuring practice meets SAMA's expectations, treat the Restructuring Principles and the monitoring of the restructuring plan as one continuous obligation rather than separate boxes. Both sit within the same SAMA credit-workout guideline: the principles (early warning identification, good faith, sustainable solutions, avoiding repeated short-term fixes) set the standard the ongoing monitoring must operationalise once a plan is live. For a compliance review this means checking that monitoring activity demonstrably follows the stated principles — e.g. that early warning signs trigger action and that repeated short-term rollovers are flagged rather than tolerated.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 83 / Appendix 3):** "If banks are effective in identifying early warning signs... this could prevent long-term default... Banks and borrowers should seek sustainable solutions and avoid repeated short-term fixes."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 46 / 5.2.1):** "the bank and the viable... borrower will agree on the restructuring strategy aiming to return the defaulted borrower to a fully performing status in the shortest feasible time frame."
