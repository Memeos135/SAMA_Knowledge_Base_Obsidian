# CCR Collateral & Mitigation

**Cohesion:** 0.18 - loosely connected
**Members:** 11 nodes

## Why this community

Counterparty credit risk (CCR) and credit risk mitigation for derivatives, repo/SFT and off-balance-sheet exposures — the rules governing how collateral, netting and exposure measurement reduce capital requirements. Drives decisions on recognized collateral value and permitted CCR measurement methods.

## How members connect

- The 'CCR Framework' is the anchor; SA-CCR is its prescribed exposure-measurement method for derivatives.
- Collateralized Transactions branch into the Simple vs. Comprehensive approaches, the latter invoking Supervisory Haircuts and Master Netting Agreements for SFTs to compute net exposure.
- Off-Balance Sheet Items/CCF and Credit Derivatives feed exposure amounts into the CCR framework.
- Worked examples (Equity Investment in Funds LTA, Leverage Adjustment) illustrate application rather than impose separate obligations.
