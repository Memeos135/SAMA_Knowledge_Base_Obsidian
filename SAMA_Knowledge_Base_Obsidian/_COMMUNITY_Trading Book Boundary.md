# Trading Book Boundary

**Cohesion:** 0.29 - loosely connected
**Members:** 8 nodes

## Why this community

Market-risk capital regime turning on the banking book / trading book boundary: how an instrument's book classification determines whether it attracts market-risk or credit-risk capital.

## How members connect

- The Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk is the governing framework; it references the boundary rules, the Trading Book Policy Statement and the credit-risk framework, allocating each position to the correct capital regime.
- Boundary determination is operationalized through the Trading Book Policy Statement and Trading Desk construct — the unit at which trading intent and eligibility are assessed and documented.
- Internal Risk Transfer, CVA and the Look-Through Approach for Funds are boundary-sensitive treatments: mis-classification moves capital between the market-risk and credit-risk (SAMA Credit Risk) frameworks, which is the decision-relevant consequence.
- Cross-reference to SAMA Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk marks where positions outside the trading book, and CVA/internal-transfer legs, fall to be capitalized as credit risk.
