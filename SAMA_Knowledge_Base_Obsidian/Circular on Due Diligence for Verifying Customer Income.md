# Circular on Due Diligence for Verifying Customer Income

## Connections

### [[Credit Card Issuance and Operation Rules]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing pre-issuance affordability and customer-due-diligence duties, these instruments appear to reinforce a common theme — verifying the customer before extending credit — but apply to distinct addressees. The Credit Card Rules define 'due diligence' as identifying and verifying customer/beneficial-owner information to assess the issuer's risk exposure, while the income-verification circular directs finance companies to exercise due diligence and validate income-proof documents before granting finance, grounded in the Finance Companies Control Law. You should note the circular binds finance companies specifically; before applying its income-verification emphasis to credit-card issuers, confirm the applicable rules for the relevant licensee category rather than assuming a single cross-product standard.

- **Caveat:** Conceptual link only; the income-verification circular is addressed to finance companies under the Finance Companies Control Law, whereas the card rules cover issuers under the Central Bank's card-issuance regime — verify which regime binds a given entity.
