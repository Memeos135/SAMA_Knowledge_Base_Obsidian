# Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining which supervisory regime governs a real estate refinance company, note that it sits under two parent laws simultaneously: the Real Estate Finance Law and its Regulations, AND the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations. The Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies expressly subject the Company to SAMA supervision 'in accordance with ... the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations', so obligations and sanctions under that Law reach these entities. For a compliance decision, this means you must check finance-company-level requirements (e.g. licensing, governance, SAMA enforcement powers) in addition to the sector-specific refinance Rules, not treat the refinance Rules as exhaustive.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_2081 Page 4 / Art 2(1)):** "subject to the supervision and control of SAMA in accordance with ... the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Ch.2 para 2):** "licensed finance companies under Finance Companies Control Law issued by Royal Decree No. (M/51) dated 13/08/1433H"

### [[Real Estate Finance Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining which obligations bind a Real Estate Refinance Company, treat the Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies as subordinate to and dependent on the Real Estate Finance Law, not as a standalone code. The Rules expressly import their defined terms from the Law and its Implementing Regulations and place the Company under SAMA supervision 'in accordance with' the Law — so definitions like Real Estate Refinance Company, Borrower and Contractual Rights carry through unchanged. Practically, read the two instruments together: the Rules cannot narrow or override the Law, and any term left undefined in the Rules must be resolved from the Law before you rely on a scope reading.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_2081_VER1 / Art 1 (Page 3)):** "The terms and phrases used in these Rules shall have the same meanings as those defined in the Real Estate Finance Law and its Implementing Regulations."
- **Grounding — related node (SAMA_EN_190_VER1 / Art 1 (Page 2)):** "Real Estate Refinance Company: a joint stock company licensed to engage in real estate refinance activities."

### [[Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining who at a real estate refinance company needs SAMA's prior non-objection before assuming senior roles, do not treat the Refinance Company Rules in isolation — the appointments regime applies to it as a SAMA-supervised 'Financial Institution' (a defined term expressly including finance companies and refinance companies). Because the Appointments Requirements independently mandate written non-objection for board nominations and senior management appointments, a refinance company must satisfy both its own Rules and this fit-and-proper regime. Conclude that senior-hire governance for these companies is sourced from the Appointments Requirements, not the Refinance Rules, and check the applicable appendix for which positions require non-objection.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Art 1):** "Real Estate Refinance Company (the Company): A joint stock company licensed to engage in real estate refinance activities."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Ch.1 Definitions):** "The entity that is supervised by SAMA, including banks... finance companies, refinance companies... operating in Saudi Arabia."
- **Caveat:** The link is a scope/definition overlap; neither document expressly cites the other, so confirm the refinance company falls within the Appointments Requirements' appendices.

### [[Secondary Market]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing what a refinance company may lawfully do with real estate finance contractual rights, note that 'Secondary Market' is a defined term (trading of rights arising from primary-market contracts) that the Rules make the object of specific pre-conditions. The Rules require a SAMA no-objection letter and a submitted comprehensive plan before trading in the secondary market, and reserve SAMA's power to restrict or prevent such trading. Conclude that secondary-market activity is not freely exercisable: verify the no-objection and reporting steps in the trading article rather than relying on the company's general Article 4 powers.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Art 1):** "Secondary Market: Trading of the right of real estate finance entities resulting from primary market contracts."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 21 / Art (trading)):** "The Company shall obtain a no-objection letter from SAMA before trading the programs and products relating to real estate finance contractual rights in the secondary market."
