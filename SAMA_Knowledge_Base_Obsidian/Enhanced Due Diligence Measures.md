# Enhanced Due Diligence Measures

## Connections

### [[Due Diligence Measures]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a customer or relationship is classified high-risk, standard due diligence is insufficient and the institution must escalate to enhanced measures: EDD is the mandatory intensification of the baseline CDD, not a separate regime. The Guide frames customer classification by risk as 'a key element in the financial institution's risk-based approach' and requires 'additional steps to collect information about high-risk customers.' A reviewer should therefore confirm that any customer rated high-risk (including via the BO-uncertainty fallback in 3.18) triggers documented EDD and closer transaction monitoring, and should not accept baseline CDD for high-risk files.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 36 / Section 4: Enhanced Due Diligence Measures):** "take additional steps to collect information about high-risk customers and business relationships in order to understand and assess risks"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 36 / Section 3 Due Diligence):** "application of intensive control measures towards them in accordance with the ML/TF risk assessment results"

### [[Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping when EDD is mandatory rather than discretionary, treat PEP status as an automatic trigger that sits inside the broader EDD regime, not as a separate obligation. The Guide places PEPs under Section 4 (Enhanced Due Diligence Measures), and grounds it in Article (8) of the AML Law and its Implementing Regulations, which require the institution to identify PEPs and 'implement enhanced due diligence measures regarding them.' Practically, you conclude that identifying a customer or beneficial owner as a PEP in a high-risk relationship compels the full EDD package (including documentation per Paragraph 6.1), extended to family members and close associates — you cannot apply ordinary CDD to a PEP relationship.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 39 / Section 4):** "In cases of high-risk business relationships with PEPs, the financial institution shall apply enhanced due diligence measures and apply the same to all types of PEPs, their family members, and persons close to those PEPs."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 39 / Section 4.B):** "Article (8) of the Anti-Money Laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations require the financial institution to develop internal procedures... to identify PEPs and implement enhanced due diligence measures regarding them."
