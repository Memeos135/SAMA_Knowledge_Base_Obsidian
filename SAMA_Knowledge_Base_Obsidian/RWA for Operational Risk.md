# RWA for Operational Risk

## Connections

### [[Risk-Weighted Assets]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining a bank's total Pillar 1 capital requirement under SAMA's capital framework, treat operational risk RWA as a distinct, additive component of the overall RWA aggregate — not a subset of market or credit risk. The framework computes total RWA by summing separately-calculated credit, market and operational risk charges (each converted to RWA, e.g. capital requirement × 12.5), so a change in the operational risk methodology affects the denominator of the capital ratio independently. For a compliance decision, verify that operational risk RWA is calculated per its own chapter and added, and do not net or offset it against the other risk classes.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 751):** "Minimum capital requirement T: Pillar 1 capital requirements at the reporting date. This will normally be RWA * 8%"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 490 / 14.1):** "The risk-weighted assets for market risk under the simplified standardised approach are determined by multiplying the capital requirements... by 12.5."

### [[SOPE - Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When calculating the operational-risk RWA component, the SOPE standard is the governing methodology and it feeds the total Pillar 1 RWA aggregate as a separate additive charge. Because total capital requirement is normally RWA × 8% and RWA is the sum of the credit, market and operational risk components, the SOPE-derived operational figure directly moves the capital ratio. For a compliance decision, confirm operational-risk RWA is derived per SOPE and correctly added into the aggregate, rather than embedded in or offset against the other risk classes.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 732 / 5.5):** "RWA for the risk posed by unsettled transactions and failed trades... within scope of the rules set out in chapter 25 of SAMA Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 751):** "Minimum capital requirement T: Pillar 1 capital requirements at the reporting date. This will normally be RWA * 8%"
