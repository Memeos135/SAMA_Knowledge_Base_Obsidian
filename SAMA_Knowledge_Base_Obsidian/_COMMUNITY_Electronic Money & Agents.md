# Electronic Money & Agents

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 3 nodes

## Why this community

E-money issuance under the payments/PSP regime: the core product (electronic money), its distribution channel (agents/distributors), and the prudential safeguarding of customer funds. Relevant to PSP licensing and conduct compliance.

## How members connect

- 'Electronic Money' is the defined instrument; the other members scope how it may be distributed and how underlying funds must be protected.
- 'Agents and E-Money Distributors' governs outsourced/intermediated distribution — the issuer remains accountable for agent conduct and typically retains AML/KYC responsibility.
- 'Protection of Safeguarded Funds' imposes a segregation/safeguarding obligation over customer float, functioning as a prudential constraint tied to the e-money product.
