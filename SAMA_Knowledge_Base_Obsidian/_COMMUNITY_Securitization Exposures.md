# Securitization Exposures

**Cohesion:** 0.09 - loosely connected
**Members:** 31 nodes

## Why this community

Securitization capital regime: classification of securitization structures, the hierarchy of capital approaches, operational requirements for risk transference, and criteria (including STC) determining preferential treatment. Governs how banks assign capital to securitization exposures.

## How members connect

- Securitization General Provisions is the definitional anchor referenced by the structure types (Traditional, Synthetic, Resecuritization) that determine which regime applies.
- Hierarchy of Securitization Approaches orders the calculation methods (SEC-IRBA, SEC-ERBA, SEC-IAA, SEC-SA), with SEC-ERBA/SEC-IAA depending on ECAI ratings and KIRB/KSA charges and parameters (attachment/detachment points, supervisory p) feeding the formulas.
- Operational Requirements for Risk Transference reference the SPE, clean-up calls and early-amortization provisions — the conditions a bank must meet to derecognise securitized exposures for capital purposes.
- STC Securitization Criteria scope preferential treatment for STC-compliant/traditional/ABCP structures; caps, CRM and NPL/purchase-price-discount rules bound the resulting capital charge.
