- **What this link tells you:** When allocating accountability for Shariah compliance in a finance company, note the Instructions place primary governance duties on the Board of Directors, not solely on the Shariah Committee. Chapter Two is dedicated to the responsibilities of the board and senior management, and Article One expects the board to have a reasonable understanding of Shariah principles and their application in Islamic finance.
