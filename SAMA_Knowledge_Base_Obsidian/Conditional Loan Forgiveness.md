# Conditional Loan Forgiveness

## Connections

### [[Long-termPermanent Restructuring|Long-term/Permanent Restructuring]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating a debt-forgiveness proposal, recognise that conditional loan forgiveness is a specific long-term/permanent restructuring instrument, not a discretionary write-off. The Guidelines define it as the bank forfeiting its legal right to recover part or all of a loan 'upon the borrower's performance of certain conditions,' and place it within the long-term/permanent measures taxonomy. You should conclude that forgiveness is only defensible where conditions and reduced-payment settlement terms are documented, and that it is typically combined with other measures (e.g. loan-to-asset swap) rather than granted standalone.
