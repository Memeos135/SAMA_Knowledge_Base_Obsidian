# SAMA Authorised Trade Repository (TR)

## Connections

### [[Section A Trade Reporting Requirements|Section A: Trade Reporting Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** To identify who the reporting obligation runs to, this link fixes the SAMA authorised Trade Repository (TR) as the mandatory, exclusive recipient of Section A reports. Section A requires all KSA banks with OTC derivative transactions to report directly to the SAMA authorised TR Operator, expressly barring reporting through agents or outsourcing to third parties, and to enter into a reporting service agreement including a counterparty-consent clause. Conclude that the reporting duty cannot be delegated and that onboarding (service agreement, systems linkage, user testing to the TR's satisfaction) is a precondition to discharging it.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / 3.1.2):** "required to be reported to the SAMA authorised Trade Repository Operator."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / 3.1.3):** "All reporting banks are required to directly report to the SAMA authorised TR Operator. Banks are not allowed to report through agents or outsource their reporting requirements to third party service providers."
