# Finance Lease Regulation

**Cohesion:** 0.50 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

Finance leasing regime: the primary Finance Lease Law and its implementing regulation, operationalized through controls on contract registration and executive deed extracts. Covers enforceability and formalities of finance lease agreements in KSA.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: Finance Lease Law sits above its Implementing Regulation, which in turn grounds the Contract Registration Controls (subordinate operational rules).
- Executive Deed Extract Controls reference the Law directly — extracts derive their enforceability (as executory instruments) from the statutory basis.
- Registration and extract controls are conceptually paired: registration is the formality that gives lease contracts and their deed extracts legal effect against third parties.
