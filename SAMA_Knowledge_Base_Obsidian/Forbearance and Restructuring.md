# Forbearance and Restructuring

## Connections

### [[Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a finance company modifies a troubled borrower's terms, use the Rules' forbearance concept to determine that the exposure cannot simply stay or return to performing status. The Rules define forbearance as a concession granted because of borrower financial difficulty, require such exposures to be reported only in Stage 2B, 3A or 3B, and set strict cure/probation periods and exit criteria before re-categorisation as performing. You should conclude that restructuring triggers mandatory downgrading and extended cure periods (including that partial write-offs or collateral repossession do not restore performing status), and check these criteria before treating any restructured exposure as recovered.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 17):** "For regulatory classification purposes, these [forborne] exposures should only be reported in Stage 2B, 3A or 3B."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12):** "minimum eligibility requirement for re-staging stage 2 exposures into stage 1 exposures, which should at least include the following conditions."

### [[Stage 123 Credit Risk Classification|Stage 1/2/3 Credit Risk Classification]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing restructured or forborne exposures, do not assume they can occupy any stage — the Rules restrict them to specific classifications, stating forborne exposures 'should only be reported in Stage 2B, 3A or 3B.' Forbearance identification (financial difficulty plus a concession) thus dictates a mandatory floor on staging and interacts with the re-staging cure periods, and certain events (partial write-off, unrealized collateral repossession, further forbearance) 'will not lead to the re-categorization of a forborne exposure as performing.' Compliance consequence: a forborne exposure sitting in Stage 1 or bare Stage 2A is a red flag, and exit to a performing stage requires satisfying the stated probation/cure conditions, so verify both the forbearance trigger and the cure evidence before accepting any upgrade.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 17 / Section 7.1.1):** "For regulatory classification purposes, these exposures should only be reported in Stage 2B, 3A or 3B."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 (re-staging conditions)):** "minimum eligibility requirement for re-staging stage 2 exposures into stage 1 exposures."
