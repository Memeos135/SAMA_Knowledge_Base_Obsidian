# Rules Governing Calculation of APR

## Connections

### [[APR Calculation Method (Net Present Value)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When checking whether an APR computation is defensible, read this method as the prescribed calculation basis defined by the Rules, not an optional approach. Article 6 mandates the net present value method with the stated formula, and Article 1's definition of APR as the discount rate equating present value of borrower payments to present value of finance advanced fixes what the method must produce; Articles 9-10 then constrain inputs (365-day basis, fixed-rate assumption for floating cost, credit-card assumptions). You should conclude that the acceptable APR is uniquely determined by this NPV method plus the defined 'Total Cost of Finance' inputs, so deviations in methodology or excluded/included costs are compliance failures regardless of the headline rate quoted.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / Art 6):** "The APR should be calculated based on the net present value method using the following formula"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / Art 1):** "APR: The discount rate at which the present value of payments ... equals the present value of all payments of the amount of financing available to the borrower"

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping APR-disclosure obligations, do not read the APR Rules as self-contained: Article 2(2) directs that they be read in conjunction with the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation (among other instruments). This makes the Control Law part of the enforceable backdrop for finance companies — the Rules operationalize APR standardization while the Control Law supplies licensing/supervisory authority over those providers. Practically, confirm a provider's Control-Law licensing status and any Implementing-Regulation obligations before concluding the APR Rules alone govern a given retail-lending offer.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 13 / Art 2(2)):** "The Rules shall be read in conjunction with the related Laws and Regulations, including but not limited to the following: Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / Art 1):** "Finance Providers: Banks, and Finance Companies Licensed to engage in retail lending."

### [[Regulations for Consumer Financing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping APR obligations for retail lending, treat the Regulations for Consumer Financing as a companion regime that the APR Rules must be applied alongside, per Article 2's 'read in conjunction with' instruction. The APR Rules standardize the calculation method, but the Regulations for Consumer Financing govern the broader consumer-financing relationship into which APR disclosure fits, so the two operate cumulatively rather than one overriding the other. You should conclude that compliance requires satisfying both instruments and should check the Consumer Financing Regulations for any product-specific disclosure or conduct requirements not spelled out in the APR Rules.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 13 / Art 2):** "The Rules shall be read in conjunction with the related Laws and Regulations, including but not limited to the following: ... The Regulations for Consumer Financing."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13 / Art 2):** "The Regulations for Consumer Financing."

### [[Regulations for Consumer Financing]] — `semantically_similar_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** These two instruments appear to overlap: the APR Rules expressly list the Regulations for Consumer Financing among the related regulations to be read alongside them, and both define APR by the same net-present-value discount-rate concept. Note the scope difference — the Consumer Financing Regulations here are stated to apply to banks licensed by SAMA and to natural-person consumer borrowers, while the APR Rules reach all finance providers in retail lending. Before relying on one as a substitute for the other, verify which regime governs your product type and borrower (e.g., consumer vs. lease/real-estate/margin, which the Consumer Financing Regulations exclude).
- **Grounding — this node (Page 13 / Art 2(2)):** "The Regulations for Consumer Financing. ... The Rules shall be read in conjunction with the related Laws and Regulations."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Art 2):** "These Regulations apply to all kinds of Consumer Financing. ... do not apply to lease or real estate Financing or margin lending."
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED/semantic; the documents overlap but differ in scope — confirm which regime applies to the specific borrower and product before treating them as interchangeable.

### [[SAMA APR Calculator (Excel)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how a finance provider must actually compute APR for compliance, treat the SAMA-issued Excel calculator as a mandatory instrument, not a convenience tool. Article 4 requires providers to 'utilize the Excel based calculator issued by SAMA,' and Article 5 makes any automated tool subordinate to it by requiring providers to verify automated results against the Excel output and to keep the customer-facing website calculator aligned with the enclosed one. You should conclude that APR figures used in advertising, offers, contracts and statements are only compliant if reconcilable to SAMA's calculator, so the calculator is the benchmark of correctness for any dispute or examination.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / Art 4):** "Finance providers shall utilize the Excel based calculator issued by SAMA for the purpose of implementing the Rules."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 14 / Art 5):** "verify the results obtained using the automated tool by comparing those results to the figures obtained by using Excel based APR Calculator provided by SAMA."

### [[SAMA Circular No. 381000095091]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When interpreting APR obligations under the Regulation for Consumer Financing, do not read that Regulation in isolation — Article 2 of the APR Rules directs that they be read together with Circular No. 381000095091, which clarifies Articles (10) and (21) and the APR calculation mechanism in that Regulation. This makes the circular an interpretive gloss forming part of the applicable body of rules on how APR is derived and disclosed under consumer financing. You should conclude that resolving an APR-disclosure question requires consulting the circular's clarification alongside the Regulation and the APR Rules, and should not rely on the Regulation's articles alone where the circular modifies their reading.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 13 / Art 2):** "The Rules shall be read in conjunction with the related Laws and Regulations, including but not limited to the following"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13 / Art 2):** "SAMA's Circular No. 381000095091 issued on 10/9/1438H to clarify Article (10) and Article (21) and the APR Calculation Mechanism in the Regulation for Consumer Financing."
