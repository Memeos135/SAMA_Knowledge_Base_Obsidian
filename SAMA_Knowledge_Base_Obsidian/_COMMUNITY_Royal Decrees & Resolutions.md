# Royal Decrees & Resolutions

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Top-of-hierarchy instruments of Saudi law — a Royal Decree and a Council of Ministers Resolution — that provide the legislative foundation from which SAMA regulations and circulars derive authority.

## How members connect

- The Royal Decree and the Council of Ministers Resolution are linked by cross-reference, reflecting the standard KSA enactment chain (Resolution approves/enacts, Decree promulgates).
- These are primary legal sources: subordinate SAMA regulations, rules, and circulars must be traced back to instruments at this level for their legal validity.
- For legal decisions, treat these as the controlling authority — any lower-tier guidance cannot override or exceed what these instruments authorize.
