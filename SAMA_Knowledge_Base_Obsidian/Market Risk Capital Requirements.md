# Market Risk Capital Requirements

## Connections

### [[Ijarah]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping capital treatment for Ijarah (operating lease and Ijarah Muntahia Bi Al Tamlik), check the market-risk column stage-by-stage, because the framework attaches market-risk capital primarily where the leased asset sits on the bank's books without a binding lease and where the asset is returned at maturity. Tables 5J and 5K show market-risk requirements apply pre-lease and on return, while credit-risk applies once a binding lease/rental is due. Note the carve-out: Table 5K expressly excludes real estate finance exposures, which stay under the existing SAMA Basel methodology — so verify the asset type before selecting the treatment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10 / Table 5J):** "Asset available for lease (prior to signing a lease contract) without binding lease agreement ... Refer to Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11 / Table 5K & Page 6 (definition)):** "(1) Table 5K does not apply to real estate finance exposures. All ijarah structured real estate finance exposures shall continue using the methodology ... in the existing SAMA Basel framework"

### [[Salam]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining capital adequacy for a Shari'ah-compliant Salam exposure, you cannot treat market risk in isolation — the SAMA framework assigns market-risk capital by contract stage. For a Salam contract (with or without Parallel Salam), the tables direct that at the stage of price payment by the bank, both credit-risk and market-risk minimum capital requirements apply, while receipt of the commodity carries N/A. Conclude that the applicable market-risk charge for Salam depends on the specific stage in Tables 5D/5E, so map each contract phase to the correct treatment rather than applying a single blanket weight.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9 / Table 5D-5E):** "Payment of purchase price by the bank to a salam customer/seller ... Refer to Minimum Capital Requirements for Market Risk"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 (definition) / Page 8 Table 5D):** "Salam: The sale of a specified commodity ... for a known price paid at the time of signing the contract for its delivery in the future"
