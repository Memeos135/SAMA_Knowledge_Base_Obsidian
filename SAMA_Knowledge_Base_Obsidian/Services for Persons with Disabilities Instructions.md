# Services for Persons with Disabilities Instructions

## Connections

### [[Consumer Protection Principles]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** In scoping fair-treatment/non-discrimination duties, read 4926 as a concrete expression of the consumer-protection principles: it requires financial institutions to remove physical and behavioral barriers and ensure persons with disabilities can access financial services on an equal footing and protects their rights 'as consumers' of financial services — the same equality/fairness principle underlying the consumer-protection regime cited in 5404. 4926 applies to all SAMA-supervised financial institutions (banks, insurance, finance companies, credit-information, exchange, payment companies) and mandated a compliance plan within a fixed window. For a decision, this means accessibility obligations are enforceable consumer-protection requirements, not voluntary CSR; confirm 4926's defined 'financial institution' scope covers your entity and note its transitional deadlines.
