# Responsible Retail Financing Principles

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining which entities the Responsible Retail Financing Principles bind and under what authority, note the Principles expressly direct that they be read together with the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51) and its Implementing Regulation. That statute defines 'finance company' and the licensed financing activities, so it sets the perimeter of the 'Financier' (الممول) to whom these lending-conduct duties apply. For a compliance decision you would confirm an entity's status under M/51 to know whether these retail-financing obligations attach, and read any Principle provision consistently with the parent law and its regulation.

### [[Financial Institution Customer Protection]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a finance company's or bank's obligations for retail lending, do not treat responsible-financing conduct and advertising/consumer-protection controls as one regime — but recognize they share the same statutory anchor (SAMA's Article 4 powers to protect financial-institution customers) and overlap in scope. Both instruments target protection of the natural-person 'customer/consumer' and both draw on the Banking Control Law and Finance Companies Control Law hierarchy. For a compliance decision you would check the Responsible Retail Financing Principles for affordability/lending conduct duties and the advertising controls for how those products may be marketed, applying both cumulatively rather than assuming one displaces the other.

### [[Real Estate Finance Law]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying the Responsible Retail Financing Principles to a housing loan, note the Principles list the Real Estate Finance Law and its Implementing Regulation among the instruments that must be read alongside them. This matters because real-estate financing carries its own regulatory constraints — such as the loan-to-value ceiling set in Article 12 of the Implementing Regulation (as amended by SAMA circulars) — that operate in addition to the general affordability/responsible-lending duties. For a compliance decision you would apply both the sector-specific LTV limits and the responsible-financing conduct requirements, since neither overrides the other.
