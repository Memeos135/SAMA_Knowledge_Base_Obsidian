# Development Process of Shariah-Compliant Products

## Connections

### [[Shariah Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a new Islamic banking product may lawfully be launched, treat prior Shariah Committee approval as a hard gating requirement, not an advisory step. Article 18 requires that all Shariah issues in product development be referred to the committee and that committee approval be obtained 'for all Islamic banking products' and any subsequent amendments, with the committee reviewing the legal contracts and documents. You should conclude that a product offered without documented committee approval (pre- and post-approval processes) is exposed to Shariah non-compliance risk and potential rejection, and verify committee sign-off before market issuance or marketing.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16 / Art 18):** "The committee approval shall be obtained for all Islamic banking products to be presented and for any subsequent amendments."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11 / Art 10):** "It shall issue decisions on Shariah matters so that the bank can comply with the Shariah principles and rules."

### [[Shariah Governance Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether an Islamic product can lawfully be offered, treat committee approval as a gating requirement under the SGF, not a formality. Article 18 requires that all Shariah issues in product development, design and process be referred to the committee, that committee approval be obtained for every product and subsequent amendment, and that both pre- and post-approval stages be covered. For a compliance decision this means a product offered or materially amended without documented committee approval is non-compliant, and the review must extend to legal contracts and related transaction documents.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16 / Art 18(2)):** "The committee approval shall be obtained for all Islamic banking products to be presented and for any subsequent amendments."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 14 / Art 16 First(2)):** "overall Islamic banking operations at the bank, including the development process of Shariah-compliant products... (See Chapter Nine of this framework)."
