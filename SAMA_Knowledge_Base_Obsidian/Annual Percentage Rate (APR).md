- **What this link tells you:** When checking cost-of-financing disclosure in this standard contract, treat the APR field as a mandatory statutory disclosure rather than a formatting convenience — the requirement to state the Annual Percentage Rate flows from the finance-company regulatory regime under the Finance Companies Control Law and its implementing consumer-protection rules that SAMA enforces on real-estate financiers. The template's summary and payment schedule both require APR alongside term cost and total amount due, so an omitted or misstated APR is a compliance breach, not a drafting gap. Conclude that APR completeness and calculation basis should be tested against the Law's implementing regulations, not just the contract template.
