## Why this community

Labor localization (Saudization) and dual-employment restrictions applied to money exchange centers, a governance/licensing-conduct requirement for the FX/remittance sector supervised by SAMA.

## How members connect

- Single-member community: the instrument imposes staffing obligations (Saudization targets and prohibition on dual employment) on licensed exchange centers.
- Legal consequence: non-compliance is a supervisory/licensing conduct breach, distinct from AML/CTF obligations that also bind exchange centers.
