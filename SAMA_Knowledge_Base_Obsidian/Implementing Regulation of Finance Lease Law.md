# Implementing Regulation of Finance Lease Law

## Connections

### [[Decision on Powers of Head and Registrars of Contract Registry]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the direct legal trigger for this Decision, focus on the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law: the Decision is issued 'in application of' Article 15(4) of that Regulation, which mandates that SAMA issue a decision defining the Registrar's powers. The Decision also cross-refers to Article 16 of the same Regulation for the appointment/non-objection procedure. You should treat the Regulation as the operative source: any gap in the Decision (e.g. registration formalities, appointment conditions) is governed by the Regulation articles it invokes, and the Decision must be read consistently with them.

### [[Finance Lease Law (M48)|Finance Lease Law (M/48)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the legal authority for finance-lease supervisory decisions, treat the Finance Lease Law (Royal Decree M/48) as the parent instrument and its Implementing Regulation as the subordinate rulebook that operationalizes it — SAMA's power to fix the competencies of the Head of the Contract Registry is drawn from Article 15(4) of the Implementing Regulation, which itself derives from the Law. For a compliance decision this means the registry-official duties (contract registration, record-keeping for at least ten years, verification of contracting parties' identity and capacity) are enforceable requirements traceable up the law→regulation→resolution chain, not standalone administrative preferences. Verify any specific competency against the Implementing Regulation article cited rather than the Law alone.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2):** "وإعمالاً للفقرة (4) من المادة الخامسة عشر من اللائحة التنفيذية لنظام الإيجار التمويلي التي نصت على "تصدر المؤسسة قراراً بتحديد اختصاصات رئيس ... سجل العقود""
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2):** "نظام الإيجار التمويلي الصادر بالمرسوم الملكي رقم (م/48) ... ولائحته التنفيذية"

### [[Guideline for Calculating Early Repayment Amount]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When calculating or reviewing an early-repayment settlement on a finance lease, use the Early Repayment Guideline as the method-defining instrument, but note it is explicitly anchored in binding regulation — it derives from Articles 9 and 11 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law (alongside the Finance Companies Control Law regulation and consumer-finance rules). This means the declining-balance calculation, the cap of three months' term cost, and the third-party-cost reimbursement conditions are enforceable rights of the beneficiary, not merely explanatory examples. A reviewer should treat the guideline's figures as illustrations of a legally mandated method and confirm the underlying articles when settling disputes.
