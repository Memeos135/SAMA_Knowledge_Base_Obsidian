# Circular on Finance Company Holidays

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining official closure days for a finance company and its branches, rely on this circular but note it is expressly grounded in Article 21 of the Finance Companies Control Law and Article 2 of its Implementing Regulation, which is what gives SAMA the authority to set holiday dates. The circular is the operative instrument (four days for Eid al-Fitr, four for Eid al-Adha, plus National Day rules), while the Law/Regulation supply the enabling power. Consequence: treat the circular's dates as binding on all operating finance companies, and expect SAMA to issue further circulars if the underlying dates change.
