### [[Guidelines to Apply for Payment Service Providers License]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping the payments regime, treat these two as different functions of the same SAMA framework rather than interchangeable: the Oversight Framework governs SAMA's supervision/oversight of payment *systems* and their operators (self-assessment, supervisory assessment, PFMI compliance), while the Licensing Guidelines govern how a firm becomes an authorised *payment service provider* (EMI/PI) under the Implementing Regulation of the Law of Payments. Both appear to sit under the same Law of Payments and Payment Services and its Implementing Regulation, but they address distinct obligation-holders and lifecycle stages. The link is INFERRED — verify the primary texts before treating oversight obligations (on system operators) as applying to a PSP licence applicant, since scope and defined roles ('member', 'client', 'operator') differ.
