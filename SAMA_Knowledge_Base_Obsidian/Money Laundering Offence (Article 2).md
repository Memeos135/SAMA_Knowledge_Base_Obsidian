# Money Laundering Offence (Article 2)

## Connections

### [[Anti-Money Laundering Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing criminal exposure, treat Article 2 as the definitional core of the ML offence — acquiring, possessing, using, concealing or disguising funds known to be proceeds of crime, plus attempt, participation, facilitation and cover-up. Article 3 extends criminal liability to legal persons without excluding directors, employees or authorised representatives who acted for the entity, and Article 4 makes ML a standalone offence not dependent on a predicate conviction, with intent inferable from objective circumstances. Conclude that liability can attach to the institution and named individuals simultaneously, and that absence of a predicate conviction is no defence.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Art 2):** "Acquires, possesses or uses funds that the person knows are proceeds of crime... Conceals or disguises the true nature, source, movement, ownership..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Art 3-4):** "A money laundering offence shall be deemed a separate offence from the predicate offense, and a conviction for the predicate offense shall not be necessary"
