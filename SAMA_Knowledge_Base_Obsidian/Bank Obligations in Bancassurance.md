# Bank Obligations in Bancassurance

## Connections

### [[Bancassurance Activity]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reading the Bank's duties, do not read them in the abstract — they are triggered by and confined to the defined 'Bancassurance Activity.' The obligations (direct practice through the Bank, qualified unit director, IFCE-certified Authorised Employees, SAMA non-objection/notification, no dealings with non-Clients) all attach to the marketing-and-distribution activity as defined. Conclude that the scope of each Bank obligation is co-extensive with the defined activity: conduct falling outside the definition is not governed by these specific Bank duties, and the reader should confirm an activity meets the Art 1.5 definition before applying the obligation set.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3):** "Bancassurance Activities shall be practiced directly through the Bank."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Art 1.5):** "Bancassurance Activities: Marketing and distribution of insurance products by the Bank to its Clients"

### [[Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping who bears the operative duties under the Rules, treat the Bank as the primary licensed obligor for the customer-facing distribution activity. The Rules impose direct, enforceable requirements on the Bank: activities practiced 'directly through the Bank,' a Bancassurance unit under an experienced director 'after obtaining SAMA's non-objection,' Authorised Employees holding the IFCE certificate, obtaining the Company's approval and notifying SAMA to expand channels, and a prohibition on serving non-Clients. Conclude that a bank cannot treat bancassurance as an insurer-only responsibility; these are the Bank's own licensing/conduct obligations, several gated by SAMA non-objection or notification.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6):** "The Bank must not conduct Bancassurance Activities with other than his Clients."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6):** "The Bank shall ensure that all Authorised Employees have the Insurance Foundations Professional Exam certificate (IFCE)"
