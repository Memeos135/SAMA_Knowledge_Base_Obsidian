# Guidance Notes on SAMA Regulatory Sandbox Application

## Connections

### [[AML Rules and Instructions Reference]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are advising a Sandbox applicant, do not treat the Sandbox AC as the ceiling of obligations — the guidance itself flags that full-scope regulatory requirements are 'most likely greater' and cites the AML Rules and Instructions (plus the AML Law, the Combating Terrorism Financing Law, the AML/CTF Guide, and the AML Implementing Regulation) as applicable existing regulation. This is a signposting citation, not an incorporation of specific articles, so the AML/CTF regime applies according to its own scope and defined terms, not the Sandbox's simplified criteria. Conclude that a FinTech onboarding clients under a Sandbox LoA must independently satisfy the AML/CTF framework, and verify which of the listed AML instruments actually bind its business model before going live.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 13, Appendix 1):** "the regulatory requirements most likely will be greater than those requirements detailed in the Regulatory Sandbox Stage 2"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13, Appendix 1):** "Existing Regulations ... 4. AML Rules and Instructions ... Laws ... 3. Anti-Money Laundering"

### [[Fit and Proper Forms Reference]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For a Sandbox applicant, the guidance ties Stage 2 Operational Readiness to 'Fit and Proper forms and approvals for management' and separately cites 'Fit and Proper Forms' as an applicable guideline — meaning management fitness/propriety clearance is a pre-go-live gate, not an optional formality. The citation is a pointer to SAMA's fit-and-proper forms regime rather than an internal standard, so the substantive criteria sit outside this document (in the fit-and-proper Requirements/forms). Conclude that the applicant must clear management fit-and-proper approvals before onboarding clients, and should use the current SAMA-issued forms since the AC is periodically updated.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 11):** "The AC requirements are a list of minimum compliance requirements that FinTechs must meet prior to being permitted to go live"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11):** "assessment and compliance requirements across: i. Fit and Proper forms and approvals for management"

### [[Regulatory Sandbox]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a FinTech on whether the Sandbox route is available, treat this Guidance Notes document as the operative description of the three-stage Regulatory Sandbox regime it defines — Application (60 business days), Operational Readiness (120 days), and Live Testing. The document is the source that scopes the concept, so the practical takeaway is that Sandbox entry confers only a temporary permission (LoA), not a licence, and firms whose business model is already regulated must apply directly for a licence rather than the Sandbox. Conclude that Sandbox status must never be represented to counterparties or customers as authorisation to operate until the Stage 3 No Objection Letter is issued.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3):** "SAMA has updated its Regulatory Sandbox operating model and processes to give greater guidance to applicants on the stages and requirements to move between the different Sandbox stages."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12):** "Regulations have been issued for your business model/concept and you should apply directly for a License not for Regulatory Sandbox permissions."
