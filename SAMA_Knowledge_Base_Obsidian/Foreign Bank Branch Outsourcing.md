# Foreign Bank Branch Outsourcing

## Connections

### [[Rules on Outsourcing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping outsourcing obligations for a foreign bank branch, treat this as a stricter sub-regime under the general Rules on Outsourcing, not a lighter one. Section vi imposes branch-specific requirements: KSA business must be booked locally, key management plus compliance and AML/CFT functions may NOT be outsourced at all, and outsourcing to the Head Office or a related party does not diminish the branch's obligations to comply with Saudi laws. You would conclude that even intra-group arrangements attract annual audit and immediate reporting to SAMA, and cannot be relied on to shift AML/CFT accountability off the KSA branch.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / Section vi Art 45):** "functions such as compliance and Anti-Money Laundering (AML)/Combatting the Financing of Terrorism (CFT) are not allowed to be outsourced... Outsourcing to Head Office or a related party does not diminish the obligations"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Governance F.11):** "The Board of Directors of the bank retains the ultimate responsibility for the outsourcing policy and all outsourcing arrangements, including compliance with all relevant legal and regulatory requirements."
