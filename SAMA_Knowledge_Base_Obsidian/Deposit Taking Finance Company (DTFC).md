# Deposit Taking Finance Company (DTFC)

## Connections

### [[Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) Regulations 2020]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether these Regulations apply to a given firm, the defined term 'Deposit Taking Finance Company' is the scoping gate: the rules apply only to finance companies authorized to mobilize savings and time deposits from non-individual customers and to lend out of those deposits, subject to the Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree M/51). The definition also frames the account architecture — General Accounts exist solely to facilitate Term Deposit Accounts, with strict linking, name-matching and permitted-credit-source rules. Conclude that a firm not authorised for deposit-taking falls outside this instrument (though still bound by general FC rules), and that the DTFC's deposit operations must respect the closed funding channels and remitter name-matching controls defined for the General Account.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / para 2; Page 55 / paras 195-197):** "finance companies that are authorized to mobilize savings and time deposits from non-individual customers... DTFC must match the name of remitter with the name on General Account"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / paras 1-2):** "These SAMA regulations are applicable to all Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) operating in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA)"

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping obligations for a deposit-taking finance company, treat the Finance Companies Control Law as the shared parent statute for both regimes: the DTFC regulations apply 'subject to the provisions of Finance Companies Control law' and require DTFCs to comply additionally with 'the Finance Companies Control Law, SAMA regulations for Finance Companies (FCs), and other relevant laws.' The ECL/provisioning Rules derive their authority from the same Law (Article 13 and Article 62 of its Implementing Regulation). Compliance consequence: a DTFC is not carved out of general FC obligations — it must satisfy DTFC-specific prudential rules on top of the base FC regime and the provisioning Rules, so scope any DTFC review as cumulative rather than substitutive.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_8383 Page 4):** "DTFCs are also required to comply with the Finance Companies Control Law, SAMA regulations for Finance Companies (FCs), and other relevant laws and regulations."
- **Grounding — related node (SAMA_EN_8357 Page 4):** "SAMA issued these rules in exercise of the powers vested upon it under Finance Companies Control Law promulgated by the Royal Decree No. (M/S1)."
