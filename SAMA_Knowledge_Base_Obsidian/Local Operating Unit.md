# Local Operating Unit

## Connections

### [[GLEIF]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing who may lawfully issue an LEI for a KSA financial institution's business customers, treat GLEIF accreditation as the source of a Local Operating Unit's authority. The circular records that SIMAH acts as the accredited Local Operating Unit approved by GLEIF (the Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation) to register and renew LEIs, operating under the ROC charter and the GLEIF master agreement, with SAMA authorising financial institutions to sign SIMAH's agreement. Conclude that only a GLEIF-accredited LOU (here, SIMAH) can validly deliver LEI issuance/renewal, so verify LOU accreditation status before relying on any issued identifier for customer-identification purposes.

### [[SIMAH (Saudi Credit Bureau)]] — `implements` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the entity that discharges the Local Operating Unit function in the Kingdom, this document establishes that SIMAH is the accredited Local Operating Unit — SAMA designates SIMAH as the approved local LEI operator, and the annexed agreement recites that SIMAH is GLEIF-accredited to register and renew LEIs under the ROC charter and the GLEIF master agreement. A compliance reader should treat 'Local Operating Unit' and SIMAH as the same practical role here, meaning LEI registration/renewal obligations run to SIMAH; confirm that no other accredited LOU is being used, since the circular routes institutions specifically to SIMAH.
