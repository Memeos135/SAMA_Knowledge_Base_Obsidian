# Supervisory LGD and EAD Estimates

## Connections

### [[Commercial and Residential Real Estate Collateral]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining capital treatment for a real-estate-secured corporate exposure, you must first decide whether the bank uses its own LGD/EAD estimates (advanced IRB) or falls back to supervisory estimates — because the eligibility and operational requirements for recognizing commercial/residential real estate as collateral (16.130) feed into loss-given-default calculations, and paragraph 16.62 states that banks not meeting the requirements for own-estimates of EAD or LGD must use the supervisory estimates set out in 16.127–16.144. This link confirms the collateral recognition rules and the LGD/EAD parameter rules sit within the same IRB risk-quantification chapter and are applied together. Conclude that recognizing real estate collateral does not by itself dictate the LGD input; you must verify whether the bank qualifies for own-estimates or is constrained to supervisory LGD/EAD, and apply the corresponding paragraph range.

### [[Minimum Requirements to Use IRB Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a bank fails to satisfy the own-estimate requirements for LGD or EAD, this link tells you the consequence is prescribed, not discretionary: the minimum requirements framework mandates fallback to supervisory estimates for corporate, sovereign and bank exposures. Paragraph 16.62 states banks that 'do not meet the requirements for own-estimates of EAD or LGD ... must use the supervisory estimates,' and the own-EAD standards at 16.88–16.89 define the threshold that must be cleared to avoid that fallback. Conclude that assessing IRB eligibility means testing each parameter against these standards, and that a shortfall does not disqualify the bank from IRB but forces the more conservative supervisory parameter treatment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 206 / 16.88):** "The additional minimum requirements for internal estimation of EAD under the advanced approach ... focus on the estimation of EAD for off-balance sheet items"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 197 / 16.62):** "banks that do not meet the requirements for own-estimates of EAD or LGD, above, must use the supervisory estimates of these parameters"

### [[Other Physical Collateral]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether non-financial physical collateral lowers LGD under the F-IRB/supervisory regime, treat the 'other physical collateral' recognition conditions as part of the supervisory LGD/EAD standards block (16.127–16.144) referenced by Section 7. SAMA discretion, liquid-market and first-lien conditions in 16.142–16.143 gate whether the collateral is even eligible, and only then does it feed the supervisory LGD outcome for banks not using own-estimates. You would therefore confirm SAMA's satisfaction on market liquidity and priority of claim before assuming any capital relief from such collateral.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 197 / 16.62):** "banks that do not meet the requirements for own-estimates of EAD or LGD... must use the supervisory estimates... Standards for use of such estimates are set out in paragraphs 16.127 to 16.144."

### [[Recognition of Financial Receivables Collateral]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether financial receivables can reduce a bank's LGD, note that the eligibility and legal-certainty requirements for receivables sit within the same paragraph block (16.127–16.144) that governs supervisory LGD/EAD estimates. Section 7 directs banks that do not qualify for own-estimates of LGD/EAD to use supervisory estimates whose standards are set out in those paragraphs, and the financial-receivables recognition rules (16.132 onward) are part of that regime. You should therefore treat receivables recognition as conditioning the supervisory-LGD outcome and verify the legal-certainty and risk-management conditions before crediting any collateral effect.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 197 / 16.62):** "banks that do not meet the requirements for own-estimates of EAD or LGD... must use the supervisory estimates of these parameters. Standards for use of such estimates are set out in paragraphs 16.127 to 16.144."
