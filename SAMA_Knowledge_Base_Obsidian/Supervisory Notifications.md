# Supervisory Notifications

## Connections

### [[Counter-Fraud Department]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assigning who discharges the SAMA notification duty, note that the Counter-Fraud Department's mandate to 'share Counter-Fraud Intelligence with SAMA' operationalises the separate Supervisory Notifications obligation, which requires immediate notification to the SAMA General Department of Cyber Risk Control of new typologies and significant fraud incidents. These are enforceable reporting requirements triggered even where no financial loss occurred. A compliance reviewer should confirm the Department's remit explicitly covers the immediacy and trigger events specified in section 3.7, and not assume routine intelligence-sharing satisfies the mandatory immediate-notification standard.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 22 / Sec 3.7):** "Member Organisations should notify the SAMA General Department of Cyber Risk Control immediately of ... Any new fraudulent typology whether it resulted in financial loss or not."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 20):** "Sharing Counter-Fraud Intelligence with SAMA and other organisations in the sector."

### [[Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining reporting obligations, note that Supervisory Notifications create a direct, time-bound duty running to SAMA: Member Organisations must 'immediately notify SAMA' of new fraud typologies and significant fraud incidents, reporting specifically to the SAMA General Department of Cyber Risk Control. This is an enforceable notification obligation, not a discretionary practice, triggered by defined events (new typology, significant external fraud, significant internal employee fraud or serious integrity misconduct). Conclude that a compliance assessment must identify the trigger events and confirm immediate escalation to the named SAMA department, and cannot treat internal case handling as sufficient.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 22 / 3.7 Supervisory Notifications):** "Member Organisations should immediately notify SAMA of new fraud typologies and significant fraud incidents ... notify the SAMA General Department of Cyber Risk Control immediately."
