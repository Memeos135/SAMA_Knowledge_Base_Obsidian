# Loan Restructuring Analysis

**Cohesion:** 0.10 - loosely connected
**Members:** 22 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA framework for managing problem loans: early identification, workout governance, viability analysis, and restructuring options for distressed exposures, anchored to credit-risk classification/provisioning rules under the Banking Control Law.

## How members connect

- An escalation chain: Early Warning Signals feed the Watch List, which routes exposures to the Workout Unit that builds the Workout Plan and monitors it via Restructuring KPIs and ongoing EWS.
- NPL Strategy drives Portfolio Segmentation and Initial Viability Assessment, which invoke coverage metrics (LLCR, DSCR) and NPV analysis to test financial/business viability.
- Restructuring/Workout Options cite the Banking Control Law and branch into short-term measures, long-term/permanent restructuring, and conditional loan forgiveness — the permitted regulatory outcomes.
- Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning Rules govern how restructured/distressed exposures must be classified and provisioned, tying the workout process back to prudential obligations.
