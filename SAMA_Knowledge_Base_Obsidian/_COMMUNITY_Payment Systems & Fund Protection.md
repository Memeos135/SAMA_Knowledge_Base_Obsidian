# Payment Systems & Fund Protection

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 3 nodes

## Why this community

Payments/PSP regime covering systemic payment-system integrity and the safeguarding of customer funds held by e-commerce payment support and PSP entities. Relevant to firms deciding how client money must be segregated and how operational reliability standards apply.

## How members connect

- Fund-safeguarding obligations attach to entities handling e-commerce payments — 'Protected Funds' must be segregated/protected rather than treated as the firm's own assets.
- Systemically important payment system standards set the reliability/risk baseline the payment-support activity operates within.
- Links are conceptual, not a formal hierarchy: read the safeguarding rule as the enforceable obligation and the systemic standards as the governing operational context.
