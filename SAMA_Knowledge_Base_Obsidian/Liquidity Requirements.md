# Liquidity Requirements

## Connections

### [[Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) Regulations 2020]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing prudential exposure for a Deposit Taking Finance Company, treat the Liquidity Requirements chapter as an operative, quantified obligation embedded in the parent DTFC Regulations 2020, not a soft standard. Chapter 5 imposes hard, enforceable minimums — a 20% liquid-asset holding against deposit liabilities, a statutory deposit with SAMA of not less than 4%, a 15x cap of deposit liabilities to total capital, and monthly Appendix B liquidity returns due by the 15th business day. You should conclude these ratios are binding licence conditions for any DTFC authorized to mobilize deposits, and check both the reporting deadline mechanics and the remedial one-month window for breaching the 15x cap.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10 / Chapter 5, paras 29-32):** "Every DTFC shall maintain a minimum holding of liquid assets of twenty per cent (20%) of all its deposit liabilities... a statutory deposits of a sum not less than 4%"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / para 4):** "observing liquidity ratios with regard to its liquid assets vis-a-vis total deposit liabilities and other prudential regulations as prescribed for DTFCs"
