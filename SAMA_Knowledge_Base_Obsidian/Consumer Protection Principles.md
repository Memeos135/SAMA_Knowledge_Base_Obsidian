- **What this link tells you:** When assessing advertising/marketing exposure, treat 4923 as an application of the broader consumer-protection principles rather than a stand-alone rule: 4923 reaffirms that promotional material must be clear and must not directly or indirectly mislead customers, which is the fair-dealing/transparency dimension the consumer-protection principles embody. ### [[Customer Signature on All Contract Pages]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a finance company's contract-execution duties, read the requirement to obtain the natural-person customer's signature on every contract page as a direct application of the Consumer Protection (finance-company customer protection) Principles, because the circular expressly grounds itself in those principles' emphasis on disclosure, transparency and customer education. The mandate is obligatory for natural persons ('shall obtain the customer's signature on all pages') and optional for legal persons, with residual risk on the company if it does not. You should therefore treat page-by-page signing as an enforceable consumer-protection obligation for individual borrowers and not assume the optional legal-person treatment relieves the company of the underlying transparency duty.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_5404 / Page 1):** "based on the finance-company customer protection principles concerning fair, transparent treatment without discrimination"

### [[Equal Treatment in Financing and Leasing Insurance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a finance company may differentiate its financing products or insurance coverage by customer age or category, treat this circular as the operative rule and the Consumer Protection Principles as its legal anchor. The circular grounds its equal-treatment mandate expressly on Principle (5/1) of the Banking Consumer Protection Principles and Principle (4/1) of the Finance Companies Consumer Protection Principles, meaning the prohibition on discrimination in leasing insurance is not a free-standing instruction but an enforcement of those principles. You should conclude that non-discriminatory, fair, transparent, reasonably-priced access is a binding obligation traceable to consumer-protection principles, and cite both the circular and the principle when evaluating age-based coverage refusals.

### [[Services for Persons with Disabilities Instructions]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** In scoping fair-treatment/non-discrimination duties, read 4926 as a concrete expression of the consumer-protection principles: it requires financial institutions to remove physical and behavioral barriers and ensure persons with disabilities can access financial services on an equal footing and protects their rights 'as consumers' of financial services — the same equality/fairness principle underlying the consumer-protection regime cited in 5404. 4926 applies to all SAMA-supervised financial institutions (banks, insurance, finance companies, credit-information, exchange, payment companies) and mandated a compliance plan within a fixed window. ### [[Whistle Blowing Policy for Financial Institutions]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** This link appears to associate Consumer Protection Principles with the Whistle Blowing Policy on a thematic 'integrity/fair treatment' basis, but the two operate on different subjects and should not be conflated when scoping duties. The Whistle Blowing Policy governs how financial institutions receive and process internal violation reports and protect reporters, whereas the consumer-protection principles concern fair, transparent, non-discriminatory treatment of customers. Note the two also address different beneficiaries — whistle-blowers are 'employees and stakeholders', not necessarily consumers — so treat this as a soft conceptual lead and rely on each instrument's own scope clause rather than assuming one imports the other.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_5404 / Page 1):** "obtaining financing services in a fair and transparent manner, without discrimination, based on the finance-company customer protection principles"
- **Grounding — related node (SAMA_EN_5096 / Page 4 (Scope)):** "This document applies to financial institution employees and stakeholders regarding all actions taken to address a report on a violation"
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED and 'conceptually_related_to'; the subjects differ (consumer fairness vs internal whistle-blowing), so verify each instrument's scope before treating them as linked obligations.
