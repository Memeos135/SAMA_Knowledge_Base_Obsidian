# BNPL Activity

## Connections

### [[Rules for Regulating BNPL Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping whether an entity needs a SAMA license, anchor on this defined term: the Rules define 'BNPL Activity' as 'a type of financing that allows a consumer to purchase goods or services without a term cost payable by the consumer,' and Article 3(2) prohibits carrying out that activity without a SAMA license under the Finance Companies Control Law. The document is the source that both defines the term and imposes the licensing obligation on it. For a compliance decision you would test a product against this definition — no term cost to the consumer, financing to purchase goods/services — to determine whether the BNPL licensing regime (and its capital, management, consumer-protection and financial-crime requirements) is triggered.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Art 1):** "BNPL Activity: A type of financing that allows a consumer to purchase goods or services without a term cost payable by the consumer."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Art 3):** "BNPL activity shall not be carried out except after obtaining a license from SAMA in accordance with the provisions of the Law"
