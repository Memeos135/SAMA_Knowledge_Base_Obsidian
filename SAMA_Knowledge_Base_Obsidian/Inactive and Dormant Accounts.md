# Inactive and Dormant Accounts

## Connections

### [[DTFC General Account Opening Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the controls attaching to a General Account over time, note that inactivity/dormancy is a separate status regime from freezing and applies even to compliant customers. Chapter 12 requires a DTFC to classify a General Account with no movement for one year as 'Inactive' (dual control to reactivate) and after two years as 'Dormant' (higher-authority dual control), expressly stating this 'has no relation with the rules of account freezing.' You should therefore treat dormancy and ID-expiry freezing as parallel, cumulative triggers, and check that reactivation controls exist independently of the opening/funding rules — and that a linked live Term Deposit Account pauses the inactivity clock.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 49 / Chapter 12, para 143):** "If a General Account completes an one year period with no movement whatsoever... DTFCs must consider such General Account as "Inactive" and should be subjected to dual control."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 55-56 (General Account funding/withdrawal rules)):** "Funds can only be withdrawn from General Account based on the instructions or agreement of the accountholder."
