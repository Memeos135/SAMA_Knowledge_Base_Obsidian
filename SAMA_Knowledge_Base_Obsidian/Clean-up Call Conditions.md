# Clean-up Call Conditions

## Connections

### [[Operational Requirements for Recognition of Risk Transference]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether an originating bank may exclude securitized exposures from RWA, do not treat the clean-up call conditions as a standalone test — they are one of the operational requirements for recognition of risk transference. Para 18.24(5) makes clean-up calls satisfying 18.28 a condition of the broader operational-requirements set that permits capital relief, and the transference framework also governs when significant credit risk must be transferred to third parties. Conclude that a defective clean-up call breaks the transference chain and, per 18.29–18.30, forces the bank to hold capital as if unsecuritized (or deduct implicit support), so the two provisions must be checked together, not in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 242 / Art 18.29):** "Securitization transactions that include a clean-up call that does not meet all of the criteria stated in 18.28 above result in a capital requirement for the originating bank."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 239 / Art 18.24(5), 18.25(4)):** "Clean-up calls must satisfy the conditions set out in 18.28... Banks must transfer significant credit risk associated with the underlying exposures to third parties."

### [[SAMA Circular No. 341000015689 (Regulatory Capital Under Basel III)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the capital consequences of a non-conforming clean-up call, note that 18.29 does not set the gain-on-sale rule itself but binds it to SAMA Circular No. 341000015689 (19 December 2012). The clean-up call provision directs that banks 'must not recognize in regulatory capital any gain on sale, in accordance with' that Circular, so the Circular is the governing authority for the gain-on-sale treatment. Conclude that any capital assessment of a defective clean-up call must consult the cited Circular for the precise gain-on-sale deduction, rather than deriving it from Chapter 18 alone.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 242 / Art 18.29):** "banks must not recognize in regulatory capital any gain on sale, in accordance with SAMA Circular No. 341000015689, Date: 19 December 2012."
- **Grounding — related node (markdown/SAMA_EN_3502_VER1.md (Circular ref)):** "SAMA Circular No. 341000015689 (Regulatory Capital Under Basel III)"
- **Caveat:** Node B's context does not reproduce the Circular's own gain-on-sale text; the link rests on the citation in 18.29. Verify the Circular's operative wording before relying on the specific treatment.
