# Securitization

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulation of Finance Lease Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a lessor's disclosure and registration duties around funding its lease book, treat 'Securitization' as a defined term within this Regulation rather than an ordinary financing concept: Article One defines it as 'Issuance of securities against Lessor's rights,' and Articles Seven and Twenty-Six attach concrete obligations to it. This tells you that securitizing lease receivables does not discharge the lessor — it must still disclose to SAMA any technical, commercial, legal or other risks relating to the contract, asset or securitization, and must record the CMA approval number in the Contracts Register. Conclude that CMA securitization approval and SAMA supervisory obligations run in parallel, and check that both the ongoing-disclosure duty and the register-recording step are met before relying on a securitization as clean.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Art 7; Page 9 / Art 26):** "the Lessor remains obliged to forthwith disclose to SAMA any technical, commercial, legal, or other risks in relation to ... the Securitization"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Art 1):** "Securitization: Issuance of securities against Lessor's rights."
