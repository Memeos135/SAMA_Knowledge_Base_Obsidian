# Template TLAC2 - Material Subgroup Creditor Ranking

## Connections

### [[Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what TLAC2 requires, read it as an application of the Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity regime rather than a standalone table: TLAC2 discloses creditor ranking at a material subgroup entity that has issued internal TLAC to a G-SIB resolution entity, and its eligibility rows (e.g. 'subset ... eligible as TLAC') turn on the TLAC definition and the FSB TLAC Term Sheet. It is mandatory only for G-SIBs and effective from the TLAC conformance date. A reviewer would therefore scope TLAC2 obligations to G-SIB material subgroup entities and rely on the TLAC Term Sheet definitions to test which liabilities qualify.
