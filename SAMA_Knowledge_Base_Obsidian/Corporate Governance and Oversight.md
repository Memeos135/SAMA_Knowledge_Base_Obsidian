# Corporate Governance and Oversight

## Connections

### [[Credit Risk Control Unit]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** These two nodes are both extracted from the same SAMA Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk framework (issued under Royal Decree No. M/36 and the Banking Control Law), so when scoping IRB eligibility you should treat governance/oversight and credit-risk-control functions as parts of a single mandatory rating-system regime rather than as separate concepts. The framework requires that rating and risk-estimation systems provide a meaningful, consistent assessment of borrower and transaction risk, which links board/senior oversight to the operation of the credit risk control unit. For a compliance decision, conclude that IRB approval depends on demonstrating both — do not treat the governance obligation as satisfied independently of the control-function requirements, and verify the specific minimum-requirement paragraphs in Chapter 16 before relying on this mapping.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / Para 1.2):** "issued by SAMA in exercise of the authority vested in SAMA under the Charter issued via Royal Decree No. M/36... and the Banking Control Law"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 178 / Para 16.4):** "rating and risk estimation systems and processes provide for a meaningful assessment of borrower and transaction characteristics"
- **Caveat:** Node labels (corporate governance, credit risk control unit) are broad concept tags; the cited excerpts ground the shared framework but not a specific article-to-article cross-reference — verify the exact governance/control paragraphs in Chapter 16.

### [[Minimum Requirements to Use IRB Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping what a bank must satisfy for IRB eligibility, do not treat 'Corporate Governance and Oversight' as separable from the minimum-requirements regime — it is Section 5 within the eleven-section chapter 16 that a bank must meet at outset and on an ongoing basis. This means board/senior-management oversight of the rating system is a supervisory precondition for using IRB, alongside rating system design, use test, risk quantification and validation. For an eligibility or breach assessment you should check governance and oversight as one of the mandatory sections, since failure there undermines IRB approval as a whole.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 (contents) / Section 5):** "Section 5: corporate governance and oversight"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 177 / para 16.1):** "the minimum requirements for entry and on-going use of the internal ratings-based (IRB) approach...(5) Corporate governance and oversight"
