# Eligible Collateral and Valuation

## Connections

### [[Expected Credit Loss Provisioning]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining provisioning levels for a finance company, do not treat eligible collateral as a standalone concept — it feeds directly into the ECL computation only through the Loss Given Default channel, not Exposure at Default. The Rules expressly state that eligible collateral in Annexure 1 'should be included in the Loss Given Default calculation,' while 'no consideration is given to collateral when determining the exposure at default.' The practical consequence: collateral recognition, its haircuts and valuation-frequency requirements (e.g. annual re-valuation, appraiser licensing under commercial pledge law) govern LGD inputs and therefore the size of the ECL provision — so a compliance reviewer should verify collateral eligibility and valuation currency when testing whether ECL provisions are adequate.

### [[Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When calculating provisions under these Rules, note that the eligible collateral list and valuation requirements in Annexure 1 feed directly into the Loss Given Default (LGD) measure and thus into provisioning outcomes — collateral is expressly excluded from Exposure at Default but included, after haircuts, in LGD. The Rules are issued under the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation (Art. 62 requiring IFRS-based provisioning), so only collateral meeting the Annexure 1 eligibility and valuation-frequency conditions (e.g. annual re-valuation for Stage 3 immovable collateral, appraisers meeting commercial pledge law) may reduce recognised losses. Conclude that collateral not satisfying these criteria cannot be relied on to lower provisions.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 24-25):** "The following list includes examples of eligible collateral ... used for calculation of provisions ... immovable collateral relating to Stage 3 or Default Exposures should be re-valued once every year."
