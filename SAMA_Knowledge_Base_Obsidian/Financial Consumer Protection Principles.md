# Financial Consumer Protection Principles

## Connections

### [[Customer Care Department (Bank)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining why a bank must establish a Customer Care Department, treat the department as the operational vehicle SAMA mandates to deliver the financial consumer-protection principles rather than a standalone administrative unit. The introduction grounds both in the SAMA Law (Royal Decree m/36, Art. 4 empowering SAMA to set instructions protecting financial institutions' customers) and the Banking Control Law, and expressly ties customer care, complaint handling and financial education to the High-Level Principles on Financial Consumer Protection. A compliance reader should conclude that the department's minimum requirements are enforceable expressions of the consumer-protection regime, so gaps in the department implicate the underlying principle obligations, not merely internal structure.
