# Preventive Measures (FIs, DNFBPs, NPOs)

## Connections

### [[Anti-Money Laundering Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the operative CTF duties that bind FIs, DNFBPs and NPOs, treat Chapter Six 'Preventive Measures' (Arts 63–71) as the enforceable obligation set, and note it explicitly ties back to the Anti-Money Laundering Law for the monitoring standard. The preventive-measures articles impose TF risk assessment, risk-based CDD/EDD, ten-year record-keeping, ongoing transaction scrutiny (per the AML Law), STR reporting and tipping-off prohibitions. For compliance decisions, conclude that these are the concrete 'who must do what' requirements, and that failure to meet them exposes the entity and named individuals to the supervisory sanctions in Article ~82ff (warnings, orders, fines up to SAR 5,000,000, bans).
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / Art 63):** "FIs, DNFBPs, and NPOs shall identify, assess, understand and document its financing of terrorism risks, taking into account a wide range of risk factors..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 15 / Art 69):** "...as prescribed by the relevant provisions stipulated in the Anti-Money Laundering Law..."
- **Caveat:** Both nodes derive from the same source file; the edge reflects the Preventive Measures chapter's internal cross-reference to the AML Law monitoring standard.

### [[General Directorate of Financial Intelligence]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining where and how FIs, DNFBPs and NPOs must report TF suspicions, connect the Preventive Measures duty (Art 70) to the General Directorate of Financial Intelligence as the mandated recipient and analytical body (Arts 76–77). Article 70 requires prompt, direct STR filing to the Directorate and response to its further information requests; Article 77 lets the Directorate demand additional information but, where no report was filed or the request is unrelated, requires it to route through the supervisory authority. For a compliance decision, conclude that STRs go directly to the Directorate, but the tipping-off prohibition (Art 71) and the confidentiality regime constrain what may be disclosed to the customer about that reporting.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / Art 70):** "Promptly and directly, Report such transaction to the General Directorate of Financial Intelligence; and provide a detailed report including all available data..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 16 / Art 76):** "The Directorate – as a national central agency... shall undertake receiving suspicious transaction reports... to analyze such reports and information, and to disseminate the results..."

### [[Supervisory Authority Powers]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing enforcement exposure for the preventive obligations (risk assessment, CDD/EDD, ten-year record keeping, transaction monitoring, STR reporting) under the CTF Law, read them together with the supervision chapter because that chapter is the enforcement backbone for those same duties. Chapter 9 grants supervisory authorities powers to inspect, compel information, and impose sanctions — written warnings, fines up to SAR 5,000,000 per violation, employment bans, management removal, and licence suspension/withdrawal — for failure to comply with 'any provision of this Law, its Implementing Regulation or relevant decisions or circulars.' Conclude that non-compliance with any Chapter Six preventive measure is directly actionable, and that liability reaches directors, board members and executive/supervisory management personally, not just the institution.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / Art 63-65):** "FIs, DNFBPs, and NPOs shall identify, assess, understand and document its financing of terrorism risks... keep all records and documents for a period of no less than ten years"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 18 / Art 82-83):** "failed to comply with any provision of this Law... may impose one or more of the following measures... Impose a monetary fine of up to 5.000.000 riyals per violation"
