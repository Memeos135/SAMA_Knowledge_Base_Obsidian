# IT Governance Framework

**Cohesion:** 0.28 - loosely connected
**Members:** 9 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA's IT Governance Framework and its component domains — the supervisory expectations for how regulated entities govern, risk-manage, and operate technology. Sits alongside the Cyber Security and Business Continuity frameworks as part of the operational/technology governance regime.

## How members connect

- The IT Governance Framework is the parent instrument that references each domain (Leadership, Risk Management, Operations, Change Management, Maturity Model), setting the scope of assessed governance areas.
- It cross-references the SAMA Cyber Security Framework and Business Continuity Management Framework, signalling these are interdependent obligations rather than isolated regimes.
- Governance obligations cascade to specific structures — IT Governance and Leadership references the mandated IT Steering Committee (ITSC) — and operational domains link back to continuity and cyber controls (Operations→BCM, Change Management→Cyber).
