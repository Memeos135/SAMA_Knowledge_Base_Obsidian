# Supervisory Authority Powers

## Connections

### [[Preventive Measures (FIs, DNFBPs, NPOs)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing enforcement exposure for the preventive obligations (risk assessment, CDD/EDD, ten-year record keeping, transaction monitoring, STR reporting) under the CTF Law, read them together with the supervision chapter because that chapter is the enforcement backbone for those same duties. Chapter 9 grants supervisory authorities powers to inspect, compel information, and impose sanctions — written warnings, fines up to SAR 5,000,000 per violation, employment bans, management removal, and licence suspension/withdrawal — for failure to comply with 'any provision of this Law, its Implementing Regulation or relevant decisions or circulars.' Conclude that non-compliance with any Chapter Six preventive measure is directly actionable, and that liability reaches directors, board members and executive/supervisory management personally, not just the institution.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18 / Art 82-83):** "failed to comply with any provision of this Law... may impose one or more of the following measures... Impose a monetary fine of up to 5.000.000 riyals per violation"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 14 / Art 63-65):** "FIs, DNFBPs, and NPOs shall identify, assess, understand and document its financing of terrorism risks... keep all records and documents for a period of no less than ten years"
