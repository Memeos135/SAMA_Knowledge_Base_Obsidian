# Cyber Security Roles and Responsibilities

## Connections

### [[Cyber Security Leadership and Governance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping accountability under the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, treat Roles and Responsibilities (3.1.4) as a mandatory component of the broader Leadership and Governance domain, not a standalone item. The governance requirement that the cyber security policy define 'general and specific responsibilities for cyber security' is operationalised by 3.1.4, which fixes ultimate responsibility on the Board and cascades duties to senior management and the CISO. A compliance reviewer should conclude that gaps in defined roles are governance-domain deficiencies affecting the Member Organization's maturity rating, and should check that board-level accountability is documented, not just delegated.
