# Electronic Payment Readiness for Retail Sector

## Connections

### [[Electronic Payment Readiness for Personal Services]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping SAMA's mandatory electronic-payment acceptance obligations on banks and PSPs, treat these two circulars as parallel members of one obligation set rather than isolated instructions, because both impose the same operational duties (readiness to open accounts/e-wallets for merchants, install SAMA-authorized payment means, apply accurate Merchant Category Codes, and cascade the decision internally) — differing only in the covered sector: retail activities in one, personal-services activities in the other, each with its own effective date. Both derive from the same national campaign against commercial concealment coordinated with the Ministries of Municipal/Rural Affairs and Commerce. A reader should conclude that a PSP's compliance posture must satisfy both sector rollouts, and should check each circular for its distinct commencement date and its distinct MCC set before assuming the retail rules alone suffice.

### [[Vision 2030  FSDP|Vision 2030 / FSDP]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are tracing why the electronic-payment readiness mandate exists and how far it reaches, note 4929 expressly grounds itself in SAMA's payment-systems strategy and the Financial Sector Development Program's target to raise electronic payments to a set share by 2030 — the Vision 2030/FSDP policy layer. The circular then converts that policy into binding operational duties on banks and payment service providers (readiness to open merchant accounts/e-wallets and install SAMA-authorized payment means) tied to a whole-of-retail-sector mandate coordinated with other ministries and the anti-concealment program. For a decision, this shows the obligation's legal force flows from SAMA's supervisory powers, with Vision 2030/FSDP supplying policy rationale not standalone enforceable content; rely on 4929's specific PSP duties and effective date, not the policy target, for compliance.
