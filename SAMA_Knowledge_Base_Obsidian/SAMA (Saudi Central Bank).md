# SAMA (Saudi Central Bank)

## Connections

### [[Disclosure and Enforcement on Bank Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a bank on how to respond to a request to freeze, deduct from, or disclose a customer's accounts, treat SAMA as the exclusive and controlling channel — the Rules for Bank Accounts vest disclosure and enforcement authority in SAMA and require banks to act only in the form, manner and period SAMA specifies. Requests may be received 'only by SAMA except for cases stated in SAMA's instructions,' so the link tells you a bank cannot lawfully action a third party's freeze/disclosure demand outside that channel. Conclude that any enforcement instruction must be validated against SAMA's authority and confidentiality requirements before execution.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1):** "Rules for Bank Accounts ... Please refer to SAMA's website (www.sama.gov.sa) for the last updated and amended version of the Rules."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 22 / section 10.1):** "Such requests shall be received only by SAMA except for cases stated in SAMA's instructions. Banks shall carry out requests for disclosure and enforcement according to the form, manner and period specified by SAMA."

### [[Embassies, Consulates and Diplomats Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When onboarding embassies, consulates or diplomats, note that SAMA is not merely the rule-maker but a required recipient of notice — for temporary-visit diplomats the Rules require both the compliance-department manager's approval and that 'SAMA must be informed about opening the account.' The link therefore signals that this account category carries a specific SAMA-notification obligation on top of standard documentation. Conclude you should confirm the notification step and closure-on-expiry requirements are met, not just the identity documents.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1):** "Rules for Bank Accounts ... refer to SAMA's website for the last updated and amended version of the Rules."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 80 / Rule 300.1.6.3):** "approval of the manager of the bank's compliance department for opening the account shall be sought. SAMA must be informed about opening the account. Such accounts must be closed when the visit (the assignment) term expires."
