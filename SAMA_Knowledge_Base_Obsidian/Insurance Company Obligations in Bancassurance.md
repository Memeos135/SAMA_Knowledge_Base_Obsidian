# Insurance Company Obligations in Bancassurance

## Connections

### [[Bancassurance Activity]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping who is accountable for a bancassurance offering, do not read the bank's marketing/distribution role and the insurance company's role as one undifferentiated obligation set. The Rules define 'Bancassurance Activities' as the bank marketing and distributing the Company's products to Clients under a signed Agreement, while separately imposing controls and honest/fair-conduct verification duties tied to the Company-Bank relationship. Conclude that obligations attach to distinct parties under one contractual framework, and confirm from the specific article which duties bind the Company versus the Bank before allocating compliance responsibility.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6):** "Establish procedures to control and verify that the marketing and distribution of insurance products are carried out in an honest, transparent and fair manner."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Art 1.5):** "Bancassurance Activities: Marketing and distribution of insurance products by the Bank to its Clients according to the signed Agreement between the Bank and the Company."
- **Caveat:** Edge is 'conceptually_related_to'; the precise split of Company vs Bank obligations should be verified against the numbered articles, as the OCR text does not cleanly isolate each party's duties.

### [[Rules Governing Bancassurance Activities]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When allocating responsibility between the two counterparties, recognise that the Rules also bind the insurance Company, not only the distributing Bank. The Company is the SAMA-licensed insurer under M/32 whose approval the Bank must obtain to expand channels, and the Rules govern 'the relationship between the Company and the Bank.' Conclude that compliance is a shared, two-sided obligation set: the insurer retains duties (including consenting to channel expansion and controlling honest, transparent, fair marketing/distribution) and cannot fully offload conduct risk to the Bank.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6):** "The Bank must obtain the Company's approval to expand the marketing and distribution channels ... along with notifying SAMA"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Objective):** "regulate Bancassurance Activities and practices in Saudi Arabia and the relationship between the Company and the Bank"
