# APR Calculation Rules

**Cohesion:** 0.14 - loosely connected
**Members:** 17 nodes

## Why this community

The prescriptive APR calculation regime for consumer finance: the mandatory net-present-value method, SAMA's certified calculator, and the finance-company rules that set fee, credit-limit, term, and dispute constraints.

## How members connect

- Rules Governing Calculation of APR anchor the cluster, referencing the NPV method, the SAMA APR Calculator, and the enabling Finance Companies Control Law / Consumer Financing Regulations as the binding methodology.
- The APR Calculator (Excel) plus its Certification Circular make SAMA's tool the authoritative computation — the method (NPV) and Annex 1 are semantically the same substantive rule expressed in different instruments.
- Consumer Financing Regulations act as the umbrella, cross-referencing Maximum Credit Limit and Term Rules, Dispute Resolution Rules, Outsourcing Rules, and Licensed Credit Bureau — defining the surrounding compliance perimeter for financing.
- The Article 83 / Article 9 fees clarification circular interprets the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law, constraining what fees may enter the APR.
