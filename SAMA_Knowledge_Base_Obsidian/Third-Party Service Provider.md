# Third-Party Service Provider

## Connections

### [[Rules on Outsourcing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether the Rules bite on a given contract, apply the defined term 'Third-Party Service Provider' precisely, because the definition both scopes in and scopes out. A TPSP is any entity undertaking the outsourced activity on behalf of the bank, but the definition expressly excludes Head Offices and related entities of foreign bank branches from being 'Third-Party Service Providers.' You would conclude that intra-group arrangements are still governed by the branch-specific rules (Section vi) even though the counterparty is not a TPSP, and that overseas TPSPs additionally require a written SAMA no-objection.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Definitions):** "An entity undertaking the outsourced activity on behalf of the Banks. (Head Offices and Related entities of Foreign Bank Branches operating in KSA are not considered as Third-Party Service Providers)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / Art 33-34):** "Banks are required to ensure that for all outsourcing arrangements, SAMA has unrestricted... access to data pertaining to the outsourcing, if located at the premises of the third-party service provider"
