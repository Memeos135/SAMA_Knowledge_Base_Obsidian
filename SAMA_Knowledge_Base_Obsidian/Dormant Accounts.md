### [[Inoperative Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For status classification, treat 'dormant' as the defined entry point of the inoperative-account lifecycle: an account becomes dormant after 24 calendar months from the last customer-initiated debit or documented correspondence, and this triggers the heightened controls (double supervision, restricted withdrawals, separate filing) that carry through the inoperative regime. The link is grounded in the Rule's own sequencing, where dormant status precedes unclaimed and then abandoned status under the same governing Rule. Conclude that the 24-month trigger and its accompanying activation controls are the baseline test you apply first, before assessing whether an account has escalated to later inoperative stages.
