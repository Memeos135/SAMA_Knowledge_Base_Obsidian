# Cyber Security Governance

**Cohesion:** 0.08 - loosely connected
**Members:** 34 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA Cyber Security Framework regime: mandatory cyber-security governance, risk management, operations, and third-party controls that SAMA-regulated financial institutions must implement, with maturity-based assessment. Covers the governance/risk regime as applied to cyber resilience.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: SAMA issues the Framework as the binding parent; the Framework references its four control domains (Leadership & Governance, Risk Management & Compliance, Operations & Technology, Third Party) which subordinate the detailed obligations.
- Governance obligation chain: Leadership & Governance mandates a Cyber Security Strategy, Policy, defined Roles/Responsibilities, a CISO, a Cyber Security Committee, and Board oversight — allocating accountability for enforceable requirements.
- Cross-references to external standards (PCI-DSS, SWIFT CSCF, (inter)national industry standards) fold third-party benchmarks into the compliance obligation; the Glossary fixes defined terms (Vulnerability, IoC, Hard/Soft Token).
- Operations domain scopes concrete control areas — incident/event/threat/vulnerability management, e-banking and payment-system security, SOC, MFA — and Third Party/Cloud provisions extend obligations to outsourced arrangements.
