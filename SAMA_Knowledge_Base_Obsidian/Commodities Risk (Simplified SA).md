# Commodities Risk (Simplified SA)

## Connections

### [[Maturity Method]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When measuring commodities position risk under the simplified standardised approach, the maturity (ladder) method is the prescribed measurement system for capturing forward gap and interest rate risk separately, so the commodities charge cannot be assessed without applying maturity-based slotting. The link is grounded in the text offering the maturity ladder approach as one of two alternatives for commodities. A reviewer should check that forward and derivative commodity positions were run through the maturity ladder (not just netted on spot price), because directional risk alone understates forward gap and cost-of-carry exposure.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 514 / 14.66):** "the maturity ladder approach, which is a measurement system that captures forward gap and interest rate risk separately"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 434 / 8.15):** "maturity scaling, defined as a fraction of the year, of positions and their hedge"

### [[Simplified Standardised Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a bank's market-risk capital charge for commodity positions, read the commodities-risk provisions as one of the four risk classes summed under the Simplified Standardised Approach, not as a stand-alone regime. Both nodes sit within SAMA_EN_3487_VER1: the SSA node sets the framework where the total capital requirement is the arithmetic sum of interest-rate, equity, FX and commodity risk charges, and the commodities section supplies the sub-methodology (maturity ladder or simplified) for that fourth class. Conclude that commodity charges cannot be assessed in isolation — they feed the aggregated SSA capital figure and must be computed under the methods cross-referenced at [14.66]–[14.73].
- **Grounding — this node (Page 490 / para 14.2):** "the capital requirement arising from the simplified standardised approach is the simple sum of the recalibrated capital requirements arising from each of the four risk classes"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 490 / para 14.1):** "The risk-weighted assets for market risk under the simplified standardised approach are determined by multiplying the capital requirements... by 12.5."
