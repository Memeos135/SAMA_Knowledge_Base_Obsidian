# Remote Work Initiative

## Connections

### [[Providing Customer Communication Channels]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** These two circulars appear to connect only at the general level of customer-service operations at SAMA-supervised financial institutions: 4790 obligates firms to provide the unified toll-free customer-contact number to all customer segments, while 4834 addresses the labour/registration status of remote customer-service workers under the MHRSD remote-work initiative. The link is thematic (both touch the customer-service channel) rather than one instrument governing the other, so treat it as a lead. A reviewer should not infer that either circular modifies the other; verify each obligation against its own primary text — the contact-channel duty and the remote-worker registration duty are independent requirements.

### [[Remote Work Compliance in Customer Service]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a financial institution that uses remote customer-service staff, read these two circulars together: 4834 confirms SAMA's requirement to register 'remote' customer-service workers in official records and social insurance (or document their contracts via the MHRSD portal), while 4778 clarifies how the Saudization/remote-work localisation decision applies — including that it binds both the job titles and the actual work performed, whether staff are engaged directly or through an intermediary, contractor or outsourcing party. Both instruments expressly extend to workers supplied via a واسيط/third party, so the registration and localisation obligations cannot be avoided by outsourcing the customer-service function. A compliance reviewer should confirm that all remote customer-service personnel, including outsourced ones, are registered/documented and fall within the localisation scope.
