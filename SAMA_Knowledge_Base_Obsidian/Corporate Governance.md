### [[Implementing Regulation of Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping board and senior-management duties for a finance company, read the corporate-governance requirements as provisions of the Implementing Regulation, which itself defines 'Board of Directors' and 'Senior Management' and the internal control functions they oversee. Because governance sits inside the same Regulation issued under the Finance Companies Control Law, board/senior-management obligations — and the risk, internal audit and compliance functions they must ensure — are enforceable regulatory requirements, not mere best practice. Conclude that governance, compliance and exposure controls form one obligation chain under this instrument, and confirm the specific governance article rather than relying on the node grouping.
