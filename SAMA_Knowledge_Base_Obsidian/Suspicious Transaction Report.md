# Suspicious Transaction Report

## Connections

### [[Directorate of Financial Intelligence]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining where and how STRs must be filed, treat the Directorate of Financial Intelligence as the exclusive statutory recipient and the authority that fixes reporting form and content. Section 17/1 lists receiving and analysing STRs among the Directorate's functions, and the closing provision states the Directorate shall specify the manner reports are made and the information transmitted. The consequence for a compliance decision: reporting obligations are discharged only by filing to this body in the form it prescribes, and its guidance/templates govern — you cannot rely on ad hoc reporting channels.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 13):** "The directorate of financial intelligence shall further specify the manner in which reports under this Article are to be made and the information that shall be transmitted"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 14 / 17/1):** "Receive suspicious transaction reports... Prepare templates for use by reporting entities to report suspicious transactions"

### [[Wire Transfer Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing wire-transfer obligations, note that the payment-information duties in Article 10 carry an embedded STR trigger: a suspicion arising in the course of ordering or processing a transfer routes into the Article 15 reporting obligation. Provision 10/3 expressly states that in case of a suspicion an STR shall be submitted according to Article 15, and that a transfer cannot be ordered where the institution cannot meet its originator/beneficiary information obligations. For a compliance decision this means wire-transfer traceability failures and STR reporting are not siloed — inability to complete required information, or suspicion, forces both refusal of the transfer and consideration of an STR.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12 / 15/1):** "suspects or has reasonable grounds to suspect that funds... are proceeds of crime or are related to money laundering"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 10 / 10/3):** "In case of a suspicion, an STR shall be submitted according to Article 15 of the Law. If a financial institution cannot comply... it shall not order the wire transfer."
