# SIMAH (Saudi Credit Bureau)

## Connections

### [[Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how a supervised institution actually obtains an LEI for its business customers, note that the LEI regime and SIMAH are operationally linked: SIMAH acts as the GLEIF-accredited local operating unit authorised to register, update and renew LEIs for legal entities under the ROC framework and the GLEIF–LOU master agreement. This tells a compliance reader that LEI issuance in the Kingdom flows through SIMAH as the designated registrar, and that fees (per the agreement's cost schedule) and data-protection duties under the Credit Information Law and its Implementing Regulation attach to that channel. Verify SIMAH's continued GLEIF accreditation and the current fee schedule in the annexed agreement before relying on this route.

### [[Local Operating Unit]] — `implements` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the entity that discharges the Local Operating Unit function in the Kingdom, this document establishes that SIMAH is the accredited Local Operating Unit — SAMA designates SIMAH as the approved local LEI operator, and the annexed agreement recites that SIMAH is GLEIF-accredited to register and renew LEIs under the ROC charter and the GLEIF master agreement. A compliance reader should treat 'Local Operating Unit' and SIMAH as the same practical role here, meaning LEI registration/renewal obligations run to SIMAH; confirm that no other accredited LOU is being used, since the circular routes institutions specifically to SIMAH.

### [[SAMA LEI Issuance Instructions & SIMAH Agreement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying who a supervised institution must contract with to issue LEIs, this circular designates SIMAH (the Saudi Credit Information Company) as the accredited local operating unit and authorises financial institutions to sign the annexed 'issuance, updating and renewal of LEI through a financial institution' agreement with SIMAH. The agreement itself confirms SIMAH is GLEIF-accredited as a local operating unit and is SAMA-licensed and supervised. A compliance reader should conclude that the SIMAH agreement is the sanctioned channel for LEI issuance on behalf of business-sector customers, and should review the annexed agreement's cost, data-protection and Credit Information Law clauses before signing.
