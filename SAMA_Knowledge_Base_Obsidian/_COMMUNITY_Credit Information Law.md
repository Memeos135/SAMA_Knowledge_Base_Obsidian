# Credit Information Law

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

The statutory credit-information regime governing licensed credit bureaus, the data they hold, and consumer credit records — core to data/credit-information compliance in KSA.

## How members connect

- The Credit Information Law is the primary statute defining the Credit Information Company (licensed bureau) and the Credit Record it maintains.
- The Law delegates operational detail to its Implementing Regulations (law -> implementing regulation hierarchy), which bind conduct not spelled out in the statute.
- Credit Information Company and Credit Record are conceptually linked: the company is the regulated custodian/processor of the record, defining the scope of permissible collection and disclosure.
