# Freezing and Unfreezing Procedures

## Connections

### [[Alert Handling and Escalation]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a financial institution's TFS obligations, treat alert handling (Chapter Five) and freezing/unfreezing (Chapter Six) as a single obligation chain, not two separate duties: both sit within the same mandatory Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules, and a confirmed match generated through alert escalation is what triggers the asset-freezing and prohibition-on-making-funds-available obligations. The Rules apply compulsorily to all SAMA-supervised financial institutions and replace the prior circulars. For a compliance decision, conclude that escalation is not an end-state — a positive match must feed directly into the freezing procedures, and both chapters must be read together to establish who does what and when after a name match.

### [[Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing freezing/unfreezing duties, treat them as a mandatory chapter of the Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules rather than a separate procedure, because the TFS Rules' own contents list 'إجراءات التجميد ورفع التجميد' (Chapter Six), and the Rules define 'العقوبات المالية المستهدفة' to include asset-freezing and prohibitions on making funds available to listed persons. This anchors the freezing obligations to the same binding instrument imposed on all SAMA-supervised institutions. For a compliance decision, read freezing steps against the Rules' definitions of designated persons and assets, and note the immediate-reporting channel (TES-FIGD@SAMA.GOV.SA) the Rules provide.
