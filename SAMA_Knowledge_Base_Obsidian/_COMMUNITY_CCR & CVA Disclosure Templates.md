# CCR & CVA Disclosure Templates

**Cohesion:** 0.06 - loosely connected
**Members:** 35 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA Pillar 3 standardized disclosure templates and their governing frameworks — capital composition/TLAC, counterparty credit risk, market risk, CVA, leverage, countercyclical buffer and RWA overview. Defines mandatory public disclosure format for banks.

## How members connect

- Framework documents (SACAP capital reforms, SCCR counterparty credit risk, SMAR market risk, SLEV leverage) are the parent standards that each family of templates references and operationalizes.
- Capital-composition templates interlock: CC1 reconciles to CC2 and the balance sheet, feeds KM1 key metrics, and links to PV1, TLAC1 and CDC — a chain tying regulatory capital definition to disclosed figures.
- CCR templates (CCR1/3/4/5/8) all reference SCCR and cross-link (e.g. central counterparty exposures in CCR8); MR and CVA templates similarly map to SMAR and the CVA approaches, each template scoped to a specific calculation approach.
- CMS templates compare modelled vs standardised RWA and tie into OV1's RWA overview, connecting IRB output back to the disclosure regime.
