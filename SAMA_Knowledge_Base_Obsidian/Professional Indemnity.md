# Professional Indemnity

## Connections

### [[Professional Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying which coverage responds to the bank's own professional-risk exposure, treat Professional Indemnity as the branch of 'Professional Risk' that indemnifies the bank itself against third-party claims for errors, omissions or imprudence by its directors, officers and employees. The guide distinguishes PI (covering only specific professional services — trust, brokerage, investment advisory) from D&O, so a reader should conclude PI is narrower in the acts covered and does not substitute for D&O. This informs how to allocate the bank-level versus individual-level slices of professional liability.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12):** "Professional Indemnity ... designed to indemnify the bank itself against litigation by customers, and other third parties alleging errors, omissions ..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11 / section 2.2):** "Exposures directly related to the provision of financial products and services currently constitute ... the single largest ... form of operational risk"
