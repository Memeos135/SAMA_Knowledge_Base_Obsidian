# Debt-Based Crowdfunding

**Cohesion:** 0.25 - loosely connected
**Members:** 9 nodes

## Why this community

Licensing and conduct regime for debt-based crowdfunding (DBC) finance companies in KSA: who may operate a lending platform, the fit-and-proper gate for control persons, and prudential/credit limits protecting participants and beneficiaries.

## How members connect

- Licensing Guidelines and the Rules for Engaging in DBC form the entry-and-conduct hierarchy; both reference the Fit and Proper Form as a mandatory licensing/control-person requirement.
- The Rules define the licensed entity (DBC Company) and its regulated activity via the Platform, tying the actor to the permitted business.
- The DBC Company is subject to Credit Limits and DBC Risk Management Requirements — ongoing prudential obligations post-licensing.
- Participant and Institutional Beneficiary are the two counterparty roles the Platform intermediates (funder vs. borrower); relevant to consumer/investor protection scoping.
