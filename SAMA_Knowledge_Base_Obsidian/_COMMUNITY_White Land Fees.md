# White Land Fees

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

White Land (vacant land) fees regime: statutory levy on undeveloped urban land and SAMA guidance on treatment of fee-liable lands. Relevant where such land serves as financing collateral or affects borrower obligations.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the White Land Fees Law is the governing statute; the circular is subordinate SAMA guidance referencing it.
- The circular references the Law to direct how supervised entities register/treat lands subject to the white-land fee.
- Legal consequence: outstanding statutory fees on such land bear on lien priority, collateral valuation, and registration obligations.
