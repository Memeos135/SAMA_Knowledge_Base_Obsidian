# Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing

## Connections

### [[Anti-Money Laundering Law]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a Money Changer's AML/CTF obligations, treat Article 32 as an incorporating provision, not a standalone code: it binds the Money Changer to the Anti-Money Laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations, the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing and its Regulations, related circulars/controls, and the TFS/WMD proliferation Guideline. The substantive AML/CTF duties therefore live in those primary instruments, while the money-changing Rules add licensing-specific enforcement (Article 33 penalties, license revocation). Conclude that AML/CTF compliance testing for a Money Changer must be conducted against the AML Law and its Implementing Regulations directly, and that breach of those upstream obligations is independently sanctionable through SAMA's license and penalty powers.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18 / Art 32):** "The Money Changer shall comply with the provisions of the Anti-money laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations; the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing and its Implementing Regulations"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Art 4(b)):** "the provisions of the Anti-Money Laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations are complied with and the transactions are documented and archived"

### [[Risk Management and Monitoring]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** This link appears to connect the investment-side risk framework to the AML/CTF regime, but the two nodes sit in different instruments: the Banks Investment Rules require AML/CTF compliance before investing (Sec 7.3), while node B is the AML/CTF article in the Money Changer Rules pointing to the AML Law and Terrorism Financing Law and their implementing regulations. The relationship is conceptual — both point back to the same underlying SAMA AML/CTF framework — rather than a direct cross-reference between these two documents. Before relying on this, verify against the primary AML Law and its Implementing Regulations, since the Money Changer article governs money changers and does not itself set the AML rules for a bank's investment activity.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18 / Art 32):** "The Money Changer shall comply with the provisions of the Anti-money laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations; the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing and its Implementing Regulations"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Sec 7.3):** "Banks should comply with all relevant Anti Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing requirements issued by SAMA."
- **Caveat:** INFERRED and cross-instrument (banks vs. money changers); the shared reference is the underlying AML/CTF Law, not a direct link between these provisions. Confirm the applicable AML source for bank investment activity separately.

### [[Rules Regulating Money Changing Business]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a money changer's AML/CTF obligations, do not read the Money Changing Rules as a self-contained AML regime — Article 32 makes the money changer subject to the full external AML/CTF stack (the Anti-Money Laundering Law and Implementing Regulations, the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing and its Regulations, related SAMA circulars, and the WMD proliferation-financing/TFS guideline). The Rules also embed AML duties at licensing (adherence to SAMA's AML/CTF instructions) and in correspondent relationships (Art. 17), and breach exposes the changer to Article 33 penalties up to license revocation. A compliance reviewer should therefore treat the underlying AML Law and TFS instruments as the controlling authority and check compliance against them, not merely against the Rules' summary reference.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18 / Art 32):** "The Money Changer shall comply with the provisions of the Anti-money laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations; the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Art 4(b)):** "the provisions of the Anti-Money Laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations are complied with and the transactions are documented and archived."
