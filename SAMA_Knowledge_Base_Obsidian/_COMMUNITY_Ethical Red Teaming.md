# Ethical Red Teaming

**Cohesion:** 0.19 - loosely connected
**Members:** 13 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA's Financial Entities Ethical Red Teaming (F.E.E.R.) framework governing intelligence-led adversarial security testing of regulated financial entities, subordinate to the SAMA Cyber Security Framework.

## How members connect

- F.E.E.R. is the governing framework defining participant roles (Red, Blue, White, Green Teams) and their mandated deliverables.
- Obligation chain runs to specific outputs: Red Teaming Provider produces the RTER, Blue Team produces the BTR, and White Team owns the Remediation Plan drawing on both reports.
- Methodology terms (Cyber Kill Chain, Threat Intelligence, Penetration Testing) are defined-by F.E.E.R. and scope how testing must be conducted.
- The Red Teaming Provider is anchored to the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, establishing F.E.E.R. as a subordinate/complementary regime within SAMA's cyber-supervision hierarchy.
