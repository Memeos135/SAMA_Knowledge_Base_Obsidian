# Recognition of Financial Receivables Collateral

## Connections

### [[Supervisory LGD and EAD Estimates]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether financial receivables can reduce a bank's LGD, note that the eligibility and legal-certainty requirements for receivables sit within the same paragraph block (16.127–16.144) that governs supervisory LGD/EAD estimates. Section 7 directs banks that do not qualify for own-estimates of LGD/EAD to use supervisory estimates whose standards are set out in those paragraphs, and the financial-receivables recognition rules (16.132 onward) are part of that regime. You should therefore treat receivables recognition as conditioning the supervisory-LGD outcome and verify the legal-certainty and risk-management conditions before crediting any collateral effect.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 223 / 16.132-16.133):** "Eligible financial receivables are claims with an original maturity of less than or equal to one year... The legal mechanism by which collateral is given must be robust"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 197 / 16.62):** "banks that do not meet the requirements for own-estimates of EAD or LGD... must use the supervisory estimates of these parameters. Standards for use of such estimates are set out in paragraphs 16.127 to 16.144."
