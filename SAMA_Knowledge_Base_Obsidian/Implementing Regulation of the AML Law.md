# Implementing Regulation of the AML Law

## Connections

### [[Anti-Money Laundering Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping precise AML obligations, do not rely on the Law alone: it repeatedly delegates operative detail to the Implementing Regulation, so the actual thresholds, triggers and measures live in the subordinate instrument. The Law itself defines 'the regulation' as its Implementing Regulation and directs it to set the instances and types of CDD measures, PEP measures, correspondent-banking risk mitigation, customs declaration thresholds and the types of mutual-legal-assistance available. Conclude that any compliance determination on the extent of due diligence or reporting must be cross-checked against the Implementing Regulation, not the Law's high-level duty statements.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Art 7):** "the Implementing Regulation shall set forth the instances in which such measures shall be taken and the types of measures to be taken."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1 / Art 1(2)):** "The regulation: the Implementing Regulation of this law."
