# Bank Remuneration Rules

**Cohesion:** 0.50 - moderately connected
**Members:** 5 nodes

## Why this community

Governance regime for bank remuneration under SAMA's Banks Remuneration Rules: aligning pay with prudent risk-taking through board oversight, a dedicated committee, deferral/adjustment mechanisms, and compliance attestation.

## How members connect

- All members reference 'Banks Remuneration Rules' as the governing instrument — it is the parent rule to which each obligation is subordinate.
- Board Responsibilities and the Nomination & Remuneration Committee are the governance bodies the Rules assign duties to; they connect as delegating body and delegate.
- Malus/Clawback is the risk-adjustment tool the Rules mandate to recover or reduce variable pay when warranted.
- The Remuneration Compliance Report is the reporting/attestation obligation demonstrating adherence to the Rules to SAMA.
