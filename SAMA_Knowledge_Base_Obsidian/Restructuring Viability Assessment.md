# Restructuring Viability Assessment

## Connections

### [[Rules on Management of Problem Loans]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When approving a restructuring of a problem loan, the Rules make viability a binding gate: long-term and short-term restructuring measures may 'only be considered viable' where specified documented affordability, arrears-treatment and repeat-restructuring conditions are met, including Senior Management Committee approval for repeat cases. The Rules also import 'Viability Assessment' as a defined term (borrower's ability to generate adequate cash flow to service outstanding loans). For a compliance decision, verify that each restructuring is supported by reasonable documented financial information demonstrating affordability and does not simply defer default; a restructuring failing these tests is not defensible as viable under section 5.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10 / 5.1):** "Long-term restructuring measures should only be considered viable where the following conditions are met: ... the borrower can realistically afford the restructuring solution."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / 1.4):** "Viability Assessment: An assessment of borrower's ability to generate adequate cash flow in order to service outstanding loans."

### [[Workout Plan]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing a proposed restructuring, do not treat the workout plan as approvable on its own — the viability conditions gate it, because the Rules require the plan to match the borrower's sustainable repayment capacity while separately requiring that long- and short-term restructuring only be treated as viable where the borrower can 'realistically afford' the solution on documented financial information. A workout plan for a borrower failing the viability tests, or one that is likely to lead to repeated consecutive restructurings, would not meet the Rules' standard. A reviewer should check that each workout plan is supported by a documented viability assessment and the required senior-management approvals, particularly for repeat restructurings.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10):** "Long-term restructuring measures should only be considered viable where... the borrower can realistically afford the restructuring solution."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / Section 6):** "Banks should develop a workout plan... matching the borrower's sustainable repayment capacity with the correct restructuring option(s)."
