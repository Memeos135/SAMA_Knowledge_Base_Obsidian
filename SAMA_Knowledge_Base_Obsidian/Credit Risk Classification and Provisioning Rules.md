# Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning Rules

## Connections

### [[Restructuring KPIs]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating how a restructured/forborne exposure should be classified and provisioned, read the credit-risk classification material together with the restructuring principles and early-warning indicators, since they are elements of the same SAMA guideline and share the concept of 'Forborne Exposures' and negative-trend triggers. The early-warning signs listed (arrears, negative rating trends, forborne status) feed both restructuring decisions and how the exposure is graded and provisioned. For a decision this means a restructuring does not neutralise classification/provisioning obligations — a forborne or early-arrears exposure remains subject to the risk-grading treatment set out in the guideline.
