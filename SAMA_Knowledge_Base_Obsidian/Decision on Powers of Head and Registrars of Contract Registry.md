# Decision on Powers of Head and Registrars of Contract Registry

## Connections

### [[Contract Registry (Sijil)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When analysing the legal status and duties of the Contract Registry (Sijil) for finance-lease contracts, treat this Decision as the instrument that defines the powers, responsibilities and liabilities of its Head and Registrars. The document establishes that registration follows the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation, that the Head/Registrar bears direct responsibility for the accuracy of registered contracts and must retain records for at least ten years after obligations expire, and that SAMA non-objection is required for appointments. You should rely on this Decision to determine who is accountable for registration validity and to whom aggrieved parties may complain (the company, then SAMA, then the competent court).

### [[Finance Lease Law (M48)|Finance Lease Law (M/48)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When establishing the legal basis for the Contract Registry's authority, note that this Decision is issued under the Finance Lease Law (M/48) and its Implementing Regulation — specifically Article 15(4), which empowers SAMA to issue a decision defining the Registrar's powers. Because the Decision is a third-tier instrument (law → implementing regulation → decision), its provisions cannot exceed or contradict the Finance Lease Law; the enabling article is the source of the Registrar's mandate. You should verify obligations against the Law and Regulation where the Decision is silent or ambiguous, and treat the Decision as valid only insofar as it operationalises Article 15(4).

### [[Implementing Regulation of Finance Lease Law]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the direct legal trigger for this Decision, focus on the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Lease Law: the Decision is issued 'in application of' Article 15(4) of that Regulation, which mandates that SAMA issue a decision defining the Registrar's powers. The Decision also cross-refers to Article 16 of the same Regulation for the appointment/non-objection procedure. You should treat the Regulation as the operative source: any gap in the Decision (e.g. registration formalities, appointment conditions) is governed by the Regulation articles it invokes, and the Decision must be read consistently with them.
