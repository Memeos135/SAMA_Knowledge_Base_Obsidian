# Sub-Committees

### [[Executive Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When tracing delegation within the finance-companies committee framework, note that the Terms of Reference treat the Executive Committee and the Sub-Committees ('اللجان الفرعية') as distinct structural layers, each with its own formation, nomination/removal, quorum, agenda/minutes and responsibilities. This link indicates a tiered arrangement in which sub-committees operate beneath the broader committee structure. For a governance decision, verify each layer's separately-stated mandate and reporting lines in the Terms of Reference rather than assuming sub-committees inherit the Executive Committee's authority.
