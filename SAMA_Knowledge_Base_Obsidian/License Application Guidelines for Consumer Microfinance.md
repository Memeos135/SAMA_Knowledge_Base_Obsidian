# License Application Guidelines for Consumer Microfinance

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying to engage in consumer microfinance, read these Guidelines as a subordinate procedural instrument to the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation — the primary legal source of the licensing provisions. The Guidelines expressly state the Law and Implementing Regulation 'set out the provisions governing the licensing', that defined terms take their meanings from the Law and Regulation, and that supplying false or incomplete information is a violation of the Law, specifically Article 7. You should therefore treat the Guidelines as the how-to for a regime whose enforceable substance and sanctions live in the Law; do not rely on the Guidelines alone for the definitive licensing conditions or penalties.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / Instruction 3):** "The terms and phrases mentioned in the license application form shall have the meanings stated in the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Introduction):** "The Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation set out the provisions governing the licensing of consumer microfinance activities."

### [[Fit and Proper Form]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When preparing a consumer microfinance licence application, treat the SAMA Fit and Proper Form as a mandatory attachment that must accompany the application for a defined set of persons, not an optional add-on. The Guidelines require a completed and signed Fit and Proper Form for each founding member, for every natural or legal person owning 5% or more of a founding entity, and for each board and sub-committee candidate. You should conclude that the ownership-threshold and role coverage determines exactly whose forms are required, and that omission renders the application incomplete (SAMA notifies completion only once all listed requirements are met), delaying or blocking initial approval.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Licensing Procedures 2):** "SAMA shall notify the Applicant, in writing, of the completion of their application upon fulfilling all the requirements..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Required Documents 7-9):** "SAMA's Fit and Proper Form for each founding member... a Fit and Proper Form shall be submitted for every (natural or legal) person owning 5% or more of this entity."
