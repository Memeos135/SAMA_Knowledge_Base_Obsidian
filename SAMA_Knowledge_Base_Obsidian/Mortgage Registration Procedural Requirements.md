# Mortgage Registration Procedural Requirements

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining which entities may act as mortgagee under this mortgage-documentation circular, note that SAMA's authority to bind finance companies here derives partly from the Finance Companies Control Law (نظام مراقبة شركات التمويل), which the circular cites among its enabling statutes. The link tells you the circular's scope reaches licensed finance companies (not only banks) and that a valid SAMA finance-activity licence is a precondition to being a lawful mortgagee. You would therefore check that any non-bank financier holds a current SAMA licence under the Finance Companies Control Law before treating its registered mortgage as validly created.

### [[Registered Mortgage Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank or finance company's mortgage documentation is enforceable, read these procedural requirements as SAMA's implementation of the Registered Mortgage Law (Royal Decree No. M/49), which the circular expressly cites as its statutory reference. The circular does not create the mortgage regime; it operationalizes registration for financiers with notaries and mandates use of the SAMA/Ministry of Justice forms, so compliance turns on both the substantive law and these procedural steps (mortgagee must be a licensed bank/finance company, mortgaged property must be owned by the mortgagor, Shariah-committee product approval, etc.). Confirm each procedural condition is met and that the underlying registration complies with the Registered Mortgage Law before relying on the mortgage as valid security.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1):** "وإشارةٌ إلى نظام الرهن العقاري المسجل الصادر بالمرسوم الملكي رقم(م/45)"
