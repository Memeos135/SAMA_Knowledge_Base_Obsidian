- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the legal authority behind the Main Governance Principles, note that SAMA issues them under its own Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36), which is the enabling source of its supervisory powers over financial institutions. The Governance Principles' cover circular expressly invokes the powers vested in SAMA under the law issued by Royal Decree M/36. You would conclude the Governance Principles are binding secondary instruments deriving force from that law, and their mandatory application to listed institution types (banks, finance companies, exchange, payments, credit information) rests on SAMA's statutory supervisory mandate.
