# Securitization IRB Approach

**Cohesion:** 0.10 - loosely connected
**Members:** 29 nodes

## Why this community

Basel III securitisation capital framework as adopted by SAMA: the ranked methods for computing RWA/capital on securitisation and resecuritisation exposures, plus the STC preferential-treatment regime. Relevance: dictates which capital approach a bank must apply and the eligibility gates for lower charges.

## How members connect

- The Hierarchy of Approaches sets a mandatory ordering (SEC-IRBA -> SEC-IAA/SEC-ERBA -> SEC-SA), so approach selection is a rule-driven determination, not a free choice.
- SEC-IRBA is the calculation core, drawing on KIRB, attachment/detachment points and supervisory parameter p; caps and look-through provisions bound the result.
- STC Framework (term and ABCP criteria) is an eligibility overlay unlocking preferential parameters only where all conditions are met; resecuritisation and NPL securitisation are carved-out stricter cases.
- SAMA Circular 341000015689 is the parent adoption instrument for Basel III capital, and BCS 242 (ECAI mapping) is cited as the external-rating-to-risk-weight bridge feeding these approaches.
