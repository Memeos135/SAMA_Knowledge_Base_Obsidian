# Standardized Approach (Operational Risk)

- **What this link tells you:** When determining how a bank must calculate its operational-risk capital charge under SAMA rules, the Standardised Approach is the prescribed methodology within the Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk (SOPE) standard — under the Basel III post-crisis reforms operational risk uses a single standardised approach rather than internal models. The two nodes are parts of one framework (SOPE) rather than separate obligations. Conclude that operational-risk RWA feeds the Pillar 1 minimum (RWA × 8%) and must be derived using this standardised method; verify the specific inputs (business indicator, loss component) in the SOPE chapters before relying on the calculation.

### [[Operational Risk Capital (ORC)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When computing a bank's Operational Risk Capital (ORC), understand that the figure is the output of applying the Standardised Approach for operational risk mandated under SOPE — the two are linked as method (Standardised Approach) to result (ORC). The ORC amount then contributes to the Pillar 1 minimum capital requirement (RWA × 8%) disclosed for all banks. Conclude that a bank cannot substitute an internal operational-risk model; it must derive ORC via the prescribed standardised method, and any capital-adequacy assessment should confirm the ORC inputs against the SOPE standardised-approach specifications.
