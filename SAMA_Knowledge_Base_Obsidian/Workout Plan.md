# Workout Plan

## Connections

### [[Business Viability Analysis]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing how a workout plan was built, treat the business viability analysis as a required upstream input that determines eligibility for restructuring at all — not merely supporting colour. The guidelines condition approval of a restructuring plan on the borrower's projected cash flows being sufficient to repay obligations, which is exactly what the viability analysis (assumptions validation, sensitivity, cash-generation focus) establishes. Conclude that a workout plan for a non-viable borrower is off-strategy: such loans should instead be referred to the legal unit or foreclosure, so verify the viability determination precedes and grounds the plan.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46):** "borrower's projected cash flows must be sufficient to repay all or a substantial portion of its past due obligations within a reasonable time frame"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 44):** "The findings of the financial and business viability analysis should be documented in writing and communicated to the credit committee"

### [[Financial Viability Analysis]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When testing whether a workout plan rests on sound footing, treat the financial viability analysis (ratios, balance-sheet review, cash-flow analysis) as the quantitative basis for the plan's core pre-condition. The guidelines make cash-flow-based repayment capacity the defining test of viability, and the plan can only be approved where projected cash flows sustain repayment — so the two are sequential, not parallel. Note also the independence requirement: this analysis must be conducted by a Workout Unit not previously involved in loan approval, so verify the analysis was performed by segregated staff before relying on the plan's viability conclusion.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46):** "For the bank to consider approving a restructuring plan, the borrower must meet two essential pre-conditions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 41):** "Cash is the ultimate source of loan repayment ... primary emphasis ... should be on its forecasted cash generation capabilities"

### [[Negotiating Strategy]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When examining how a workout plan was finalized with the borrower, treat the negotiating strategy (including SWOT analysis of the account) as the guideline-expected preparatory step that shapes the plan's terms rather than an informal tactic. The guidelines direct that before initiating negotiations the bank should prepare a strategy covering defined objectives, borrower needs, and root-cause analysis, which then builds into the negotiated plan. Conclude that a workout plan reached without a documented negotiating strategy lacks the analytical basis SAMA expects, so check that the plan's terms trace back to a pre-negotiation assessment of the account's strengths and weaknesses.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46):** "the Workout Unit must choose from a variety of options to tailor a restructuring plan that meets the needs of specific borrower"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 58):** "On critical assessment of these factors, bank can build the plan into negotiating strategy ... cover the defined objectives along with needs of the borrower, reason for restructuring"

### [[RestructuringWorkout Options|Restructuring/Workout Options]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing a finalized workout plan, treat the identification and comparison of restructuring/workout options as the mandatory selection stage that must precede and justify the plan's chosen structure. The guidelines expressly reject a 'one size fits all' approach and require the Workout Unit to select from a range of options, then document that the revised terms yield greater recovery than legal procedures, with the restructuring agreement (or amended loan agreement) formalizing the result. Conclude that the plan is only defensible where the file shows the option set was considered and the selected option documented as the higher-recovery choice, with legal drafting decisions (new vs amended agreement) resolved by the legal department.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 67):** "the restructuring documentation will determine the conditions and effectiveness of the restructuring ... all parties should agree and sign the documents before implementing the workout"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 46):** "There is no one standard ('one size fits all') approach ... the Workout Unit must choose from a variety of options to tailor a restructuring plan"

### [[Workout Unit]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping who is accountable for a restructuring outcome, treat the workout plan as the product the Workout Unit is required to design and negotiate: the WU tailors the restructuring option to the borrower, and the bank may only approve a plan where projected cash flows suffice and the borrower is cooperative. The link confirms the WU's segregation from Loan Originating Units and its reporting to Risk Management, so plan approval sits within an independent decision chain. Check that plan approval authority and pre-conditions are documented and that conflicts of interest with originating officers are excluded.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46 / 5.2.1):** "the Workout Unit must choose from a variety of options to tailor a restructuring plan ... borrower must meet two essential pre-conditions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 32 / 4):** "Workout Unit (WU) should be established as a permanent unit ... reporting directly to the Risk Management function rather than the Business / Loan Originating Units"
