# Know Your Customer (KYC)

## Connections

### [[Account Opening Rules for Juristic Persons]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a DTFC on onboarding, do not treat the account-opening documentation rules and the KYC standards as separable checklists — they operate as one gate to opening any account. Chapter 8 fixes the minimum identity data and beneficial-owner acknowledgement required (including that the account holder is the real beneficiary), while Chapter 13 makes clear identity establishment, customer-acceptance policy and purpose/BO verification are mandatory KYC preconditions; if KYC suspicions arise on accuracy or sufficiency, the DTFC must refuse to open, refuse the relationship, refuse transactions, and report to SAMA's FIU. You should conclude that satisfying the account-opening document list alone is insufficient — the opening decision must also clear the KYC acceptance and BO-verification standard, and remote opening for new customers remains prohibited.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 50 / Chapter 13, para 157):** "the DTFCs shall not allow to open the account, start the relationship or execute any transactions. DTFCs must information Financial Investigation Unit at SAMA about the suspicions."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 37 / para 85(viii)):** "Acknowledges and verify that Account holder is the real beneficiary of the account."

### [[Financial Investigation Unit at SAMA]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping a DTFC's AML reporting duties, read the KYC obligation as the trigger and the SAMA Financial Investigation Unit as the mandatory recipient. Chapter 13 requires that where KYC concerns (accuracy, correctness, insufficient data, or non-compliance) prevent opening, the DTFC must decline the relationship and inform the FIU at SAMA of the suspicions; ongoing monitoring likewise routes suspicious transactions to the FIU. You should conclude that suspicion identified through KYC/monitoring is not discretionary intelligence — it is an escalation obligation to a named SAMA body, and refusal to onboard does not discharge the separate duty to report.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 50 / Chapter 13, para 153):** "customer acceptance policy, customer identification as set in these Rules (at minimum), on-going monitoring of high-risk accounts, and risk management"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 50 / Chapter 13, para 157):** "DTFCs must information Financial Investigation Unit at SAMA about the suspicions."
