# Loan Life Coverage Ratio (LLCR)

## Connections

### [[Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When selecting the coverage metric for a given exposure, understand that DSCR and LLCR are alternative solvency measures distinguished by time horizon, not interchangeable tools. The Appendix 2 text draws the contrast directly: DSCR 'captures just a single point in time' whereas LLCR 'allows for several time periods more suitable for medium to long time horizons.' You would conclude that using DSCR alone for a multi-year or project-finance workout would understate the applicable viability test, since the guidance steers those exposures to LLCR.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 82 / Appendix 2):** "LLCR allows for several time periods more suitable for understanding liquidity available for loans of medium to long time horizons"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 82 / Appendix 2):** "Unlike Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) which captures just a single point in time"

### [[Initial Viability Assessment]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating viability of longer-dated exposures, note that the guidance designates LLCR as a specific viability metric for project finance and multi-year loans, with an indicative benchmark of 1.4. Both provisions are in SAMA_EN_3575; the viability-assessment text points to LLCR 'as defined below' and cross-refers to Appendix 2 for computation. You would conclude that for multi-year and project-finance NPLs, LLCR (not just Debt/EBITDA or LTV) is the expected input to the initial viability determination, while remembering the thresholds are 'indicative rather than prescriptive.'
- **Grounding — this node (Page 82 / Appendix 2):** "LLCR should be used by Workout teams to assess the viability of a given amount of debt"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 23):** "Loan life coverage ratio (LLCR), as defined below should be 1.4, which would give a cushion of 40%"

### [[Workout Unit]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing how a bank decides whether to restructure a distressed loan, note that the LLCR is the analytical tool assigned to Workout teams for viability assessment of medium- to long-term and project-finance debt. This link tells you the Workout Unit's approval of a restructuring plan should be grounded in defined viability metrics (LLCR alongside DSCR/Debt-EBITDA), with the Guidelines indicating an indicative 1.4 LLCR threshold for multi-year loans. Check that WU viability assessments actually apply LLCR to long-horizon exposures rather than relying on single-point ratios.
