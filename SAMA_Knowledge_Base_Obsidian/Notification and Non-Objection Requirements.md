# Notification and Non-Objection Requirements

## Connections

### [[New Banking Products and Services Regulation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a bank must do before launching a new product, read the Notification and Non-Objection provisions (Section 7) as the operative gate within the New Products Regulation, and note they are not interchangeable. Notification (7.1) is the default and is only available to banks that satisfy the required maturity level in the Cyber Security, Counter-Fraud, Business Continuity and IT Governance Frameworks; non-objection (7.2) is the exception required for specified products (home loans, financial leases, derivatives, and products not covered by existing SAMA rules) and is mandatory for banks that do not meet that maturity level. Conclude that you must first classify the product and confirm the bank's framework-maturity status to know whether mere notification suffices or prior SAMA non-objection is legally required before launch.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12 / 7.2.1):** "Banks are required to seek SAMA's non-objection for the below products and services prior launching, as an exception to the notification requirements"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Sec 2):** "regulatory requirements of notifying SAMA prior offering a new product or service, and the required supporting documents to be submitted"

### [[Offering of Financial Derivatives Products]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the pre-launch approval path for a derivatives product, do not rely on the lighter notification track — financial derivatives are expressly carved into the non-objection regime. Clause 7.2.1 lists 'Financial Derivatives' among products requiring SAMA's prior non-objection 'as an exception to the notification requirements,' and Section 7.3 layers additional suitability and bona-fide-economic-purpose conditions on top. A reader should conclude that a derivative cannot be launched on acknowledgment receipt alone; the bank must obtain affirmative non-objection, launch within 12 months, and satisfy the 7.3 customer-suitability tests before offering.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12 / Sec 7.2.1):** "Banks are required to seek SAMA's non-objection for the below products and services prior launching, as an exception to the notification requirements... 3. Financial Derivatives"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13 / Sec 7.3.6):** "Banks seeking to introduce new financial derivative products must demonstrate that the proposed financial derivative instrument has a bona fide economic purpose"
