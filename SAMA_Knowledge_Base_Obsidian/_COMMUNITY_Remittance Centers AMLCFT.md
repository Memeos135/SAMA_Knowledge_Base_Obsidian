# Remittance Centers AML/CFT

**Cohesion:** 0.09 - loosely connected
**Members:** 27 nodes

## Why this community

A mixed licensing-and-conduct cluster spanning bank remittance-center AML/CFT obligations, bank/digital-bank licensing and governance, and the real-estate finance/refinance regime. The common thread is SAMA-authorized activity: who may operate, under what fit-and-proper/governance conditions, and what CDD/consumer-protection duties attach.

## How members connect

- The Remittance Centers Guide is subordinate to the Banking Control Law and operationalizes AML/CFT Rules, CDD Measures, KYC, SAFIU reporting and FATF standards — a guide-level layer on top of the AML regime.
- Licensing Guidelines (and the digital-only bank add-on) sit under the Banking Control Law and cross-reference Corporate Governance Principles, the Cyber Security Framework, and Fit-and-Proper/senior-appointment requirements as licensing preconditions.
- A distinct real-estate finance sub-chain: Real Estate Finance Law -> its Implementing Regulation -> Rules Governing Real Estate Refinance Companies / SRC, plus Responsible Lending and Deductible Ratio consumer limits.
- Finance Companies Control Law anchors outsourcing rules, material-function definitions, and compliance-manager certification for the finance-company side — read these as separate obligation stacks that happen to co-cluster, not one unified regime.
