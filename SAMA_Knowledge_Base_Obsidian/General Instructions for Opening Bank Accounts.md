# General Instructions for Opening Bank Accounts

## Connections

### [[Chapter III Procedural Rules|Chapter III: Procedural Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying account-opening requirements, treat the '100 General Instructions for Opening Bank Accounts' as the opening (and most general) rule-set of Chapter III, which then breaks down into specific procedural rules by customer type (natural persons, juristic persons, government entities). The general instructions — covering disclosure/enforcement searches and remote-opening identity verification — govern all account types unless a more specific Chapter III rule modifies them. Conclude that for any account-opening decision you must apply the general instructions first and then layer the customer-specific procedural rules on top, checking for any specific provision that overrides the general one.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 29 / 100 sec.5):** "The bank is responsible for verifying the identity of the corporation by using documents, data or information acquired from a reliable and independent source."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Chapter III):** "Chapter III Procedural Rules ... 100 General Instructions for Opening Bank Accounts 23"

### [[Remote Opening of Bank Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing remote (non-face-to-face) account opening for corporations, read the remote-opening rules as a specialized application of the general account-opening framework, not as a relaxation of it. The remote-opening rules retain the core identity-verification obligation — reliable and independent sourcing of the corporation's name, legal form, capital, owners, ownership percentages and authorized persons — and add a risk-management standard to avoid opening accounts for prohibited or incompetent persons. Conclude that remote channels do not lower the substantive CDD/identity standard set by the general instructions; the same verification content must be satisfied through the remote mechanism.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 29 / General Instructions):** "The bank is responsible for verifying the identity of the corporation by using documents, data or information acquired from a reliable and independent source"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 29 / Rule 5 (Remote Opening)):** "Appropriate standards must be set to manage risks associated with these accounts before approving the opening ... to avoid opening an account for a company or a person with whom dealing is prohibited"
