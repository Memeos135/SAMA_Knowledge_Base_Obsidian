# Residents Investing under Foreign Investment Law

## Connections

### [[Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When opening accounts for entities owned wholly or jointly by foreign investors under the Foreign Investment Law (300.1.4), treat them as a juristic-persons sub-category with added documentation demands (Ministry of Investment license, embassy-certified foreign partner documents, beneficial-owner verification), not as a lighter-touch route. Both provisions are part of the same 300-series juristic-persons chapter and share its identification logic — verify the partners named in the memorandum of association except for listed joint-stock companies — while the foreign-investment rules add cross-border authentication. Conclude that the general juristic-person conditions apply cumulatively with the foreign-investment specific evidence before an account may be opened.
