# SAMA Business Continuity Minimum Requirements (referenced)

## Connections

### [[Business Continuity Management Framework]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are scoping a Member Organization's resilience obligations, treat the BCM Framework and the Cyber Security Framework as complementary rather than a single source — the BCM Framework itself directs readers to the Cyber Security Framework for controls like threat and vulnerability management, and the CSF in turn incorporates 'business continuity' as a defined availability concept (per ISO 22301). This apparent cross-reference means BCP/DRP obligations and cyber-resilience obligations overlap but are governed by two distinct SAMA instruments. Because this link is INFERRED, verify the actual cross-reference in the BCM Framework's resilience note before relying on one framework to satisfy the other; each has its own control set and compliance testing.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46 / Glossary):** "Business continuity ... capability of an organization to continue delivery of IT and business services at acceptable predefined levels following a disruptive incident. (ISO 22301:2012)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / section 2.7 note):** "For more control considerations to improve the overall resilience, e.g., threat management, vulnerability management, please refer to the SAMA - Cyber Security Framework."

### [[Cyber Security Framework (CSF)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a Member Organization's cyber security obligations extend to resilience and continuity, treat business continuity as an in-scope requirement rather than a separate discipline, because the CSF expressly requires that 'cyber security is reflected in business continuity management.' The CSF also defines 'business continuity' in its glossary (per ISO 22301) and applies its controls to all information assets. For a compliance decision, conclude that a firm cannot demonstrate CSF compliance while excluding continuity considerations from its cyber security program.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46 (glossary)):** "Business continuity — the capability of an organization to continue delivery of IT and business services at acceptable predefined levels following a disruptive incident"
