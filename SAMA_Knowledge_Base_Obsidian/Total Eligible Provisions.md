# Total Eligible Provisions

## Connections

### [[Treatment of Expected Losses and Provisions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank has excess or shortfall of provisions against EL for capital purposes, you need both sides of the comparison: chapter 15 sets the EL/provisions treatment, and 'Total Eligible Provisions' (para 15.4) fixes what may be counted on the provisions side. This matters because eligibility is scoped — only provisions attributed to IRB-treated exposures qualify, discounts on defaulted assets may be added, and provisions set aside against securitization exposures must be excluded. For the EL-vs-provisions offset you should therefore verify each provision's classification before including it, and apply the pro-rata/attribution rules of paras 15.5–15.7 where the bank uses both standardized and IRB approaches.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 175 / para 15.4):** "Total eligible provisions are defined as the sum of all provisions...that are attributed to exposures treated under the IRB approach...General and specific provisions set aside against securitization exposures must not be included"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 174 / para 15.1):** "the difference between provisions...and EL may be included in or must be deducted from regulatory capital"
