# Deposit Taking Finance Companies Regulations

## Connections

### [[Capital Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping prudential obligations for a deposit-taking finance company, treat the Capital Requirements chapter as an operative part of the DTFC Regulations, not a standalone standard — Chapter 4 sits within the Regulations and imposes the binding 20% capital adequacy and minimum capital requirements that every DTFC must maintain 'at all times.' The Regulations themselves are subordinate to the Finance Companies Control Law and require DTFCs to also comply with general FC regulations. For a capital-adequacy assessment, conclude that the specific ratios (20% capital adequacy, SAMA's power to impose higher ratios) are mandatory components of the wider DTFC regime and must be read with its scope and definitions.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Chapter 1, para 3):** "In addition to these DTFC-specific prudential requirements, DTFCs are also required to comply with the Finance Companies Control Law, SAMA regulations for Finance Companies (FCs)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Chapter 4, para 20):** "Every DTFC shall, at all times- ... enable proper computation of the institution's capital adequacy of 20%; and maintain the prescribed minimum capital requirements."

### [[Corporate Governance and Risk Management]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping governance and risk obligations for a DTFC, read the Corporate Governance and Risk Management chapter as an integral, binding part of the DTFC Regulations applicable to all DTFCs — Chapter 3 sets SAMA's requirements for internal governance, senior-management job descriptions, fit-and-proper pre-approval, and the Management Responsibilities Description Document, all proportionate to the firm's risk profile. Because the parent Regulations are issued subject to the Finance Companies Control Law and require compliance with general FC rules, these governance duties layer on top of that framework. For a governance decision, conclude that senior-manager JDs and the MRDD are mandatory deliverables tied to SAMA's fit-and-proper regime, not optional best practice.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Chapter 1, para 1):** "These SAMA regulations are applicable to all Deposit Taking Finance Companies (OTFCs) operating in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA)."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / Chapter 3, para 10-11):** "All DTFCs are required to put in place a Job description (JD) for each member of the senior management ... A DTFC is also required to produce and maintain a Management Responsibilities Description Document (MRDD)"

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping what governs a Deposit Taking Finance Company, treat the DTFC Regulations as subordinate to the Finance Companies Control Law rather than as a self-contained rulebook. The Regulations expressly operate 'subject to' the Law (Royal Decree M/51) and its Implementing Regulation, and paragraph 3 stacks the Law, general FC regulations, and DTFC-specific prudential rules cumulatively. You should conclude that a DTFC must comply with all three layers at once, and that any DTFC provision cannot be read to override the primary Law.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / para 2-3):** "Subject to the provisions of Finance Companies Control law, promulgated by Royal Decree No. M/51... these Rules determine the requirements of exercising deposit-taking activity"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / para 3):** "DTFCs are also required to comply with the Finance Companies Control Law, SAMA regulations for Finance Companies (FCs), and other relevant laws and regulations"

### [[Liquidity Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a DTFC's prudential obligations, treat the Chapter 5 liquidity requirements as binding minimums embedded within the DTFC Regulations, not optional guidance. Paragraph 4 of the introduction ties deposit-taking authorization to 'observing liquidity ratios,' and Chapter 5 sets the concrete thresholds — 20% liquid assets, 4% statutory deposit with SAMA, and a 15x deposit-to-capital cap. You should check compliance against these specific figures and note the monthly Appendix B reporting deadline (15th business day) as a hard obligation triggering remedial action within one month if the leverage limit is breached.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / para 4):** "grant loans, credits and advances out of such deposits while observing liquidity ratios with regard to its liquid assets vis-a-vis total deposit liabilities"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 10 / Chapter 5, paras 29-31):** "Every DTFC shall maintain a minimum holding of liquid assets of twenty per cent (20%) of all its deposit liabilities... a statutory deposits of a sum not less than 4%"
