# APR Calculator

## Connections

### [[Rules Governing Calculation of APR]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a finance provider computes APR correctly, treat the SAMA Excel APR Calculator as the mandatory reference tool within the Rules, not an optional aid — Article 4 requires providers to 'utilize the Excel based calculator issued by SAMA for the purpose of implementing the Rules,' and any automated in-house tool must be reconciled against it. This means the Calculator is subordinate to and embedded within the Rules; the Rules set the method (Article 6 net present value formula) and the Calculator operationalises it. For compliance, conclude that a provider cannot demonstrate APR compliance by pointing only to its own tool without verifying results against SAMA's Calculator.
