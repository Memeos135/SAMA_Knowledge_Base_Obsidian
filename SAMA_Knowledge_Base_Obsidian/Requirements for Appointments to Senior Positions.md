# Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions

## Connections

### [[Fit and Proper Criteria]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating a senior appointment at a SAMA-supervised entity, understand that the fit-and-proper criteria are the substantive test the Requirements impose, not an optional benchmark: the document expressly obliges the Financial Institution to establish policies ensuring candidates 'meet and continue to meet the fitness and propriety criteria' throughout their tenure. The criteria (integrity, competence and capability, financial capability, independence) are defined within the same instrument, so they bind both positions requiring SAMA non-objection and positions that do not. You should conclude that a firm's own assessment must apply these minimum criteria continuously, and that SAMA can cancel a non-objection if a holder later fails them.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Chapter 3 clause 12):** "establish policies, standards and procedures for nomination to Senior Positions to ensure that all candidates ... meet and continue to meet the fitness and propriety criteria."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7):** "Competence and capability: The candidate must have the necessary academic qualification, or adequate experience, skills to perform his/her role effectively."

The Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions appear to be that referenced regime, since its defined 'Financial Institution' scope expressly includes finance companies. This link is a lead: read the DTFC senior-manager pre-approval duty as sitting within the senior-positions non-objection framework, but verify that the cited 'fit and proper regulations' are this specific instrument (and confirm the finance-company appendix) before relying on it.

### [[Senior Positions Requiring SAMA Non-objection]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
