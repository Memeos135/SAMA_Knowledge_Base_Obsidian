# Repurchase Agreements

**Cohesion:** 0.20 - loosely connected
**Members:** 11 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA's repo-market conduct framework governing repurchase and reverse-repurchase transactions in KSA, including documentation, eligible collateral, settlement and Shari'ah requirements. Relevant to legal/contractual compliance for banks and market participants transacting repos.

## How members connect

- 'SAMA Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements' is the governing instrument that defines the Repo/Reverse Repo transaction and references market infrastructure (Tadawul, SARIE settlement).
- The Guidelines mandate documentation via a Master Repo Agreement (MRA), with Confirmation as the trade-level record and GMRA as the internationally recognized MRA equivalent.
- Repo transactions must reference Eligible Security (held via a Custodian), scoping permitted collateral.
- Shari'ah Compliance is cross-referenced at the MRA level, flagging the parallel Islamic-finance requirement for repo structuring.
