# Banking Control Law Article 9

## Connections

### [[Related Parties Rules for Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the legal enforceability of the Related Parties Rules, treat them as subordinate implementing rules that operationalize a primary statutory obligation rather than as free-standing SAMA policy. The Rules are issued under the Banking Control Law and expressly 'reinforce and provide further guidance to Article 9,' and Section 6 confirms Article 9 is the source of the substantive prohibition on granting unsecured loans, facilities or guarantees to specified parties. For a compliance decision this means a bank cannot satisfy the security requirement by reading the Rules alone; the binding obligation traces to Article 9 of the Banking Control Law, and any dispute over scope of 'parties specified in the law' must be resolved against that statute.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10 / Section 6):** "Article 9 of the Banking Control Law requires banks not to grant, without security, a loan or credit facilities, or issue a guarantee or incur any other financial liability in respect to parties specified in the law."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Section 1):** "These rules define related parties... and reinforce and provide further guidance to Article 9 of the Banking Control Law."
