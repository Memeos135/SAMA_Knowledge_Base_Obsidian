# Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing

## Connections

### [[AMLCTF Guide|AML/CTF Guide]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping AML/CTF obligations, do not stop at money-laundering exposure — the Guide equally implements the terrorism-financing regime, which is a distinct offence with distinct typologies. The Guide names the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing (Royal Decree No. M/21) alongside the AML Law as its legal basis, and its monitoring and reporting duties expressly invoke articles of that Law (e.g. Article 69 for ongoing monitoring). The text stresses TF transactions differ from ML — small transfers and currency exchange can finance terrorism — so conclude that TF risk must be assessed on its own footing and cannot be assumed subsumed under ML controls; check whether obligations you rely on cite the CTF Law's articles.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14):** "Small transactions, including bank transfers and currency exchange, can be used to finance terrorist activities."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Chapter II Purpose):** "the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing, issued by Royal Decree No. (M/21) dated 12/02/1439H, and its Implementing Regulations"
