# Bank Investment Rules

**Cohesion:** 0.16 - loosely connected
**Members:** 14 nodes

## Why this community

Statutory supervision of banks' investment activity and money-changing business under the Banking Control Law and Central Bank Law, with cross-cutting AML/CTF, licensing, customer protection and outsourcing obligations.

## How members connect

- Banks Investment Rules cite the Banking Control Law and Central Bank Law as enabling authority and reference investment governance, risk management/monitoring, approval controls and investment policy as its operative requirements.
- Rules Regulating Money Changing Business form a parallel licensed-activity regime, referencing the defined term Money Changer, licensing procedures, customer protection and third-party outsourcing controls.
- AML/CTF is the shared cross-regime obligation: both the investment risk-monitoring provisions and the money-changing rules link to Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing, which cites the Anti-Money Laundering Law as its statutory base.
