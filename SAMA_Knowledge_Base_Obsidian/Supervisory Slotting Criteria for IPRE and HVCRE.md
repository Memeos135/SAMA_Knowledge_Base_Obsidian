# Supervisory Slotting Criteria for IPRE and HVCRE

## Connections

### [[High-Volatility Commercial Real Estate (HVCRE)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When slotting high-volatility commercial real estate (HVCRE) exposures, do not look for a separate HVCRE criteria table: paragraph 13.5 states the slotting criteria for HVCRE are the same as those for IPRE (para 13.14), even though HVCRE carries its own risk-weight table (Table 21) reflecting higher asset correlation. So the assessment factors are shared with IPRE while the resulting risk weights differ. Conclude that for HVCRE you apply the IPRE criteria table to place exposures into categories, then read across to the HVCRE-specific risk weights — and confirm the exposure meets the HVCRE definition (para 10.15) rather than being ordinary IPRE, since that classification drives which risk-weight table applies.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 139 / Art 13.5):** "The slotting criteria on which this mapping must be based are the same as those for IPRE, as provided in paragraph 13.14"
