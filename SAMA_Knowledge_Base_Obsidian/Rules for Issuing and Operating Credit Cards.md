- **What this link tells you:** When assessing how a lender may pursue overdue credit-card balances from individual consumers, read the Credit Card Rules together with the Collection Regulations and Procedures for Individual Customers. Both are SAMA instructions issued under the same statutory authority and apply to banks and finance companies, and the Credit Card Rules govern the payment/late-payment relationship ('المبلغ المستحق', 'رسوم السداد المتأخر') while the Collection Regulations govern conduct toward defaulting natural-person customers and their guarantors ('العميل', 'الكفيل', 'التعثر').
