# Profit Sharing Investment Accounts Rules

## Connections

### [[LCR and NSFR Prudential Requirements for PSIA]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a bank on how PSIA funding affects its prudential position, treat this link as the operative cross-reference from the PSIA Rules into SAMA's separate capital and liquidity regimes. The Rules do not set the LCR/NSFR treatment themselves; they command banks to apply SAMA's existing liquidity requirements to PSIA funds and, critically, prohibit PSIA funds and PER/IRR reserves from counting toward regulatory capital. You would therefore conclude that the numerical treatment must be sourced from the LCR/NSFR and capital instruments, and confirm the exclusion of PSIA/PER/IRR from own funds is applied.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Section 8 Prudential Requirements):** "Banks must follow SAMA's prudential requirements pertaining to regulatory capital for the calculation of risk weights for assets funded by PSIAs"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Section 8):** "ensure appropriate calculation of PSIA funds in their Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) and Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR) as per SAMA's liquidity requirements"

### [[Profit Sharing Investment Account (PSIA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether an arrangement falls within these Rules at all, start from the defined term PSIA, because the document's obligations attach only to accounts meeting that definition. The Rules define a PSIA as a Shari'ah-compliant, bank-managed account under a management agreement sharing profits in a specified ratio with the IAH bearing loss not caused by the bank's fault; SAMA may also direct that a given mudarabah/musharakah/wakalah arrangement be treated, or not treated, as a PSIA. You would conclude scope is definition-driven and confirm each product against the definition rather than its label.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5):** "A Profit Sharing Investment Account (or PSIA): an account that satisfies the following conditions... managed by a bank in accordance with Shari'ah principles"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Section 8):** "SAMA may, by way of prudential assessment, direct a bank to treat, or not to treat, an arrangement between the bank and a client... to be a PSIA"
