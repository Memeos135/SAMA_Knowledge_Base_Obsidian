# SAMA General Guidance concerning Amended LCR

## Connections

### [[Basel III The Liquidity Coverage Ratio and Liquidity Monitoring Tools Jan 2013|Basel III: The Liquidity Coverage Ratio and Liquidity Monitoring Tools Jan 2013]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When interpreting SAMA's Amended LCR return, treat the SAMA guidance as the binding local instrument that deliberately mirrors the BCBS January 2013 standard paragraph-by-paragraph. SAMA states its paras are adopted from the BCBS document and the Prudential returns carry a 'Paragraph in document' column keyed to it, so where SAMA is silent or ambiguous, the corresponding BCBS paragraph is the authoritative reference source. Practically, verify each requirement against both texts, but recognise national discretions (e.g. Level 2B non-use, no deposit-insurance run-off) override the BCBS default for KSA banks.

### [[Liquidity Coverage Ratio]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For scoping the LCR reporting obligation, read SAMA's General Guidance as the instrument that operationalises the Liquidity Coverage Ratio requirement itself, not merely mentions it. The guidance implements the Amended LCR package through this circular and sets the enforceable submission cadence (monthly returns due 30 days after month-end for all banks except foreign branches). Conclude that the LCR ratio and the guidance's reporting rules must be read together to determine who files, when, and against which national-discretion parameters.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 11 / section 2):** "all Banks (except foreign bank's branches) will be expected to provide their returns to SAMA on a monthly basis to be due 30 days following each month end."
