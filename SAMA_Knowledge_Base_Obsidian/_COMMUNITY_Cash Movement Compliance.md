# Cash Movement Compliance

**Cohesion:** 0.40 - moderately connected
**Members:** 5 nodes

## Why this community

Cross-border and domestic cash/precious-metals movement controls — an AML/CTF-adjacent regime governing physical transport and declaration of currency and precious metals, sitting under primary law.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Cash and Precious Metals Transport Law and Banking Control Law are the primary instruments; the circulars (general Cash Movement Compliance, and the King Fahd Causeway entry/exit circular) implement/reference them.
- The Causeway circular is a location-specific application of the Transport Law's declaration obligations.
- Compliance consequence: obligations flow from statute to circular — banks and transporters must read the circulars as operational instructions grounded in enforceable declaration/transport duties, not standalone rules.
