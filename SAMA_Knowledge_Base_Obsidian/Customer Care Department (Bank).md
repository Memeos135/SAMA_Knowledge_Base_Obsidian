# Customer Care Department (Bank)

### [[Complaints Handling Unit]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping who owns complaint handling in a licensed bank, note that this circular folds the former standalone complaints-unit rules into the broader Customer Care Department — the covering letter states it replaces the earlier controls on establishing complaints units, and the new department must contain a complaints-handling unit as one of its three mandatory units. Because the Department reports directly to the bank's most senior executive and must not be administratively tied to any other bank department, complaint handling gains organizational independence. Conclude that reliance on the superseded complaints-unit circular is misplaced and that complaints handling must now sit within the independently-governed Customer Care Department.

### [[Financial Awareness and Education Unit]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a bank's obligations under SAMA's Customer Care Department rules, treat the Financial Awareness and Education Unit as a mandatory sub-component of that department, not a discretionary add-on. The instrument requires the Department to comprise at least three units — complaints handling, quality/performance analysis, and financial awareness and education — reflecting the OECD High-Level Principles on Financial Consumer Protection that this circular implements. For compliance you should conclude that failing to establish and resource the awareness unit is itself a breach of the department-structuring requirement, and that its consumer-education function is a supervised obligation.

### [[Quality and Performance Analysis Unit]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
