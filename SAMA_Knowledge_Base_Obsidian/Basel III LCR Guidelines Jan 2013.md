# Basel III LCR Guidelines Jan 2013

## Connections

### [[Liquidity Coverage Ratio]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When computing the LCR numerator, rely on the Basel III LCR Guidelines Jan 2013 as the source defining HQLA categories, caps and haircuts, but apply them as filtered by SAMA's local overrides. The guidance sets the phase-in (60% in 2015 rising to 100% by 2019), permits use of HQLA below the minimum during stress subject to SAMA assessment, and expressly disapplies BCBS parameters that do not fit KSA (e.g. deposit-insurance run-off rates are invalid because Saudi Arabia has no effective deposit insurance scheme). Conclude that BCBS supplies the calculation architecture while SAMA fixes the jurisdiction-specific parameters that actually bind.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 25 / para 70):** "a few parameters are to be determined by supervisory authorities at the national level ... any run-off rate subject to deposit insurance is not valid for KSA banks."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 16):** "(Refer to footnotes 19 and 20 of Basel III: The Liquidity Coverage Ratio and liquidity risk monitoring tools — Jan 2013)"
