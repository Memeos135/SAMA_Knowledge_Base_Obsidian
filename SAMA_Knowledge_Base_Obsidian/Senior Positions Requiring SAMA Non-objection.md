# Senior Positions Requiring SAMA Non-objection

## Connections

### [[Director of Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining which appointments need SAMA's prior written non-objection, note that the AML/CTF Director is expressly enumerated as a Senior Position in the sector appendices (banks and insurers), so it falls squarely within the non-objection regime — the appointment cannot be assumed or announced before SAMA clears it. Because Article 7 defines Senior Positions by reference to those appendices, listing the 'Director of Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing' has the binding consequence of importing the fit-and-proper criteria, submission timelines, and background-check powers to that role. Additionally check the Saudization restriction: the AML/CTF Director is among positions limited to Saudi candidates, so nationality is a gating factor before any non-objection request.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Art 7):** "The Financial Institution shall obtain SAMA’s written non-objection ... indicated in the appendices especially prepared for each Financial Institution"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11, Appendix (1)):** "6. Director of Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing"

### [[Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** To determine whether a given appointment needs SAMA's prior written non-objection, use the 'Senior Positions requiring SAMA non-objection' concept as the gating scope of the Requirements: Chapter 3 lists these positions in entity-specific appendices, and candidates 'shall not assume their responsibilities ... prior to obtaining SAMA's written non-objection.' The document sets the submission timelines (30 business days for board nominations, 20 for senior management) and an exemption for interim appointments not exceeding 20 business days. You should conclude the trigger is position-based per your entity's appendix, and confirm which appendix applies before treating any appointment as outside the non-objection regime.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 clause 8):** "Candidates for Senior Positions shall not assume their responsibilities ... prior to obtaining SAMA's written non-objection."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Chapter 3 clause 7):** "The Financial Institution shall obtain SAMA's written non-objection on the nomination of the members of the board of directors, as well as the appointment ... of senior management positions indicated in the appendices."

### [[Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority (SAMA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing who controls senior appointments in a SAMA-supervised institution, recognise that SAMA is the sole authority granting (and able to cancel) the written non-objection that gates every Senior Position — the requirement runs to SAMA, not to any other regulator. The Requirements define SAMA and then make its non-objection the operative precondition under Article 7, with power under later articles to run background checks and revoke a granted non-objection for concealment, neglect, or misreporting. For your decision this means the institution's own board approval is insufficient: no listed candidate may assume the role, exercise it, or be announced until SAMA's written non-objection is in hand, and that clearance remains revocable.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Art 8):** "Candidates for Senior Positions shall not assume their responsibilities ... prior to obtaining SAMA’s written non-objection."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3, Ch.1):** "SAMA — The Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority."
