# Compliance Principles for Finance and Real Estate Refinance Companies

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping a finance company's compliance obligations, treat the Compliance Principles as an instrument issued under the Finance Companies Control Law that also aggregates the wider instruction set the company must observe. The Principles adopt the Law's defined terms and list the corpus a compliance unit must monitor — AML/CTF Law, BNPL Rules, Consumer Protection Principles, Debt Collection Regulations and more — signalling cross-regime reach beyond the Law itself. For scoping, conclude that compliance monitoring cannot be limited to the Finance Companies Control Law; the Principles bind licensed finance/refinance companies mandatorily and apply to finance support and lease-registration companies only as guidance until SAMA enforces them.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Scope):** "The provisions of these Principles shall apply to finance and real estate refinance companies... shall serve as a guide to finance support companies"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Art 1):** "Law: Finance Companies Control Law. Regulations: Implementing Regulations of this Law."

### [[Internal Audit Principles for Finance and Real Estate Refinance Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping the governance/control obligations of a finance or real estate refinance company, treat the Internal Audit Principles and Compliance Principles as a linked control framework rather than isolated documents. Both share the same defined subjects ('Company', 'Board', 'Executive Management', 'Audit Committee') and the same scope (binding on finance and real estate refinance companies, advisory-only for finance support and lease-registration companies), and the Compliance Principles list SAMA instructions as a body the Compliance Unit must monitor — including internal-audit-adjacent duties. Practically, you should reconcile the two 'second-line'/'third-line' functions when scoping responsibilities: the Compliance Unit reports to the Audit Committee and reviews specific departments, while Internal Audit provides independent assurance over the whole control system, so an obligation satisfied for one does not discharge the other.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Scope of Application (a)):** "The provisions of these Principles shall apply to finance and real estate refinance companies."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / Scope of Application 3.1):** "The provisions of these Principles shall apply to finance companies and real estate refinance companies."
