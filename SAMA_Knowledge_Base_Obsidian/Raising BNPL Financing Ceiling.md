# Raising BNPL Financing Ceiling

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the BNPL per-client financing ceiling, note that this circular amends a limit set in the BNPL Regulating Rules — not the Implementing Regulations text quoted here — so the link between the two documents appears indirect (both sit under the Finance Companies Control Law and share defined terms such as 'شركة الدفع الآجل'/BNPL company). The circular raises the total outstanding financing per individual client from SAR 5,000 to SAR 10,000, expressly preserving responsible-lending burden ratios. Treat this circular as the current ceiling for BNPL exposure per client, but verify the amended BNPL Rules directly rather than relying on the Implementing Regulations as the source of the numeric limit.
