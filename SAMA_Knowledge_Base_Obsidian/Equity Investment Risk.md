# Equity Investment Risk

## Connections

### [[Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping RMF obligations for a bank holding Mudarabah or Musharakah investments, note that Equity Investment Risk is a distinct enumerated category with its own Principle (7.0), not a generic market exposure. The framework's introduction lists it among the risks unique to Shari'ah-compliant banking, and Section 11 imposes specific duties: defining investment objectives/risk tolerance, maintaining review policies for acquiring/holding/exiting profit-sharing investments, and monitoring risk transformation across investment lifecycles. You would conclude that profit-sharing partnership exposures trigger dedicated obligations under the RMF that cannot be discharged solely through generic credit or market risk controls.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / Section 11.1 (Principle 7.0)):** "Banks must have in place appropriate strategies, risk management and reporting processes in respect of the risk characteristics of equity investments, including Mudarabah and Musharakah investments."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Introduction):** "various types of risks including, but not limited to... Equity Investment Risk"
