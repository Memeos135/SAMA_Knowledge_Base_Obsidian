# Group of Connected Counterparties

## Connections

### [[Maximum Exposure Limits]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying an exposure limit, you must first test whether separate counterparties are in fact a single risk, because the limit applies to a 'Group of Connected Counterparties' collectively, not just to each legal person. Connection is triggered by a control relationship, economic interdependence, or other single-risk indicators, and identification is mandatory once aggregate exposures exceed 5% of eligible capital; exposures within an in-scope group are aggregated and subjected to the limit and reporting. Conclude that you cannot lawfully fragment exposures across affiliated or economically dependent parties to stay under 15%/25%, though the Rules allow a bank to rebut connection to SAMA in exceptional, documented cases.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8):** "Two or more natural or legal persons shall be deemed a group of connected counterparties if at least one of the following criteria is satisfied: a. The existence of a control relationship; or b. The existence of Economic interdependence."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 18 / sec 6.ii):** "banks shall measure, monitor, and report all exposures net of amounts reduced by eligible CRM techniques"
