# Know Your Customer Principle

## Connections

### [[Customer Due Diligence Measures]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a remittance center's AML/CFT customer obligations, treat KYC and CDD as two limbs of one continuous requirement rather than separable tasks, because the Guide lists them together as mandatory conditions for accepting and maintaining a customer relationship. KYC governs identity verification at membership creation while CDD extends to ongoing monitoring, beneficial-owner identification, and enhanced measures for high-risk customers and PEPs. For a compliance decision you would conclude that satisfying one does not discharge the other — verification at onboarding plus continuous due diligence are both required, and the text appears to make failure to verify identity or sources a ground for terminating the relationship, though you should confirm the exact article wording before relying on it.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Section 9):** "'Know Your Customer (KYC)' requirements, and Customer Due Diligence (CDD) procedures for customers of various types and categories"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / Section 9.6):** "remittance centers apply the essential due diligence procedures to all permanent and temporary customers, including real beneficiaries, and that these procedures be continuous"
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED; the two concepts are listed together as related requirements but the Guide does not formally define one in terms of the other. Verify section text before relying.

### [[Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When establishing a remittance membership, treat KYC as a gating condition tied to membership opening, not an optional check: the Guide requires official-ID capture, rejection of fictitious/anonymous names, and manager approval only after verifying customer information against the volume and nature of activity, and mandates termination where sources or identity cannot be verified. KYC here sits alongside CDD and AML/CFT rules issued by SAMA as an integrated acceptance standard. A reader should conclude that a membership cannot lawfully be opened or maintained without documented KYC, and that failure to verify identity or source is an express ground to end the relationship.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 11 / Section 9.5):** "The requirements of KYC, AML/CFT rules issued by SAMA and other relevant regulations and instructions shall be applied."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Section 9):** "'Know Your Customer (KYC)' requirements, and Customer Due Diligence (CDD) procedures for customers of various types and categories"
