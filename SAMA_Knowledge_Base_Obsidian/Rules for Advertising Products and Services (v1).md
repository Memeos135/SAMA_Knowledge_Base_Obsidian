# Rules for Advertising Products and Services (v1)

### [[Compliance Department Advertising Review]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assigning responsibility for advertising controls at a SAMA-supervised financial institution, treat Articles 8–14 as the operative source of the compliance obligation rather than a standalone 'compliance department' function. The Rules impose institution-level duties — vetting content against reputational/fair-competition limits (Art 8), consent for use of customer data (Art 9), suitability restrictions (Art 10), and an obligation to adopt an advertising policy aligned with SAMA instructions (Art 14) — and hold the institution liable even where a licensed third party runs the ad (Art 13). Conclude that advertising-review accountability flows from these enforceable articles and cannot be delegated away; check that the institution's advertising policy under Art 14 actually maps to each requirement, since the institution bears the liability.

### [[Saudi Central Bank Law]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When testing whether these advertising Rules are binding and what they can override, trace their authority to the Saudi Central Bank Law (Royal Decree M/36). The Rules are secondary instruments issued under Article 4 of that Law, which empowers SAMA to issue 'regulations and instructions for the financial institutions' and to develop 'procedures that would protect the customers.' Conclude that the Rules carry SAMA's full supervisory force over all licensed financial institutions but remain subordinate to and must be read consistently with the parent Law and the other sector laws they list; do not treat the Rules as capable of displacing a higher statutory requirement.
