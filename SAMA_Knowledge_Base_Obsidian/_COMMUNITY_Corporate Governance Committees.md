# Corporate Governance Committees

**Cohesion:** 0.29 - loosely connected
**Members:** 8 nodes

## Why this community

Corporate governance regime for SAMA-supervised financial institutions: allocation of authority and oversight duties among the board, its committees, and executive management, anchored in the Saudi Central Bank Law.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Saudi Central Bank Law is the enabling statute the Key Principles of Governance cite as their legal basis, making the governance principles a subordinate implementing framework.
- Obligation source: the Key Principles reference the Board of Directors and Executive Management as the accountable bodies on which governance duties are imposed.
- Committee structure: the Board delegates defined oversight functions to the Audit, Nomination and Remuneration, Risk, and Executive Committees — each a board-level control body with distinct mandates.
- Decision use: identifies who is legally responsible for a given governance failing (board vs. executive vs. specific committee) when assessing accountability.
