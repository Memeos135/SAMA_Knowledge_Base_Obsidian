# Third Party Cyber Security

## Connections

### [[Cloud Computing Cyber Security]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a Member Organization's use of cloud services, treat cloud computing security as a specific instance of third-party cyber security rather than a standalone regime, because cloud providers are external parties handling the organization's information assets. Both sit within the SAMA Framework's control domains and the board retains ultimate responsibility for cyber security across all parties involved. A compliance reviewer should therefore expect cloud arrangements to satisfy the third-party control expectations (contractual cyber security obligations, risk assessment, oversight) and should not treat a cloud engagement as exempt from third-party due diligence.

### [[Outsourcing Cyber Security]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing an outsourcing arrangement, treat its cyber security obligations as part of the third-party control set, since an outsourced service provider is an external party accessing or processing the Member Organization's information assets. The Framework's risk-response controls allow transferring risk but require that any accepted residual risk 'does not contradict SAMA regulations,' so outsourcing does not offload the organization's own accountability. A reviewer should conclude that outsourcing and third-party controls are read together — outsourced providers must meet the same third-party cyber security expectations, and the board remains ultimately responsible.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / 3.1.4):** "all parties involved in cyber security should understand and take their role and responsibilities"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 21 / control considerations):** "the accepted cyber security risk does not contradict SAMA regulations"
- **Caveat:** Specific outsourcing-to-third-party cross-reference not shown verbatim in provided excerpts; treat as structurally linked and confirm against the Framework's outsourcing and third-party sections.

### [[SAMA Cyber Security Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a Member Organization's exposure, do not stop at its own systems: the Framework extends cyber security obligations to third-party service providers, so vendor arrangements fall within the same compliance perimeter. The Framework requires that providers used for disposal, transport and storage comply with the applicable standards and that their effectiveness be periodically measured, and it mandates compliance with contractual obligations. Conclude that outsourcing does not transfer the MO's accountability; supervisory review will look at whether third-party controls meet Framework standards.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 31):** "third party service providers ... comply with the secure disposal standard and procedure and the effectiveness is periodically measured and evaluated."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 15 / 4.f):** "compliance with regulatory and contractual obligations are being met;"
