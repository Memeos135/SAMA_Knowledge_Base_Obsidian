# Operational Risk Manager

## Connections

### [[Operational Risk Management Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating a bank's operational-risk governance structure against this guidance, treat the Operational Risk Management Committee and the Operational Risk Manager as a defined reporting hierarchy, not interchangeable functions. The glossary defines the Committee as 'reporting directly to the Operational Risk Manager,' and the Manager as the senior person responsible for developing and implementing the plan and reporting to the Managing Director/General Manager. The two also jointly develop the Operational Risk Management Plan for Board approval, so you would check that both roles exist, that the reporting lines run Committee → Manager → MD/GM, and that plan ownership is not left ambiguous.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 51 / Glossary):** "Operational Risk Manager - The senior manager ... responsible for the development of the bank's Operational Risk Management Plan ... should report directly to the managing Director/General Manager"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 51 / Glossary):** "Operational Risk Management Committee - An operational committee of the bank reporting directly to the Operational Risk Manager"
