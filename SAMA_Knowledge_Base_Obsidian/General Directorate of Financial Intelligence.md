# General Directorate of Financial Intelligence

## Connections

### [[Preventive Measures (FIs, DNFBPs, NPOs)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining where and how FIs, DNFBPs and NPOs must report TF suspicions, connect the Preventive Measures duty (Art 70) to the General Directorate of Financial Intelligence as the mandated recipient and analytical body (Arts 76–77). Article 70 requires prompt, direct STR filing to the Directorate and response to its further information requests; Article 77 lets the Directorate demand additional information but, where no report was filed or the request is unrelated, requires it to route through the supervisory authority. For a compliance decision, conclude that STRs go directly to the Directorate, but the tipping-off prohibition (Art 71) and the confidentiality regime constrain what may be disclosed to the customer about that reporting.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16 / Art 76):** "The Directorate – as a national central agency... shall undertake receiving suspicious transaction reports... to analyze such reports and information, and to disseminate the results..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 15 / Art 70):** "Promptly and directly, Report such transaction to the General Directorate of Financial Intelligence; and provide a detailed report including all available data..."
