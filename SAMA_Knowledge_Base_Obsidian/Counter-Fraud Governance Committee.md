# Counter-Fraud Governance Committee

## Connections

### [[Governance Domain]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping accountability for the Counter-Fraud Programme, the Counter-Fraud Governance Committee (CFGC) is the specific body the Governance domain requires each Member Organisation to establish, so governance obligations cannot be satisfied without it. The Framework mandates a CFGC headed by an Executive Committee member and populated at minimum by the Head of Counter-Fraud, CRO, COO and Head of Digital, and it is the body that approves the CFGC charter and endorses the Strategy, Policy, Fraud Risk Assessment output and Fraud Risk Appetite. You would conclude that the CFGC's composition and its endorsement of these documents are checkable evidence of governance compliance, and their absence is a governance-domain deficiency.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / 3.1):** "Member Organisations should establish and maintain a dedicated Counter-Fraud Governance Committee (CFGC)... headed by a member of the Executive Committee."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 14 / 3):** "a Governance Structure with associated internal and external reporting... to monitor and oversee fraud risk management."

### [[Head of Counter-Fraud]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When allocating governance accountability under SAMA_EN_2217, read the Counter-Fraud Governance Committee (CFGC) and the Head of Counter-Fraud as distinct but linked roles in one approval chain: the Head develops the Strategy, Policy, Fraud Risk Assessment and Risk Appetite, while these are approved by the CFGC and then endorsed at board/senior level. This means the same person cannot both draft and finally approve those instruments — the CFGC provides the independent challenge and sign-off. A reviewer should confirm the CFGC charter and the Head's mandate are separately documented and that the endorsement sequence (Head develops → CFGC approves → endorsed) is respected.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18):** "Endorsing (after being approved by the CFGC): ... The Counter-Fraud Strategy ... Policy ... output of the Fraud Risk Assessment ... Fraud Risk Appetite."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 18):** "The Head of Counter-Fraud should be accountable for: Developing, implementing, and maintaining: Counter-Fraud Strategy ... Policy ... Fraud Risk Assessment ... Fraud Risk Appetite."
