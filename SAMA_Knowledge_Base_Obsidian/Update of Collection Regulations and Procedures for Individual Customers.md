# Update of Collection Regulations and Procedures for Individual Customers

### [[Rules for Issuing and Operating Credit Cards]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing how a lender may pursue overdue credit-card balances from individual consumers, read the Credit Card Rules together with the Collection Regulations and Procedures for Individual Customers. Both are SAMA instructions issued under the same statutory authority and apply to banks and finance companies, and the Credit Card Rules govern the payment/late-payment relationship ('المبلغ المستحق', 'رسوم السداد المتأخر') while the Collection Regulations govern conduct toward defaulting natural-person customers and their guarantors ('العميل', 'الكفيل', 'التعثر'). Practically, a card issuer's late-payment and recovery activity must satisfy the collection-conduct minimums (documented contact, guarantor treatment, rescheduling for defaulters) in addition to the card-specific fee and statement rules — one does not override the other.
