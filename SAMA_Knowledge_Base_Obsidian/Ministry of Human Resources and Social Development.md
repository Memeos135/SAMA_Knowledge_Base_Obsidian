# Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development

## Connections

### [[Private Associations Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When onboarding a private association, treat MHRSD licensing and approval as a gating precondition, not merely a background fact. The rule scopes 'private associations' to those licensed by MHRSD, and sub-accounts for branches/activities and surplus-fund investment require MHRSD approval (and approved association rules) before the bank may proceed. Conclude that the bank must obtain and review the MHRSD licence and relevant MHRSD approvals as documentary conditions of opening and operating the account and its sub-accounts, and cannot open activity/branch sub-accounts absent that approval.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 61 / 300.1.5.2):** "private associations licensed by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) to carry out different activities, including their branches"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 63):** "sub-accounts may be opened under the main account upon obtaining the approval of the MHRSD and the supervisory authority for establishing such a branch"

### [[Private Foundations Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what documents a bank must obtain before opening an account for a private association or foundation, treat the MHRSD license as the gating credential — the account-opening rules for private foundations expressly condition acceptance on a license issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development plus a board decision approving the business and the authorized signatories. This link tells you the eligibility test is not internal to the bank but tied to an external licensing authority. You would conclude that absent a valid MHRSD license (and board minutes on signatories), the account cannot be opened under this section.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46 / Rule 300.1.5.2):** "a copy of the license issued by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, a copy of the decision of the board of directors of the society/foundation ... approving the establishment of the business"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 61 / Rule 300.1.5.2):** "private associations licensed by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) to carry out different activities, including their branches"
