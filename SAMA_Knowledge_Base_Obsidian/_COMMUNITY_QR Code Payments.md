## Why this community

Payment-acceptance pricing and QR-based instruments within the merchant payments/POS-e-commerce space.

## How members connect

- The QR Code Payments Development Project and the Unified Pricing Model for POS and E-commerce are conceptually linked as parts of SAMA's merchant-acceptance framework.
- The link is thematic rather than a strict hierarchy — pricing rules for POS/e-commerce inform the economics of QR acceptance channels.
- Decision consequence: QR payment offerings fall within the same merchant-acquiring pricing discipline as POS and e-commerce.
