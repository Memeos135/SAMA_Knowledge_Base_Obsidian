# Permanent Committee for Combating Terrorism and its Financing

## Connections

### [[Law on Combating Financing of Terrorism]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping who implements UN Security Council TFS obligations in KSA, identify the Permanent Committee for Combating Terrorism and its Financing as the body designated by the CTF Law to receive and operationalize such resolutions. Article 75 vests the Committee with receiving requests for implementation of UNSCRs and putting in place mechanisms, with those mechanisms issued by decision of the President of State Security. For a sanctions/TFS compliance decision, conclude that the primary listing/implementation mechanics flow from this Committee's issuances — verify the current mechanism it has set rather than relying on the Law's text alone, since the Law delegates the detail.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16 / Art 75):** "The Committee shall put in place, and update, mechanisms... The mechanisms shall be issued pursuant to a decision by the President of State Security."
