# Financial Market Infrastructure Principles

## Connections

### [[Payment Systems Classification]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a payment system operator's obligations, note that the FMI Principles are incorporated by reference as a compliance standard whose applicability depends on the system's classification. Article 106 requires an unclassified payment system operator to conduct periodic self-assessment against the FMI Principles per its licence, and the Regulation separately structures 'classification of important payment systems' (Part Nine, Chapter One) and 'FMI principles requirements' (Chapter Two). You would conclude that classification (important vs. unclassified) drives which FMI Principle obligations and capital/resource requirements apply, so verify the operator's classification before determining the applicable standard.
