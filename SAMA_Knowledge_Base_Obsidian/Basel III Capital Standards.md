# Basel III Capital Standards

## Connections

### [[Countercyclical Capital Buffer (CCyB)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When calculating the countercyclical capital buffer, treat SAMA's circular as the operative KSA requirement and Basel III as the conceptual origin it invokes. The circular records that BCBS 'released the Basel III capital standards, which contained detailed information about CCyB' and then sets the enforceable local parameters — a 0%–2.5% range of total RWA, weighted-average calculation across exposure jurisdictions, mandatory use from 1 January 2016, and SAMA's power to set a more prudent rate. Conclude that banks compute CCyB per the SAMA circular's specific rules (using BCBS-published country rates), while Basel III provides the framework rationale, not the direct compliance yardstick.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1):** "In 2010, the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS) released the Basel III capital standards, which contained detailed information about CCyB."
