# SAMA Rules on Outsourcing

## Connections

### [[Regulations for Consumer Financing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a Creditor delegates any part of its Consumer Financing Business to a service provider, do not treat outsourcing compliance as governed by these Regulations alone: the Regulations define Outsourcing and, in Article 15's obligations, expressly make it 'subject to the requirement to fully comply with SAMA Rules on Outsourcing issued vide 34720/BCS/424 dated 17/7/1429H.' This is a cross-reference to a separate binding SAMA instrument, and the definition confirms outsourcing captures onshore, overseas, intra-group and independent third-party arrangements alike. Conclude that a Creditor must satisfy both regimes concurrently, and check the referenced Outsourcing Rules directly for the substantive requirements, since this document only incorporates them by reference.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 19 / Art 15(11)):** "A Creditor engaging in Outsourcing any component of its Consumer Financing Business must comply with the Rules on Outsourcing issued by SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 (Definitions)):** "Outsourcing ... is subject to the requirement to fully comply with SAMA Rules on Outsourcing issued vide 34720/BCS/424 dated 17/7/1429H"
