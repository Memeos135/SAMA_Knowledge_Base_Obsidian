# Compliance Certification Requirement for Finance Company Compliance Managers

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a finance company's obligations regarding its compliance manager, treat the certification circular as a subordinate instrument grounded in the Finance Companies Control Law's Implementing Regulations: SAMA relies on Article 2 of those Regulations (its mandate to develop the sector, localise its functions and raise staff competence) to require compliance managers to hold the 'Compliance for the Finance Companies Sector' certificate. Because the circular derives its authority from the same parent Law, non-compliance is enforceable as a supervisory matter, not merely advisory. For a compliance decision, confirm certificate acquisition within one year of the circular or before requesting SAMA's no-objection for the appointment, and provide the certificate to SAMA.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_2125 Page 1):** "compliance managers in finance companies must obtain the 'Compliance for the Finance Companies Sector' certificate within a maximum of one year"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Ch.2 para 3):** "read in conjunction with the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations"
- **Caveat:** Node B source Arabic is OCR-garbled; clause_b is an English paraphrase of the circular's operative requirement.
