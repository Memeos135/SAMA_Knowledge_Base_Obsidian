# Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law

## Connections

### [[Real Estate Finance Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what governs a real estate finance entity, treat this Implementing Regulation as subordinate to and derivative of the Real Estate Finance Law: the Regulation expressly defines 'Law' as the Real Estate Finance Law and requires entities to comply with 'the Law, this Regulation, other related laws and regulations.' This establishes the law-then-implementing-regulation hierarchy, so any obligation in the Regulation must be read against the enabling Law and cannot override it. Conclude that compliance analysis starts with the Law's mandate and uses the Regulation for operational detail, checking the primary Law text where the Regulation is silent.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / Art 1):** "Law: Real Estate Finance Law. Regulation: Implementing Regulation of the Real Estate Finance Law."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Art 8(1)):** "A real estate finance entity shall comply with the requirements stipulated in the Law, this Regulation, other related laws and regulations..."

### [[Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are assessing who may conduct real estate refinance activity, note that this Regulation both creates and constrains the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company: it mandates PIF (or a wholly-owned entity) to incorporate the named joint stock company, sets a minimum capital of five billion riyals, restricts the licence to refinance activity, and subjects it to SAMA supervision. The entity is therefore a statutorily-defined vehicle, not an ordinary market participant, and its share-ownership limits (PIF ≥51%, finance entities ≤30%) are hard-coded. Conclude that any transaction, share acquisition, or product by this Company must be checked against Articles 16–20 and the required SAMA no-objection letters.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9 / Art 16-17):** "PIF or any entity wholly owned by it shall...incorporate a joint stock company to be named 'The Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company'"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9-10 / Art 16-17):** "The capital of the real estate refinance company be not less than five billion riyals... shares of PIF...shall not, at any time, be less than 51 percent."
