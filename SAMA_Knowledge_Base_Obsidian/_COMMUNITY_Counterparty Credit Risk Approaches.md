# Counterparty Credit Risk Approaches

**Cohesion:** 0.17 - loosely connected
**Members:** 16 nodes

## Why this community

Counterparty credit risk and fund/equity exposure measurement: the hierarchy of approaches banks must apply to look through fund holdings, capitalise settlement risk, and observe the aggregate constraints under SAMA's Basel III adoption.

## How members connect

- Equity Investments in Funds sets a mandated cascade — Look-Through (LTA), then Mandate-Based (MBA), then Fall-Back (FBA) — determining how underlying exposures are risk-weighted; LTA/MBA reference the IRB Approach and carry Leverage Adjustment and CVA/CCR treatment.
- Unsettled Transactions/Failed Trades and its Capital Treatment split obligations by settlement mechanism: DvP versus Non-DvP (free deliveries), the latter drawing IRB-style treatment.
- Basel III Framework is the cited authority for CCR, Output Floor and Pillar 3; Output Floor constrains IRB-derived RWA and is disclosed via the Pillar 3 framework — a hierarchy linking measurement approach to aggregate floor and public disclosure.
