# Indirect Investment Fund Participation in Financing

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a finance company may take funding from investment funds, treat this circular as subordinate to, and authorised by, the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51). SAMA issues the circular "استناداً إلى الصلاحيات الممنوحة للمؤسسة بموجب نظام مراقبة شركات التمويل" and expressly permits certain fund activities (portfolio sales, loan participation agreements, fund investment) "دون الإخلال بما تضمنه نظام مراقبة شركات التمويل ولائحته التنفيذية." Conclude that these permitted arrangements do not override the parent Law/Implementing Regulation constraints on financing activity — the credit-granting decision must remain with the finance company, and the Law's licensing and prudential requirements still bind.
