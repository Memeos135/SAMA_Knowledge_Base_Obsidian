# Customer Protection Requirements

## Connections

### [[Rules Regulating Money Changing Business]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping a money changer's consumer-facing duties, treat the Customer Protection Requirements (Section 4, Art. 25) as the enforceable core of the Rules' stated purpose to 'protect the rights of Customers.' The provision imposes concrete, testable obligations — displaying customer-rights and exchange-rate boards, issuing receipts, resolving complaints within seven days with escalation to SAMA, and safeguarding customer information from disclosure to third parties without SAMA's prior non-objection. A reviewer should conclude that failure on any of these is a discrete violation, and note that Article 33's penalty ladder (including where conduct is 'detrimental to the interest of its Customers') attaches to these duties.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / Art 25):** "Specific channels must be made available for receiving Customer complaints and handling them within a reasonable period not exceeding seven (7) days."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Art 2(e)):** "The objective of these Rules is to... protect the rights of Customers."
