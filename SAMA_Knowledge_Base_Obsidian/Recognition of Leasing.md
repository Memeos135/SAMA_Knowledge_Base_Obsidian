# Recognition of Leasing

## Connections

### [[Commercial and Residential Real Estate Collateral]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When classifying a real-estate-backed exposure whose repayment depends on leasing performance, read the leasing/specialised-lending criteria together with the real-estate collateral and exposure-class rules, because the same property can fall into different regulatory buckets. The leasing tables (SL supervisory slotting) assess leasing activity, stabilisation and security package, while 16.129 expressly excludes income-producing real estate under the SL asset class from recognition as collateral for corporate exposures, and 7.61–7.62 distinguish exposures 'materially dependent on cash flows generated by the property'. The consequence: where a property is leased and treated as specialised lending, you cannot also count it as eligible corporate collateral — confirm whether the exposure is IPRE/SL or general real estate before assigning the treatment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 161 / SL slotting table):** "Leasing activity meets or exceeds projections. The project should achieve stabilisation in the near future"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 228 / 16.129):** "income producing real estate that falls under the SL asset class is specifically excluded from recognition as collateral for corporate exposures."
