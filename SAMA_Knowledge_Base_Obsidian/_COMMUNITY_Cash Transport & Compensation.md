# Cash Transport & Compensation

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 3 nodes

## Why this community

Physical cash-handling security regime — secure transport, tamper/security-ink protection, and the compensation mechanism for damaged (security-inked) banknotes. Governs operational currency-integrity obligations.

## How members connect

- The Damaged Cash Compensation Mechanism references both the Cash Transport recommendations and Security Cash Containers requirement, making compensation eligibility contingent on compliant transport/container use.
- Transport recommendations and container requirements set the preventive controls; compensation sets the consequence/remedy where security-ink activation occurs.
- Practical effect: non-compliance with transport/container standards may limit or defeat compensation claims for inked/damaged cash.
