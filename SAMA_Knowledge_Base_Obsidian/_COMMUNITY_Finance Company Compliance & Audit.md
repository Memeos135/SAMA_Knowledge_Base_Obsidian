# Finance Company Compliance & Audit

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

Governance, compliance and internal-audit obligations for finance and real estate refinance companies, all anchored in the Finance Companies Control Law.

## How members connect

- The Finance Companies Control Law is the parent statute; the Compliance Principles, Internal Audit Principles, and Notification requirement are subordinate SAMA instruments referencing it as their legal basis.
- Compliance Principles cross-reference Internal Audit Principles, reflecting the intended interaction between the compliance and audit control functions.
- The Notification Before Announcing Periodic Financial Statements imposes a specific disclosure-timing duty on finance companies under the same statutory authority.
- Hierarchy for legal reading: law (Control Law) -> implementing principles/circulars binding on licensed finance and refinance companies.
