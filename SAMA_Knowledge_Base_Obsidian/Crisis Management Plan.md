# Crisis Management Plan

## Connections

### [[Business Continuity Management Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping the framework's required documents, treat the Crisis Management Plan as a distinct mandatory component (section 2.8) separate from the BCP — it governs the response to major/declared incidents, including crisis declaration criteria and a command center, rather than routine activity recovery. The framework requires it to be defined, approved, implemented, monitored for compliance, and periodically evaluated, and BCP testing scenarios must cover activation of the crisis management team. A reviewer should verify the CMP exists as its own approved plan with declaration criteria, and that testing exercises the crisis team, not merely the recovery plans.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12 / 2.8):** "a. Criteria for declaring a crisis. b. The member organization should establish a command center for centralized management"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / 2.8):** "The Member Organization should define, approve and implement a crisis management plan that would facilitate a well-managed response for major incidents"
