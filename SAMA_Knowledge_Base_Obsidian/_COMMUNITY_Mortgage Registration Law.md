# Mortgage Registration Law

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Real estate mortgage registration regime: the statutory framework for registering property mortgages and SAMA's operational instruction that registration reflect the actual contractual terms. Relevant to secured-lending and finance-company collateral perfection decisions.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Registered Real Estate Mortgage Law is the primary statute; the circular is a subordinate SAMA instruction implementing/directing compliance.
- The circular references the Law to require that mortgage registration match contract reality — an obligation on lenders to register mortgages consistent with the underlying financing agreement.
- Legal consequence: collateral validity/enforceability depends on registration conforming to both the statute and the circular's fidelity-to-contract requirement.
