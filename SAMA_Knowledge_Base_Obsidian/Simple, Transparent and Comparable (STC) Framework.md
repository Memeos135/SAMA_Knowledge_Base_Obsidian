# Simple, Transparent and Comparable (STC) Framework

## Connections

### [[STC Short-Term (ABCP) Securitization Criteria]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether an ABCP conduit exposure qualifies for the more favorable STC alternative capital treatment, apply the short-term criteria set in 18.96-18.165 rather than the term criteria: the STC scope provision (18.67(2)) makes ABCP conduits and conduit-financed transactions STC-compliant only if they meet that dedicated short-term criteria block. The framework further conditions relief on the originator/sponsor disclosing transaction-level information and the investor making its own STC assessment, and STC criteria must be met at all times. Conclude that ABCP STC relief cannot be assumed from the conduit rating alone — verify satisfaction of the 18.96+ short-term criteria at both conduit and transaction levels before applying reduced parameters.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 253 / Art 18.67(2)):** "Exposures to ABCP conduits and/or transactions financed by ABCP conduits, where the conduit and/or transactions financed by it meet the criteria in 18.96 to 18.165"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 253 / Art 18.70):** "STC criteria need to be met at all times."

### [[STC Term Securitization Criteria]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When testing whether a non-ABCP traditional securitization can access STC alternative capital treatment, apply the term-securitization criteria in 18.72-18.95 as the operative gate: the STC scope provision (18.67(1)) makes only such exposures STC-compliant when those criteria are met, and the resulting relief is delivered through 19.20-19.22, 20.11-20.14 and 22.27-22.29 (e.g. the SEC-SA parameter p set to 0.5). Note that for retained positions where significant risk transfer is achieved, only the originator makes the STC determination. Conclude that the alternative treatment applies only after confirming the full term-criteria set (A1-D16) and that these criteria must hold on an ongoing basis, not just at origination.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 253 / Art 18.67(1)):** "Exposures to non-ABCP, traditional securitizations that meet the criteria in 18.72 to 18.95"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 298 / Art 19.20):** "Securitization transactions that are assessed as simple, transparent and comparable (STC)-compliant for capital purposes as defined in 18.67 can be subject to capital requirements under the securitization framework"

### [[Securitization Internal Ratings-Based Approach (SEC-IRBA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a securitization qualifies as STC-compliant, the SEC-IRBA calculation is modified rather than replaced, so check STC status before finalizing capital. The STC framework expressly points to the SEC-IRBA alternative-treatment paragraphs (22.27 to 22.29) as the provisions engaged for STC exposures, and the SEC-IRBA chapter contains a dedicated 'alternative capital treatment' section for STC securitizations. Conclude that eligibility hinges on the originator/sponsor disclosure and the investor's own STC assessment (18.67–18.68) — without that, the standard SEC-IRBA parameters, not the reduced ones, apply.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 253 / 18.66):** "Exposures to securitizations that are STC-compliant can be subject to alternative capital treatment as determined by 19.20 to 19.22, 20.11 to 20.14 and 22.27 to 22.29."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / ch.22):** "Alternative capital treatment for term securitizations and short-term securitizations meeting the STC criteria for capital purposes"

### [[Securitization Standardized Approach (SEC-SA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying SEC-SA to an exposure you have assessed as STC-compliant, you must substitute the STC alternative treatment: 19.21 sets p to 0.5 and 19.22 lowers the floor risk weight to 10% for senior tranches, applying instead of the ordinary 19.12/19.15 values. Eligibility is gated by 18.66-18.70, which limit STC to traditional securitizations and require the investor to make its own STC assessment before applying the alternative treatment. Conclude that the beneficial SEC-SA parameters are contingent on documented STC compliance at all times; if STC status is not established or lapses, you revert to the standard SEC-SA parameters and floor.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 253 / 18.66):** "Exposures to securitizations that are STC-compliant can be subject to alternative capital treatment as determined by 19.20 to 19.22"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 298 / 19.20-19.21):** "when the SEC-SA is used, 19.21 and 19.22 are applicable instead of 19.12 and 19.15 ... p ... is set equal to 0.5"
