# Standardized Credit Risk Approach

**Cohesion:** 0.25 - loosely connected
**Members:** 9 nodes

## Why this community

Standardized Approach for credit-risk capital under SAMA: risk-weighting of exposures using external/internal assessment methods, with specific treatment of real estate and residual market risks.

## How members connect

- The Standardized Approach is the parent methodology, offering ECRA (external ratings-based) and SCRA (internal, for unrated/certain jurisdictions) as alternative assessment routes.
- Real Estate Exposure Class is risk-weighted under the Approach with the Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV) as the defined determinant.
- Template CR4 is the disclosure/reporting instrument evidencing Standardized Approach exposures and credit risk mitigation.
- The Residual Risk Add-On (RRAO) is conceptually adjacent, capturing exotic underlyings and other residual risks not covered by the main charge.
