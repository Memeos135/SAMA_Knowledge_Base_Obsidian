# Nomination and Remuneration Committee

## Connections

### [[Banks Remuneration Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's remuneration governance, treat the Nomination and Remuneration Committee as a mandatory organ created and constrained by the Banks Remuneration Rules, not an optional structure. The Rules require the committee (min. three members), tie its composition and responsibilities to SAMA's Key Principles of Governance, and route Board approval of senior-management remuneration through its recommendations; the terms of reference must be submitted to SAMA with the Compliance Report each cycle. Conclude that remuneration decisions and policy documents must show the committee's role and interaction with Risk Management/CRO, and that absence of a compliant NRC is itself a breach of these Rules.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / 2.2):** "Banks shall have a Nomination and Remuneration Committee comprising of at least three members... consistent with SAMA's Key Principles of Governance"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / 1.3):** "These rules shall apply to banks... The objective of these rules is to set the minimum requirements... on remuneration"

### [[Board of Directors Responsibilities (Remuneration)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping who decides remuneration, read the Board and the NRC as linked but distinct roles rather than interchangeable: the NRC recommends and the Board reviews and approves, and remuneration-adjustment decisions must be noticed to the Board or the NRC. The Board retains non-delegable overall responsibility, so the NRC's advisory function does not displace Board accountability. A reviewer should check that recommendation-then-approval sequencing is respected and that the NRC's role is not treated as final authority, since supervisory scrutiny attaches ultimately to the Board.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / §4.3 item 53(e)):** "communicating in writing to all affected individuals the value of remuneration adjustments ... That includes noticing the Board or Nomination and Remuneration Committee."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / §2.1 item 5):** "The Board shall review and, if satisfied, approve the remunerations of the senior management based on the recommendations of the Nomination and Remuneration Committee."
