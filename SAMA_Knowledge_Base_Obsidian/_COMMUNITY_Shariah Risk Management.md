# Shariah Risk Management

**Cohesion:** 0.22 - loosely connected
**Members:** 9 nodes

## Why this community

Prudential risk-management regime for Shari'ah-compliant banking, mapping conventional risk categories plus Islamic-specific risks onto a dedicated SAMA framework.

## How members connect

- The Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking is the parent instrument referencing each risk type (credit, market, operational, liquidity, equity investment) it obliges banks to manage.
- It cross-references the Shari'ah Governance Framework, establishing governance oversight as a companion requirement.
- Shari'ah Non-compliance Risk (SNCR) is treated as a subset/manifestation of Operational Risk, defining the compliance consequence of non-adherence to Shari'ah rulings.
- PSIAs Rules connect via the framework's treatment of profit-sharing investment accounts, a distinctly Islamic funding/risk-transfer feature.
