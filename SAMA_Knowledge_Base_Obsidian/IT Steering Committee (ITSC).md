# IT Steering Committee (ITSC)

## Connections

### [[IT Governance and Leadership]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining who is accountable for IT governance obligations at a SAMA Member Organization, treat the ITSC as the delegated operational locus but not the ultimate accountable party. Under the IT Governance and Leadership domain, the board is 'ultimately responsible' and can delegate to the ITSC, which the framework then makes mandatory ('Member organizations should establish ITSC and be mandated by the board'), with prescribed composition, a board-approved charter, and quarterly minimum meetings. Conclude that establishing an ITSC and appointing a qualified Saudi-national CIO are hard control requirements, but board-level accountability and endorsement of strategy/policy cannot be delegated away.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12 / 3.1.1):** "Member organizations should establish ITSC and be mandated by the board. The ITSC should be headed by senior manager responsible for Member Organizations operations."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / 3.1):** "Member Organizations board is ultimately responsible for setting the Information Technology (IT) Governance... can delegate its IT Governance responsibilities to senior management or IT steering committee (ITSC)."
