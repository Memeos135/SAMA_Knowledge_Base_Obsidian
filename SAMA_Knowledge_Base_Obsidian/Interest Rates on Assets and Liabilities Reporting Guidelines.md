# Interest Rates on Assets and Liabilities Reporting Guidelines

## Connections

### [[Assets and Liabilities Reporting Categories]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When completing the quarterly Assets and Liabilities interest-rate returns, treat the Reporting Categories as the mandatory taxonomy the guideline requires banks to populate — they are not illustrative. The guideline requires all banks (including foreign branches) to submit within 30 days, signed by the CFO, and Section C states the classifications 'are mutually exclusive,' meaning each exposure maps to exactly one category (e.g. Loans to Governments and Quasi Government, Loans to Corporates, Retail Loans). A preparer should confirm each reported item aligns to the Annexure-2 validation table linking these categories to M1 items, since misclassification breaches the mutual-exclusivity rule.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Reporting Guidance):** "Banks should comply with the following in completing the report related to Assets and Liabilities interest rates... Template (1) - by Product... Template (2) - by Sector."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Reporting Categories, Section C):** "Reporting Categories A. Assets: Accrued rates receivable. 1. Loans to Governments and Quasi Government... classifications of Assets and Liabilities are mutually exclusive."

### [[Weighted Average Rates Calculation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When preparing the interest-rate returns, note the guideline binds the reported figures to a prescribed Weighted Average rate method — you cannot substitute an internal averaging approach. The guideline requires W.A rates to reflect annual contractual rates on outstanding balances, to include benchmark rates on booking value (e.g. SAIBOR + margin), to be reported in SAR 000's for M1 Domestic, and to be computed 'as described in Annexure-1,' which supplies the balance-weighted worked example. A preparer should confirm the calculation follows the Annexure-1 formula and the no-movement-between-maturity-buckets rule before submission, since deviations would make the reported rates non-compliant.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Reporting Guidance B–G):** "Banks should calculate the W.A rate as described in Annexure —1... Benchmark rates must be included in the rate calculation based on booking value."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9 / Annexure 1):** "Example of Calculating Weighted Average Rates... WA Rates = (3/2)*100 (14000/360000)*100 Weighted Average Rate 3.89%"
