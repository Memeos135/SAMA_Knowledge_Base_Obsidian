# Circular on Non-Objection for Senior Positions in Financial Institutions

## Connections

### [[Fit and Proper Form]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on senior-appointment obligations, read the Fit and Proper Form together with this circular because both operationalise the same underlying 'Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions in Financial Institutions Supervised by SAMA.' The Form requires it to 'be read in conjunction with' those Requirements, and the circular expressly cites the same Requirements and adds a distinct obligation: senior post-holders must obtain the institution's approval and SAMA's non-objection before accepting external nominations, mandates, or board memberships. You should conclude that the fit-and-proper assessment captured in the Form is not a one-time hurdle — the circular extends ongoing non-objection duties (with non-compliance falling under Article 70 of the Requirements) across the appointee's tenure.
