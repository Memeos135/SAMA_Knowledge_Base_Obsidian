# Finance Fees & Governance

**Cohesion:** 0.14 - loosely connected
**Members:** 14 nodes

## Why this community

Prudential and conduct regime for licensed finance companies: licensing, capital/liquidity, governance, risk and compliance obligations, plus consumer-facing fee, APR and contract requirements. Covers what a finance company must satisfy to operate and how it must price and document consumer finance.

## How members connect

- The Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law is the parent instrument, referencing licensing, capital adequacy/liquidity, corporate governance, risk management, compliance function, exposure limits and financing policies as subordinate obligations.
- Consumer-conduct branch links Administrative Fees Regulatory Provisions and the Services Tariff Guide (fee ceilings/disclosure) to Consumer Finance Controls; Finance Contract Requirements incorporate the mandated APR Formula for cost-of-credit disclosure.
- Financing Policies and Procedures bridge the prudential governance layer to the contract-level requirements, tying internal controls to enforceable customer documentation.
- The two Tariff Guide entries are duplicates (semantically similar) and should be treated as one authority.
