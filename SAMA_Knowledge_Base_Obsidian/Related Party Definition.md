# Related Party Definition

## Connections

### [[Related Parties Rules for Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Before applying any exposure limit or governance control, determine whether a counterparty meets the defined term 'Related Party,' because the entire regime turns on that classification. The Rules define Related Party to include substantial shareholders, board members of the bank or its subsidiaries/affiliates and their relatives, and Shariah Committee members, and tie the trigger to a 'Control Relationship' (deemed at >50% voting rights plus qualitative influence tests). For a compliance decision, scope the population first: mislabelling a counterparty as non-related would wrongly exempt it from the 5%/10%/25%/50% caps, arm's-length pricing, and board-approval and recusal requirements.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Section 2.vi):** "Related Party: a. Substantial Shareholders of the bank. b. Board members of the bank or any of its subsidiaries/ affiliates ... and their relatives."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Section 4):** "A bank is required to have adequate systems and controls in place to identify, measure, monitor and report related party exposures/transactions."
