# Funding Ratio (FR) Requirements

## Connections

### [[RWA and Funding Report (Attachment D)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When meeting FR reporting duties, Attachment D is the prescribed return form through which the substantive Funding Ratio obligation is discharged. Section 3.4 requires FBBs subject to FR to calculate and report their ratio quarterly 'using the attached Attachment D', submitted to the relationship manager by the end of the month following quarter-end. You should conclude that the reporting obligation only bites where the branch is subject to FR, and that the RWA components in Attachment D define what must be captured in the calculation for those branches.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 17 / Section 3.4):** "FBBs that are subject to FR requirements are required to calculate and report to SAMA its Funding Ratio (FR) during the period using the attached Attachment D"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 36 (Attachment D) / RWA and funding components):** "Net cash outflows ... Total retail deposits ... Total unsecured wholesale funding (Attachment D reporting template)"
- **Caveat:** Attachment D pages are OCR-extracted tabular data; the excerpt is a tight paraphrase of the template's captured line items.

### [[Regulations for Foreign Banks Branches (FBB)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing prudential exposure for a foreign bank branch, treat the Funding Ratio as a discretionary, case-by-case quasi-capital requirement rather than a universal one. The FBB Regulations (Section 3) establish that FBBs are not required to maintain capital in KSA, but SAMA may impose an FR on branches conducting high-risk business or significant retail operations, calculated on total RWA (Pillar 1 plus Pillar 2). You should conclude that FR applicability depends on the branch's business profile and any specific SAMA determination, and verify whether your branch has been made subject to FR before treating it as binding.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16-17 / Section 3.1):** "a quasi-capital in the form of a Funding Ratio requirement may be set on a case-by-case basis, for example, those intending to conduct high-risk businesses"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Section 1):** "SAMA requires all FBBs to comply with the Banking Control Law (BCL), its prudential requirements, and other relevant laws and regulations"
