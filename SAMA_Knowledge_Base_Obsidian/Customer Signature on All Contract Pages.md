# Customer Signature on All Contract Pages

## Connections

### [[Consumer Protection Principles]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a finance company's contract-execution duties, read the requirement to obtain the natural-person customer's signature on every contract page as a direct application of the Consumer Protection (finance-company customer protection) Principles, because the circular expressly grounds itself in those principles' emphasis on disclosure, transparency and customer education. The mandate is obligatory for natural persons ('shall obtain the customer's signature on all pages') and optional for legal persons, with residual risk on the company if it does not. You should therefore treat page-by-page signing as an enforceable consumer-protection obligation for individual borrowers and not assume the optional legal-person treatment relieves the company of the underlying transparency duty.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_5392 / Page 1):** "in line with finance-company customer protection principles ... finance companies must obtain the customer's (natural person's) signature on all pages of contracts"
- **Grounding — related node (SAMA_EN_5404 / Page 1):** "based on the finance-company customer protection principles concerning fair, transparent treatment without discrimination"

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing a finance company's contracting practices with individuals, read the requirement to obtain the customer's signature on every contract page as an enforceable consumer-protection obligation grounded in the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51). The circular expressly invokes SAMA's authority under the Law (Royal Decree M/51) and the finance-customer protection principles of disclosure and transparency. You should conclude the page-signature rule is mandatory for natural persons and optional for legal persons—but note the circular places responsibility for risks of unsigned pages on the company itself where it declines to apply the rule to legal persons.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1):** "يتعين على شركات التمويل الحصول على توقيع العميل (الشخص الطبيعي) على جميع صفحات العقود والاتفاقيات"
