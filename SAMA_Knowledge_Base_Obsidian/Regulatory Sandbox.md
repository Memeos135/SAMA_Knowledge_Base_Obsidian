# Regulatory Sandbox

## Connections

### [[Guidance Notes on SAMA Regulatory Sandbox Application]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a FinTech on whether the Sandbox route is available, treat this Guidance Notes document as the operative description of the three-stage Regulatory Sandbox regime it defines — Application (60 business days), Operational Readiness (120 days), and Live Testing. The document is the source that scopes the concept, so the practical takeaway is that Sandbox entry confers only a temporary permission (LoA), not a licence, and firms whose business model is already regulated must apply directly for a licence rather than the Sandbox. Conclude that Sandbox status must never be represented to counterparties or customers as authorisation to operate until the Stage 3 No Objection Letter is issued.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12):** "Regulations have been issued for your business model/concept and you should apply directly for a License not for Regulatory Sandbox permissions."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3):** "SAMA has updated its Regulatory Sandbox operating model and processes to give greater guidance to applicants on the stages and requirements to move between the different Sandbox stages."

### [[Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria (AC)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing what a FinTech must actually satisfy before it can onboard customers, note that the Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria (AC) is the gating requirement within the Regulatory Sandbox — it is the Stage 2 checkpoint between initial approval and go-live. The AC is expressly a list of minimum compliance requirements that must be met before a No Objection Letter is issued for Stage 3 live operations. Conclude that the Sandbox initial approval letter does not permit commencement of operations, and that only demonstrated AC compliance, using the latest AC version sourced from SAMA, unlocks live testing and customer onboarding.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12):** "Once the AC requirements have been met, the Regulatory Sandbox will issue a No Objection Letter for the Fintech to commence operations, which is Regulatory Sandbox Stage 3."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11):** "The AC requirements are a list of minimum compliance requirements that FinTechs must meet prior to being permitted to go live with operations and onboarding their clients/customers."
