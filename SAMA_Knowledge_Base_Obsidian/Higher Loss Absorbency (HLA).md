# Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA)

## Connections

### [[D-SIBs Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the capital consequences of D-SIB designation, read the Framework as directly imposing the HLA obligation: banks designated as D-SIBs 'will be required to meet additional Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA) capital requirements as prescribed in the enclosed methodology.' The Framework establishes the obligation and the HLA provisions specify its content — met fully by CET1, calibrated to the bank's bucket. For a compliance decision, once a bank is designated the HLA charge is not discretionary as to existence, only as to magnitude (bucket-based), and it stacks on top of the target CAR SAMA sets for the bank's risk profile.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6, paras 11-12):** "The HLA requirement ... should be met fully by common equity Tier 1 (CET1) ... will be in addition to the target CAR determined by SAMA"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1 (covering circular)):** "banks designated as D-SIBs will be required to meet additional Higher Loss Absorbency(HLA) capital requirements as prescribed in the enclosed methodology"

### [[Scoring and Bucketing]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When estimating the magnitude of a bank's capital add-on, read Scoring and Bucketing and HLA as a single mechanism rather than two topics: the bucket a bank is allocated to under scoring is what sets its HLA charge, since 'the HLA requirement imposed on a bank is commensurate with the degree of systemic importance' and 'the HLA capital charge will be calculated by SAMA based on the bank's degree of systemic importance determined in the scoring exercise.' For a compliance decision, a change in the bank's score or bucket directly changes its CET1 HLA obligation, so the two must be monitored together, not in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6, para 12):** "The HLA capital charge will be calculated by SAMA based on the bank's degree of systemic importance determined in the scoring exercise"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6, para 9):** "D-SIB's will be allocated to a bucket based on their scores"
