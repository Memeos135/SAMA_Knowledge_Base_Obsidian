# Risk Management and Monitoring

## Connections

### [[Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** This link appears to connect the investment-side risk framework to the AML/CTF regime, but the two nodes sit in different instruments: the Banks Investment Rules require AML/CTF compliance before investing (Sec 7.3), while node B is the AML/CTF article in the Money Changer Rules pointing to the AML Law and Terrorism Financing Law and their implementing regulations. The relationship is conceptual — both point back to the same underlying SAMA AML/CTF framework — rather than a direct cross-reference between these two documents. Before relying on this, verify against the primary AML Law and its Implementing Regulations, since the Money Changer article governs money changers and does not itself set the AML rules for a bank's investment activity.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Sec 7.3):** "Banks should comply with all relevant Anti Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing requirements issued by SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 18 / Art 32):** "The Money Changer shall comply with the provisions of the Anti-money laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations; the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing and its Implementing Regulations"
- **Caveat:** INFERRED and cross-instrument (banks vs. money changers); the shared reference is the underlying AML/CTF Law, not a direct link between these provisions. Confirm the applicable AML source for bank investment activity separately.

### [[Banks Investment Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the obligations attaching to investment activity, treat Section 7 as binding requirements: banks must align investments with their risk management framework, maintain a system to identify, measure and manage investment risks, conduct annual portfolio stress-testing, and provide results to SAMA on request. Critically, Section 7.3 embeds a cross-regime obligation — banks must satisfy all SAMA AML/CTF requirements before engaging in any investment. In a compliance decision you would treat investment onboarding and counterparty dealing as also triggering AML/CTF duties, not just prudential risk controls.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Sec 7.3):** "Before engaging in any investments, Banks should comply with all relevant Anti Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing requirements issued by SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Sec 2.1):** "Ensure banks put in place a comprehensive internal investment framework, with proper risk management in accordance with SAMA regulatory requirements."
