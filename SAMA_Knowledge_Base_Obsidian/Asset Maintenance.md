# Asset Maintenance

## Connections

### [[Finance Lease Law and Executive Regulation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining who is legally responsible for maintaining the leased asset under an individual real-estate Ijara, read the model contract's 'Asset Maintenance' article (صيانة الأصل المؤجر) as an exercise of SAMA's authority under the Finance Lease Law and its Executive Regulation, which empower SAMA to approve model finance-lease contracts protecting the rights of related parties. The maintenance clause is therefore not freely negotiable — it is a standardised term derived from the parent law's mandate. The practical conclusion: confirm the maintenance allocation against the mandatory model wording and treat the Finance Lease Law/Regulation as the enabling basis for its binding force, not as a mere background reference.

### [[Total and Partial Loss of Asset]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When allocating risk for damage to the financed (leased) asset under this model Ijarah contract, read the Asset Maintenance article together with the Total/Partial Loss article: maintenance defines the tenant's ongoing upkeep duties, while the loss article governs what happens when the asset is destroyed in whole or in part, and the boundary between recoverable maintenance failures and a loss event determines which party bears the cost. Both are enumerated articles of the same SAMA-mandated standard form (صيانة الأصل المؤجر and الهلاك الكلي والجزئي للأصل المؤجر appear consecutively in the contract index). For a compliance decision, confirm that the contract's maintenance obligation does not shift onto the tenant losses that the total/partial-loss article assigns elsewhere.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 — صيانة الأصل المؤجر):** "صيانة الأصل المؤجر"

- **Caveat:** The interaction between the maintenance and loss articles is inferred from adjacency in the contract's table of contents; the Arabic source is OCR-degraded, so verify the operative risk-allocation text before relying on this reading.
