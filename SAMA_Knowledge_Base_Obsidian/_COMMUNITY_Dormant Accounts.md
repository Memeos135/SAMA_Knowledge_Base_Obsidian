## Why this community

## How members connect

- 'Inoperative Accounts' is the anchor provision referencing the staged status terms (dormant, unclaimed, abandoned) — these are sequential/related classifications, not synonyms, so distinguish the trigger and obligations at each stage.
- Communication Policy for Inoperative Accounts imposes the contact/notification duty tied to dormant classification, so status change and customer outreach obligations are linked.
- Dormant/unclaimed/abandoned are conceptually related escalation states; precise definitional boundaries determine when funds-handling and reporting duties attach.
