# Land Financing Regulation

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Real estate finance rules as applied to land financing, connecting the primary law to a circular that clarifies scope and conditions for financing land.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Real Estate Finance Law is the primary statute; the Circular on Land Financing is subordinate interpretive/scoping detail under the Real Estate Finance Regulation.
- The circular references the Law to define when and how land (as distinct from built property) may be financed — read as a scope condition on permissible products.
- Compliance consequence: lenders must confirm land-financing offerings fall within the circular's conditions before treating them as compliant real estate finance.
