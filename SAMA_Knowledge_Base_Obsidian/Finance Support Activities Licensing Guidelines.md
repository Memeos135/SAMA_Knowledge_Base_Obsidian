### [[Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)  GLEIF|Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) / GLEIF]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on the licensing pathway for a finance support activity, note that obtaining a Legal Entity Identifier is a mandatory post-incorporation gate, not an optional formality. The Guidelines require the company to give SAMA proof it has obtained the LEI from the Saudi Operating Unit approved by GLEIF, sequenced after commercial registration and capital payment. You should confirm the LEI is sourced specifically from the GLEIF-approved Saudi Operating Unit, since a non-conforming identifier would leave the licensing conditions unsatisfied.

### [[Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** Read these Guidelines as procedural/application-facing material subordinate to the Rules of Licensing Finance Support Activities, which are the substantive provisions. The Guidelines expressly state that terms used in the application form carry the same meanings as in the Rules, and that supplying false or incomplete information is a violation of the Rules that may cause rejection or cancellation. When scoping obligations, treat the Rules as the controlling text for definitions and licensing conditions and use the Guidelines only for how to apply — do not rely on the Guidelines for substantive licensing requirements the Rules govern.
