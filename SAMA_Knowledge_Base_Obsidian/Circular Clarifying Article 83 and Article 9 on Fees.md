# Circular Clarifying Article 83 and Article 9 on Fees

## Connections

### [[Consumer Financing Regulations]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When calculating the permissible ceiling on fees, commissions and administrative service costs charged to a beneficiary, read this circular as the authoritative interpretation of Article 9 of the Consumer Financing Regulations (parallel to Article 83 of the Implementing Regulation). Both provisions cap such charges at the lesser of 1% of the financing amount or SAR 5,000; the circular clarifies that this cap covers ALL amounts the beneficiary is compelled to pay beyond principal and term cost — including application-study, real-estate valuation, inspection and registration fees. Consequence: do not treat third-party or ancillary fees as outside the cap, and note the financier alone bears dealings with valuers/registrars — the customer cannot be delegated to pay third-party amounts, and all such costs must be reflected in the APR.

### [[Implementing Regulation of Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying the fee cap to a finance company (as distinct from a bank), rely on this circular's interpretation of Article 83 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law, which caps fees, commissions and administrative service costs a finance company collects from a beneficiary at the lesser of 1% of the financing amount or SAR 5,000. The circular clarifies that this ceiling captures every unavoidable charge, not merely nominal 'administrative' fees. Consequence: assess compliance of a finance company's fee schedule against the full inclusive definition, and include all such fees in the APR calculation; the same cap runs in parallel with Article 9 of the Consumer Financing controls.
