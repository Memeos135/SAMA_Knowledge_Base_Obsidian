# Risk Management Evaluation Questionnaire

## Connections

### [[SAMA Operational Risk Management Guide]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating a bank against the operational-risk expectations, use the Risk Management Evaluation Questionnaire as the assessment instrument that operationalizes the Guide's substantive concepts. The Guide defines the terms — Operational Risk, Risk Assessment and Control Matrices, the Operational Risk Management Plan — and the Questionnaire tests them directly, e.g. whether the bank can 'identify actual and potential loss exposures and risk events for all products and services' and whether 'formal written programs... including risk assessment and control matrices' exist. A reader should conclude the two are the standard-and-checklist pair: the Guide sets the definitions and expectations, the Questionnaire is the yardstick for demonstrating compliance.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 45 / Operational Risk Reduction and Control):** "Have formal written programs of operational risk and loss control including risk assessment and control matrices been developed for all operational and staff areas ?"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 51 / Glossary):** "Risk Assessment and Control Matrices - These matrices should be developed by each functional area and reviewed by both the Operational Risk Manager and the Internal Auditor"
