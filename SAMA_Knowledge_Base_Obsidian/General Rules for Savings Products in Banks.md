# General Rules for Savings Products in Banks

## Connections

### [[Annual Equivalent Rate (AER)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying the Savings Products Rules to a bank's deposit products, treat AER as a rule-defined term, not a marketing figure: the Rules' definitions article expressly ties the customer's return to the annual equivalent rate and points to SAMA's separate Disclosure of Rates rules for how AER is computed. This means AER disclosures in savings products must be consistent with that cross-referenced disclosure regime, so you cannot assess advertising/disclosure compliance under these Rules in isolation. Check the referenced Disclosure of Financing and Savings Product Prices rules for the binding AER calculation methodology before signing off.
