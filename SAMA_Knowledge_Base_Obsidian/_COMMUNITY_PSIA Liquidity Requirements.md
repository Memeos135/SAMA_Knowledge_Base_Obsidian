# PSIA Liquidity Requirements

**Cohesion:** 0.11 - loosely connected
**Members:** 18 nodes

## Why this community

Liquidity and funding prudential regime for Islamic profit-sharing accounts and the broader ILAAP framework, addressing how PSIA (Mudarabah-based, non-guaranteed) balances are treated for LCR/NSFR and internal liquidity adequacy, plus supporting reporting on rates and loan-to-deposit ratios.

## How members connect

- PSIA Rules define the account and its Mudarabah/PERR-IRR mechanics, then feed the LCR/NSFR Prudential Requirements for PSIA — the defined term drives distinct prudential treatment because PSIA holders bear investment risk.
- ILAAP Guidelines govern the ILAAP Process, which cross-references LCR/NSFR ratios, Pillar 2 liquidity drivers, stress-testing, CFP and Early Warning Indicators as an integrated internal assessment obligation.
- Reporting guidelines (Interest Rates on Assets/Liabilities, LDR) supply the calculation inputs (weighted average rates, weighted denominator, A&L categories) that share data with the prudential ratios.
- Concepts cluster by shared liquidity-metric dependency rather than strict hierarchy — LDR and LCR/NSFR are conceptually related monitoring measures.
