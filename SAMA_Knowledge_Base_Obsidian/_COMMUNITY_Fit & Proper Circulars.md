# Fit & Proper Circulars

**Cohesion:** 0.33 - loosely connected
**Members:** 6 nodes

## Why this community

Fit-and-proper / senior-appointment vetting and related consumer-finance credit controls, hierarchically grounded in the Banking Control Law and operationalised through SAMA circulars and rules.

## How members connect

- The Banking Control Law is the parent statute; the 'Fit and Proper Form' and 'Credit Card Issuance and Operation Rules' reference it as their legal basis (law -> subordinate rule).
- The 'Circular on Non-Objection for Senior Positions' references the Fit and Proper Form — it operationalises the no-objection/appointment-approval obligation for senior officers.
- The consumer-finance clarifying circular and the income-due-diligence circular attach to the Credit Card Rules, refining creditworthiness/verification obligations for consumer lending.
- Two threads coexist: governance vetting of persons (fit-and-proper) and consumer-finance conduct controls, linked only through the shared statutory anchor and the Credit Card Rules.
