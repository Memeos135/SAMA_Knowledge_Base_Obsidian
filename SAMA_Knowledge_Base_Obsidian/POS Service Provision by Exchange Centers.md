# POS Service Provision by Exchange Centers

## Connections

### [[Rules Regulating Exchange Business]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing SAMA's power to mandate that exchange centers offer POS (mada) services to customers, note that this POS circular derives its authority from the same enabling clause — Article 14(b) of the Rules Regulating Exchange Business empowering SAMA to issue instructions it deems necessary for developing/supervising the sector. This confirms the POS-provision requirement is a binding instruction under the Rules, not a commercial recommendation. A reviewer should conclude the obligation to provide and activate POS is enforceable and that non-compliance triggers the statutory penalties the circular expressly reserves.
