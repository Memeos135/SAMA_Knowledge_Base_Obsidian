# IT & Shariah Governance

**Cohesion:** 0.13 - loosely connected
**Members:** 17 nodes

## Why this community

Internal control and technology-risk governance for finance companies: the cluster binds the three-lines control functions (compliance, internal audit, Shariah governance, code of conduct) to SAMA's IT, cyber, and business-continuity frameworks, plus the operational IT sub-disciplines they mandate.

## How members connect

- Control-function instruments (Compliance, Internal Audit, Shariah Governance, Code of Conduct) cross-reference each other, establishing that audit and compliance test conduct and Shariah adherence as enforceable governance obligations.
- Internal Audit ties into the IT Governance Framework, extending audit scope to technology controls; the Framework in turn references the Cyber Security and Business Continuity Frameworks as the anchoring SAMA-issued standards.
- Operational IT topics (change management, patching, development, testing, incident management, data backup) are subordinate practices that reference the Cyber Security and Business Continuity Frameworks — treat the Frameworks as the source of obligation and these as implementing detail.
- IT Governance Maturity Model is a measurement companion to the Framework, not an independent obligation source.
