# Branch Network Regulation

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

Branch network licensing/expansion regime — rules on opening, distributing and zoning branches, tied to the SAMA financial-inclusion policy objective.

## How members connect

- The Branch Network Regulation is the governing instrument; the Branch Network Policy and Zones are subordinate elaborations of geographic distribution requirements.
- The Regulation references Financial Inclusion, framing branch-distribution obligations as a means to a policy end (access to services across zones).
- Policy and Financial Inclusion are conceptually linked — zoning/distribution controls exist to serve underserved areas.
