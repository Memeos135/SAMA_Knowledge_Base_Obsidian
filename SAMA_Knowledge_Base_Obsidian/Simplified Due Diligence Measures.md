# Simplified Due Diligence Measures

## Connections

### [[Due Diligence Measures]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a relationship is rated low-risk, the institution may streamline CDD, but SDD is an intensity setting within the due diligence regime — not an exemption from it. The Guide is explicit that simplified measures are permitted only 'when the ML/TF risks are low' with 'no suspicion,' and that 'application of the simplified measures does not mean exemption from the requirements of customer due diligence'; core obligations (verifying customer identity, identifying/verifying the BO, understanding purpose and ownership) still apply. A reviewer should therefore confirm SDD is supported by a documented low-risk rating and remains conditioned on the necessary conditions of Article (5/5) AML IR and Article (17) CTF IR, and should treat any 'no CDD at all' approach as non-compliant.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 43 / para 5.1):** "When the level of ML/TF risks is low, the financial institution may take simplified measures, provided that there is no suspicion"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 43 / para 5.3):** "Application of the simplified measures does not mean exemption from the requirements of customer due diligence"
