# Audit Committee

## Connections

### [[Board of Directors]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining oversight lines, understand the Audit Committee is a Board-formed body whose powers and responsibilities the Board determines and monitors, yet it is composed of non-executive members chaired by an independent member and cannot be chaired by the Chairman of the Board. This tells you the committee both reports up to and is supervised by the Board while retaining structural independence to safeguard the integrity of financial reporting, internal audit, external auditor and compliance oversight. When allocating accountability, note that forming the committee does not relieve the Board of its own responsibilities (item 5th Principle), so ultimate governance duty remains with the Board.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 19 / items 77-78):** "The Board shall determine committee powers and responsibilities... all of whom shall be non-executive members, including at least one independent member... may not be chaired by the Chairman of the Board."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 18 / 5th Principle):** "The formation of these committees shall not relieve the Board of its responsibilities."
