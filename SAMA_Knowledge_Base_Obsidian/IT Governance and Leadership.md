# IT Governance and Leadership

## Connections

### [[IT Steering Committee (ITSC)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining who is accountable for IT governance obligations at a SAMA Member Organization, treat the ITSC as the delegated operational locus but not the ultimate accountable party. Under the IT Governance and Leadership domain, the board is 'ultimately responsible' and can delegate to the ITSC, which the framework then makes mandatory ('Member organizations should establish ITSC and be mandated by the board'), with prescribed composition, a board-approved charter, and quarterly minimum meetings. Conclude that establishing an ITSC and appointing a qualified Saudi-national CIO are hard control requirements, but board-level accountability and endorsement of strategy/policy cannot be delegated away.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12 / 3.1):** "Member Organizations board is ultimately responsible for setting the Information Technology (IT) Governance... can delegate its IT Governance responsibilities to senior management or IT steering committee (ITSC)."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / 3.1.1):** "Member organizations should establish ITSC and be mandated by the board. The ITSC should be headed by senior manager responsible for Member Organizations operations."

### [[Information Technology Governance Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping which IT controls a SAMA-regulated Member Organization must implement, treat 'IT Governance and Leadership' as one of the mandated domains of the parent Framework, not an optional best-practice topic. The Framework is structured into domains and subdomains, each stating a Principle and Control Requirements that are 'mandated IT controls', and this concept is the 3.1 domain within that structure. For compliance decisions, conclude that governance/leadership control requirements are enforceable against Member Organizations and are directly scored in SAMA's maturity assessment and audit, so a gap here counts as framework non-compliance.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8):** "3.1— Information Technology Governance & Leadership"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8):** "The Control Requirements reflects the mandated IT controls that should be considered."
