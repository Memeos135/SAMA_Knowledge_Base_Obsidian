# Real Estate Refinance Companies Regulation Rules

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the supervisory basis for a real estate refinance company, note that the Regulation Rules invoke two parent statutes: they are issued under the Real Estate Finance Law, yet the Rules themselves state that such companies are also subject to the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation. This means a refinance company cannot rely solely on the real-estate-finance regime; the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51) supplies an additional layer of supervisory and licensing obligation. For compliance decisions, check obligations against both statutory tracks, since a gap in one does not excuse compliance with the other.

### [[Main Governance Principles for Financial Institutions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping governance obligations for a real estate refinance company, do not treat the Refinance Companies Rules as self-contained: the Main Governance Principles apply to that entity as a 'financial institution' subject to SAMA supervision, and its distribution scope expressly lists finance and real estate refinance companies. The Refinance Rules themselves devote a chapter to governance (الفصل الثالث: حوكمة) while the Governance Principles supply the cross-cutting board-composition, independence and disclosure standards. You should therefore read both together, and where the Rules are silent on a governance element (e.g. independent-member criteria), apply the Governance Principles rather than assume no requirement exists.

### [[Outsourcing to External Service Providers]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing outsourcing arrangements of a real estate refinance company, read the Rules for Outsourcing to External Service Providers as the applicable operative regime — the Refinance Companies Regulation Rules contain a dedicated chapter on 'outsourcing tasks to external service providers,' and the Outsourcing Rules impose the enforceable substance (board-approved written policy, non-delegable responsibility, due diligence on the provider's licences, and SAMA/auditor access rights). The refinance company remains liable even where the external provider fails to comply with applicable rules. For compliance decisions, treat the Outsourcing Rules as the detailed standard governing that chapter, and verify that refinance-company outsourcing meets its material/non-material task classification and retained-liability requirements.

### [[Real Estate Finance Law]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the primary enabling statute for the Refinance Companies Regulation Rules, treat the Real Estate Finance Law as the parent law: the Rules state SAMA issued them under powers granted by that Law, and defined terms in the Rules take the meanings assigned in the Real Estate Finance Law and its Implementing Regulation. This creates a definitional dependency — terms such as 'real estate finance,' 'financier,' and 'beneficiary' are governed by the Law, not freely redefined. For compliance decisions, interpret the Rules' scope and defined terms by reference to the Real Estate Finance Law, and note that separate SAMA circulars (e.g. loan-to-value caps) modify Law-based requirements and must be read alongside.

### [[Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying the Refinance Companies Regulation Rules, note that the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company (SRC) is a concrete licensed instance of the regulated category, and a separate circular grants it specific treatment — a shortened recourse period on purchased residential portfolios and an exemption from the general no-objection requirement when portfolios are sold to it. This means the generic Rules do not fully capture SRC-specific transaction terms. For compliance decisions, a financier selling portfolios to the SRC should apply the SRC-specific circular's recourse and notification timelines (notify SAMA within five business days), while still meeting all statutory periods for the transferred contracts.
