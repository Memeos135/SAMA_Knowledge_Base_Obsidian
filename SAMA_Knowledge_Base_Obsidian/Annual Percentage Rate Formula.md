# Annual Percentage Rate Formula

## Connections

### [[Finance Contract Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When checking disclosure compliance in finance contracts, treat the Annual Percentage Rate as a defined, formula-bound figure rather than a marketing number, because the Regulation defines APR by reference to a specific calculation article. The definitions tie 'معدل النسبة السنوي' (APR) to the discount rate computed under Article 85 of the Regulation, and this rate is a required element informing the cost figures disclosed to the beneficiary in the finance contract. You should conclude that any APR shown in a finance contract must be derived using the prescribed Article 85 method, and verify the contract's cost disclosures against that computation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Article One):** "معدل النسبة السنوي: معدل الخصم محسوباً وفق أحكام المادة الخامسة والثمانون من هذه اللائحة"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / definitions):** "إجمالي المبلغ المستحق سداده من المستفيد: مبلغ التمويل مضافاً إليه إجمالي كلفة التمويل"
