# Exchange Centers Supervisory Data Forms Update (RMS)

## Connections

### [[Returns Management System (RMS)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the mandatory channel for supervisory returns, note that the circular designates the Returns Management System (RMS) as the exclusive submission platform: exchange centers must complete and periodically deliver the supervisory data forms through the RMS portal, and the attached manual defines RMS as the digital platform enabling supervised firms to submit returns. This ties the reporting obligation to a specific delivery mode, not merely to the substance of the data. A compliance reviewer should conclude that filings outside RMS, or that fail its validation rules, do not discharge the obligation and expose the center to statutory action.

### [[Rules Regulating Exchange Business]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining SAMA's legal authority to compel exchange centers to file the updated supervisory data forms, rely on the Rules Regulating Exchange Business as the enabling instrument: the circular grounds its obligations in Article 31 (center must supply SAMA with any data or reports it requests on the timeline it sets) and Article 14(b) (SAMA may issue any instructions it deems necessary for supervisory and oversight requirements). This makes the circular a subordinate implementing measure, not a free-standing rule. A reviewer should conclude the reporting deadlines are enforceable under the Rules, and that non-compliance exposes the center to statutory action as the circular warns.
