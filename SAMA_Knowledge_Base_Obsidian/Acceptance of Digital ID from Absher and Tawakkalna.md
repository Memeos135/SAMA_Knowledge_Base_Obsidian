# Acceptance of Digital ID from Absher and Tawakkalna

## Connections

### [[Acceptance of Family Record from Absher and Tawakkalna]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what identity/civil-status documents financial institutions must accept, read these two circulars together: the family-record circular (3244) is issued expressly as a follow-on ('إلحاقاً') to the earlier digital-ID circular (3365), extending the same acceptance regime from the national digital ID to the electronic family record from Absher/Tawakkalna. Both impose an obligation on all SAMA-supervised financial institutions to accept these Absher/Tawakkalna documents in transactions that do not require taking a physical copy, and both provide that the same statutory rules applicable to printed versions apply to the electronic versions. A compliance reader should treat the digital-ID acceptance rules as the baseline and the family-record circular as a scoped extension, and should check that acceptance procedures cover both document types under the same 'no-copy-required' limitation.
- **Grounding — this node (3365 / Page 1):** "Subject: Acceptance of digital identity from Absher platform and Tawakkalna application; financial institutions must accept the electronic identity for existing customers in dealings not requiring a copy. (source Arabic paraphrased)"
- **Grounding — related node (3244 / Page 1):** "Follow-on to circular... on acceptance of digital identity from Absher and Tawakkalna; all financial institutions must accept the (citizens' family record) document in operations not requiring a copy. (source Arabic paraphrased)"
- **Caveat:** Both source texts are OCR-extracted Arabic; excerpts are English paraphrases of the readable content, not verbatim quotes.
