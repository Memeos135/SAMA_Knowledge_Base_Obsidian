# Notification Before Announcing Periodic Financial Statements

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you advise a listed finance company on disclosure timing, note this circular derives directly from the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations, so the pre-announcement notification is an enforceable supervisory requirement, not a courtesy. The circular obliges finance companies listed on the Saudi market to notify SAMA and supply the draft results announcement in Arabic and English before publishing periodic financial statements on Tadawul's site. Conclude that the obligation is confined to listed finance companies and is subordinate to — and additive to — other applicable regulations, which the circular expressly preserves.
