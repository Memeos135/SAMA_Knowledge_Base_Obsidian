# Collection Controls for Individual Customers Circular

## Connections

### [[Real Estate Financier Obligations Circular]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a finance company's or real-estate financier's collection obligations, these two SAMA circulars appear to share the same principle: outsourcing debt collection to a third party or transferring rights in the secondary market does not relieve the originating financier of responsibility for compliance with SAMA's Collection Controls and Procedures for Individual Customers. Circular 1652 confirms the finance company remains responsible for ensuring external service providers comply with those controls, and Circular 1657 confirms a real-estate financier retains its obligations (including credit-record updating and early-repayment/rescheduling rules) throughout the contract even after transferring rights. Conclude that responsibility for collection-conduct compliance stays with the financier regardless of outsourcing or sale; verify each circular's exact scope (finance companies generally vs. real-estate financiers under the Real Estate Finance Law) before applying one to the other's context.
