# Rules for Licensing Finance Support Activities

## Connections

### [[Amendment to Finance Support Activities Licensing Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the current text of the Finance Support Activities licensing regime, treat these two as the same instrument under amendment: node A is the Rules for Licensing Finance Support Activities and node B is the Governor's decision approving amendments to those same rules (both bear the identical subject line 'تعديل قواعد الترخيص للنشاطات المساندة لنشاط التمويل' and cite the same enabling authority — the SAMA Law and the Finance Companies Control Law). Because the amendment supersedes affected articles, do not rely on the pre-amendment wording for capital thresholds, licensing conditions, or scope of 'digital brokerage' without confirming which version is in force. Read the two together and apply the amended text to any digital-brokerage licensee.

### [[Cancellation of Debt Collection Licensing Requirement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on whether a finance-debt collection business needs a SAMA licence, read this cancellation decision alongside the finance-support licensing rules: node B is SAMA's decision cancelling the licensing requirement for practising finance-debt collection, and it grounds itself in the amendments to both the Finance Companies Control Law Implementing Regulations and the finance-support licensing rules (which already exclude debt collection from licensable support activities). The consequence is that debt collection is no longer a licensed activity, but previously licensed or pre-approved collection entities are directed to update their commercial-register activity accordingly. Conclude that a fresh SAMA collection licence is not required, while confirming the register-update obligation applies to legacy licensees.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Art 1):** "ولا يشمل ذلك نشاط تحصيل ديون جهات التمويل"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1):** "الموضوع: قرار إلغاء البنك المركزي السعودي لمتطلب الترخيص لمزاولة نشاط تحصيل ديون جهات التمويل"

### [[Circular on Outsourcing Debt Collection Operations]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping whether a debt-collection outsourcing arrangement must use a SAMA-licensed collection entity, note that the finance-support licensing rules expressly exclude debt collection from 'finance support activities,' and this circular is the instrument that removes the prior requirement that banks/finance companies only outsource collection to SAMA-licensed collection entities. The circular cites the amendments to both the Finance Companies Control Law Implementing Regulations and these support-activity licensing rules as its legal basis, cancelling the earlier restrictive circulars. For a compliance decision: outsourced collection now needs only a SAMA non-objection letter and compliance with relevant rules — you should not treat 'licensed collection entity' as a mandatory precondition, but must still verify the non-objection and outsourcing requirements.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Art 1):** "ولا يشمل ذلك نشاط تحصيل ديون جهات التمويل"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1-2):** "يكون إسناد عمليات تحصيل ديون البنوك والمصارف وشركات التمويل ... بعد الحصول على خطاب عدم ممانعة البنك المركزي"

### [[Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing licensing of finance-support businesses such as digital brokerage, read the Rules for Licensing Finance Support Activities as subordinate to, and definition-dependent on, the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law. The Support-Activities Rules expressly state that terms carry the meanings given in the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation, and that applications are made 'without prejudice' to that Law and Regulation. You should conclude that support-activity licensees are bound by the Regulation's defined terms and cannot be assessed in isolation; where the Rules are silent, the parent Regulation governs, so both instruments must be read together.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Article One–Four):** "يقصد بالألفاظ والعبارات الواردة في هذه القواعد المعاني المبيّنة لها في نظام مراقبة شركات التمويل ولائحته التنفيذية... مع عدم الإخلال بنظام مراقبة شركات التمويل ولائحته التنفيذية"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2):** "أصدر البنك المركزي السعودي هذه اللائحة وفقاً للصلاحيات المخولة له بموجب نظام مراقبة شركات التمويل"
