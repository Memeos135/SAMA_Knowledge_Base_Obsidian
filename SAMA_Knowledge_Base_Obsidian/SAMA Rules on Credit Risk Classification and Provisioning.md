# SAMA Rules on Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning

## Connections

### [[Rules on Management of Problem Loans]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's obligations for stressed and non-performing exposures, do not treat the Problem Loans Rules as self-contained: they are expressly issued to be read alongside the Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning Rules, so definitions and requirements on restructuring, forbearance, financial-difficulty identification, concession identification and stage allocation are sourced from the latter. Both instruments rest on SAMA's authority under the Banking Control Law and apply to all licensed banks. Practically, when determining whether a loan is problem/NPL or whether a restructuring counts as forbearance, verify the operative definitions in the Credit Risk Classification Rules rather than relying on the Problem Loans Rules alone.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / section 7):** "Guarantee- is a promise from a bank, corporate, any other entity or individual, that the liabilities of a borrower will be met in the event of failure to fulfil contractual obligations."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / 1.1):** "These rules should be read in conjunction with SAMA rules on Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning."
