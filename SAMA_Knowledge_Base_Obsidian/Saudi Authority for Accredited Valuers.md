# Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers

## Connections

### [[Circular on Real Estate Valuation Client Obligations]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank, real-estate finance company or insurer can lawfully engage a valuer, treat membership in the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers (Taqeem) as a hard eligibility gate, not a preference: the circular expressly forbids dealing with valuation practitioners who do not hold a primary-member card of the Authority. This obligation derives from the circular's stated legal basis in the Banking Control Law, the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law, the Finance Companies Control Law and the Accredited Valuers Law and its real-estate branch implementing regulation, which together designate these licensees as 'clients' of real-estate valuation. The reader should conclude that verifying Authority membership is a precondition to instructing any valuer, and a valuation obtained from a non-member is non-compliant regardless of its quality.
