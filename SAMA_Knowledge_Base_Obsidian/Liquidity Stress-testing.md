# Liquidity Stress-testing

## Connections

### [[Contingency Funding Plan (CFP)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When preparing a Contingency Funding Plan, do not design it in isolation from stress-testing: the guidelines require CFP invocation triggers, funding-source assessments and early-warning signals to be calibrated to and tested against liquidity stress scenarios, and both sit together in Annexure (1). The CFP must be a standalone document attached to the ILAAP, and its testing explicitly includes liquidity stress-tests and quantitative decline triggers. For a compliance decision, conclude that CFP assumptions (amount and speed of contingency funding, ability to sell assets under stress) must be consistent with the stress-testing outputs; a CFP whose triggers or funding estimates ignore the stress results would be internally inconsistent with SAMA's requirements.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 22 / Annexure (1) A.1):** "A Bank must conduct on a regular basis appropriate stress-tests so as to: a) Identify sources of potential liquidity strain"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 26 / Section C.i–ii):** "it should be prepared as a standalone document and attached to the ILAAP document... Regularly tested and updated... - Liquidity stress-test[ing]"

### [[ILAAP Process]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When judging whether an ILAAP submission is defensible, treat liquidity stress-testing as an embedded requirement of the ILAAP rather than a separable exercise: the guidelines require the ILAAP to analyse the internal stress-testing framework and feed its results into the risk framework and strategic planning. Section 9 and Annexure (1) set the substance (scenario design, governance, sensitivity analysis, results by risk driver), and stress results must inform the liquidity adequacy conclusion the Board signs. For a compliance decision, conclude that an ILAAP lacking a governed, challenged stress-testing programme whose outputs drive adequacy assessment would not meet the 'comprehensive and rigorous' standard SAMA expects.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 22 / Annexure (1)):** "Stress-testing is a generic term for the assessment of vulnerability of individual financial institutions and the financial system to internal and external shocks."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 19 / Section 9):** "Analyse the internal liquidity risk stress-testing framework, including the process and governance of and challenge to scenario design..."
