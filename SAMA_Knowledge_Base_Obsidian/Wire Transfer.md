# Wire Transfer

## Connections

### [[Record Keeping]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining retention duties for payment data, read the wire-transfer obligations as feeding directly into the record-keeping regime rather than as a self-contained set. Section 14 requires the institution to obtain, keep and verify originator/beneficiary information with each transfer, and Paragraph 14.6/14.7 expressly says the receiving institution 'shall verify the beneficiary's identity and keep this information as stated under the Record Keeping Section' — which imposes the ten-year retention floor from Paragraph 6.1. You would conclude that wire-transfer originator/beneficiary records inherit the general 10-year retention and retrievability/auditability standards, and check the Section 14 scope exclusions (14.8) before treating any transfer as outside the requirement.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 67 / Section 14):** "receiving a wire transfer shall verify the beneficiary's identity and keep this information as stated under the Record Keeping Section."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 45 / Section 6.1):** "The financial institution shall keep records for a period of no less than ten years from the date of the end of the business relationship... or completion of a transaction for a customer who has no business relationship."
