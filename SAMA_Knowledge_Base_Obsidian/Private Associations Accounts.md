# Private Associations Accounts

## Connections

### [[Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When onboarding a private association, treat MHRSD licensing and approval as a gating precondition, not merely a background fact. The rule scopes 'private associations' to those licensed by MHRSD, and sub-accounts for branches/activities and surplus-fund investment require MHRSD approval (and approved association rules) before the bank may proceed. Conclude that the bank must obtain and review the MHRSD licence and relevant MHRSD approvals as documentary conditions of opening and operating the account and its sub-accounts, and cannot open activity/branch sub-accounts absent that approval.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 63):** "sub-accounts may be opened under the main account upon obtaining the approval of the MHRSD and the supervisory authority for establishing such a branch"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 61 / 300.1.5.2):** "private associations licensed by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) to carry out different activities, including their branches"

### [[Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the account-opening documentation and controls for a private association, treat it as one of the juristic-person categories governed by the 300-series rules rather than a stand-alone regime. The private associations regime (300.1.5.2) sits under the broader 'Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons' framework, so the general juristic-person supervisory controls, dormant-account handling and disclosure/enforcement duties apply on top of the association-specific requirements (MHRSD licence, sub-account restrictions). Conclude that you must satisfy both layers — the general juristic-person rules and the association-specific overlay — and not rely on the association section in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16 / 5.2.2):** "the person authorized to operate the account if the account is for a juristic person"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Section 300 (Table of Contents)):** "Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons ... 300.2 Non-resident juristic persons"
