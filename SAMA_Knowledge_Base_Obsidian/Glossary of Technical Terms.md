### [[Key Covenants]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing restructuring documentation, use the guidance's definition of covenants to understand what the bank can enforce, since 'Key Covenants' is a technical term explained within the same SAMA restructuring guidance. The text defines covenants as undertakings whose breach gives the bank the right to call the loan, charge fees, or apply higher interest, and states that all restructuring agreements should contain them at a minimum. Conclude that covenant provisions are the enforceable trigger points a compliance reviewer should confirm are present (financial statements, tax payment, no sale without approval), and that a covenant default does not always translate into practical acceleration where the loan is performing.
