# Liquidity HQLA & Cash Flows

**Cohesion:** 0.06 - loosely connected
**Members:** 34 nodes

## Why this community

Basel III liquidity regime as implemented by SAMA: the Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), its HQLA stock and stressed net cash-flow denominator, the NSFR, and supplementary monitoring tools — defining minimum liquidity holdings for banks.

## How members connect

- LCR is the organizing obligation: it draws HQLA (numerator) and Total Net Cash Outflows (denominator = Cash Outflows less capped Cash Inflows), and cites the Basel III/II frameworks and the BCBS Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk Management as parent standards.
- HQLA is defined by tiering into Level 1/2A/2B assets, with Level 2B further conditioned by LTV — a definition/scope hierarchy.
- Cash-outflow categories (retail/wholesale/secured run-off, operational deposits) specify the assumed stress rates that populate the denominator.
- Alternative Liquidity Approaches (ALA Options 1–3, with peer-review requirement) are conditional exceptions for jurisdictions/currencies short of HQLA, and the Shari'ah-compliant bank treatment adapts HQLA eligibility — both scope-modifying links to the core LCR; NSFR sits as the complementary structural-funding measure.
