# Liquidity Risk

## Connections

### [[Risk Management Framework for Shari'ah Compliant Banking]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping a Shari'ah-compliant bank's risk obligations, treat Liquidity Risk as one of the enumerated categories the RMF requires banks to identify, measure, mitigate and report. The framework's introduction lists Liquidity Risk among the exposures arising from Shari'ah-compliant activities, and Section 10 requires banks to periodically review their liquidity risk profile and management policies and implement measures as market conditions change. You would conclude that liquidity obligations here supplement, not replace, SAMA's existing prudential liquidity requirements and must be integrated into the overall risk framework under Section 6.
