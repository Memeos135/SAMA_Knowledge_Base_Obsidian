# Reverse Stress Testing

## Connections

### [[Stress Testing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's stress-testing program is complete, note that reverse stress testing is a mandatory component, not merely one available technique. The Rules require banks to reverse stress test their business plan to failure and, where results reveal a business-failure risk inconsistent with risk appetite, to take effective remedial measures — this ties reverse stress testing directly to the remedial-action and viability requirements of the broader stress-testing framework. Conclude that a program lacking a viability-threatening reverse scenario would fall short of the Section 6 scenario-selection requirement and be exposed on SAMA's Section 12 review.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18 / Section 5.4):** "Banks are required to reverse stress test their business plan to failure i.e. the point at which the bank becomes unable to carry out its business activities"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 22 / Section 6):** "Stress tests should include scenario(s) that could threaten the viability of the institution (reverse stress testing)."
