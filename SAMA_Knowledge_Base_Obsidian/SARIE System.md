# SARIE System

## Connections

### [[Guide to Rules Governing Banks' Remittance Centers]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing domestic transfer obligations, note that SARIE is the designated channel for in-Kingdom transfers and carries a specific originator-information requirement: domestic transfers performed exclusively through SARIE must ensure the transferor's name and account number are present. This ties the payments-rail choice directly to the AML/CFT traceability requirements for wire transfers. A reader should conclude that domestic remittances routed through SARIE must satisfy the originator-data completeness standard, and that incomplete-originator handling rules apply where that information is absent.
