# Qualifying Master Netting Agreement

## Connections

### [[Securities Financing Transaction Exposures Calculation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When calculating SFT exposures with netting recognition, whether you may net across positions depends on satisfying the qualifying master netting agreement legal criteria — a written, bilateral agreement creating a single legal obligation, supported by reasoned legal opinions enforceable in all relevant jurisdictions on default/insolvency. The SFT netting formula (paragraph 9.63/9.64) can only be applied to transactions that meet those legal enforceability and daily-mark-to-market conditions. Before recognising netting benefits in the SFT exposure calculation, confirm the MNA meets the enforceability tests; absent qualifying documentation, exposures must be computed on a gross basis.
