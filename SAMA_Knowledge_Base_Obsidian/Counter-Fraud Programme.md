# Counter-Fraud Programme

## Connections

### [[Fraud Risk Assessment]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** Do not scope the Counter-Fraud Programme independently of the enterprise-wide Fraud Risk Assessment: the programme is required to be 'risk-based' and proportional, and 3.1.12 makes the Fraud Risk Assessment the mechanism that identifies inherent risks and informs which controls are required. In practice a compliance reader should treat the assessment as the input that justifies the programme's design and residual-risk decisions, and expect the assessment to be refreshed on new products, platforms, acquisitions or threats. Conclude that a programme not grounded in a current Fraud Risk Assessment is unlikely to satisfy the risk-based requirement.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10 / 3.1):** "develop a risk-based Counter-Fraud Programme proportional to the size and nature of its business"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11 / 3.1.12):** "conduct an enterprise-wide Fraud Risk Assessment... inform the controls required based on the level of inherent fraud risk identified"
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED/conceptual; the linkage rests on the 'risk-based' language rather than an explicit cross-reference — verify the control text before treating one as a precondition of the other.

### [[Supervisor Notification Form]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the fraud-response element of the Counter-Fraud Programme, note that it carries an external SAMA reporting duty: Appendix C requires immediate notification to SAMA's ORC department of new fraud typologies and significant internal/external fraud incidents and accounting irregularities. The programme's 'response' domain therefore is not purely internal — it triggers a supervisory obligation with prompt supplementation of information gaps as investigations progress. A compliance reader should conclude that significant incidents must be escalated to SAMA using the notification form, not merely managed within the organisation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10 / 3.1):** "adequate systems and controls to prevent, detect and respond to fraud"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 20 / Appendix C):** "SAMA requires immediate notification of new fraud typologies and significant fraud incidents to mitigate the risk of the fraud impacting additional customers"
- **Caveat:** Edge is INFERRED/conceptual; the notification duty is stated in Appendix C but its explicit tie to the programme concept is not verbatim — confirm the response-domain control text.
