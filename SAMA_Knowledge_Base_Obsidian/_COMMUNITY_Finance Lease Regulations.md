# Finance Lease Regulations

**Cohesion:** 0.06 - loosely connected
**Members:** 39 nodes

## Why this community

Non-bank finance company regime under SAMA: finance leasing (Ijara) and its standard contracts, the contract registry, BNPL, bancassurance, and the Shariah/governance controls that overlay these licensed activities. Covers who may conduct finance-company activity and the mandatory terms of consumer finance and lease contracts.

## How members connect

- Two parent laws anchor the cluster: Finance Companies Control Law (M/51) with its Implementing Regulation licenses and supervises the companies, while Finance Lease Law (M/48) and its Executive Regulation govern the lease product itself and the Contract Registry (Sijil) — subordinate decisions (Registrar powers, General Committee formation) sit beneath M/51.
- Contract-level nodes (Ijara contract, Acceptance Certificate, Loss/Insurance/Maintenance provisions, assignment/secondary market, Ownership Transfer) derive their mandatory content from the Finance Lease Law/Executive Regulation and register in the Sijil al-Uqud.
- Shariah Governance Instructions cite M/51 and its Implementing Regulation, imposing Board, Shariah Committee and Shariah oversight duties on finance companies — a governance layer applying across lease and BNPL activity.
- Consumer-facing rules (Consumer Finance Controls, Standard Vehicle Lease Contract, Early Repayment Guideline using Declining Balance, default/termination) cite the finance-company framework; note BNPL and bancassurance are separately scoped activities cross-referencing M/51, M/5 and M/32 respectively — distinct licensing regimes clustered by shared supervisor.
