# IRB Models Qualitative Disclosure

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure regime for banks permitted to use Internal Ratings-Based (IRB) approaches for credit risk capital. Covers transparency around internally modelled risk parameters.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: Template CRE prescribes qualitative disclosures on IRB model scope, governance, and parameter estimation.
- Applies only to banks with SAMA approval to use IRB; distinct from the standardised/external-rating regime (Table CRD) as an alternative capital measurement basis.
