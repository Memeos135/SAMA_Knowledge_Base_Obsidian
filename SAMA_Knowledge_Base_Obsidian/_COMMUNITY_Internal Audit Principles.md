# Internal Audit Principles

**Cohesion:** 0.83 - tightly connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

Governance/risk regime: SAMA's internal audit expectations for local banks, establishing the assurance function's structural independence and its risk-driven mandate as the third line of defence.

## How members connect

- The 'Internal Audit Principles for Local Banks' is the governing instrument; it references and operationalises the Three Lines of Defence Model, audit independence/objectivity, and risk-based planning as component obligations.
- Independence and Objectivity define the enforceable positioning of the audit unit within the Three Lines model — audit must sit in the third line, free of management influence.
- The Risk-Based Internal Audit Plan is the required output through which audit discharges its third-line role; its scope is driven by the bank's risk profile.
