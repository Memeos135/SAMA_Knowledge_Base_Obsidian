# Fit and Proper Form

## Connections

### [[Banking Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the legal force of the Fit and Proper Form's acknowledgements and penalties, treat them as anchored in primary banking legislation rather than as standalone administrative paperwork. The Form's undertaking invokes 'the Applicable Laws, Regulations, policies, and Instructions' and 'the prescribed penalties,' and for SAMA-supervised banks the enabling statute is the Banking Control Law, under which SAMA licenses banks and must give prior written approval for directors and managers (Article 12). You should therefore read the candidate's/institution's obligations here as enforcement points feeding SAMA's statutory no-objection power over senior appointments, not as mere contractual formalities.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16 / Acknowledgement):** "I hereby acknowledge that I have read all of the Applicable Laws, Regulations, policies, and Instructions... is a punishable violation under the provisions of the relevant laws and regulations."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 10 / Article 12):** "No person... shall be elected as a director or shall become a manager of any bank without prior written approval of SAMA"
- **Caveat:** Link is inferred from the Form's generic reference to 'Applicable Laws'; the Form does not cite the Banking Control Law by name, so verify the appointment regime's exact statutory basis for each supervised sector.

### [[Circular on Non-Objection for Senior Positions in Financial Institutions]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on senior-appointment obligations, read the Fit and Proper Form together with this circular because both operationalise the same underlying 'Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions in Financial Institutions Supervised by SAMA.' The Form requires it to 'be read in conjunction with' those Requirements, and the circular expressly cites the same Requirements and adds a distinct obligation: senior post-holders must obtain the institution's approval and SAMA's non-objection before accepting external nominations, mandates, or board memberships. You should conclude that the fit-and-proper assessment captured in the Form is not a one-time hurdle — the circular extends ongoing non-objection duties (with non-compliance falling under Article 70 of the Requirements) across the appointee's tenure.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3):** "This Form must be read in conjunction with the Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions in Financial Institutions Supervised by SAMA (Second Edition 2019)."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2):** "عدم الالتزام بذلك يُعد مشمولاً بحكم المادة (70) من المتطلبات المشار إليها أعلاه"
- **Caveat:** Link is inferred from the shared 'Requirements for Appointments' rather than a direct citation of the Form by the circular; confirm both instruments trace to the same edition of the Requirements.
