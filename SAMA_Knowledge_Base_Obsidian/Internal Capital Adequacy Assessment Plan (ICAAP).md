# Internal Capital Adequacy Assessment Plan (ICAAP)

## Connections

### [[Stress Testing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping a Saudi bank's regulatory reporting obligations, treat ICAAP and Stress Testing as linked but distinct duties under these Rules. Stress test results feed the capital planning and remedial-action assessment that underpins the ICAAP, yet the corpus makes clear the two carry separate reporting streams: ICAAP reporting serves capital planning while stress-testing reporting assesses the effectiveness of the framework. Conclude that a bank must satisfy both reporting requirements independently, and note the ICAAP submission requirement does not apply to branches of foreign banks, so scope obligations by entity type.
