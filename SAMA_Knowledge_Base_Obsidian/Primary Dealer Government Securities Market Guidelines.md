# Primary Dealer Government Securities Market Guidelines

## Connections

### [[Debt Management Office (DMO)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank-PD's compliance obligations, treat the Debt Management Office (DMO) as a second oversight point alongside SAMA, not a mere counterparty. The Guidelines require the bank to sign an undertaking agreement with the DMO, to report to SAMA any violations of that agreement's terms, and to subject DMO regulatory returns to concurrent annual audit; SAMA's no-objection requirement is even waived for banks already appointed by the DMO before the effective date. You should therefore check both the DMO undertaking agreement and these SAMA Rules when scoping a bank-PD's duties, since breach of the DMO agreement is itself a reportable event to SAMA.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5, section 2.G):** "Bank-PDs should report to SAMA any violations of the terms and conditions of undertaking agreement they sign with the Debt Management Office (DMO)."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6, section 3.C):** "subject the PD transactions and any regulatory returns submitted to the DMO and SAMA to concurrent annual audit."
