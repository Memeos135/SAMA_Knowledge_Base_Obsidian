# Fraud Risk Programme

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 3 nodes

## Why this community

Governance/risk regime for counter-fraud: the obligation to run a fraud programme, assess fraud risk, and notify the supervisor. Relevant to deciding required fraud controls and mandatory SAMA reporting.

## How members connect

- The Counter-Fraud Programme is the anchor obligation; Fraud Risk Assessment is a component input feeding it.
- The Supervisor Notification Form operationalises the reporting duty to SAMA arising under the programme.
- Distinguish from AML/CTF suspicious-activity reporting — this is a fraud-specific control and notification chain.
