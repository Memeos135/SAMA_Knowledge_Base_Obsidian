# Problem Loan Management

**Cohesion:** 0.27 - loosely connected
**Members:** 10 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA's regime for managing non-performing and problem loans: early identification, workout governance, restructuring viability, and conduct standards toward distressed borrowers. Sits at the intersection of prudential credit-risk classification and borrower-treatment conduct.

## How members connect

- The 'Rules on Management of Problem Loans' is the parent instrument referencing all operational components — Early Warning Signals, NPL Strategy, Workout Unit, Workout Plan, Restructuring Viability Assessment, Collateral Management, and the Problem Loans Code of Conduct.
- It cites BCBS 403 as the international prudential benchmark for problem-asset treatment and cross-references the SAMA Rules on Credit Risk Classification and Provisioning, linking workout activity to provisioning consequences.
- Component obligations chain operationally: NPL Strategy drives the Workout Unit; Workout Plans depend on Restructuring Viability Assessment and must comply with the Problem Loans Code of Conduct governing borrower treatment.
