# Risk-Based Approach

## Connections

### [[AMLCTF Guide|AML/CTF Guide]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a program meets SAMA expectations, treat the risk-based approach as the organizing principle the entire Guide references and requires institutions to adopt, calibrate, and update. The Guide requires the AML/CTF program, customer classification, monitoring and preventive measures all to be built 'in line with the risk assessment results' and mandates the risk-based approach be reassessed at least once every two years or when risk factors change. Conclude that program adequacy is judged against proportionality to identified risk — a static or one-size-fits-all program is non-compliant regardless of how thorough it appears; verify the update cadence and risk-linkage of any control you rely on.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 20 / Chapter IX Section 1):** "the risk-based approach shall be regularly reassessed and updated ... once every two years at a minimum or when risk factors change."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 25 / 2.1):** "develop an AML/CTF program that includes internal policies, procedures and controls to mitigate ML/TF risks in line with the risk assessment results approved by it"

### [[MLTF Risk Assessment|ML/TF Risk Assessment]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reading these two concepts, do not treat them as interchangeable: the ML/TF Risk Assessment is the input step and the Risk-Based Approach is the overarching method that consumes it. The Guide states 'the main step for a financial institution to adopt a risk-based approach is to assess, understand and document its ML/TF risks,' and requires customers to be classified and preventive measures calibrated 'according to the risk assessment results.' Conclude that a defensible risk-based program presupposes a documented, comprehensive risk assessment covering customers, products, geographies and channels; if the assessment is absent or outdated, the downstream classification and mitigation cannot be relied on as compliant.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 23 / 1.4):** "The financial institution shall classify all its customers according to the risk assessment results and take the necessary preventive measures"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 20 / Chapter IX Section 1):** "The main step for a financial institution to adopt a risk-based approach is to assess, understand and document its ML/TF risks"
- **Caveat:** Relation is 'conceptually_related_to'; the linkage of assessment as precondition to the approach is textually supported but the edge type is generic — confirm the specific sub-section dependencies in the primary text before relying.
