# Maturity Mismatch Treatment

## Connections

### [[Treatment of Credit Risk Mitigation for Securitization Exposures]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank may recognize purchased credit protection on a securitization exposure for capital relief, do not apply the securitization CRM rules in isolation: paragraph 18.64 explicitly routes maturity-mismatch capital treatment back to the general CRM maturity-mismatch mechanics in 9.10 to 9.14, and where hedged exposures have different maturities the longest must be used. This means the eligibility floor (original maturity ≥1 year, residual ≥3 months) and the Pa adjustment formula in Chapter 9 govern securitization hedges too. Conclude that any securitization CRM analysis must incorporate the Chapter 9 maturity thresholds, and note the carve-out that 1250% risk-weighted exposures ignore maturity mismatch entirely.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 252 / Art 18.64):** "for the purpose of setting regulatory capital against a maturity mismatch, the capital requirement will be determined in accordance with 9.10 to 9.14"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 244 / Art 18.35):** "Regulatory capital is required for banks' securitization exposures, including those arising from the provision of credit risk mitigants to a securitization transaction"
