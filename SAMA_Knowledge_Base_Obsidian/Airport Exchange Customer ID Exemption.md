# Airport Exchange Customer ID Exemption

## Connections

### [[Minimum Requirements for Exchange Center Premises]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the regulatory obligations of a licensed exchange center, read the premises-standards circular and the airport customer-ID exemption as complementary parts of the same money-changing (Sarrafa) supervisory regime rather than unrelated items, since both are issued by SAMA's exchange-centers supervision division and both draw on the rules governing money-changing business (the premises rules cite Article 15 of the Minister of Finance's Sarrafa regulations). Note however they operate at different layers: one sets minimum physical/technical requirements for premises (including AML systems and international sanctions-list linkage), the other is a narrow identity-verification exception. For a CDD decision, conclude that the airport exemption — accepting passport plus boarding pass for outbound-only travellers instead of the national ID — is a specific carve-out from the ordinary customer-acceptance/identity-verification rules referenced in the exchange procedures manual, and applies only to that defined customer category; the general AML and premises obligations remain in force.
