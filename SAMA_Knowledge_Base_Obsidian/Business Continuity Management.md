# Business Continuity Management

## Connections

### [[Material Outsourcing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When planning continuity for outsourced functions, note that the enhanced BCP duty is triggered specifically by the Material classification, not by outsourcing generally. Art 31-32 require a separate contingency plan for each Material outsourcing arrangement covering sudden termination or provider failure, plus documented alternatives and an in-house fallback procedure for each Material contract. You would conclude that materiality drives the depth of the continuity obligation — a non-Material arrangement does not attract the per-contract separate-contingency-plan requirement, but a Material one does.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12 / Art 31):** "For all Material outsourcing, banks should have a separate contingency plan for each outsourcing arrangement, which outlines the procedures to be followed in the event that the arrangement is suddenly terminated"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13 / Art 32):** "banks must set a procedure if they choose to bring the outsourced function in-house for each of their Material outsourcing contracts"
