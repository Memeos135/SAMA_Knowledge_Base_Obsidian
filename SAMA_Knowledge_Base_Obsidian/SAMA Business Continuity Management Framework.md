# SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework

## Connections

### [[Data Backup and Recoverability]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a Member Organization's data backup and recovery controls, do not read the Data Backup and Recoverability subdomain (3.3.10) in isolation, because it expressly requires alignment with a separate SAMA instrument. Control Requirement 3.3.10(2)(a) mandates that the data backup management policy be aligned with the 'SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework,' and the subdomain's RPO/RTO obligations tie recovery capability to BCM objectives. For a compliance decision you should conclude that backup adequacy must be tested against the BCM Framework's continuity requirements, and that satisfying backup controls alone does not evidence BCM alignment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 25 / 3.3.10):** "The data backup management policy should consider the following... a. alignment with SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 26 / 3.3.10):** "RTOs for critical services such as payment systems, customer related services... adequately defined considering the high availability."

### [[IT Governance Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the scope of IT-governance obligations, be aware the IT Governance Framework expressly disclaims the non-IT requirements of related corporate areas and coexists with SAMA's other mandated frameworks. The Business Continuity Management Framework is a distinct SAMA instrument addressing operational resilience, so obligations there are not discharged by complying with IT governance and vice versa. For a compliance decision you would conclude that BCM requirements must be assessed under their own framework and not folded into IT-governance self-assessment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 (referenced concept)):** "SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework (distinct mandated framework referenced as a related area)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / Section 1.3):** "This framework does not address the non-IT requirements for those areas."
- **Caveat:** BCM is referenced as a related SAMA framework concept; confirm the exact citation and interaction against the primary BCM Framework text before relying on any boundary between the two.

### [[IT Incident Management]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping incident-handling obligations, treat IT Incident Management (3.3.8) as connected to business continuity because both address service disruption and this subdomain carries a mandatory SAMA-reporting duty. The subdomain requires timely identification, response and handling of incidents impacting information assets and 'to report relevant incidents to SAMA, according to a defined communication protocol,' complementing the continuity objectives the BCM Framework governs. For a compliance decision you should conclude that incident response and external SAMA notification are enforceable Control Requirements, and that continuity planning does not substitute for the incident-reporting obligation. The specific reporting protocol/thresholds are not detailed in the provided text and should be verified in the primary instruments.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6-8 / Framework scope):** "The framework is mandated by SAMA... Member Organizations are responsible for implementing and complying with the framework."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 24 / 3.3.8):** "IT incident Management process should be established to timely identify, respond and handle IT incidents... and to report relevant incidents to SAMA, according to a defined communication protocol."
- **Caveat:** Link rests on shared continuity/disruption objectives; the exact BCM-to-incident cross-reference and SAMA reporting thresholds are not stated in the provided context and should be verified against the primary instruments.
