# Corporate Governance and Risk Management (FBB)

## Connections

### [[Regulations for Foreign Banks Branches (FBB)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping an FBB's governance and risk obligations, read the corporate-governance/risk-management provisions as an integral part of the FBB regulation, not an optional appendix: the same instrument imposes documented liquidity risk tolerance, an independent Head of Risk Management pre-approved under Fit and Proper rules, and (for significant-retail or systemic wholesale FBBs) a Risk Oversight Team. The link tells you these governance duties are conditioned on the branch's activity profile, so applicability depends on whether the FBB meets the significant-retail or systemic thresholds defined elsewhere in the regulation. Conclude that you must first classify the FBB's activity level, then apply the corresponding governance and risk-oversight requirements as mandatory for that class.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / para 44):** "a FBB that is involved in significant retail business or is a systemically important wholesale FBB should establish a mechanism for providing risk oversight to the branch's business activities"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / para 6):** "SAMA considers retail banking activities to be significant where an FBB: Has more than SAR 5 billion of retail and... account balances or more than 1,000 retail and MSME account holders"

### [[Senior Management Function (GMCEO)|Senior Management Function (GM/CEO)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When allocating accountability within an FBB, the GM/CEO Senior Management Function is the anchor of the corporate-governance and risk-management regime, not a peer of it. Section 2.4 fixes ultimate, non-delegable accountability in the GM/CEO for the branch's operations and risk governance, and the risk-management provisions build on that (e.g. the Head of Risk Management reports to and is accountable to the GM/CEO). You should conclude that responsibility for governance and risk failures traces to the pre-approved GM/CEO even where functions are performed by others, and that this individual must be SAMA-approved before assuming the role.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / Section 2.12):** "the branch Head of Risk Management should report into the GM/CEO"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Section 2.4):** "SAMA requires the GM/CEO to retain his or her overall accountability for the operations of the FBB"
