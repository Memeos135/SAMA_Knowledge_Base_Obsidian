# Use of Internal Ratings

## Connections

- **What this link tells you:** When checking whether a bank genuinely qualifies for IRB, note that the minimum requirements include a 'use test' — Section 6 (use of internal ratings) is listed inside Chapter 16 — meaning internal ratings and estimates must actually drive credit decisioning and risk management, not exist solely for capital calculation.
