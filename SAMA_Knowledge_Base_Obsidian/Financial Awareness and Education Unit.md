# Financial Awareness and Education Unit

- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a bank's obligations under SAMA's Customer Care Department rules, treat the Financial Awareness and Education Unit as a mandatory sub-component of that department, not a discretionary add-on. The instrument requires the Department to comprise at least three units — complaints handling, quality/performance analysis, and financial awareness and education — reflecting the OECD High-Level Principles on Financial Consumer Protection that this circular implements. For compliance you should conclude that failing to establish and resource the awareness unit is itself a breach of the department-structuring requirement, and that its consumer-education function is a supervised obligation.
