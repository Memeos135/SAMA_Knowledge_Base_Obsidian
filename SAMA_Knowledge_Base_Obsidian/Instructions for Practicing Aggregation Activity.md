# Instructions for Practicing Aggregation Activity

## Connections

### [[Aggregation Entity]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying who bears the compliance burden under these Instructions, note that the 'Entity' is the defined, licensed actor on whom nearly every operative obligation falls. Article 1.2.6 defines Entity as 'an entity licensed by SAMA to practice aggregation activity,' and Article 2.2 confirms the Instructions apply to such licensed entities, so the duties in Sections II–III (accuracy verification, retention, cybersecurity, disclosure, contracting) all attach to this single defined role. Conclude that any obligation phrased 'the entity shall' is enforceable against the license holder, and that carrying out the activity without the SAMA license (Art 2.3) is itself prohibited.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Art 2.2):** "These Instructions shall apply to entities licensed by SAMA to engage in the activity."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Art 1.2.6):** "Entity: An entity licensed by SAMA to practice aggregation activity."

### [[SAMA Business Continuity Management Framework (referenced)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the obligations of an entity licensed for aggregation activity, do not read the Aggregation Instructions in isolation: clause 2.4 expressly provides they 'shall not prejudice' other SAMA instruments, and lists the Business Continuity Management Framework among the binding overlay. This means BCM requirements continue to apply to the aggregation entity independently of the Instructions' own technical/operational provisions. You should check the BCM Framework directly when assessing operational-resilience compliance, rather than assuming the Aggregation Instructions capture the full continuity obligation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4, clause 2.4 / 2.4.4):** "These Instructions shall not prejudice the provisions contained in relevant laws and instructions, including ... The Business Continuity Management Framework issued by SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4, item 2.4.4):** "The Business Continuity Management Framework issued by SAMA."

### [[SAMA Cyber Security Framework (referenced)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing an aggregation entity's cybersecurity obligations, do not treat them as self-contained within these Instructions; the Instructions impose a standing duty to strengthen platform cybersecurity and immediately notify SAMA of any breach, which operates alongside SAMA's broader cyber and data-protection instructions referenced by the document. Article 2.4 states the Instructions 'shall not prejudice the provisions contained in relevant laws and instructions,' so any applicable SAMA cybersecurity framework continues to bind the entity in parallel. Conclude that breach-handling and notification duties must be read against SAMA's wider cyber regime rather than the aggregation text alone, and verify which specific SAMA framework applies to the licensed entity.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Art 8.1):** "The entity shall strengthen the platform’s cybersecurity and promptly deal with any cases of breach. In case of a breach, the entity shall immediately notify SAMA."
