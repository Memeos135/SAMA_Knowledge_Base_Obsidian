# Anti-Money Laundering Law

## Connections

### [[Law on Combating Financing of Terrorism]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping AML/CTF obligations for a Saudi FI or DNFBP, do not treat money-laundering and terrorist-financing rules as fully separate codes, because the CTF Law's own text cross-refers to the Anti-Money Laundering Law for the monitoring standard. Article 69 requires ongoing scrutiny of transactions 'as prescribed by the relevant provisions stipulated in the Anti-Money Laundering Law,' so the CTF preventive-measures regime borrows the AML transaction-monitoring baseline. For a compliance decision, conclude that TF-specific duties (risk assessment, EDD, STR reporting to the Directorate) sit on top of, and incorporate, the AML monitoring provisions — check both instruments together, not the CTF Law alone.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / Art 69):** "FIs and DNFBPs must monitor and scrutinize transactions... as prescribed by the relevant provisions stipulated in the Anti-Money Laundering Law..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1 / Chapter 1):** "Law: Combating Terrorism Crimes and their Financing Law"
- **Caveat:** The two node labels are drawn from the same source file; the link reflects an internal cross-reference from the CTF Law to the AML Law rather than two independent documents.

### [[Preventive Measures (FIs, DNFBPs, NPOs)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the operative CTF duties that bind FIs, DNFBPs and NPOs, treat Chapter Six 'Preventive Measures' (Arts 63–71) as the enforceable obligation set, and note it explicitly ties back to the Anti-Money Laundering Law for the monitoring standard. The preventive-measures articles impose TF risk assessment, risk-based CDD/EDD, ten-year record-keeping, ongoing transaction scrutiny (per the AML Law), STR reporting and tipping-off prohibitions. For compliance decisions, conclude that these are the concrete 'who must do what' requirements, and that failure to meet them exposes the entity and named individuals to the supervisory sanctions in Article ~82ff (warnings, orders, fines up to SAR 5,000,000, bans).
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / Art 69):** "...as prescribed by the relevant provisions stipulated in the Anti-Money Laundering Law..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 14 / Art 63):** "FIs, DNFBPs, and NPOs shall identify, assess, understand and document its financing of terrorism risks, taking into account a wide range of risk factors..."
- **Caveat:** Both nodes derive from the same source file; the edge reflects the Preventive Measures chapter's internal cross-reference to the AML Law monitoring standard.
