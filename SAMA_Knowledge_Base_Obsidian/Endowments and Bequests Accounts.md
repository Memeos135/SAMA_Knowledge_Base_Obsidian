# Endowments and Bequests Accounts

## Connections

### [[General Authority for Awqaf]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When opening or servicing an endowment (waqf) account, the General Authority for Awqaf is the controlling authority whose documents and approvals govern the whole relationship — the endowment rules require an Awqaf-issued registration certificate to open the account and Awqaf's written approval before any cross-border transfers or receipts to/from beneficiaries outside Saudi Arabia. This link shows Awqaf's role is not merely evidentiary at onboarding but a continuing gatekeeper over transactional permissions. You would conclude that outbound remittances or foreign beneficiary payments on these accounts are prohibited unless Awqaf approval is on file.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 73):** "Receiving remittances or checks from beneficiaries outside Saudi Arabia shall not be permitted without written approval from the General Authority for Awqaf"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 72 / Rule 300.1.5.8(b)):** "copy of the valid endowment registration certificate issued by the General Authority for Awqaf ... except ... after the bank obtains the official approval therefor from the General Authority for Awqaf"

### [[Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the account-opening and operating requirements for endowment (waqf) and bequest accounts, treat them as a sub-category governed by the broader juristic-persons framework in this same SAMA Account Rules document, not as a standalone regime. Both provisions sit within the '300' juristic-persons chapter and share its core controls (identification of authorized signatories, dual supervision, agent-of-heirs handling), so the endowments/bequests requirements supplement rather than replace the general juristic-person conditions. Practically, verify the specific endowment/bequest controls alongside the general 300-series requirements before concluding a compliant opening or operation is in place.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 16):** "the person authorized to operate the account if the account is for a juristic person"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Chapter III (300)):** "Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons"
