# Scoring and Bucketing

## Connections

### [[D-SIB Assessment Methodology]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing where a bank lands in the D-SIB regime, treat Scoring and Bucketing as the operative output stage of the Assessment Methodology: the methodology produces a score, and banks 'with a score above a certain level (the cut-off score) will automatically be classified as D-SIBs.' The bucket a bank is allocated to (four buckets plus a 4+1) directly determines the size of its HLA charge. For a compliance decision, note two things: designation is automatic above the 10% cut-off, and SAMA retains discretion to add banks scoring below it — so a below-cut-off score does not guarantee exemption.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6, paras 8-9):** "banks with a score above a certain level (the cut-off score) will automatically be classified as D-SIBs ... allocated to a bucket based on their scores"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4, para 3):** "an indicator-based measurement approach for assessing and designating D-SIBs in Saudi Arabia"

### [[Higher Loss Absorbency (HLA)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When estimating the magnitude of a bank's capital add-on, read Scoring and Bucketing and HLA as a single mechanism rather than two topics: the bucket a bank is allocated to under scoring is what sets its HLA charge, since 'the HLA requirement imposed on a bank is commensurate with the degree of systemic importance' and 'the HLA capital charge will be calculated by SAMA based on the bank's degree of systemic importance determined in the scoring exercise.' For a compliance decision, a change in the bank's score or bucket directly changes its CET1 HLA obligation, so the two must be monitored together, not in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6, para 9):** "D-SIB's will be allocated to a bucket based on their scores"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6, para 12):** "The HLA capital charge will be calculated by SAMA based on the bank's degree of systemic importance determined in the scoring exercise"
