# Master Netting Agreements for SFTs

## Connections

### [[Comprehensive Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When recognizing bilateral netting across SFTs, read the master-netting provisions as an integral part of the comprehensive approach rather than a separate regime: 9.63 states that all other haircut rules under the comprehensive approach (9.40-9.60) apply equivalently to banks using bilateral netting agreements for SFTs. Recognition is gated on the 9.61-9.62 conditions (legal enforceability in each jurisdiction, daily mark-to-market, eligible collateral), and the resulting E* becomes the exposure value/EAD. Conclude that netting benefit for SFTs is only available where those enforceability and revaluation conditions are met, and the comprehensive-approach haircut mechanics still govern the calculation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 79 / 9.61):** "The effects of bilateral netting agreements covering SFTs may be recognized on a counterparty-by-counterparty basis if the agreements are legally enforceable in each relevant jurisdiction"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 80 / 9.63):** "All other rules regarding the calculation of haircuts under the comprehensive approach stated in paragraphs 9.40 to 9.60 equivalently apply for banks using bilateral netting agreements for SFTs"
