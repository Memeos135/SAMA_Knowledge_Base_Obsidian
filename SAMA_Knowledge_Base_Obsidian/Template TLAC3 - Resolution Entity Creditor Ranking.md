# Template TLAC3 - Resolution Entity Creditor Ranking

## Connections

### [[Total Loss-Absorbing Capacity]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping TLAC3, treat it as the resolution-entity counterpart within the TLAC disclosure regime: TLAC3 gives creditors their ranking in each G-SIB resolution entity's liability structure and its eligibility rows ('potentially eligible as TLAC') depend on the TLAC standard's definitions. The template is expressly the same as TLAC2 except it omits exposure-to-the-resolution-entity data because it describes the entity itself. For a reviewer, this confirms TLAC3 applies to G-SIB resolution entities under the TLAC standard, and its qualifying-liability tests must be read against the same TLAC eligibility criteria rather than general capital rules.
