### [[Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a real-estate financier on selling residential mortgage portfolios to the Saudi Real Estate Refinance Company (SRC), use this circular as the operative rule: it reduces the financier's recourse period and — critically — exempts SRC-destined portfolios from the SAMA no-objection requirement that otherwise governs disposal of finance assets. The exemption is grounded in the circular's carve-out from the general 'transfer of finance assets' rules requiring a prior no-objection request. Conclude that for SRC sales the no-objection step is waived, but check that all other statutory contract periods still apply and that SAMA is notified of the disposal within five business days of signing the final sale contract.
