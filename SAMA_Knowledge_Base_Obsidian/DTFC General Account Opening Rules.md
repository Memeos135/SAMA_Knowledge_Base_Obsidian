# DTFC General Account Opening Rules

## Connections

### [[Account Freezing and Updating Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a DTFC's ongoing obligations for General Accounts, do not read the opening rules as the end of the diligence duty — the freezing/updating chapter converts the account-opening acknowledgements into live enforcement triggers. The opening agreement obliges the customer to renew ID documents and consents to freezing if data is not updated, and Chapter 11 makes freezing mandatory (e.g. within 90 days of expiry of the juristic entity's authorization). You should conclude that the DTFC must monitor validity of IDs and authorizations continuously and freeze non-compliant accounts, and that the contractual freeze right documented at opening is what makes that action legally clean.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 37 / account opening acknowledgements):** "Acknowledges that the DTFC will freeze the account if account holder does not update or renew its data."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 47 / Chapter 11, para 127):** "All DTFCs must freeze all General Accounts of juristic entities after 90 days from the expiration date of the respective authorization."

### [[Accounts Operating Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how a DTFC must respond to official disclosure or blocking demands, read the operating rules together with the opening data-capture requirements, because the search obligation depends on data gathered at opening. Chapter 14 requires that all communications on disclosure/blocking pass through SAMA only, and that any search cover every category of account (active, closed, suspense, inactive, dormant), while the opening chapter fixes what customer and authorized-person data must be recorded. You should conclude that a DTFC cannot respond directly to a court or other authority — only via SAMA — and that its disclosure reply must confirm all account categories were searched.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 36 / Chapter 8, para 84):** "DTFCs must obtain and record the following information / documents (at a minimum) before opening General Account."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 53 / Chapter 14, para 178-179):** "communications between these official authorities and the DTFCs should be made through SAMA only... include... all active, closed, suspense, inactive, dormant... accounts."

### [[Closing of the Account]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a Deposit-Taking Finance Company (DTFC) on the full lifecycle of a General Account, treat closing rules and opening/funding rules as a single continuous regime rather than isolated chapters. The opening rules fix the source-of-funds principle (General Account may only be credited from the accountholder's own bank account or linked Term Deposit Account), and the closing/withdrawal rules enforce the mirror: funds must be remitted back to the same bank account from which the General Account was initially funded. You should therefore conclude that at closing you cannot pay out to an unrelated third-party account, and that the audit trail captured at opening (remitter name, IBAN) governs where money may legitimately return.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 55 / para 196-197):** "General Account can ONLY be funded... DTFC must match the name of remitter with the name on General Account before crediting the funds."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 56 / para 206):** "Finance must remit the funds back to the same Bank account of the account holder from which General Account was initially funded i.e. the source."

### [[Inactive and Dormant Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the controls attaching to a General Account over time, note that inactivity/dormancy is a separate status regime from freezing and applies even to compliant customers. Chapter 12 requires a DTFC to classify a General Account with no movement for one year as 'Inactive' (dual control to reactivate) and after two years as 'Dormant' (higher-authority dual control), expressly stating this 'has no relation with the rules of account freezing.' You should therefore treat dormancy and ID-expiry freezing as parallel, cumulative triggers, and check that reactivation controls exist independently of the opening/funding rules — and that a linked live Term Deposit Account pauses the inactivity clock.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 55-56 (General Account funding/withdrawal rules)):** "Funds can only be withdrawn from General Account based on the instructions or agreement of the accountholder."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 49 / Chapter 12, para 143):** "If a General Account completes an one year period with no movement whatsoever... DTFCs must consider such General Account as "Inactive" and should be subjected to dual control."

### [[Term Deposit Account Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When structuring a DTFC's deposit products, treat the General Account and Term Deposit Account as strictly dependent, not free-standing: a Term Deposit Account can only be opened where the same customer already holds an active General Account, and it may only be credited from — and repaid to — the General Account to which it was linked at initiation. The opening rules state the General Account's sole purpose is to facilitate Term Deposit operations, and Chapter 10 forbids de-linking and re-linking a Term Deposit Account to a different General Account. You should conclude that funds cannot circulate between unrelated customers or accounts through this pairing, and that the linkage fixed at initiation is legally binding and non-transferable.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 55 / para 195, 200):** "the sole purpose of the General Account is to facilitate operations of Term Deposit Accounts... DTFC must credit the principle and profit amount separately to the General Account from which Term Deposit Account was funded."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 46 / Chapter 10, para 119-122):** "A General Deposit Account of the same customer is already open and active... DTFCs must not de-link the Term Deposit Account with the General Account to which it was linked."
