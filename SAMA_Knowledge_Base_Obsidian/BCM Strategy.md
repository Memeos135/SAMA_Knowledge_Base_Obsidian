# BCM Strategy

## Connections

### [[Business Continuity Management Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating BCM Framework compliance, treat BCM Strategy (clause 2.2) as a required Framework element that must be defined, approved, implemented and maintained, and aligned to the organization's strategic objectives. The Framework sets out the strategy's minimum content — long-term objectives, a road map with timelines, and requirements for continual review of alignment — so a program lacking a documented, approved strategy is non-compliant. You should confirm the strategy exists as an approved artefact and that its stated objectives and roadmap tie into the wider BCM program rather than sitting as an abstract statement.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7–8 / 2.2):** "To ensure that business continuity Initiatives are in alignment with the strategic business objectives and embeds BCM as part of the good management practice."
