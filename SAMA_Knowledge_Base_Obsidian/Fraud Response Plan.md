# Fraud Response Plan

## Connections

### [[Respond Domain]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping the mandatory documents under the Respond domain, treat the Fraud Response Plan as the core deliverable of that domain, not an optional add-on. Section 6 (Respond) states its principle as requiring Member Organisations to 'define, approve, implement and maintain a Fraud Response Plan,' with control requirements mandating monitoring of compliance and periodic effectiveness evaluation. A compliance assessor should verify the plan exists, is approved and maintained, and covers the minimum contents (alerting methods, roles, escalation authority) before concluding the Respond domain is satisfied.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 51 / Sec 6.1):** "Member Organisations should define, approve, implement and maintain a Fraud Response Plan to outline the organisational response to an actual or suspected fraud incident."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9 / Sec 2.1):** "The Framework is structured around four main domains, namely: Governance, Prevent, Detect, Respond."
