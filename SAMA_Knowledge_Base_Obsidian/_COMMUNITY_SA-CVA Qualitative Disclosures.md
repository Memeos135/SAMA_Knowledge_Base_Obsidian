## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure regime specific to the Standardised Approach for CVA risk (SA-CVA), a more risk-sensitive method requiring SAMA approval.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: Table CVAB prescribes qualitative disclosures for banks using SA-CVA.
- Subordinate/complementary to the general CVA disclosure (Table CVAA, community 282); SA-CVA use is conditional on supervisory approval, so CVAB applies only to that subset of banks.
