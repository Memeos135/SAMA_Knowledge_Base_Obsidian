# Threat Intelligence

## Connections

### [[Red Team]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a Member Organization's obligations under the F.E.E.R. Framework, treat threat intelligence provision as a mandatory input to Red Team activity rather than an optional aid. The scenario phase requires the Green and White Teams to independently supply available Threat Intelligence to the Red Team, which combines it with its own sources to design attack scenarios subject to Green Team approval. A compliance reviewer should therefore check that the intelligence-sharing and combining step actually occurred and was approved, because scenario validity — and the resilience conclusions drawn from the exercise — depend on it.
