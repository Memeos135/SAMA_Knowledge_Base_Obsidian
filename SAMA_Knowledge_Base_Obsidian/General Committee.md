# General Committee

### [[Decision Forming General Committee of GMs in Finance Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping who holds authority in the finance-companies committee structure created by this circular, read the General Committee as the apex body: the circular is the Governor's decision forming the 'General Committee of General Managers in Finance Companies' under Article 98 of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law, which empowers SAMA to form committees to submit proposals and recommendations to develop the finance sector. The document defines and constitutes this Committee, so its Terms of Reference govern the Committee's composition, nomination/removal, quorum, and responsibilities. For a compliance decision you would treat the General Committee's mandate as advisory (proposals/recommendations) rather than a rule-making body, and check the attached Terms of Reference for its precise remit.

### [[Executive Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** In reading the committee hierarchy for finance companies, distinguish the Executive Committee from the General Committee rather than conflating them: the Terms of Reference for finance-companies committees lays out separate sections for 'اللجنة العامة' (General Committee) and 'اللجنة التنفيذية' (Executive Committee), each with its own formation, nomination/removal, quorum, agenda/minutes, and responsibilities. The link signals that both sit within the same governance framework issued under Article 98 of the Implementing Regulation. For a governance or compliance decision, do not assume the two share the same authority or membership rules; consult each committee's dedicated section in the Terms of Reference for its distinct remit.
