# Amendment to Finance Support Activities Licensing Rules

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping which rules bind a digital brokerage or other finance-support entity, treat these two instruments as one hierarchy: the Finance Support Activities Licensing Rules expressly derive their defined terms from, and operate subordinate to, the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulations. The Rules state that undefined terms carry the meaning given in the Control Law and its Implementing Regulations, and apply 'without prejudice' to them. Conclude that support-activity licensees remain bound by the Implementing Regulations' definitions and general provisions; do not read the licensing Rules as a self-contained regime.

### [[Rules for Licensing Finance Support Activities]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the current text of the Finance Support Activities licensing regime, treat these two as the same instrument under amendment: node A is the Rules for Licensing Finance Support Activities and node B is the Governor's decision approving amendments to those same rules (both bear the identical subject line 'تعديل قواعد الترخيص للنشاطات المساندة لنشاط التمويل' and cite the same enabling authority — the SAMA Law and the Finance Companies Control Law). Because the amendment supersedes affected articles, do not rely on the pre-amendment wording for capital thresholds, licensing conditions, or scope of 'digital brokerage' without confirming which version is in force. Read the two together and apply the amended text to any digital-brokerage licensee.
