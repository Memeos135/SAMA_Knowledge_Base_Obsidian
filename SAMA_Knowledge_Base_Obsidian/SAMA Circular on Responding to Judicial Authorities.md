# SAMA Circular on Responding to Judicial Authorities

## Connections

### [[Responding to Banking Dispute Committee Requests]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's or finance company's obligations toward SAMA-supervised dispute bodies, treat these two circulars as a chain: the later circular (4827) expressly builds on the earlier one (4437) requiring prompt response to judicial authorities, then extends it to requests from the General Secretariat of the Banking and Finance Dispute Committees, adding fixed response deadlines set out in the annexed schedule. The 4827 text states it is issued 'in continuation of' the earlier SAMA instruction on speed of response to judicial bodies. Conclude that the earlier general duty of timely response still applies, but the operative, enforceable deadlines (e.g. seven or ten working days by document type, and a not-to-exceed period from the date of request) come from 4827's annex; both must be read together, and SAMA warns it will take regulatory action for non-compliance.
- **Grounding — this node (4437 Page 1):** "English paraphrase: circular emphasising the duty of SAMA-supervised entities to respond promptly to judicial authorities and their requests within statutory time limits."
- **Grounding — related node (4827 Page 1-2):** "English paraphrase: in continuation of SAMA circular [4437], confirming prompt response to judicial bodies; entities must respond to General Secretariat requests within the periods in the attached schedule."
- **Caveat:** Both source documents are Arabic and OCR-garbled; excerpts are English paraphrases of the readable content, and the cross-reference circular number in 4827 is partly illegible. Verify the primary Arabic text before relying on specific deadlines.
