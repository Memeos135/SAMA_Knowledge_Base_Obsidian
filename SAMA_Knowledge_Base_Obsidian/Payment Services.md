# Payment Services

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulation for Payments Law (SAMA 1430)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When deciding whether a given activity falls within the Payments Law regime, anchor the analysis in this Regulation's own scope provisions, because it defines its terms by reference to the Payments Law (النظام: نظام المدفوعات وخدماتها, Royal Decree M/26) and dedicates Book Six to related payment services (الباب السادس: خدمات المدفوعات ذات الصلة). Whether an activity is a 'payment service' determines whether licensing, customer-protection and complaint obligations bite at all. A reader should first classify the activity against the Regulation's payment-services definitions before applying downstream duties, and rely on the Law's defined meanings where the Regulation does not restate them.

### [[Licensing of Payment Service Providers]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether an activity requires SAMA authorisation, read licensing and 'payment services' together: the licensing requirements are framed as licensing of payment service providers, so the scope of what counts as a payment service determines who must be licensed. The Regulation's contents place licensing requirements (Part Four, Chapter Three) and the related-payment-services provisions (Part Six) within the same instrument implementing the Payments and Services Law. You would conclude that classification of an activity as a regulated payment service is the trigger for the licensing obligation, so verify the definitional articles and the licensing chapter in the primary text before treating any activity as in or out of scope.
