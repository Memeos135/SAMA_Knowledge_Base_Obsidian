# Resident Companies Accounts

## Connections

### [[Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When onboarding a resident company, apply the juristic-persons rulebook as the governing frame — resident-company accounts are one branch of the 300-series covering resident and non-resident juristic persons. This matters because the same document sets differentiated documentation and AML/CTF conditions by category (e.g. GCC vs non-GCC, contracts/projects in KSA, payment-card companies), and cross-refers duties such as Article (11) AML Law and Article (66) Terrorism Crimes Law for related juristic-person accounts. Conclude that you must place the resident company in the correct 300-series sub-category to identify the precise verification and prohibited-dealing checks, rather than treating 'resident company' as a single generic bucket.
