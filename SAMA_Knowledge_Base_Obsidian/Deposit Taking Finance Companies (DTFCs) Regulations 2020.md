# Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) Regulations 2020

## Connections

### [[Account Opening Rules for Juristic Persons]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping account-onboarding obligations for a DTFC, treat the Account Opening Rules for Juristic Persons as a binding sub-regime of the parent DTFC Regulations rather than a standalone practice — the document expressly prohibits opening General or Term Deposit Accounts for natural persons and confines them to juristic persons under Section 3, so a DTFC cannot accept individual depositors. Note the AML/CTF overlap embedded here: the account-opening acknowledgments require the holder to confirm legitimacy of funds and beneficial ownership, and the DTFC's freeze rights on suspected fraud. Conclude that any DTFC deposit onboarding must document board members, senior managers, authorised persons and beneficial-owner verification, and that remote onboarding for new customers is not permitted (in-person verification of originals/signatures is required).
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / paras 4-5):** "DTFCs are authorized to mobilize savings and time deposits from non-individual customers... maintain one or more records of specified particulars in the case of every depositor"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 36 / paras 82-83):** "DTFCs must NOT open General Accounts and Term Deposit Accounts in the name of Natural Persons... only ... for juristic Persons in accordance with the rules stipulated in the Section 3"

### [[Asset Quality and Loan Classification]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a DTFC's credit and provisioning obligations, read the Asset Quality chapter as mandatory prudential rules of the parent Regulations, not as accounting discretion — every DTFC must review, classify and provision its loan portfolio at least quarterly using the classification criteria and the fixed minimum provisioning percentages (Normal 1% through Loss 100%). The rules also impose contagion classification (one non-performing loan taints all loans to that borrower) and profit-suspension on non-performing exposures. Conclude that provisioning cannot simply follow IFRS where these Regulations require more: where regulatory provisions exceed IFRS impairment, the higher regulatory figure and statutory loan loss reserve treatment governs.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / para 3):** "DTFCs are also required to comply with the Finance Companies Control Law, SAMA regulations for Finance Companies (FCs), and other relevant laws and regulations"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 11-12 / paras 35-45):** "Every DTFC shall review, classify and appropriately make provisions for its loan portfolio at least once every three months... For loans classified 'Loss', 100%"

### [[Capital Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining a DTFC's minimum solvency obligations, the Capital Requirements chapter binds every DTFC to a 20% capital adequacy ratio at all times and to prescribed minimum capital, with SAMA empowered to impose higher ratios on individual firms exhibiting losses, high risk exposure, poor asset quality or rapid under-capitalised growth. This links directly to the asset-quality regime because risk-weighting and the loan loss reserve (capped at 1.25% of risk-weighted assets as supplementary capital) feed the ratio calculation. Conclude that a DTFC cannot treat 20% as a fixed ceiling — SAMA retains discretion to set a firm-specific higher minimum, and capital planning must reconcile total capital against reported shareholders' funds.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / para 20):** "maintain records ... to enable proper computation of the institution's capital adequacy of 20%; and maintain the prescribed minimum capital requirements"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / para 22):** "SAMA may require higher minimum capital ratios for an individual DTFC based on, but not limited to the following criteria"

### [[Corporate Governance and Risk Management]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping a DTFC's governance and control obligations, note that the Corporate Governance and Risk Management chapter imposes mandatory, proportionate arrangements including per-senior-manager job descriptions tied to SAMA fit-and-proper pre-approval and a maintained Management Responsibilities Description Document (MRDD). Critically, this chapter's related provisions also carry the AML/CTF supervisory-officer and ongoing-monitoring duties — the Compliance Officer coordinates with Internal Audit, must have unrestricted access to customer and transaction records, and DTFCs must monitor, risk-classify and report suspicious activity to the Financial Investigation Unit and SAMA. Conclude that governance and AML functions here are intertwined obligations of the same firm and must be scoped together, and that senior-manager appointments require SAMA pre-approval.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / para 3):** "DTFCs are also required to comply with the Finance Companies Control Law, SAMA regulations for Finance Companies (FCs), and other relevant laws and regulations"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / paras 10-11; Page 51 / paras 165-169):** "robust governance and risk management arrangements... Be included in every application to SAMA for pre-approval as a senior manager... report these to the Financial Investigation Unit, and inform SAMA"

### [[Deposit Taking Finance Company (DTFC)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether these Regulations apply to a given firm, the defined term 'Deposit Taking Finance Company' is the scoping gate: the rules apply only to finance companies authorized to mobilize savings and time deposits from non-individual customers and to lend out of those deposits, subject to the Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree M/51). The definition also frames the account architecture — General Accounts exist solely to facilitate Term Deposit Accounts, with strict linking, name-matching and permitted-credit-source rules. Conclude that a firm not authorised for deposit-taking falls outside this instrument (though still bound by general FC rules), and that the DTFC's deposit operations must respect the closed funding channels and remitter name-matching controls defined for the General Account.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / paras 1-2):** "These SAMA regulations are applicable to all Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) operating in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / para 2; Page 55 / paras 195-197):** "finance companies that are authorized to mobilize savings and time deposits from non-individual customers... DTFC must match the name of remitter with the name on General Account"

### [[Liquidity Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing prudential exposure for a Deposit Taking Finance Company, treat the Liquidity Requirements chapter as an operative, quantified obligation embedded in the parent DTFC Regulations 2020, not a soft standard. Chapter 5 imposes hard, enforceable minimums — a 20% liquid-asset holding against deposit liabilities, a statutory deposit with SAMA of not less than 4%, a 15x cap of deposit liabilities to total capital, and monthly Appendix B liquidity returns due by the 15th business day. You should conclude these ratios are binding licence conditions for any DTFC authorized to mobilize deposits, and check both the reporting deadline mechanics and the remedial one-month window for breaching the 15x cap.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / para 4):** "observing liquidity ratios with regard to its liquid assets vis-a-vis total deposit liabilities and other prudential regulations as prescribed for DTFCs"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 10 / Chapter 5, paras 29-32):** "Every DTFC shall maintain a minimum holding of liquid assets of twenty per cent (20%) of all its deposit liabilities... a statutory deposits of a sum not less than 4%"

### [[Rules Governing Credit Risk Exposure Classification and Provisioning]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping which SAMA rules apply to a Deposit Taking Finance Company, do not treat the DTFC Regulations as self-contained: both instruments derive from the same parent (Finance Companies Control Law, Royal Decree M/51, and its Implementing Regulation), and the DTFC Regulations expressly require DTFCs to also comply with SAMA regulations applicable to all Finance Companies. You would conclude the credit-risk classification and provisioning Rules apply to a DTFC in addition to its DTFC-specific prudential requirements, so both must be checked together for a full obligation set.
