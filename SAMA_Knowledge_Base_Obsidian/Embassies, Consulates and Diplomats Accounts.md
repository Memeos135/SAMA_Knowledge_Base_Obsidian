# Embassies, Consulates and Diplomats Accounts

## Connections

### [[Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When classifying embassy, consulate and diplomat accounts, note they are enumerated under Section 300 (Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons), specifically 300.1.6 for 'foreign embassies, consulates, diplomats, airlines, multilateral organizations, and their employees.' This tells you the diplomatic-account rules inherit the juristic-persons framework's structure and controls, with sub-rules distinguishing resident diplomats (300.1.6.2) from temporary-visit diplomats (300.1.6.3). Conclude that compliance requires applying the correct juristic-persons sub-rule for the diplomatic entity's status alongside the specific MOFA-documentation and closure requirements.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Rule 300.1.6):** "300.1.6 Rules for foreign embassies, consulates, diplomats, airlines, multilateral organizations, and their employees"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Section 300):** "300. Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons ... 300.1 Resident juristic persons (including embassies and multilateral organizations)"

### [[SAMA (Saudi Central Bank)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When onboarding embassies, consulates or diplomats, note that SAMA is not merely the rule-maker but a required recipient of notice — for temporary-visit diplomats the Rules require both the compliance-department manager's approval and that 'SAMA must be informed about opening the account.' The link therefore signals that this account category carries a specific SAMA-notification obligation on top of standard documentation. Conclude you should confirm the notification step and closure-on-expiry requirements are met, not just the identity documents.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 80 / Rule 300.1.6.3):** "approval of the manager of the bank's compliance department for opening the account shall be sought. SAMA must be informed about opening the account. Such accounts must be closed when the visit (the assignment) term expires."
