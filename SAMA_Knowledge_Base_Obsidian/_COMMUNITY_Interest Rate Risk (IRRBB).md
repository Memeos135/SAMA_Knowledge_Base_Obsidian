## Why this community

Interest rate risk in the banking book — a Pillar 2 / supervisory risk regime for measuring and managing IRRBB in KSA banks.

## How members connect

- The SAMA IRRBB rules cite the Basel IRRBB Standard (2016) as their source, transposing its measurement and disclosure expectations.
- Legal linkage: the Basel standard supplies the substantive methodology, but enforceable obligations derive from SAMA's adoption; apply the standard as implemented locally, not in the abstract.
