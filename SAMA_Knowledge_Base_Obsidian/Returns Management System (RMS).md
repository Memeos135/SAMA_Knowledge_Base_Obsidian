# Returns Management System (RMS)

## Connections

### [[Exchange Centers Supervisory Data Forms Update (RMS)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the mandatory channel for supervisory returns, note that the circular designates the Returns Management System (RMS) as the exclusive submission platform: exchange centers must complete and periodically deliver the supervisory data forms through the RMS portal, and the attached manual defines RMS as the digital platform enabling supervised firms to submit returns. This ties the reporting obligation to a specific delivery mode, not merely to the substance of the data. A compliance reviewer should conclude that filings outside RMS, or that fail its validation rules, do not discharge the obligation and expose the center to statutory action.
