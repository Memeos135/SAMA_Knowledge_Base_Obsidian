# Leased Asset Recovery

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Recovery/repossession rights over leased movable assets under the finance-lease regime, linking the enabling law to SAMA's operational instruction.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Finance Lease Law is the primary statute; the Circular on Recovery of Leased Movable Assets is subordinate implementing detail.
- The circular references the Law to authorize and condition how lessors recover leased movables — treat the circular as setting the permissible recovery procedure.
- Compliance consequence: repossession must be exercised within the statutory lease framework as operationalized by the circular; deviation risks unlawful recovery.
