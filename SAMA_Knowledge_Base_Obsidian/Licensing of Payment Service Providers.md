### [[Implementing Regulation for Payments Law (SAMA 1430)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether an entity may lawfully provide payment services in KSA, treat licensing as a threshold requirement embedded in this Regulation, since the Regulation contains a dedicated chapter on licensing requirements for payment service providers (الفصل الثالث - متطلبات الترخيص لمقدمي خدمات المدفوعات) and defines 'Licence' (الترخيص) as the authorisation granted by the Central Bank. The covering circular confirms SAMA mandates that PSPs and payment-system operators subject to the Law and Regulation comply with all its provisions. A reader should conclude that unlicensed provision is not permitted and should check the licensing chapter and the 'controller' definition for who must be assessed on ownership/control.
