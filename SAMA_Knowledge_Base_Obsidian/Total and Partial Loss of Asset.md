# Total and Partial Loss of Asset

### [[Asset Maintenance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When allocating risk for damage to the financed (leased) asset under this model Ijarah contract, read the Asset Maintenance article together with the Total/Partial Loss article: maintenance defines the tenant's ongoing upkeep duties, while the loss article governs what happens when the asset is destroyed in whole or in part, and the boundary between recoverable maintenance failures and a loss event determines which party bears the cost. Both are enumerated articles of the same SAMA-mandated standard form (صيانة الأصل المؤجر and الهلاك الكلي والجزئي للأصل المؤجر appear consecutively in the contract index). For a compliance decision, confirm that the contract's maintenance obligation does not shift onto the tenant losses that the total/partial-loss article assigns elsewhere.

### [[Insurance Provisions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a lessor on how to structure the Ijara real-estate finance contract, treat the insurance provisions and the total/partial-loss provisions as a single risk-allocation package rather than two separate clauses. The mandatory model contract issued under this circular contains a dedicated 'Total and Partial Loss of Asset' article (الهلاك الكلي والجزئي للأصل المؤجر), and insurance is the mechanism that determines who bears the financial consequence when that loss materialises. The practical conclusion: verify in the primary model text that the insurance article and the loss article are internally consistent on who insures, who claims, and how proceeds settle the outstanding amount — the lessor cannot deviate from these standardised clauses.

### [[Standard Vehicle Finance Lease Contract for Individuals]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are mapping loss-of-asset treatment across SAMA's standard individual finance contracts, note that the total/partial-loss concept here appears in the real-estate Ijara model, while the linked node is the separate Standard Vehicle Finance Lease Contract for Individuals — both are SAMA-mandated model contracts issued under the Finance Lease Law and its Executive Regulation.
