# Governance

## Connections

### [[Expected Credit Loss Provisioning]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assigning accountability for provisioning, note the Rules place the ECL obligation squarely on the board of directors — it is the board's responsibility to maintain adequate ECL provisions and oversee appropriate credit risk practices, with methodologies reviewed at least annually and adequacy reported to the board or delegated committees at least quarterly. The Governance section defines the required framework (key terms, roles, methods, inputs, validation) that ECL estimation must satisfy. For compliance you should conclude that provisioning is a board-level oversight duty with defined minimum framework content, so gaps in governance documentation are themselves a breach of the ECL requirement, not just a best-practice shortfall.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9 / Section 2):** "It is the responsibility of the board of directors of finance companies to maintain ECL provisions at an adequate level and to oversee that the company has adopted appropriate credit risk practices"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 23 / Section 4):** "Review, evaluate, update, and report to the Board or Board delegated committees on the adequacy of its exposure and expected credit losses at least quarterly."
