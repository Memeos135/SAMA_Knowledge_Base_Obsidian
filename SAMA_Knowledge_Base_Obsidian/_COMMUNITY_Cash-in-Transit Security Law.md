# Cash-in-Transit Security Law

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

Statutory regime governing the physical transport of money, precious metals and valuable documents in KSA (cash-in-transit), including nationality restrictions on security escort personnel.

## How members connect

- The Law on Transport of Money, Precious Metals & Valuable Documents (enacted by Royal Decree) is the primary legislation; the two node forms represent the law and its enacting decree.
- The Prohibition on Dealing with Non-Saudi Security Escort Reps and the Contracting Cash-in-Transit Companies & Security Bags provisions are subordinate obligations referencing and implementing that law.
- For compliance readers: these impose enforceable duties on institutions contracting cash-in-transit services, including who may staff security escorts.
