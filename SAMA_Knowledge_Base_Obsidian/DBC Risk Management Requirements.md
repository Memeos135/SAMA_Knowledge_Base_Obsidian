# DBC Risk Management Requirements

## Connections

### [[Debt-Based Crowdfunding Company]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a DBC Company's obligations, the risk-management article is a mandatory, board-owned framework rather than a general aspiration. Article 19 requires a written, board-approved and annually-updated policy covering an enumerated risk list — including money laundering/terrorist financing, fraud, outsourcing and Participant-protection (Institutional Beneficiary default) risk — which ties this AML/CTF and consumer-protection exposure directly into the Company's governance duty. Conclude that a DBC's AML/CTF and default-risk controls are legally inseparable from its risk-management policy, and any compliance assessment must confirm board approval and coverage of the full listed risk taxonomy.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10 / Art 19):** "Establish a written policy that includes a clear business strategy for risk management, approved and annually updated by its board of directors"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 21):** "SAMA may take necessary actions to ensure the compliance of Debt-Based Crowdfunding Companies with the relevant rules, laws, regulations, and instructions"
