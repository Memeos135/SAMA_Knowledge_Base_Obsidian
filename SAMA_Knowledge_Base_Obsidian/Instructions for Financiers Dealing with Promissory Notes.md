# Instructions for Financiers Dealing with Promissory Notes

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When establishing why the Promissory Note Instructions bind finance companies, note they are issued under both the Central Bank Law and the Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree M/51) — the latter being the statutory basis for SAMA's supervision of finance companies. The Instructions' cover circular expressly cites the Finance Companies Control Law issued by Royal Decree M/51. You would conclude that finance companies (within the defined 'financier' scope) are bound by these Instructions as enforceable minimum requirements deriving from M/51, and that the Instructions supplement rather than override related SAMA instructions.

### [[Outsourcing Rules for Finance Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a finance company on enforcing a promissory note through a third party, do not treat the Promissory Note Instructions in isolation — they expressly incorporate the Outsourcing Rules where enforcement tasks are outsourced. The Promissory Note Instructions state that if promissory-note enforcement is assigned to a third party the financier must comply with SAMA's related instructions, and their 'related instructions' list explicitly cites the finance-company outsourcing rules. You would therefore apply the full outsourcing regime (board-approved policy, risk review, retained liability) on top of the promissory-note requirements whenever collection/enforcement is delegated.
