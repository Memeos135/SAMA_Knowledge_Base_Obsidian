## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure regime for Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) risk capital — counterparty credit risk arising from potential deterioration of derivative counterparties.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: Table CVAA sets general qualitative disclosure requirements for a bank's CVA risk management and capital treatment.
- Serves as the umbrella CVA disclosure, with Table CVAB (community 283) as the approach-specific counterpart for the standardised (SA-CVA) method.
