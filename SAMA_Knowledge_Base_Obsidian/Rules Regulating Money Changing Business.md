# Rules Regulating Money Changing Business

## Connections

### [[Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a money changer's AML/CTF obligations, do not read the Money Changing Rules as a self-contained AML regime — Article 32 makes the money changer subject to the full external AML/CTF stack (the Anti-Money Laundering Law and Implementing Regulations, the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing and its Regulations, related SAMA circulars, and the WMD proliferation-financing/TFS guideline). The Rules also embed AML duties at licensing (adherence to SAMA's AML/CTF instructions) and in correspondent relationships (Art. 17), and breach exposes the changer to Article 33 penalties up to license revocation. A compliance reviewer should therefore treat the underlying AML Law and TFS instruments as the controlling authority and check compliance against them, not merely against the Rules' summary reference.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Art 4(b)):** "the provisions of the Anti-Money Laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations are complied with and the transactions are documented and archived."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 18 / Art 32):** "The Money Changer shall comply with the provisions of the Anti-money laundering Law and its Implementing Regulations; the Law of Terrorism Crimes and Financing..."

### [[Customer Protection Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping a money changer's consumer-facing duties, treat the Customer Protection Requirements (Section 4, Art. 25) as the enforceable core of the Rules' stated purpose to 'protect the rights of Customers.' The provision imposes concrete, testable obligations — displaying customer-rights and exchange-rate boards, issuing receipts, resolving complaints within seven days with escalation to SAMA, and safeguarding customer information from disclosure to third parties without SAMA's prior non-objection. A reviewer should conclude that failure on any of these is a discrete violation, and note that Article 33's penalty ladder (including where conduct is 'detrimental to the interest of its Customers') attaches to these duties.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Art 2(e)):** "The objective of these Rules is to... protect the rights of Customers."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 15 / Art 25):** "Specific channels must be made available for receiving Customer complaints and handling them within a reasonable period not exceeding seven (7) days."

### [[Licensing Requirements and Procedures]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on market entry, treat the Licensing Requirements (Section 2, Art. 6 onward) as the mandatory gate defined by these Rules: Article 4 prohibits practicing Money Changing Business without a valid SAMA license, and Article 6 sets the entry conditions (permitted legal forms, founder eligibility, feasibility study, bank guarantee equal to issued capital). Because licensing also embeds baseline AML/CTF, fraud, and information-security adherence, the license conditions are continuing obligations, not one-off entry criteria. A reviewer should conclude that any lapse in a license requirement (per Art. 32/violations provisions) is itself grounds for SAMA action up to revocation, so ongoing maintenance of licensing conditions must be verified, not just initial grant.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Art 4(a)(1)):** "Practicing Money Changing Business in the Kingdom without a valid license issued by SAMA in accordance with the provisions of these Rules."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / Art 6):** "The application for a license to practice Money Changing Business shall be submitted to SAMA. The following are the requirements for issuing the license..."

### [[Money Changer]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining who bears the obligations under this instrument, note that 'Money Changer' is the defined regulated subject — 'An establishment licensed by SAMA to conduct Money Changing Business' — so every duty, penalty, and license-termination trigger in the Rules attaches to that defined entity. Article 14 concentrates responsibility on the Money Changer's management to maintain oversight, controls, and transaction monitoring, and the termination grounds (Page 19) make continued licensing conditional on meeting these standards and cooperating with SAMA inspection. A reviewer should therefore anchor scope analysis to the Money Changer definition and confirm that management-level accountability and record/reporting duties are being met, since SAMA may revoke the license for failure to maintain requirements or for conduct detrimental to customers.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Art 1):** "Money Changer: An establishment licensed by SAMA to conduct Money Changing Business in accordance with the provisions of the Rules."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 19 / termination grounds):** "If the Money Changer fails to maintain one of the license requirements... If SAMA discovers that the Money Changer's conduct of business has been detrimental to the interest of its Customers."
