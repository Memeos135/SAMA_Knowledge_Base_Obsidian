# Credit Information Company

## Connections

### [[Credit Information Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing who is authorised to operate as a credit bureau in KSA, treat 'Companies' as a defined status created and constrained by this Law, not a generic label. Article 1 defines credit information companies as entities 'licensed to collect and maintain credit information ... and provide the same to members,' and Article 12 makes operating without a SAMA licence a violation. Conclude that any entity collecting/exchanging consumer credit data must hold a SAMA licence and that the Law's confidentiality, accuracy and consumer-consent obligations attach to that licensed status.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / Art 1):** "Companies: Credit information companies licensed to collect and maintain credit information on consumers and provide the same to members upon request"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / Art 12):** "engaging in activities of credit information companies without obtaining a license from SAMA"

### [[Credit Record]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a licensed credit bureau's obligations under the Credit Information Law, treat 'Credit Record' as the defined output that 'Companies' are legally required to produce, maintain and exchange — the two terms are locked together by Article 1's definitions and Article 4(3). This means a credit information company's core statutory duties (accuracy, first-time consumer consent under Article 9, error correction, retention under Article 10) all attach to the credit record it issues; you cannot scope the company's liability without reference to the record. Conclude that any compliance review of the company must test the record's lifecycle — consent, accuracy, correction, retention and confidentiality — as inseparable duties.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2 / Art 1 (Definitions)):** "Companies: Credit information companies licensed to collect and maintain credit information on consumers and provide the same to members upon request."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Art 1 (Definitions)):** "Credit Record: A report issued by companies containing consumer credit information."
