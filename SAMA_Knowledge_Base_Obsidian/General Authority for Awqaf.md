# General Authority for Awqaf

## Connections

### [[Endowments and Bequests Accounts]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When opening or servicing an endowment (waqf) account, the General Authority for Awqaf is the controlling authority whose documents and approvals govern the whole relationship — the endowment rules require an Awqaf-issued registration certificate to open the account and Awqaf's written approval before any cross-border transfers or receipts to/from beneficiaries outside Saudi Arabia. This link shows Awqaf's role is not merely evidentiary at onboarding but a continuing gatekeeper over transactional permissions. You would conclude that outbound remittances or foreign beneficiary payments on these accounts are prohibited unless Awqaf approval is on file.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 72 / Rule 300.1.5.8(b)):** "copy of the valid endowment registration certificate issued by the General Authority for Awqaf ... except ... after the bank obtains the official approval therefor from the General Authority for Awqaf"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 73):** "Receiving remittances or checks from beneficiaries outside Saudi Arabia shall not be permitted without written approval from the General Authority for Awqaf"
