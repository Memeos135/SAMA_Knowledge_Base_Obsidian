# Beneficial Owner

## Connections

### [[Due Diligence Measures]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When performing CDD on a legal-person customer, identifying and verifying the beneficial owner is a mandatory component of the due diligence measures, not an optional add-on. The Guide grounds this in Article (7/3) of the AML Implementing Regulations and Article (17) of the CTF Implementing Regulations, and sets the operative threshold: the institution 'shall know the identity of the natural person who owns or controls (25%) or more' and must not rely primarily on the customer's written statement. A reviewer should therefore confirm that CDD files evidence independent verification of the natural-person BO (including the fallback senior-management route where no 25% owner exists), regardless of the CDD tier applied.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 33 / para 3.18-3.19):** "shall not rely primarily on the written statement of a customer to identify the beneficial owner; it shall take reasonable and adequate measures to verify"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 32 / Section 3 (Due Diligence)):** "the financial institution shall be certain of the identity of the beneficial owner or any person controlling the business relationship"

### [[Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether EDD applies to a legal-person customer, do not confine PEP screening to the named account holder — the two concepts appear deliberately linked because a PEP may sit behind the customer as beneficial owner. The Guide's PEP provision expressly directs the institution to determine 'if a customer or beneficial owner is a PEP,' while the beneficial-owner section requires identifying the natural person who ultimately owns or controls the customer (the 25% control test and fallback measures). The link is a strong lead but the corpus does not spell out a single cross-reference article joining them, so verify the primary EDD/BO text: you would conclude that PEP determination must run through to identified beneficial owners, not stop at the front-facing customer.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 32 / Section 3.B):** "the financial institution shall be certain of the identity of the beneficial owner or any person controlling the business relationship... a natural person who owns or controls the legal person directly or indirectly."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 39 / Section 4.9):** "The financial institution shall use appropriate tools and measures to determine if a customer or beneficial owner is a PEP, whether that person is foreign or local."
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED — both concepts co-appear ('customer or beneficial owner is a PEP') but no explicit cross-referencing article is quoted; confirm in the primary EDD and beneficial-owner text before relying on it.
