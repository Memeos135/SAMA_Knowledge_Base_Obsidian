# Fit and Proper Forms Reference

## Connections

### [[Fit and Proper Criteria]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are scoping a FinTech's Sandbox obligations, treat the 'Fit and Proper forms and approvals for management' listed in the Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria as pointing back to the substantive fit-and-proper regime in the Requirements for Appointments to Senior Positions — the Sandbox document appears to reference the forms/criteria rather than create its own standard. The connection is inferential: the Sandbox guidance lists 'Fit and Proper Forms' among applicable guidelines but does not reproduce the criteria, so the actual fitness/propriety and SAMA non-objection tests live in the 8294 Requirements. Verify against the current fit-and-proper forms on SAMA's website before assuming which management roles need approval, since the Sandbox AC is periodically updated and the applicant may not yet be a 'Financial Institution' under 8294's defined scope.

### [[Guidance Notes on SAMA Regulatory Sandbox Application]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** For a Sandbox applicant, the guidance ties Stage 2 Operational Readiness to 'Fit and Proper forms and approvals for management' and separately cites 'Fit and Proper Forms' as an applicable guideline — meaning management fitness/propriety clearance is a pre-go-live gate, not an optional formality. The citation is a pointer to SAMA's fit-and-proper forms regime rather than an internal standard, so the substantive criteria sit outside this document (in the fit-and-proper Requirements/forms). Conclude that the applicant must clear management fit-and-proper approvals before onboarding clients, and should use the current SAMA-issued forms since the AC is periodically updated.

- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the specific deliverables that make up the Stage 2 gate, note that Fit and Proper forms and approvals for management are the first enumerated component of the Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria, alongside shareholders' approval and the financial model. This tells you the AC is not a general readiness declaration but a defined itemised set, so governance/fit-and-proper clearance for management is a hard precondition to obtaining the No Objection Letter. Conclude that management fitness cannot be deferred to post-go-live and must be evidenced within Stage 2.
