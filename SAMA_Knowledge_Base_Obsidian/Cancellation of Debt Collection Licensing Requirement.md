# Cancellation of Debt Collection Licensing Requirement

## Connections

### [[Circular on Outsourcing Debt Collection Operations]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a bank or finance company on outsourcing debt collection, treat these two circulars as a single evolving regime rather than independent instruments: the earlier circular (10866) removed the requirement that the outsourced collector be SAMA-licensed (allowing contracting with a non-licensed collector after a SAMA no-objection letter), and the later circular (10908) formally records SAMA's decision to cancel the licensing requirement for financing-entity debt-collection activity. Both rest on the same authority chain — the SAMA Law (Royal Decree M/36) and the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51) — plus the Governor-approved amendments to the Finance Companies Implementing Regulation and the Support Activities Licensing Rules. For a compliance decision you would conclude that licensing is no longer the gating condition, but verify the current no-objection/no-impediment obligations and that previously licensed collectors update their commercial-register activity as 10908 requires.

### [[Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** To decide whether a debt-collection entity for finance-provider debts still needs a SAMA licence, read this circular together with the amended Implementing Regulations it references: SAMA cancels the licensing requirement for practising the debt-collection activity, issuing this decision in reference to the Governor's approval of the amendments to the Implementing Regulations and the Finance Support Activities Licensing Rules. The circular also requires previously licensed or pre-approved collection entities to update their commercial-registry activity accordingly. Conclude the licensing prerequisite is removed, but affected entities carry a transitional compliance obligation to align their registrations.

### [[Rules for Licensing Finance Support Activities]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on whether a finance-debt collection business needs a SAMA licence, read this cancellation decision alongside the finance-support licensing rules: node B is SAMA's decision cancelling the licensing requirement for practising finance-debt collection, and it grounds itself in the amendments to both the Finance Companies Control Law Implementing Regulations and the finance-support licensing rules (which already exclude debt collection from licensable support activities).
