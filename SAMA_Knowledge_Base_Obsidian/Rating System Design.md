# Rating System Design

## Connections

### [[Minimum Requirements to Use IRB Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank qualifies to use the IRB approach for capital purposes, treat rating system design not as an optional design choice but as a component of the mandatory minimum requirements — Section 3 (rating system design) sits inside Chapter 16 ('Minimum requirements to use IRB approach') per the framework's own table of contents. This means a bank cannot claim IRB eligibility while treating its two-dimensional rating structure, multiple-system rationale, and design documentation as merely internal governance; those are enforceable qualifying conditions SAMA can test. Conclude that any IRB approval assessment must verify Section 3 compliance at outset and on an ongoing basis, and that failures in rating system design directly undermine IRB eligibility rather than being a separable issue.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 180 / 16.10):** "Banks must demonstrate that each system used for IRB purposes is in compliance with the minimum requirements at the outset and on an ongoing basis."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Ch.16):** "16. IRB Approach: Minimum requirements to use IRB approach ... Section 3: rating system design 180"

### [[Rating Criteria]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's IRB approach meets SAMA's minimum standards, treat rating criteria as a mandatory documented component of overall rating system design rather than a separate topic. Para 16.34 lists 'rating criteria' among the design and operational details banks must document to evidence compliance, so the criteria requirements sit inside the broader Section 3 design obligations (16.9 onward). A reviewer should therefore check that a bank can both justify its choice of rating criteria and show the criteria are embedded in a documented, periodically reviewed system that meaningfully differentiates risk — not accept criteria in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 180 / 16.9):** "The term “rating system” comprises all of the methods, processes, controls, and data collection and IT systems that support the assessment of credit risk"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 188 / 16.34):** "must address topics such as portfolio differentiation, rating criteria... A bank must document the rationale for its choice of internal rating criteria"

### [[Rating Dimensions (Borrower and Transaction)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When validating an IRB rating system, confirm it carries the two mandatory dimensions — borrower default risk and transaction-specific factors — because SAMA treats this as a defining structural requirement of a qualifying rating system, not an optional feature. Para 16.11 sits within Section 3 (rating system design, 16.9) and states a qualifying system 'must have two separate and distinct dimensions,' with 16.12 requiring same-borrower exposures to share a borrower grade subject only to the country-transfer and guarantee exceptions. A reviewer should therefore conclude that a single-dimension system, or one that varies borrower grade by transaction outside the stated exceptions, fails the design standard.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 180 / 16.9-16.10):** "Banks must demonstrate that each system used for IRB purposes is in compliance with the minimum requirements at the outset and on an ongoing basis."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 180 / 16.11):** "A qualifying IRB rating system must have two separate and distinct dimensions: (1) the risk of borrower default; and (2) transaction-specific factors."

### [[Use of Statistical Models]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a bank uses statistical models within its rating system, do not treat model methodology as separately governed — it is a documented element of the rating-system design obligation. Para 16.36 provides that if the bank employs statistical models it must document their methodologies (theory, assumptions, mathematical/empirical basis), and 16.77 conditions model-based PD estimation on meeting the standards in 16.33. A reviewer should therefore check model documentation and validation against the same Section 3 design and minimum-standard requirements, and confirm added conservatism where data are limited or systems have changed.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 188 / 16.36):** "If the bank employs statistical models in the rating process, the bank must document their methodologies."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 202 / 16.77):** "The bank’s use of default probability models for this purpose must meet the standards specified in paragraph 16.33."
