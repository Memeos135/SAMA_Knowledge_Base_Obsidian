# Directorate of Financial Intelligence

## Connections

### [[AML Implementing Regulation (SAMA 1428)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining where suspicious activity must be reported, the Directorate of Financial Intelligence is the mandatory recipient of STRs under this Regulation — including where CDD is aborted to avoid tip-off, in which case the institution must file and state why due diligence was not applied (Art 7/9). The Regulation frames the Directorate as operationally independent and the analytical/receiving node for financial intelligence (Art 17). Conclude that reporting obligations run to this named body specifically, and that opting not to complete CDD does not remove the reporting duty — it triggers it.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / Art 17/4):** "The Directorate of financial intelligence shall be operationally independent and autonomous"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / Art 7/9):** "shall submit a suspicious transaction report to the Directorate of financial intelligence, and stating the reasons as to why due diligence was not applied"

### [[Cross-Border Declaration]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** This link appears to connect cross-border transfer/declaration handling with the Directorate of Financial Intelligence, but the provided context for node A is the Article 10 wire-transfer information regime rather than a distinct customs-style cross-border currency declaration, and no text directly ties that node to the Directorate. The Directorate's role (17/1) is to receive reports and information relating to money laundering and proceeds of crime, which would encompass cross-border transaction reporting. Before relying on this edge, verify the primary text: confirm whether the intended 'cross-border declaration' obligation actually routes to the Directorate or to another competent authority, since the excerpts do not establish a direct reporting channel.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / 17/1):** "Receive suspicious transaction reports, or other information or reports relating to money laundering, predicate offenses or proceeds of crime"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 10 / 10/4):** "Where several individual cross-border wire transfers from a single originator are bundled in a batch file... the ordering financial institution shall include... required and verified originator information"
- **Caveat:** Relation labelled 'references' but the node A context is the wire-transfer regime, not a clearly distinct cross-border currency declaration; no provided text directly links this node to the Directorate. Verify the primary provision before relying.

### [[Suspicious Transaction Report]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining where and how STRs must be filed, treat the Directorate of Financial Intelligence as the exclusive statutory recipient and the authority that fixes reporting form and content. Section 17/1 lists receiving and analysing STRs among the Directorate's functions, and the closing provision states the Directorate shall specify the manner reports are made and the information transmitted. The consequence for a compliance decision: reporting obligations are discharged only by filing to this body in the form it prescribes, and its guidance/templates govern — you cannot rely on ad hoc reporting channels.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 14 / 17/1):** "Receive suspicious transaction reports... Prepare templates for use by reporting entities to report suspicious transactions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 13):** "The directorate of financial intelligence shall further specify the manner in which reports under this Article are to be made and the information that shall be transmitted"
