# Fraud Remediation

## Connections

### [[Counter-Fraud Department]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying which function owns remediation of fraud incidents, attribute it to the Counter-Fraud Department: its minimum responsibilities expressly include performing comprehensive investigations, identifying root causes, and documenting corrective actions — the substance of Fraud Remediation. This places accountability for closing out incidents within the day-to-day operation of the Counter-Fraud Programme. A reviewer should therefore test the Department's investigation and corrective-action records as evidence of remediation, rather than looking to a separate business-unit process.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 20):** "Performing comprehensive fraud investigations, identifying root causes of fraud incidents and documenting corrective actions."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 20):** "Member Organisations should establish and maintain a Counter-Fraud Department that has responsibility for the day-to-day operation of the Counter-Fraud Programme."

### [[Respond Domain]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping where fraud-remediation duties sit in SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework (SAMA_EN_2217), treat Fraud Remediation as an operational output of the Respond domain rather than a free-standing control. The Respond domain requires timely investigation, prompt fair resolution, and post-incident root-cause evaluation and corrective action, and the Counter-Fraud Department is charged with 'identifying root causes of fraud incidents and documenting corrective actions.' A compliance reviewer should therefore assess remediation adequacy against the Respond domain's principle and control requirements, not judge it in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 20):** "Performing comprehensive fraud investigations, identifying root causes of fraud incidents and documenting corrective actions."
