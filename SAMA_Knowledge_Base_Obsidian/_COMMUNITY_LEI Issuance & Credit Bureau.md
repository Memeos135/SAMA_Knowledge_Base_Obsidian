# LEI Issuance & Credit Bureau

**Cohesion:** 0.50 - moderately connected
**Members:** 5 nodes

## Why this community

Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) issuance in KSA, where SAMA authorizes SIMAH to act as GLEIF-accredited issuer within the global LEI framework. Relevant to entity-identification obligations in reporting and onboarding for supervised firms.

## How members connect

- SAMA's LEI Issuance Instructions & SIMAH Agreement is the source instrument designating SIMAH's issuance role and referencing the LEI as the identifier standard.
- SIMAH operates as the Local Operating Unit, the operational role defined within the GLEIF global governance hierarchy.
- GLEIF sits above the Local Operating Unit as the standard-setter; SIMAH's issuance authority is subordinate to and accredited under it.
- For compliance readers: LEI use is enforceable via the SAMA instruction, with SIMAH as the sole domestic issuance channel.
