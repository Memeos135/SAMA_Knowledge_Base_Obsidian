# Shari'ah Compliance

## Connections

### [[Master Repo Agreement (MRA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a participant that trades only on Shari'ah-compliant terms, note that the MRA does not itself certify Shari'ah compliance and shifts that burden entirely onto each party. Under the Agreement's representations, a party that requires Shari'ah compliance must have conducted its own investigation (including obtaining a fatwa where required), cannot rely on the counterparty's documents, and waives any dispute raised on Shari'ah grounds. You would therefore conclude that using the SAMA MRA does not discharge a firm's own Shari'ah-governance obligations, and that no contractual recourse exists against the counterparty for non-compliance.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 25):** "it has made its own investigation into and satisfied itself as to the Shari'ah Compliance... and it will not claim any dispute on the grounds of Shari'ah Compliance"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / 7.3):** "The MRA shall be subject to and governed by Saudi law."
