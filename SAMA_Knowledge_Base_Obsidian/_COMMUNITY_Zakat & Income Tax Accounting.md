# Zakat & Income Tax Accounting

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Accounting treatment of zakat and income tax in the financial statements of SAMA-supervised entities, anchored to the accounting framework SAMA mandates.

## How members connect

- The Circular is the operative SAMA instruction on how to account for zakat and income tax; it references IFRS as adopted by SAMA as the governing accounting basis.
- Compliance consequence: entities must reconcile zakat/tax accounting to the SAMA-adopted IFRS baseline rather than IFRS as issued by the IASB, so any local carve-outs or SAMA overlays control.
