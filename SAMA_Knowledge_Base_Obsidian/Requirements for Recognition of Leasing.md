# Requirements for Recognition of Leasing

## Connections

### [[Minimum Requirements to Use IRB Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing IRB treatment of leasing exposures, treat 'Requirements for Recognition of Leasing' as Section 10 within the mandatory chapter 16 minimum requirements, not a separate standalone regime. The chapter states these requirements cut across asset classes and apply for both entry and ongoing use, so leasing-specific recognition conditions must be satisfied in addition to the general rating-system and quantification standards. For a bank using IRB for leased assets you should confirm the leasing recognition section is met as part of overall IRB compliance.
