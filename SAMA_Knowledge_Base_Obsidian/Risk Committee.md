# Risk Committee

## Connections

### [[Board of Directors]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing risk-governance obligations, note that the Risk Committee sits under the Board and channels the risk manager's reporting line: the risk manager reports to the Board through the Risk Committee, which must submit its opinion on risk reports to the Board. The Board determines the committee's powers and monitors implementation, so ultimate risk-oversight accountability remains with the Board even where the committee performs the detailed work. You should check that the committee's composition (three non-executive members, a cyber/technical-risk expert, not chaired by the CEO) and its reporting flow to the Board are in place before relying on the risk-governance structure.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 22 / para 92):** "The financial institution's risk manager shall report to the Board through the risk committee, which shall have to submit its opinion on the risk management reports to the Board."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 18 / para 77):** "The Board shall determine committee powers and responsibilities, and shall monitor the implementation thereof."
