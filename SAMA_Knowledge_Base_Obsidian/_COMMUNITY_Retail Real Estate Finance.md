# Retail Real Estate Finance

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

Consumer-protection regime for retail real estate finance: SAMA's mandatory instructions governing disclosure, advice, and risk acknowledgement owed to individual borrowers by real estate financiers.

## How members connect

- The 'SAMA Mandatory Instructions on Retail Real Estate Finance' is the parent obligation-setting instrument; it references the Credit Advisor role and the Real Estate Finance Offer as required conduct and documentation.
- The Real Estate Finance Offer is the disclosure vehicle to the consumer; it references the Variable-Rate Credit Risk Acknowledgement, an enforceable step ensuring borrowers accept rate-variation risk.
- Credit Advisor duties connect to the Offer, positioning advice obligations upstream of the binding financing terms presented to the consumer.
