# Banking Control & Enforcement

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Statutory basis for SAMA's prudential supervision of banks and the procedural rules for enforcing judicial decisions against/through banks. Bridges the primary banking law and its operational enforcement guidance.

## How members connect

- The Banking Control Law is the primary statute; the Judicial Decisions Enforcement Procedures Circular is subordinate guidance that references and operates within that legal authority.
- Hierarchy runs law -> circular: the circular derives its enforceability from the Banking Control Law and does not expand SAMA's statutory powers.
- Use the Law to establish supervisory jurisdiction/obligation and the circular for the specific procedure banks must follow when handling enforcement of judicial decisions.
