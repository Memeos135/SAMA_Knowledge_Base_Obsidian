# Beneficial Owner Identification

## Connections

### [[Beneficial Owner Data Verification (Awqaf)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a financial institution onboards or monitors an Awqaf (endowment) relationship, treat the Awqaf-data circular as an operational channel for satisfying the standing beneficial-owner identification duty, not a substitute for it. The AML rules require verifying the identity of the beneficial owner — the person owning or controlling 25% or more, or otherwise exercising ultimate control — while the circular directs institutions to use the General Authority for Awqaf's channels to verify beneficial-owner data and to notify discrepancies without prejudice to STR obligations. Conclude that Awqaf verification results feed the CDD determination but the institution retains the full beneficial-owner obligation, including reporting where data diverges.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Art (CDD)):** "verifying the identity of the beneficial owner ... the person who owns or controls (25%) or more of the legal person."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1):** "the General Authority for Awqaf has made available a number of channels enabling financial institutions to access beneficial-owner data of endowments (paraphrase; source Arabic is OCR-garbled)"
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED; node A Arabic is OCR-garbled so its text is paraphrased. Confirm the circular's exact obligations against a clean copy.

### [[Due Diligence Measures (CTF)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a firm's CTF onboarding is complete, treat beneficial owner identification as an integral element of the due diligence obligation, not an optional add-on. Within the same Implementing Regulation, verifying the identity of the person who owns or controls 25% or more of a legal person is one of the prescribed due diligence measures, and the 25% threshold (with fallback to the natural person exercising control) is the operative test. You would conclude that CDD on a legal-person customer is deficient unless the ultimate beneficial owner has been identified and verified from a reliable and independent source per this threshold.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Art 16):** "verifying the identity of the person who owns or controls (25%) or more of the legal person... the identity of the natural person exercising control over the legal person shall be verified by all means possible"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9 / Art 16(5)):** "shall apply due diligence measures to all business relationships according to risk level... applying measures of due diligence to current customers and beneficial owners"
