# Head of Counter-Fraud

## Connections

### [[Counter-Fraud Department]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping accountability under SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework, treat the Counter-Fraud Department and the Head of Counter-Fraud as distinct but linked roles: the Department (a function Member Organisations must establish) runs day-to-day operations of the Counter-Fraud Programme, while the Head of Counter-Fraud is the accountable individual owning strategy, policy, the Fraud Risk Assessment, Fraud Risk Appetite and fraud KRIs. This means when assessing whether an organisation meets the framework, you check both an operational function AND a named senior accountability owner — the two cannot be conflated. Conclude that gaps in personal accountability (Head role) are separate compliance findings from gaps in departmental operations.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18 / section (Head of Counter-Fraud)):** "The Head of Counter-Fraud should be accountable for: 1. Developing, implementing, and maintaining: a. Counter-Fraud Strategy. b. Counter-Fraud Policy..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 20 / section 4 (Counter-Fraud Department)):** "Member Organisations should establish and maintain a Counter-Fraud Department that has responsibility for the day-to-day operation of the Counter-Fraud Programme"

### [[Counter-Fraud Governance Committee]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When allocating governance accountability under SAMA_EN_2217, read the Counter-Fraud Governance Committee (CFGC) and the Head of Counter-Fraud as distinct but linked roles in one approval chain: the Head develops the Strategy, Policy, Fraud Risk Assessment and Risk Appetite, while these are approved by the CFGC and then endorsed at board/senior level. This means the same person cannot both draft and finally approve those instruments — the CFGC provides the independent challenge and sign-off. A reviewer should confirm the CFGC charter and the Head's mandate are separately documented and that the endorsement sequence (Head develops → CFGC approves → endorsed) is respected.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18):** "The Head of Counter-Fraud should be accountable for: Developing, implementing, and maintaining: Counter-Fraud Strategy ... Policy ... Fraud Risk Assessment ... Fraud Risk Appetite."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 18):** "Endorsing (after being approved by the CFGC): ... The Counter-Fraud Strategy ... Policy ... output of the Fraud Risk Assessment ... Fraud Risk Appetite."
