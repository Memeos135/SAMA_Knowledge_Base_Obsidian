# Penalty Publication Instructions

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a finance company's disclosure and penalty-publication duties, treat this circular as an exercise of SAMA's authority under the Finance Companies Control Law (M/51) and its Implementing Regulation, not a free-standing rule. The circular expressly bases the obligation to publish executive decisions/penalties in the board report on Articles of the Implementing Regulation (financial-statement filing and mandatory website disclosure), which in turn derive from the parent Law. You should conclude that non-compliance with the publication instruction is enforceable under the Law's supervisory/penalty regime, and that the required disclosure categories (supervisory violations, consumer-protection violations, AML/CTF violations) are the binding taxonomy to report against.
