# Risk Rating System Operations

## Connections

### [[Data Maintenance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating whether a bank's rating operations are compliant, treat the data-maintenance obligations as the evidentiary backbone of the operating system, not a back-office add-on. Para 16.45 requires collection and storage of key borrower and facility data to support credit risk measurement, meet the framework's other requirements, and serve as a basis for supervisory reporting; 16.46-16.49 then specify rating histories, default identification, and PD/LGD/EAD data by exposure type. A reviewer should conclude that a rating system cannot be demonstrated as operating soundly without the retained data histories that allow retrospective re-allocation and tracking of predictive power.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 180 / 16.9):** "the assignment of internal risk ratings, and the quantification of default and loss estimates."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 191 / 16.45):** "A bank must collect and store data on key borrower and facility characteristics to provide effective support to its internal credit risk measurement and management process"

### [[Stress Tests for Capital Adequacy]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing an IRB bank's rating operations for capital-adequacy purposes, note that a sound stress-testing process is a required operational component, not a discretionary enhancement. Para 16.50 mandates that an IRB bank 'must have in place sound stress testing processes' identifying adverse events (economic/industry downturns, market-risk events, liquidity conditions) and assessing resilience of credit exposures. A reviewer should therefore treat the rating system's data and estimates (PDs/LGDs/EADs) as inputs that must feed a documented stress-testing capability, and conclude that absence of such stress testing is itself a minimum-standards deficiency.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 192 / 16.49):** "Banks must retain data used in the process of allocating exposures to pools... as well as data on delinquency."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 192 / 16.50):** "An IRB bank must have in place sound stress testing processes for use in the assessment of capital adequacy."
