# Counter-Fraud Framework

**Cohesion:** 0.10 - loosely connected
**Members:** 28 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA Counter-Fraud Framework: a supervisory framework imposing on regulated entities a structured fraud-risk regime organised into Governance, Prevent, Detect and Respond domains, assessed against a maturity model.

## How members connect

- SAMA is the issuing authority; the Framework is the parent instrument that decomposes into the four domains and is benchmarked by the Counter-Fraud Maturity Model.
- Governance Domain establishes accountable structures (Committee, Department, Head, Strategy, Policy) — i.e., the who-is-responsible obligations.
- Prevent/Detect/Respond domains assign operational duties (due diligence, authentication, risk assessment; detection and whistle-blowing; investigation, remediation, response planning and supervisory notification to SAMA).
- Cross-references to the Cyber Security and IT Governance Frameworks show fraud controls interlock with adjacent SAMA operational-resilience regimes rather than standing alone.
