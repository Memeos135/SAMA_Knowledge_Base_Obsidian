# Master Repo Agreement (MRA)

## Connections

### [[Confirmation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing which document controls a disputed repo term, treat the Confirmation as a subordinate instrument that supplements and forms part of the MRA rather than a standalone contract. The Confirmation expressly incorporates the Agreement's definitions and provisions and, together with the MRA, constitutes prima facie evidence of the transaction terms — but in a direct conflict the Confirmation prevails for that transaction only. You would therefore read the two together, check that any negotiated variation was recorded in the Confirmation or an agreed alternative form (per section 7.2), and not rely on the Confirmation to override the MRA outside its specific transaction.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18):** "In the event of any conflict... the Confirmation shall prevail in respect of that Transaction and those terms only."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 39 / Annex II):** "This Confirmation supplements and forms part of, and is subject to, the Master Agreement for the Sale and Purchase of Securities"

### [[Global Master Repurchase Agreement (GMRA)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When deciding which master agreement governs a KSA repo, distinguish the two: the SAMA-approved MRA (2020 version) is mandatory for repo transactions in Saudi Arabia and is subject to Saudi law, whereas the GMRA is only tolerated for pre-existing cross-border transactions with a foreign-currency leg, subject to compliance with Saudi rules. Both are functionally equivalent industry master agreements for sale-and-repurchase of securities, which is why the guideline treats them in parallel. You would conclude that new SAR-denominated repo activity must use the MRA and cannot default to the GMRA, and that reliance on the GMRA is a narrow carve-out to verify against section 5.3.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / 7.1):** "repo transactions in Saudi Arabia must be governed by the standard Master Repurchase Agreement (MRA)... (2020 version)... approved by SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / 5.3):** "there is no prohibition on bank's existing repo transaction... governed by the GMRA, subject to compliance to the relevant and applicable rules and regulations in Saudi Arabia."

### [[Repurchase Agreement (Repo)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When documenting a repo in Saudi Arabia, treat the SAMA-approved standard Master Repurchase Agreement (MRA, 2020 version) as mandatory documentation, not a template: Section 7.1 requires all repo market participants to ensure their repo transactions are governed by the MRA, and any variations must be captured in Annexures and mutually agreed. The MRA is also subject to and governed by Saudi law. Conclude that a repo not documented under the approved MRA (or without properly incorporated annexures for any deviation) would be non-compliant, and that deviations require documented mutual agreement rather than informal adjustment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / 7.1):** "repo transactions in Saudi Arabia must be governed by the standard Master Repurchase Agreement (MRA)... (2020 version) as attached in Appendix A, approved by SAMA"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / 7.1):** "All repo market participants shall ensure that their repo transactions in Saudi Arabia must be governed by the standard Master Repurchase Agreement (MRA)"

### [[SAMA Guidelines on Repurchase Agreements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a repo market participant on documentation, note that the Guidelines make use of the SAMA-approved standard Master Repurchase Agreement mandatory, not optional: Section 7.1 requires all SAR repo transactions in Saudi Arabia to be governed by the standard MRA (2020 version, Appendix A), any variations must sit in Annexures and be mutually agreed, and the MRA is governed by Saudi law. Because Section 3.2 states all policy requirements are binding on participants, you would conclude that deviating from the standard MRA form (outside agreed annexures/confirmations) is a compliance breach — check that live templates are the approved 2020 version and that amendments are properly documented.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / Section 7.4):** "The MRA shall be subject to and governed by Saudi law."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6-7 / Section 7.1):** "All repo market participants shall ensure that their repo transactions in Saudi Arabia must be governed by the standard Master Repurchase Agreement (MRA)"

### [[Shari'ah Compliance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a participant that trades only on Shari'ah-compliant terms, note that the MRA does not itself certify Shari'ah compliance and shifts that burden entirely onto each party. Under the Agreement's representations, a party that requires Shari'ah compliance must have conducted its own investigation (including obtaining a fatwa where required), cannot rely on the counterparty's documents, and waives any dispute raised on Shari'ah grounds. You would therefore conclude that using the SAMA MRA does not discharge a firm's own Shari'ah-governance obligations, and that no contractual recourse exists against the counterparty for non-compliance.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / 7.3):** "The MRA shall be subject to and governed by Saudi law."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 25):** "it has made its own investigation into and satisfied itself as to the Shari'ah Compliance... and it will not claim any dispute on the grounds of Shari'ah Compliance"
