## Why this community

Insurance/coverage concepts addressing management and professional liability exposures within the governance and operational-risk regime — the protections a firm and its officers carry against professional and fiduciary claims.

## How members connect

- 'Professional Risk' is the parent concept, referencing both 'Directors and Officers Liability' and 'Professional Indemnity' as specific coverage types.
- The linkage distinguishes officer/fiduciary exposure (D&O) from firm/professional-service exposure (PI), both flowing from the same professional-risk category.
- Decision relevance: these define what liability transfer is contemplated, not a standalone SAMA obligation — check the referencing instrument for any coverage mandate.
