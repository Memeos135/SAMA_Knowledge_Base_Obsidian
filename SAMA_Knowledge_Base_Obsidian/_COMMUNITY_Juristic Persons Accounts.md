# Juristic Persons Accounts

**Cohesion:** 0.18 - loosely connected
**Members:** 12 nodes

## Why this community

Account-opening rules for juristic (non-natural) persons under SAMA's Rules for Opening Bank Accounts, covering the differentiated KYB/documentation regimes applicable to distinct legal-entity categories (companies, foreign investors, awqaf, associations, foundations, diplomatic missions, and special-purpose accounts).

## How members connect

- The parent 'Rules for Opening Bank Accounts for Juristic Persons' is the governing instrument; each entity-type account (companies, escrow, embassies, endowments, foreign-investor residents, Hajj/Umrah, etc.) is a scoped sub-regime whose specific identification and documentation conditions the Rules reference.
- Cross-references to external authorities set the licensing/registration precondition banks must verify: Private Associations and Private Foundations accounts depend on Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development oversight; Endowments and Bequests accounts depend on the General Authority for Awqaf.
- For compliance decisions, each account type imposes a distinct customer-verification obligation — the correct CDD/KYB package turns on which sub-regime the applicant falls under, not a single generic standard.
