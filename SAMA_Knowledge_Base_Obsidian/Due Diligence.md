# Due Diligence

## Connections

### [[Fraud Risk Assessment]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing a Member Organisation's fraud Due Diligence standards, do not evaluate them in isolation: the framework requires that Due Diligence standards be aligned to the risks identified in the Fraud Risk Assessment, so the FRA output is the benchmark against which DD scope and intensity are judged. Practically, an organisation cannot demonstrate adequate employee, customer and third-party DD without showing those standards trace back to the risks its FRA identified. Conclude that a stale or missing FRA undermines the defensibility of the DD regime, and any DD review should first confirm the underlying FRA linkage. Note this is fraud-regime Due Diligence, distinct from AML/CTF CDD.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 31 / 4.2 Due Diligence):** "Due Diligence standards should be aligned to the risks identified in the Fraud Risk Assessment."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 28 / 4.1.2 Fraud Risk Assessment):** "Member Organisations should conduct a Fraud Risk Assessment to identify fraud risks to which they or their customers are subject"

### [[Prevent Domain]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping prevention obligations, treat fraud Due Diligence (sub-domain 4.2) as a Prevent-domain control distinct from AML/CTF Customer Due Diligence — here it targets fraud risk across employees, customers and third parties, aiming to prevent relationships outside fraud risk appetite. The Prevent domain lists 'due diligence and integrity checks' among the minimum prevention controls, and 4.2 requires DD standards aligned to the Fraud Risk Assessment, covering employee background checks, customer identity validation and third-party vetting. A reviewer should not treat this as satisfied by AML CDD alone: the fraud DD standard must be separately defined, approved and periodically updated, and must address internal-fraud (employee) and third-party dimensions that AML customer-focused DD does not cover.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 31 / sub-domain 4.2 Due Diligence):** "standards for assessing the fraud risk associated with employees, customers and third parties to prevent the establishment of relationships outside risk appetite."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 40 / Section 4 Prevent):** "The controls implemented to prevent fraud (e.g., segregation of duties, ... due diligence and integrity checks, ...)."
