# Eligible Credit Assessment Institution (ECAI)

## Connections

### [[Use of External Ratings]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining risk weights for externally-rated or securitization exposures, do not treat 'use of external ratings' as a free-standing permission: a rating only counts if it comes from an institution that satisfies the ECAI eligibility criteria in chapter 8. The Use of External Ratings chapter expressly routes back to chapter 8 ('The requirements covering the use of external ratings are set out in chapter 8'), and the ECAI provisions set the recognition conditions (public availability of ratings/methodologies, demonstrated expertise, cooperation with SAMA). Conclude that before relying on any external rating for capital purposes you must confirm SAMA recognition of the ECAI and that the specific rating meets the publication/transparency conditions; unpublished or transaction-only ratings must be treated as unrated.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 302 / Page 54):** "an eligible credit assessment... must be publicly available, on a non-selective basis and free of charge... ratings made available only to the parties to a transaction do not satisfy this requirement."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9):** "The requirements covering the use of external ratings are set out in chapter 8."
