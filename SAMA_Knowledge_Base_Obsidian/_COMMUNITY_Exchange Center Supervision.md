# Exchange Center Supervision

**Cohesion:** 0.40 - moderately connected
**Members:** 5 nodes

## Why this community

Supervisory framework for licensed exchange centers (money changers): governing rules, prudential/AML testing, permitted activities, and supervisory data reporting.

## How members connect

- The Rules Regulating Exchange Business is the foundational instrument that the other members reference — supervisory data forms, the periodic independent test requirement, and POS service provision each derive their authority from it.
- The RMS data-forms update ties reporting obligations to the Returns Management System channel while remaining scoped by the Rules.
- Compliance consequence: activity scope (e.g. POS provision) and mandatory independent testing are conditions of the exchange-business licence; a firm's permitted activities and reporting duties are all defined by reference to the Rules.
