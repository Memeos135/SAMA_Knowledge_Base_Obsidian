# Licensing Requirements and Procedures

## Connections

### [[Money Changer]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether an entity may lawfully offer money changing services in KSA, read the licensing requirements as the gateway that defines who qualifies as a 'Money Changer'—only an establishment licensed by SAMA under these Rules holds that status. Article 6's conditions (legal form, eligible founders, feasibility study, bank guarantee) attach directly to the defined term, and continued licensing depends on maintaining those requirements (Article 30 grounds for termination). Conclude that Money Changer obligations throughout the Rules apply only to licensed entities, and that any unlicensed party performing these activities is instead caught by the Article 4 prohibition rather than the operational regime.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Art 6):** "The application for a license to practice Money Changing Business shall be submitted to SAMA. The following are the requirements for issuing the license"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Art 1):** "Money Changer: An establishment licensed by SAMA to conduct Money Changing Business in accordance with the provisions of the Rules."

### [[Rules Regulating Money Changing Business]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on market entry, treat the Licensing Requirements (Section 2, Art. 6 onward) as the mandatory gate defined by these Rules: Article 4 prohibits practicing Money Changing Business without a valid SAMA license, and Article 6 sets the entry conditions (permitted legal forms, founder eligibility, feasibility study, bank guarantee equal to issued capital). Because licensing also embeds baseline AML/CTF, fraud, and information-security adherence, the license conditions are continuing obligations, not one-off entry criteria. A reviewer should conclude that any lapse in a license requirement (per Art. 32/violations provisions) is itself grounds for SAMA action up to revocation, so ongoing maintenance of licensing conditions must be verified, not just initial grant.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Art 6):** "The application for a license to practice Money Changing Business shall be submitted to SAMA. The following are the requirements for issuing the license..."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Art 4(a)(1)):** "Practicing Money Changing Business in the Kingdom without a valid license issued by SAMA in accordance with the provisions of these Rules."
