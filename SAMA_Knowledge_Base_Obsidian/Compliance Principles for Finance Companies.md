# Compliance Principles for Finance Companies

## Connections

### [[Code of Conduct and Work Ethics in Financial Institutions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping compliance obligations for a finance company, do not treat the Code of Conduct and the Compliance Principles as interchangeable — the Compliance Principles expressly list the Code of Conduct among the other related instruments that continue to bind alongside them. The Compliance Principles state they do not derogate from other applicable requirements, "منها على سبيل المثال لا الحصر ... مبادئ السلوك وأخلاقيات العمل في المؤسسات المالية." Conclude that a finance company must satisfy both: the Code sets the conduct/ethics floor for staff, while the Compliance Principles allocate compliance-function responsibilities, and compliance with one does not discharge the other.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5):** "لا تخل هذه المبادئ بالمتطلبات ... الأخرى ذات العلاقة ... مبادئ السلوك وأخلاقيات العمل في المؤسسات المالية"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4):** "تسري هذه المبادئ على جميع منسوبي المؤسسة المالية ... وتحدد هذه المبادئ الحد الأدنى لقواعد السلوك"

### [[Internal Audit Principles for Finance Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping the governance obligations of a finance company, do not treat compliance and internal audit as isolated silos: both instruments share the same parent authority (the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation) and both allocate duties across the Board, Audit Committee and Executive Management, with the Compliance Principles expressly assigning the internal audit function responsibilities toward compliance. The Compliance Principles list Principle Seven on 'responsibilities of internal audit toward compliance,' which the Internal Audit Principles then elaborate as an independent assurance function. A reviewer should therefore cross-read both texts to confirm that internal audit's assurance role over compliance is not double-counted or left with a gap in the three-lines-of-defence structure.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3, Principle Seven):** "المبدأ السابع: مسؤوليات إدارة المراجعة الداخلية تجاه الالتزام"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4, Definitions):** "وظيفة المراجعة الداخلية | نشاط ... مستقل يقدم تأكيدات وخدمات استشارية موضوعية ومستقلة عن جودة وكفاية وفعالية نظام الرقابة الداخلية"

### [[Shariah Governance Instructions for Finance Companies]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** These two instruments appear to sit side-by-side in the finance-company governance regime rather than one governing the other: both derive from the Finance Companies Control Law, and the Compliance Principles' non-exhaustive list of 'other related instructions' that continue to apply signals that Shariah governance is a parallel obligation a finance company must satisfy alongside its compliance-unit duties. The Shariah Governance Instructions establish a separate Shariah Committee and Shariah oversight function, distinct from the compliance unit reporting to the Audit Committee. Because this link is inferred, verify the primary texts before treating either as subordinate — the practical conclusion is that an Islamic finance company owes both compliance-function and Shariah-committee obligations, and satisfying one does not discharge the other.
