- **What this link tells you:** If you are locating where the compliance function's mandate to cover financial-crime requirements sits in the governance framework, note that this document ties the AML/CTF dimension into the compliance-risk definition rather than being a standalone AML rulebook. The Compliance Principles define compliance risk to include exposure from failing to meet 'financial-crime combating requirements' (مكافحة الجرائم المالية), making AML/CTF one of the risk domains the independent compliance unit must identify, assess and report. You would conclude that the compliance unit's remit expressly encompasses AML/CTF obligations, but for the substantive AML/CTF rules themselves you should consult the dedicated AML/CTF instruments, not this governance circular.

### [[Banking Control Law (M5)|Banking Control Law (M/5)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the legal force of the Compliance Principles, treat them as subordinate mandatory instructions issued under statutory authority, not mere guidance. The circular states it is issued under SAMA's charter (Royal Decree) and the Banking Control Law (Royal Decree M/5), and it replaces the earlier compliance manual with effect that becomes binding on the stated date. You would conclude the Principles are enforceable against banks and foreign-bank branches licensed under the Banking Control Law, and that the defined term 'Bank' derives its scope from that Law.
