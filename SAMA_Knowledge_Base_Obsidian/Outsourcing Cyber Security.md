# Outsourcing Cyber Security

## Connections

### [[SAMA (Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When deciding whether cyber-security functions can be outsourced, note that SAMA remains the supervisory authority whose regulations bound any risk-treatment decision, including transfer/outsourcing of controls. The Framework requires that accepting or transferring cyber-security risk 'does not contradict SAMA regulations' and that third-party providers comply with the entity's standards, so outsourcing does not transfer regulatory accountability away from the member organization. You would conclude that outsourcing arrangements must be assessed against SAMA's Framework and remain the member organization's responsibility, not the vendor's.

### [[Third Party Cyber Security]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing an outsourcing arrangement, treat its cyber security obligations as part of the third-party control set, since an outsourced service provider is an external party accessing or processing the Member Organization's information assets. The Framework's risk-response controls allow transferring risk but require that any accepted residual risk 'does not contradict SAMA regulations,' so outsourcing does not offload the organization's own accountability. A reviewer should conclude that outsourcing and third-party controls are read together — outsourced providers must meet the same third-party cyber security expectations, and the board remains ultimately responsible.
