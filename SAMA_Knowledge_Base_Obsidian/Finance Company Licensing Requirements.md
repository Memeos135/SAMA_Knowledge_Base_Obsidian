# Finance Company Licensing Requirements

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulation of Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether a firm may lawfully conduct any financing activity in KSA, treat licensing not as a preliminary formality but as a substantive gate defined by the Implementing Regulation itself. The Regulation is issued under the Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree م/51) and, in Article Two, expressly assigns SAMA the function of licensing financing activities, so the licensing requirements derive their force from the same parent instrument and definitions (e.g. 'financing company' as a licence-holder for a single activity). You should therefore conclude that a licence scoped to one activity does not authorise others, and that licensing conditions must be read together with the Regulation's defined terms rather than as a stand-alone checklist.
