# Key Risk Indicators

## Connections

### [[Fraud Risk Appetite]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a Member Organisation satisfies SAMA's Counter-Fraud requirements, do not treat Fraud Risk Appetite and KRIs as independent controls: the standard requires KRIs to be defined specifically to measure position against the agreed Fraud Risk Appetite and to flag rising exposure early. Because control 4.1.4 ties KRIs directly to appetite thresholds (which in turn drive escalation to Senior Management and crisis management when breached), a firm claiming compliance must show its KRIs are calibrated to those appetite limits, not to generic metrics. Conclude that absence of KRIs mapped to defined appetite thresholds is a gap that undermines both provisions.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 30 / 4.1.4):** "monitor KRIs to measure and evaluate position against agreed Fraud Risk Appetite and provide an early indication of increasing fraud risk exposure"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 30 / 4.1.3):** "Fraud Risk Appetite ... should put in place measures with associated thresholds and limits"

### [[Fraud Risk Management Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the Fraud Risk Management Framework, note that its effectiveness must be measured using indicators, and KRIs (4.1.4) are a defined mechanism for measuring position against Fraud Risk Appetite and providing early warning of increasing exposure within that framework. Both provisions sit under the same Risk Management principle (4.1), so KRIs are part of how the Framework demonstrates it is working, not an optional add-on. Conclude that a firm should be able to show KRIs are embedded in the Framework's measurement and periodic evaluation; a framework lacking defined, methodology-based KRIs would not satisfy the measurement expectation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 30 / 4.1.4):** "KRIs to monitor exposure against the risks identified in the Fraud Risk Assessment"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 26 / 4.1(b)):** "The effectiveness of the Fraud Risk Management Framework should be measured and periodically evaluated using Key Performance Indicators"
