# Capital Adequacy and Liquidity

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulation of Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping capital and liquidity obligations for a finance company, treat the capital adequacy requirements as sub-provisions of the Implementing Regulation of the Finance Companies Control Law, not as free-standing rules. Both nodes are extracted from the same instrument, the Implementing Regulation issued by SAMA under the Finance Companies Control Law (Royal Decree M/51), which SAMA administers via licensing and supervision of finance companies. This means capital adequacy thresholds derive their enforceability and defined terms (e.g. paid-up capital and reserves, used in the 'large exposure' definition) from the parent Regulation — so verify the applicable article within the Regulation rather than reading capital rules in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2):** "أصدر البنك المركزي السعودي هذه اللائحة وفقاً للصلاحيات المخولة له بموجب نظام مراقبة شركات التمويل"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3, Article 1):** "أنظمة التمويل: نظام التمويل العقاري ونظام الإيجار التمويلي ونظام مراقبة شركات التمويل"
- **Caveat:** Both nodes share the same source file; the split into topical sub-nodes is editorial, so confirm the specific capital-adequacy article number in the primary Regulation text.
