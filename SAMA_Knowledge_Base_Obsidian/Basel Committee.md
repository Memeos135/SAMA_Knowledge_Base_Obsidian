# Basel Committee

## Connections

### [[Alternative Liquidity Approaches (ALA) Options]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the standing of the ALA guidance in Annex 3, understand that these options exist within a Basel Committee framework and that jurisdictional eligibility is externally validated, not self-declared. The document ties ALA use to Basel Committee oversight — notification of transition plans, self-assessment of eligibility, and an independent peer review conducted as part of the Committee's work programme. Conclude that a bank's ability to rely on ALA options in KSA depends on the jurisdiction's continuing eligibility under that Basel-supervised process, and that SAMA-level rules governing usage sit beneath this international framework; verify the current eligibility status rather than assuming the options remain permanently available.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 68 / para 41):** "The Basel Committee will oversee the independent peer review process for determining the eligibility of its member jurisdictions to adopt alternative treatment."

### [[Independent Peer Review of ALA Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing the durability of SAMA's authority to permit ALA treatment, note that eligibility is not self-certified: the independent peer review is conducted as part of the Basel Committee's work programme, and the Committee oversees the process determining a jurisdiction's eligibility to adopt alternative treatment. The jurisdiction must permit the review and 'address the comments made,' and may be required to re-assess ahead of schedule if circumstances change. You should conclude that the continued availability of ALA options underpinning any bank's LCR reliance is contingent on SAMA maintaining its peer-review standing — a condition external to the bank and subject to Basel Committee oversight.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 68, para 41):** "The Basel Committee will oversee the independent peer review process for determining the eligibility of its member jurisdictions to adopt alternative treatment."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 68, Criterion (d)):** "The jurisdiction must permit an independent peer review of its framework for alternative treatment to be conducted as part of the Basel Committee's work programme and address the comments made."
