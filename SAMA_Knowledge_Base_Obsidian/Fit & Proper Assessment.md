# Fit & Proper Assessment

## Connections

### [[Bank (Agent Banking Principal)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When onboarding an Agent, the Bank cannot rely on a light-touch commercial check — it must conduct and evidence a formal fit-and-proper assessment as a licensing prerequisite. Article 13's assessment tests the moral, business and professional suitability of the Agent and its proprietors/managers, screens for negative credit-bureau data and ML/TF, fraud or integrity-related criminal records, and adequacy of funding sources; Article 4 further requires the CEO or a designated senior officer to sign a declaration confirming the assessment was performed. You should conclude that a documented, senior-attested fit-and-proper file is a condition of SAMA non-objection, and its absence is itself a compliance failure by the Bank.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 12 / Art 13):** "carry out a 'fit and proper' assessment ... ensuring that the Agent, its proprietors, and persons involved in the management ... are sufficiently competent, 'fit and proper'"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / Art 4):** "signed declaration by its Chief Executive Officer or a duly designated senior officer, confirming that the Bank has carried out the proper and fit assessment"
