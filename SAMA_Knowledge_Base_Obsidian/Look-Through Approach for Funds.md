- **What this link tells you:** When classifying fund and index exposures at the banking/trading book boundary, note that the boundary determines when the look-through approach applies: funds assigned to the trading book but that cannot be looked through must use the standardised approach ([3.11] and [5.8](5)(b)), while funds that can be looked through are treated as if the underlying positions were held directly.
