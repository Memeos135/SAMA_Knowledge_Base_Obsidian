# Bank Accounts for Non-Resident Real Estate Owners

## Connections

### [[Banking Account Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how a bank may onboard a non-resident who owns (or seeks to own) KSA real estate, read this as an amendment inserting new clauses into the existing Banking Account Rules rather than a standalone regime: the circular adds sub-rules under the 'natural persons non-resident' and 'non-resident juristic persons' categories to enable account opening solely for real-estate ownership under the Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law. You would conclude that these accounts carry scope limits and specific controls — no joint accounts or out-of-Kingdom authorised signatories, no payment/credit cards, remote-opening constraints, verification via a trusted independent KSA source, and property-related transactions only through approved electronic channels — and that these override the general account-opening baseline for this customer class.

### [[Real Estate Finance Law]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a bank on opening accounts for non-resident foreign real estate owners, do not assume the Real Estate Finance Law governs this activity — the two instruments address different transactions. The circular implements the Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law (Royal Decree M/186) and sets KYC/account-opening controls for non-residents buying property, while the Real Estate Finance Law regulates deferred-payment finance contracts extended to a 'Borrower' to own a dwelling and applies to 'Real Estate Finance Entities'. They appear connected only through the common subject of real estate; verify the primary texts before treating account-opening controls as if they carried finance-contract obligations.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2):** "opening bank accounts for the category of non-resident customers covered by the Non-Saudi Real Estate Ownership Law for the purpose of owning real estate (paraphrase)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Art 1):** "Real Estate Finance Contract: A deferred payment contract extended to a borrower to own a dwelling."
