# Dispute Resolution Rules

## Connections

### [[Regulations for Consumer Financing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on a Borrower complaint under a consumer financing account, use Section Seven (Article 23) as the controlling procedure embedded within these Regulations, not a separate regime. It imposes concrete duties on the Creditor: investigate and resolve within 60 (max 90) Calendar Days, suspend collection of the disputed amount and its Term Cost/charges until Satisfactorily Resolved, and refrain from adverse credit reporting during that window. You would conclude that a Creditor cannot pursue or report a disputed balance prematurely, and would check the 30-day notice window, the 60/90-day resolution timeline, and that any unresolved dispute is escalated to the Committee for Settlement of Banking Disputes as the final authority.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 31, Art 23):** "The Creditor may not try to collect any amount, Term Cost or other charges related to the account statement error/dispute until the dispute is Satisfactorily Resolved"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Page 33):** "The Committee for Settlement of Banking Disputes is the final authority in resolving any unresolved disputes between the Borrower and the Creditor"
