# Use of External Ratings

## Connections

### [[ECAI Rating Mapping]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying external ratings to determine risk weights, use the mapping rules as the operative constraint on how ratings feed into the framework: banks must apply chosen ECAIs consistently (no cherry-picking), use the global scale, and follow the multiple-ratings tie-break rules (two ratings → higher weight; three or more → the two lowest, then higher if they differ). This mapping chapter operationalizes the 'use of external ratings' regime that the framework routes through chapter 8. Conclude that selecting the most favourable available rating or mixing ECAIs inconsistently is non-compliant, and that where national scales are used they must be mapped to the global scale before a risk weight is derived.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9):** "The requirements covering the use of external ratings are set out in chapter 8."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 57 / Art 8.8–8.12):** "Banks are not allowed to 'cherry-pick' the ratings... If there are two ratings by ECAIs... that map into different risk weights, the higher risk weight will be applied."

### [[Eligible Credit Assessment Institution (ECAI)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining risk weights for externally-rated or securitization exposures, do not treat 'use of external ratings' as a free-standing permission: a rating only counts if it comes from an institution that satisfies the ECAI eligibility criteria in chapter 8. The Use of External Ratings chapter expressly routes back to chapter 8 ('The requirements covering the use of external ratings are set out in chapter 8'), and the ECAI provisions set the recognition conditions (public availability of ratings/methodologies, demonstrated expertise, cooperation with SAMA). Conclude that before relying on any external rating for capital purposes you must confirm SAMA recognition of the ECAI and that the specific rating meets the publication/transparency conditions; unpublished or transaction-only ratings must be treated as unrated.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 9):** "The requirements covering the use of external ratings are set out in chapter 8."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 302 / Page 54):** "an eligible credit assessment... must be publicly available, on a non-selective basis and free of charge... ratings made available only to the parties to a transaction do not satisfy this requirement."

### [[External Credit Risk Assessment Approach (ECRA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining risk weights for externally-rated securitization exposures under SEC-ERBA, you cannot treat the external rating as self-executing: the ECRA/SEC-ERBA machinery is only usable if the operational criteria for external credit assessments are met. This link ties the SEC-ERBA risk-weighting steps to the general 'use of external ratings' operational regime (ECAI eligibility, full reflection of credit risk, SAMA recognition per chapter 8). Conclude that any SEC-ERBA risk weight is contingent on the rating coming from a SAMA-recognized ECAI and reflecting the entire credit risk exposure — check the operational criteria at 20.8–20.10 and chapter 8 before relying on a rated treatment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 299 / 20.1):** "For securitization exposures that are externally rated... provided that the operational criteria in 20.8 to 20.10 are met."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 301 / 20.8):** "the external credit assessment must take into account and reflect the entire amount of credit risk exposure the bank has with regard to all payments owed to it."
