### [[Contracting Cash-in-Transit Companies & Security Bags]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's, exchange house's or large merchant's cash-handling obligations, treat the SAMA circular as the operational enforcement layer sitting on top of the cash-transport Law: the Law licenses and regulates the activity of transporting money, precious metals and valuable documents, while the circular directs regulated entities to contract only with licensed cash-in-transit companies and use SAMA-approved security bags. Both texts flow from the same security-committee framework (General Security, Ministry of Finance, SAMA) governing this activity, so the circular's mandate is a concrete application of the licensing regime the Law establishes. Practically, a compliance reader should read the two together — the Law tells you who may lawfully perform the transport, and the circular tells regulated firms they may not move cash except through those licensed carriers and approved bags, with SAMA field inspections and penalties for non-compliance. The 'references' link is inferred from subject-matter overlap, so verify the circular expressly invokes the Law before treating it as its formal implementing instrument.

### [[Prohibition on Dealing with Non-Saudi Security Escort Reps]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising banks on engaging security-escort or cash-transport providers, read the SAMA prohibition circular as an enforcement instruction operating under the Law on Transport of Money, Precious Metals & Valuable Documents (and the Private Civil Security Guarding Law) — the circular expressly reaffirms compliance with that law and its Implementing Regulation while barring dealings with non-Saudi representatives of such firms. The consequence: banks must not negotiate with any non-Saudi representative of these companies and must report them to regional police, and this duty sits atop the licensing/eligibility requirements the parent law places on cash-transport activity. Check counterparty representatives' status and licensing under the transport law before contracting.
