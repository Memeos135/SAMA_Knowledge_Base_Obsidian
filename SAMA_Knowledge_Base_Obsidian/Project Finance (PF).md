# Project Finance (PF)

## Connections

### [[Specialized Lending (SL)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When setting the capital treatment for project finance, treat PF as a defined sub-class of Specialized Lending so that SL-specific risk weights and phase-based adjustments apply. Para 10.8 lists PF as one of the five SL sub-classes; para 7.44 then imposes distinct weights (130% pre-operational, 100% operational, 80% for high-quality operational-phase PF meeting para 7.45 conditions). Conclude that PF exposures cannot be weighted like generic corporate exposures — verify the project's operational phase and the para 7.45 high-quality conditions before applying a reduced weight.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 34 / 7.44(2)):** "Project finance exposures will be risk-weighted at 130% during the pre-operational phase and 100% during the operational phase ... deemed to be high quality ... will be risk weighted at 80%."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 100 / 10.8):** "The five sub-classes of SL are project finance (PF), object finance (OF), commodities finance (CF), income-producing real estate (IPRE) lending, and high-volatility commercial real estate (HVCRE) lending."

### [[Supervisory Slotting Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When deciding how a specialized-lending project finance exposure is capitalised, note that project finance is a defined SL sub-class whose RWA — where no eligible external rating exists — is derived through the supervisory slotting approach, which the disclosure templates locate in the IRB family under SCRE13. The project finance provisions set the risk weights (130% pre-operational, 100%/80% operational) and the 'high quality' conditions that feed the slotting categorisation, while the slotting node confirms slotting is reported separately from F-IRB/A-IRB. Conclude that PF capital treatment must be traced through the slotting criteria rather than assumed to follow ordinary corporate IRB, and that its RWA is disclosed in the dedicated supervisory-slotting row.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 34 / para 7.44-7.45):** "Project finance exposures will be risk-weighted at 130% during the pre-operational phase and 100% during the operational phase... deemed to be high quality... risk weighted at 80%."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 751 / row 4):** "Of which: supervisory slotting approach: RWA and capital requirements according to the supervisory slotting approach (as specified in SCRE13)."
