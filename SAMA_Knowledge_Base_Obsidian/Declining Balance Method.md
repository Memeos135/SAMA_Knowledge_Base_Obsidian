### [[Guideline for Calculating Early Repayment Amount]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When calculating an early-repayment settlement or the lender's permissible compensation, apply the declining-balance method as mandated by this Guideline: term cost must be distributed proportionally across installments on the basis of the outstanding principal at the start of each period, and on early repayment the borrower pays the remaining principal plus term cost for only the following three months, computed on the declining balance. This is the unified method SAMA imposes on all finance providers for monthly-installment contracts (personal, auto finance-lease, real estate). You would conclude that lender charges exceeding the three-month declining-balance ceiling (plus limited third-party costs) are non-compliant.
