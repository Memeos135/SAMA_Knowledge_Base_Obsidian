# Money Changing Business Rules

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Prudential and accounting conduct standards for licensed money changers, binding the sectoral rulebook to specific external-audit/accounting expectations.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Rules Governing Money Changing Business set the primary licensing/conduct regime; the Circular on External Auditor Accounting Standards is a subordinate instruction operating within that regime.
- The circular references the Rules to specify accounting/audit standards money changers' external auditors must apply — read as an enforceable reporting obligation, not guidance.
- Compliance consequence: money changers must satisfy both the general conduct rules and the audit-standard circular; the circular narrows how financial statements are prepared and verified.
