# IRB Disclosure Requirements

## Connections

### [[Minimum Requirements to Use IRB Approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a bank must do to rely on its own internal risk estimates, read the IRB disclosure requirements as an integral condition of the minimum-requirements chapter, not an optional add-on — chapter 10 makes IRB approval 'subject to certain minimum conditions and disclosure requirements', and disclosure is listed as Section 11 of chapter 16. This means adequate public disclosure is a supervisory precondition for continued IRB use. For an eligibility assessment you should confirm the disclosure section is met alongside the other mandatory sections, since it is one of the standing conditions of IRB authorization.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 91 / para 10.1):** "Subject to certain minimum conditions and disclosure requirements, banks that have received SAMA's approval to use the IRB approach may rely on their own internal estimates"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 177 / para 16.1):** "minimum requirements for entry and on-going use of the internal ratings-based (IRB) approach...(11) Disclosure requirements"
