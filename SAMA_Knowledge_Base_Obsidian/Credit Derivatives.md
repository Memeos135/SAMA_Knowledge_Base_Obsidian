# Credit Derivatives

## Connections

### [[Off-Balance Sheet Items and CCF]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When capitalising credit derivatives, note they sit adjacent to the off-balance sheet/CCF rules but follow a distinct treatment: the credit derivatives provisions (7.95) apply protection-provider capital rules and reference 7.94's CCR routing, while the off-balance sheet section (7.86–7.93) governs commitments and CCFs. Both are in the same standardized-approach exposure chapter and share the 7.86 commitment definition carried into the IRB EAD rules. Conclude that a first-/nth-to-default derivative on which the bank provides protection is not treated as an ordinary off-balance sheet commitment under CCF; apply the aggregation-of-risk-weights method in 7.95 rather than a CCF.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 48 / 7.95):** "A bank providing credit protection through a first-to-default or second-to-default credit derivative is subject to capital requirements on such instruments."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 126 / 12.33):** "The types of instruments and the CCFs applied to them under the F-IRB approach are the same as those in the standardized approach, as set out in paragraphs 7.86 to 7.93."
