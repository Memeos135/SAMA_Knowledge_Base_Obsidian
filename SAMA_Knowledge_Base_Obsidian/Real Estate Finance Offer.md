- **What this link tells you:** When mapping the pre-contract sequence for a retail mortgage, link the credit advisor role directly to the offer timeline, because the circular ties the advisor's mandated explanation to the life of the Real Estate Finance Offer. ### [[SAMA Mandatory Instructions on Retail Real Estate Finance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on retail mortgage sales conduct, treat the Real Estate Finance Offer as a regulated instrument with mandatory minimum terms set by this SAMA circular, not a freely-drafted commercial document. The circular requires the financier to give the customer an offer valid for not less than fifteen business days, containing clear printed copies of all documents in the same form that would be signed at contract, and to allow the customer to take those documents away for independent advice; it also imposes a cooling/review period of not less than five business days from receipt. Conclude that offer validity duration, document completeness and the review window are compliance-mandatory features whose breach, per paragraph 6, bars conclusion of any retail real estate finance contract.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2-3, paras 3 and 5):** "a review period of not less than five business days from the customer's receipt of the offer (English paraphrase)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2, para 3):** "the financier provides the customer a real estate finance offer valid for not less than fifteen business days from delivery (English paraphrase)"
- **Caveat:** Source Arabic is OCR-garbled; wording paraphrased — confirm exact validity periods and offer contents in the primary text.

### [[Variable-Rate Credit Risk Acknowledgement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a real estate finance offer to an individual is compliant, treat the Variable-Rate Credit Risk Acknowledgement as a conditional mandatory attachment, not an optional add-on: the circular requires the offer package to include this signed acknowledgement form specifically for variable-cost mortgage products, and prohibits the financier from concluding any real estate finance contract until the customer has been given these documents and allowed to take them away. Because paragraph 6 bars contract conclusion until all preceding paragraphs are satisfied and documented in the finance file, an offer for a variable-rate product that omits or fails to evidence receipt of this acknowledgement is defective. A compliance reviewer should therefore verify, for variable-cost products only, that the acknowledgement was included in the offer, that customer receipt was documented, and that the customer was enabled to remove it from the financier's premises before signing.
