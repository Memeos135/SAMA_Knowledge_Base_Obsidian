# Treatment of Expected Losses and Provisions

## Connections

### [[Calculation of Expected Losses]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how expected losses affect a bank's regulatory capital under the IRB approach, treat 'Calculation of Expected Losses' as a sub-step of the broader chapter on EL and provisions, not a standalone rule. Chapter 15 both defines how EL is computed (PD x LGD x EAD, with supervisory or best-estimate values for defaulted exposures) and directs that the difference between EL and total eligible provisions is then included in or deducted from capital per the Basel III capital rules (Circular 341000015689). For a capital-adequacy assessment you should read the calculation and the provisions-comparison together, and exclude securitization EL, which is handled separately at paragraph 18.36.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 174 / para 15.1):** "the method by which the difference between provisions...and EL may be included in or must be deducted from regulatory capital"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 174 / para 15.2):** "A bank must sum the EL amount (defined as EL multiplied by exposure at default) associated with its exposures to which the IRB approach is applied...to obtain a total EL amount."

### [[Total Eligible Provisions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank has excess or shortfall of provisions against EL for capital purposes, you need both sides of the comparison: chapter 15 sets the EL/provisions treatment, and 'Total Eligible Provisions' (para 15.4) fixes what may be counted on the provisions side. This matters because eligibility is scoped — only provisions attributed to IRB-treated exposures qualify, discounts on defaulted assets may be added, and provisions set aside against securitization exposures must be excluded. For the EL-vs-provisions offset you should therefore verify each provision's classification before including it, and apply the pro-rata/attribution rules of paras 15.5–15.7 where the bank uses both standardized and IRB approaches.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 174 / para 15.1):** "the difference between provisions...and EL may be included in or must be deducted from regulatory capital"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 175 / para 15.4):** "Total eligible provisions are defined as the sum of all provisions...that are attributed to exposures treated under the IRB approach...General and specific provisions set aside against securitization exposures must not be included"
