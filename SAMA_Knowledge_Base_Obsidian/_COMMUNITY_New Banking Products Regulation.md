# New Banking Products Regulation

**Cohesion:** 0.50 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

Pre-launch supervisory control over new banking products/services, including financial derivatives. This regime governs when a bank must assess risk and obtain SAMA's non-objection before offering a product.

## How members connect

- The Regulation is the parent instrument; it imposes a duty to conduct a Products and Services Risk Assessment before launch.
- It ties that assessment to the Notification and Non-Objection Requirements — a bank must notify SAMA and, where required, secure non-objection prior to offering.
- Offering of Financial Derivatives Products is a specific scoped case governed by the same notification/non-objection gate, so derivatives offerings inherit the general regime's approval obligations.
