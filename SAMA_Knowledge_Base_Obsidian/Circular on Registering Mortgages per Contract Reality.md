# Circular on Registering Mortgages per Contract Reality

## Connections

### [[Registered Real Estate Mortgage Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing how banks and finance companies must document real estate collateral, read this circular as SAMA exercising powers under the SAMA Law, Banking Control Law and Finance Companies Control Law, with express reference to the Registered Real Estate Mortgage Law (Royal Decree M/49). The circular directs financiers to document mortgages according to the true nature of the contract, to cease property title-transfer procedures in lieu of mortgage, and to rectify properties currently registered in the financier's name within a set period. You should conclude that title transfer used as disguised collateral is non-compliant and that existing arrangements require correction under the mortgage-law regime.
