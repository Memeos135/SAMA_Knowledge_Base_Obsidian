# Credit Bureaus Fintech Membership Circular (SAMA 1608)

## Connections

### [[Credit Information Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a credit information (credit bureau) company on entering membership agreements with fintechs, read this circular as expressly subordinate to the Credit Information Law and its implementing regulation: the circular relaxes the prior no-objection requirement but explicitly places full responsibility on the bureau to comply with the Law, its Implementing Regulation and related SAMA instructions. The circular therefore cannot be relied on in isolation. Conclude that the permission it grants is conditioned on continued conformity with the primary Credit Information Law regime, which governs access to consumers' credit records and information.
