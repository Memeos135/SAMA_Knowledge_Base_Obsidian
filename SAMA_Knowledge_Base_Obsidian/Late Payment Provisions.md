# Late Payment Provisions

## Connections

### [[Breach and Contract Termination]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating whether a lender can lawfully terminate an individual real-estate finance contract for non-payment, read the Breach/Termination article and the Late Payment Provisions as a chain: late payment is the triggering default, and termination is the escalated remedy, so the enforceability of termination depends on the late-payment consequences (and any cure steps) being satisfied first. Both are enumerated articles of the same SAMA-mandated model contract, and the summary sheet cross-lists 'consequences of late payment of installments' with 'procedures for exercising the right to terminate the finance contract.' For a compliance decision, do not treat termination as available on breach alone — check that the late-payment article's prerequisites were met before termination was invoked.

### [[Default Notice (Ish'ar Ta'aththur)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a financier's remedies for missed payments, read the default notice as the trigger tied to the contract's late-payment provisions, which set out the consequences of late instalment payment. The contract's late-payment article (أحكام التأخر في السداد) and its summary of 'effects of late payment' define what may follow non-payment, and a default notice is the mechanism that engages those consequences before escalation to breach/termination. You should conclude that any late-payment charge or acceleration depends on the substantive limits in this article, and verify those limits (and any required prior notice) in the article's text before relying on a remedy.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Article (16)):** "المادة (16) (أحكام التأخر في السداد)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / summary):** "الآثار المترتبة على التأخر في سداد الدفعات"
- **Caveat:** Default notice is a concept inferred from the late-payment article; confirm the exact interaction (notice requirement, permissible charges) against the article text, which is partly OCR-degraded.

### [[Delay Notice (Ish'ar Ta'akhkhur)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a finance company may charge or enforce once an individual lessee misses an Ijara payment, treat the delay-notice concept and the Late Payment Provisions (أحكام التأخر في السداد) as one operative block: the notice is the procedural gateway that engages the substantive late-payment effects the same article sets out. The contract summary confirms this by listing the delay article alongside 'الآثار المترتبة على التأخر في سداد الدفعات' (the effects arising from delayed payment), meaning consequences flow from the delay provisions rather than from an independent power. Conclude that any late-payment charge or remedy must be grounded in the mandatory template's late-payment article and preceded by a compliant delay notice, not imposed unilaterally.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / summary):** "الآثار المترتبة على التأخرفي سداد الدفعات"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / أحكام التأخر في السداد):** "أحكام التأخرفي السداد"
- **Caveat:** The two labels appear to describe the same template article (Art on delayed payment); the source Arabic is OCR-degraded, so confirm the exact article number and text in the primary contract before relying on this.
