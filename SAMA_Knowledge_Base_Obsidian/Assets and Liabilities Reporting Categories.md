# Assets and Liabilities Reporting Categories

## Connections

### [[Interest Rates on Assets and Liabilities Reporting Guidelines]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When completing the quarterly Assets and Liabilities interest-rate returns, treat the Reporting Categories as the mandatory taxonomy the guideline requires banks to populate — they are not illustrative. The guideline requires all banks (including foreign branches) to submit within 30 days, signed by the CFO, and Section C states the classifications 'are mutually exclusive,' meaning each exposure maps to exactly one category (e.g. Loans to Governments and Quasi Government, Loans to Corporates, Retail Loans). A preparer should confirm each reported item aligns to the Annexure-2 validation table linking these categories to M1 items, since misclassification breaches the mutual-exclusivity rule.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Reporting Categories, Section C):** "Reporting Categories A. Assets: Accrued rates receivable. 1. Loans to Governments and Quasi Government... classifications of Assets and Liabilities are mutually exclusive."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Reporting Guidance):** "Banks should comply with the following in completing the report related to Assets and Liabilities interest rates... Template (1) - by Product... Template (2) - by Sector."

### [[Weighted Denominator Calculation]] — `shares_data_with` [AMBIGUOUS]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are reconciling prudential returns, treat these two as distinct reporting regimes rather than a shared data feed. The Interest Rates on Assets and Liabilities guidelines classify assets/liabilities by product, sector and Sharia type at a domestic level, while the LDR guidelines define a separate net-loan numerator and weighted-deposit denominator reported monthly on a consolidated basis with different inclusion rules (e.g. interbank and SAMA transactions excluded). The claimed data-sharing link is not established in the text — the definitions, scope and periodicity differ. Before mapping figures across the two returns, verify each item's definition in its own guideline, as balances qualifying for one report will not necessarily match the LDR components.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_3276_VER1 Page 4-5):** "Assets and Liabilities Weighted Average (W.A) rates and balances... Reports should be completed at a domestic level only"
- **Grounding — related node (SAMA_EN_3343_VER1 Page 5 / 4.3-4.4):** "interbank transactions and transactions with SAMA should not be included in the LDR calculation, unless specifically stated by SAMA"
- **Caveat:** Relation is AMBIGUOUS; no textual basis for actual data-sharing between the two returns — verify definitions independently before cross-mapping.
