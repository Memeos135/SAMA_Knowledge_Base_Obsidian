# Watch List

## Connections

### [[Early Warning Signals (EWS)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's problem-loan controls under these Guidelines, treat the watch list as the operational output of the EWS process, not a standalone register: the EWS unit interprets signals and decides which borrowers go onto the watch list for corrective action. The link tells you that a compliant EWS framework must feed a properly maintained watch list, with risk-management approval to remove loans and defined maximum tenure before escalation. Check that inclusion criteria, minimum data fields, and monthly reporting to a management committee/Board are documented, since SAMA treats an effective EWS framework as a 'mandatory requirement.'
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Action & Monitoring):** "A loan is added to the watch list prepared on the basis of EWS ... Risk management approval required to remove the loan from watch list"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / 2.3 Identification):** "determine whether that borrower should be included in the watch list (prepared on the basis of EWS) for further corrective action"

### [[Workout Unit]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing escalation controls, read the watch list and Workout Unit as sequential stages of the same mandatory pathway: a loan may remain on the watch list only temporarily, and once the bank's specified time expires it must be either returned to the originating unit (if resolved) or transferred to the Workout Unit. The Guidelines require a documented, time-bound policy with clear criteria and approvals for the mandatory transfer to the WU. Check that the bank has defined the maximum watch-list tenure and the objective transfer triggers, because leaving stressed loans in the originating unit is expressly flagged as raising NPLs and lowering recovery.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Monitoring):** "After that period, loan must be either returned to originating unit or transferred to Workout Unit"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 15 / B):** "Banks shall establish and document a policy with clear and objective time-bound criteria for the mandatory transfer of loans ... to the Workout Unit"
