# Scenario Analysis

## Connections

### [[Stress Testing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating whether a bank's methodology choices meet the Rules, understand that Scenario Analysis is one of the named methodologies banks may adopt within their stress-testing framework, capturing simultaneous moves in multiple risk factors. The Rules leave methodology selection to the bank based on business nature, size and risk profile, but SAMA reviews the adequacy of the scenarios and methodologies adopted under Section 12. Conclude that a bank has discretion in choosing scenario vs. sensitivity approaches, yet must be able to justify that choice against its portfolio and defend it on supervisory review — SAMA does not prescribe risk factors on the bank's behalf.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 17 / Section 5.2):** "Scenario Analysis measures the change in value of portfolio due to simultaneous moves in a number of risk factors."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 40 / Para 5):** "It is up to the banks to choose appropriate methodologies and techniques in line with their risk profiles and stress testing frameworks."
