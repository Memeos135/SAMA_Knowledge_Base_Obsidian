# Corporate Governance and Risk Management

## Connections

### [[Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) Regulations 2020]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping a DTFC's governance and control obligations, note that the Corporate Governance and Risk Management chapter imposes mandatory, proportionate arrangements including per-senior-manager job descriptions tied to SAMA fit-and-proper pre-approval and a maintained Management Responsibilities Description Document (MRDD). Critically, this chapter's related provisions also carry the AML/CTF supervisory-officer and ongoing-monitoring duties — the Compliance Officer coordinates with Internal Audit, must have unrestricted access to customer and transaction records, and DTFCs must monitor, risk-classify and report suspicious activity to the Financial Investigation Unit and SAMA. Conclude that governance and AML functions here are intertwined obligations of the same firm and must be scoped together, and that senior-manager appointments require SAMA pre-approval.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / paras 10-11; Page 51 / paras 165-169):** "robust governance and risk management arrangements... Be included in every application to SAMA for pre-approval as a senior manager... report these to the Financial Investigation Unit, and inform SAMA"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / para 3):** "DTFCs are also required to comply with the Finance Companies Control Law, SAMA regulations for Finance Companies (FCs), and other relevant laws and regulations"

### [[SAMA Fit and Proper Regulations Reference]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a DTFC's senior-management appointments, treat the corporate-governance obligation and the Fit and Proper regime as a single gating process. Chapter 3 requires a Job Description for each senior manager that must be included in every application to SAMA for pre-approval 'as per SAMA's fit and proper regulations,' and re-submitted on any significant change of responsibilities. You should conclude that senior manager pre-approval is conditioned on the separate SAMA Fit and Proper Regulations — so a governance structure cannot be evidenced as complete without confirming each pre-approval and JD/MRDD was processed under that fit-and-proper standard.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Chapter 3, para 10(ii)-(iii)):** "Be included in every application to SAMA for pre-approval as a senior manager as per SAMA's fit and proper regulations"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / Chapter 3, para 10):** "be updated and resubmitted if there is a significant change to the senior manager's responsibilities as per SAMA's fit and proper regulations"
