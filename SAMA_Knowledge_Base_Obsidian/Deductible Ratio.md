# Deductible Ratio

## Connections

### [[Responsible Lending Principles for Individual Customers]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a creditor lawfully granted individual finance, the Deductible Ratio is the operative quantitative test defined and capped within these Principles, not a discretionary metric. The document defines Deductible Ratio as the ratio of the consumer's monthly credit obligations to total monthly income and then sets binding ceilings by income band (e.g. non-real-estate obligations capped at 45%, total obligations at 55%/65% for Housing/REDF beneficiaries). So to test compliance, apply the specific income-band caps in paragraphs 15–17 using the defined inputs (Monthly Credit Obligations, Gross Salary, Total Monthly Income) rather than a general affordability judgment.
- **Grounding — this node (SAMA_EN_1948_VER1 / Ch IV para 15 (Page 9)):** "Monthly credit obligations of finance must not exceed 55% of the total monthly income of the consumer..."
- **Grounding — related node (SAMA_EN_1948_VER1 / Ch II (Page 5)):** "taking into account reasonable deductible ratios that the consumer can afford."
