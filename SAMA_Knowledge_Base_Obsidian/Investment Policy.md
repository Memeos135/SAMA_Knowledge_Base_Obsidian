# Investment Policy

## Connections

### [[Approval Requirements and Controls]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's investment governance is complete, treat the SAMA non-objection/approval regime and the internal Investment Policy as one interlocking obligation set rather than separate checklists. The Approval Requirements (Art. 8) require SAMA non-objection for regulated investments and for changes to their structure/limits, while the Investment Policy (Art. 6.4) must itself specify investment channels and the approval requirement per product — so the policy is the internal instrument that operationalizes the external approval gate. A reviewer should conclude that an Investment Policy silent on approval channels and prior-non-objection triggers is non-compliant, and should confirm the policy cross-refers the Banking Control Law/Ministerial Decree No. 3/2149 basis rather than relying on the approval clause in isolation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Art 6.4):** "Banks' Investment Policy should include... Governance requirements including setting controls, responsibilities, and delegation of authority... Prudential exposure limits."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9 / Art 8.1–8.2):** "Banks should obtain SAMA non-objection on investments that require regulatory approval... Any changes in these investments' structure or limits... will require prior non-objection from SAMA."
- **Caveat:** Link inferred: both are provisions of the same Rules and are functionally connected, but no explicit cross-reference between Art. 8 and Art. 6.4 appears in the extracted text — verify the primary Rules before relying on it.

### [[Investment Governance Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When testing governance compliance, treat the Investment Policy as the mandatory instrument through which the governance requirements are operationalised — Section 6.4 prescribes its minimum contents, including governance controls and delegation, investment eligibility criteria, prudential exposure limits tied to SAMA capital/liquidity/reserve requirements, counterparty criteria and data-keeping. The two are not separable: a bank cannot demonstrate governance compliance without a Board-approved Policy containing these elements. In a review you would check that the Policy exists, is Board-approved, and actually covers each enumerated item rather than treating governance as a general expectation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6-7 / Sec 6.4):** "Prudential exposure limits taking into consideration minimum capital, liquidity and reserve requirements set by SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / Sec 6.4):** "Banks' Investment Policy should include, but not limited to, the following: Governance requirements including setting controls, responsibilities, and delegation of authority."
