# Confiscation of Proceeds and Instrumentalities

## Connections

### [[Anti-Money Laundering Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on the downstream consequences of an ML conviction, treat confiscation as a mandatory court-ordered outcome running from the offence provisions of the same Law, not a discretionary add-on. Article 33 requires the competent court, upon conviction, to confiscate laundered funds, proceeds (including intermingled funds up to their value) and instrumentalities, and Articles 34-35 extend this to non-conviction scenarios and value-equivalent substitute assets. Note the express carve-out for bona fide third parties who paid fair value without knowledge of illicit origin, which is the key point to check when assessing exposure of counterparties or asset holders.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 11 / Art 33):** "in the event of a conviction... the competent Court shall issue an order to confiscate... Laundered funds; Proceeds of the crime... and Instrumentalities."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / Art 39):** "assistance with the tracing, seizure, recovery and confiscation of funds, or proceeds of crime or Instrumentalities related to the Money Laundering Crimes"
