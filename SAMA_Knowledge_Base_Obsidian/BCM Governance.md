# BCM Governance

## Connections

### [[Business Continuity Management Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a Member Organization's BCM program is compliant, treat BCM Governance as a mandatory constituent requirement of the Framework, not an optional best practice. The Framework (Section 2, Business Continuity Requirements) opens with clause 2.1 BCM Governance, which fixes board-level accountability, a mandated BCM committee, and a staffed BCM function. You should therefore check that ultimate responsibility sits with the board or a delegated executive and that the committee/charter/function exist, since absence of this governance layer is itself a Framework gap.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / 2.1):** "Board of directors or a delegated executive member should have the ultimate responsibility for the BCM program."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 7 / Section 2):** "2 Business Continuity Requirements 2.1 BCM Governance... The business continuity governance framework should be defined, approved, implemented and maintained."
