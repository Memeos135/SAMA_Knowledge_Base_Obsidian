# Finance Company Holidays

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Operational-continuity and holiday scheduling obligations for licensed finance companies under the Finance Companies Control Law.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy: the Finance Companies Control Law is the enabling statute; the Circular on Finance Company Holidays is a subordinate directive issued under SAMA's supervisory authority.
- The circular references the Control Law as its legal basis for prescribing holiday/closure arrangements.
- Compliance consequence: finance companies must align working-day and closure practices with the circular; relevant for consumer-facing timing, payment due dates, and grace-period calculations.
