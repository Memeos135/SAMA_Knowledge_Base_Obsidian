# Initial Viability Assessment

## Connections

### [[Loan Life Coverage Ratio (LLCR)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating viability of longer-dated exposures, note that the guidance designates LLCR as a specific viability metric for project finance and multi-year loans, with an indicative benchmark of 1.4. Both provisions are in SAMA_EN_3575; the viability-assessment text points to LLCR 'as defined below' and cross-refers to Appendix 2 for computation. You would conclude that for multi-year and project-finance NPLs, LLCR (not just Debt/EBITDA or LTV) is the expected input to the initial viability determination, while remembering the thresholds are 'indicative rather than prescriptive.'
- **Grounding — this node (Page 23):** "Loan life coverage ratio (LLCR), as defined below should be 1.4, which would give a cushion of 40%"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 82 / Appendix 2):** "LLCR should be used by Workout teams to assess the viability of a given amount of debt"

### [[Portfolio Segmentation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing how a bank triages NPLs, read the initial viability assessment as the second, embedded stage of the segmentation process, not a standalone step. The guidance sequences them explicitly: 'Stage two — initial viability assessment' follows the Stage one segmentation, and both are to be done 'immediately after the non-performing loan is transferred to the Workout Unit.' You would verify that viable, marginally-viable and non-viable classifications flow directly from the segmentation exercise and drive resolution routing (workout vs. legal/foreclosure), since the guidance says non-viable loans should be 'promptly referred to legal unit.'
- **Grounding — this node (Page 23):** "determining viable, marginally-viable and non-viable borrowers"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 22):** "Stage two — initial viability assessment. Following the initial segmentation, NPLs which are currently not in legal procedure should be further screened"
