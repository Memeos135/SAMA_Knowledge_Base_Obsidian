# Outsourcing Rules for Finance Companies

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (M51)|Finance Companies Control Law (M/51)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the legal basis for a finance company's outsourcing obligations, trace them to the Finance Companies Control Law: the Outsourcing Rules are issued under SAMA's powers 'pursuant to the Finance Companies Control Law issued by Royal Decree M/51' and its Implementing Regulation, and the Rules direct that they be read together with that law and regulation. This is a parent-law to implementing-rules link. You should conclude that outsourcing responsibilities (including the non-delegable retention of liability where a service provider breaches applicable rules) rest on and are interpreted through the Finance Companies Control Law and its Implementing Regulation.

### [[Instructions for Financiers Dealing with Promissory Notes]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a finance company on enforcing a promissory note through a third party, do not treat the Promissory Note Instructions in isolation — they expressly incorporate the Outsourcing Rules where enforcement tasks are outsourced. The Promissory Note Instructions state that if promissory-note enforcement is assigned to a third party the financier must comply with SAMA's related instructions, and their 'related instructions' list explicitly cites the finance-company outsourcing rules. You would therefore apply the full outsourcing regime (board-approved policy, risk review, retained liability) on top of the promissory-note requirements whenever collection/enforcement is delegated.

### [[Outsourcing to External Service Providers]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what an outsourcing arrangement legally requires of a licensed finance company, treat this concept as the subject matter of the Outsourcing Rules document itself — the document IS the source of the concept's defined terms and obligations. The Rules define 'outsourcing to an external service provider' as any contract/agreement under which an external provider delivers functions to the finance company, and expressly make the finance company non-exempt from liability if the provider breaches applicable rules. You would conclude that outsourcing does not transfer regulatory responsibility: the finance company must have a board-approved policy, conduct risk review at contracting and renewal, and remain liable for the outsourced functions.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Chapter 3 (Responsibility & Compliance)):** "ولا تعفى شركة التمويل من المسئولية في حال عدم التزام مقدم الخدمات الخارجي بالأنظمة واللوائح والتعليمات السارية"
