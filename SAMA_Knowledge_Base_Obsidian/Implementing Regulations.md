# Implementing Regulations

## Connections

### [[Credit Information Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the full compliance perimeter under the Credit Information Law, do not rely on the primary Law alone — it repeatedly delegates operative detail (fees, update timelines, retention periods, committee mechanics, licensing controls) to the Implementing Regulations, which SAMA's Governor must issue within 180 days and publish in the Official Gazette. Article 12 further makes breach of the Implementing Regulations itself a violation carrying Article 13 penalties (fines up to one million riyals, suspension, or licence revocation). Conclude that any assessment of update-timing or retention obligations must be verified against the current Implementing Regulations, not just the Law's articles.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 8 / Art 12(8), Art 13):** "any other violation of the provisions of this Law or its Implementing Regulations ... a fine not exceeding one million riyals"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9 / Art 16):** "The Governor of SAMA shall issue the Implementing Regulations of this Law within (180) days ... and ... shall be published in the Official Gazette"
