# Finance Consumer Protection Laws

**Cohesion:** 0.06 - loosely connected
**Members:** 42 nodes

## Why this community

Consumer-protection and conduct regime for finance companies, real-estate finance and banks — advertising, contracting, responsible lending, governance, outsourcing and beneficial-owner verification obligations, sitting under the core enabling laws.

## How members connect

- Foundational laws (SAMA Central Bank Law M/36, Finance Companies Control Law M/51, Real Estate Finance Law, Banking Control Law M/5) are cited as the legal basis for the operational instructions and rules above them.
- Conduct instruments (Responsible Retail Financing, Advertising Controls, Consumer Protection Principles, Customer Signature on All Contract Pages, disability-services, SAMA-logo prohibition) reference Financial Institution Customer Protection as their shared obligation set.
- Beneficial-owner verification instruments (Waathiq integration, nonprofit inquiry service) cross-reference the AML Law and BO Verification — a cross-regime link between consumer-facing onboarding and AML/CTF KYB duties.
- Real-estate refinance rules cite both the Real Estate Finance Law and Finance Companies Control Law and pull in Outsourcing Rules and Main Governance Principles, showing the layered law -> regulation -> instruction hierarchy; scope carve-outs (optional compliance for SME/large-enterprise dealings) limit where consumer protections apply.
