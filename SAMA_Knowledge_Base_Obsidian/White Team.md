# White Team

## Connections

### [[Financial Entities Ethical Red Teaming Framework (F.E.E.R.)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing who bears responsibility for a controlled red teaming exercise under F.E.E.R., treat the White Team as the Member Organization's mandated internal owner, not the framework's SAMA-side actors. The Framework requires each Member Organization to establish and formalize a White Team (including a White Team Leader) to facilitate, oversee and control the exercise across all phases, and it is the White Team that procures the Red Teaming Provider under the Appendix A requirements. For a compliance decision, this means accountability for controlled execution, contracting (NDA, LOA, liability) and scope stays with the tested institution — the White Team is where you look to confirm the exercise was run in a controlled manner.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 11 / Section 3.4):** "The Member Organization should carefully establish a White Team and nominate a White Team Leader in order to facilitate, oversee and lead the red teaming exercises during all phases"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / Section 1.1):** "F.E.E.R. is intended as a guide for Member Organizations ... in preparing and executing controlled attacks ... with the help of certified and experienced Red Teaming Providers"

### [[Remediation Plan (RP)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying who owns post-exercise follow-up, the Remediation Plan is a White Team deliverable, not a vendor product — the White Team must draft it based on the Red Teaming Evaluation Report and the Blue Team Report, and monitor and report its progress to the institution's Cyber Security Committee and the Green Team. This anchors accountability for closing identified gaps (prevention, detection, response, recover) inside the Member Organization. For a compliance decision, check that the White Team both produced the RP and maintained periodic status reporting to the Cyber Security Committee and SAMA's Green Team, since remediation follow-through — not just the test — is what the Framework expects.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 18 / Section 6.4):** "The White Team should create a remediation plan based on the recommendations provided by the Red Team and Blue Team"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 24 / Remediation Plan (RP)):** "The White Team should draft a Remediation Plan ... status and progress ... is monitored and periodically reported to the Cyber Security Committee ... as well as the Green Team"
