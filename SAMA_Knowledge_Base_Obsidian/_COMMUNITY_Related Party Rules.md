# Related Party Rules

**Cohesion:** 0.40 - moderately connected
**Members:** 5 nodes

## Why this community

Prudential controls on bank dealings with related parties and connected exposures: definitional scope, quantitative limits, and their statutory anchor in the Banking Control Law, guarding against concentration and conflict-of-interest risk.

## How members connect

- 'Related Parties Rules for Banks' references Banking Control Law Article 9 — the Rules are the implementing/subordinate instrument giving effect to the statutory basis.
- 'Related Party Definition' scopes who falls within the regime; the definition determines which counterparties trigger the restrictions.
- 'Exposure/Transaction Limits' operationalizes both the Related Parties Rules and the SAMA Rules on Large Exposures — the same limit concept bridges related-party and general concentration regimes.
- The cross-reference between the two exposure rulebooks signals overlapping quantitative ceilings; related-party exposures must be assessed against both frameworks.
