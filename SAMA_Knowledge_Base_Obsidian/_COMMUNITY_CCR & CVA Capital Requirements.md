# CCR & CVA Capital Requirements

**Cohesion:** 0.14 - loosely connected
**Members:** 16 nodes

## Why this community

Minimum capital for counterparty credit risk (CCR) and credit valuation adjustment (CVA): the two-track (internal-model IMM and standardised SA-CVA) regime SAMA requires, including validation and disclosure templates.

## How members connect

- Minimum Capital Requirements for CCR and CVA is the governing provision referencing both the IMM and the CVA Framework as permitted calculation routes.
- IMM eligibility is conditioned on Netting Set/Cross-Product Netting rules, Wrong-Way Risk, Stress Testing and Backtesting/Model Validation — obligations a firm must meet, subject to SAMA approval.
- CVA Framework references SA-CVA, which drives Regulatory CVA Calculation via Delta/Vega sensitivities; SFT collateral haircut floors and disclosure templates (CCR5, CVA3) map the numbers to reporting.
- SAMA is referenced as the approving/supervising authority for IMM and SA-CVA use.
