# Regulations for Consumer Financing

## Connections

### [[Annex 1 APR Calculation|Annex 1: APR Calculation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When checking disclosure and pricing compliance under the Consumer Financing Regulations, do not treat the APR as a free-standing figure — the Regulations mandate disclosure of the Annual Percentage Rate but define its value strictly by reference to Annex 1. Annex 1 fixes the calculation mechanics (12 equal-month year, inclusion of all unavoidable costs and fees but exclusion of non-compliance charges, assumption the contract runs to term). You would conclude that any APR shown to a Borrower or in advertising must be computed on Annex 1's assumptions, and check that excluded default charges and variable-rate assumptions are handled exactly as the Annex prescribes.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 (Definitions)):** "Annual Percentage Rate or APR: the discount rate at which the present value of all payments ... calculated in accordance with Annex 1"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 35 (Annex 1)):** "the Total Amount Payable by the Borrower shall be determined, including all unavoidable costs and fees with the exception of charges ... as a result of non-compliance"

### [[Banking Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]

This also fixes scope — the Regulations bind Financing Contracts executed by banks licensed and authorized by SAMA. You would conclude that a breach of these Regulations exposes a bank to Banking Control Law sanctions, and check that the entity is a SAMA-licensed bank (rather than a finance company under a different law) before treating this regime as controlling.

### [[Dispute Resolution Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on a Borrower complaint under a consumer financing account, use Section Seven (Article 23) as the controlling procedure embedded within these Regulations, not a separate regime. It imposes concrete duties on the Creditor: investigate and resolve within 60 (max 90) Calendar Days, suspend collection of the disputed amount and its Term Cost/charges until Satisfactorily Resolved, and refrain from adverse credit reporting during that window. You would conclude that a Creditor cannot pursue or report a disputed balance prematurely, and would check the 30-day notice window, the 60/90-day resolution timeline, and that any unresolved dispute is escalated to the Committee for Settlement of Banking Disputes as the final authority.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 3 / Page 33):** "The Committee for Settlement of Banking Disputes is the final authority in resolving any unresolved disputes between the Borrower and the Creditor"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 31, Art 23):** "The Creditor may not try to collect any amount, Term Cost or other charges related to the account statement error/dispute until the dispute is Satisfactorily Resolved"
