# SAMA Rules on Large Exposures of Banks

## Connections

### [[ExposureTransaction Limits|Exposure/Transaction Limits]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When measuring related-party exposure, do not compute it in isolation from the Large Exposures regime — the Related Parties Rules incorporate the Large Exposures measurement methodology and its group limits by reference. Section 5.1 states exposures 'are subject to measurement requirements as prescribed in SAMA Rules on Large Exposures of Banks,' and where a related party sits within a Group of Connected Counterparties the related-party limit applies 'in addition to' the overall group exposure limit. For a compliance decision this means two limits can bind simultaneously; check both the related-party cap and the Large Exposures group cap, and use the Large Exposures methodology to value the exposure.
