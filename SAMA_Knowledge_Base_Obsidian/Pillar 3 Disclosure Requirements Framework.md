# Pillar 3 Disclosure Requirements Framework

## Connections

### [[Basel III Post-Crisis Reforms]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the substantive content behind SAMA's Pillar 3 disclosure obligations, read them as SAMA's local adoption of the Basel III post-crisis reforms, not as free-standing KSA rules. The Framework itself states it derives from the BCBS December 2017 'Finalizing post-crisis reforms' document, and its templates reference SAMA-issued Basel III components (SCRE, SCCR, SMAR, SOPE, SLEV) as the source of the RWA and capital figures disclosed. Conclude that to interpret a disclosure requirement you must trace it to the corresponding SAMA Basel III capital standard; the disclosure numbers are outputs of those calculation frameworks, and the Framework supersedes prior SAMA Pillar 3 instructions.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Introduction):** "Basel Committee on Banking Supervision issued a document on Basel III: Finalizing post-crisis reforms in December 2017. Which includes the revised disclosure requirements"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Glossary):** "SCRE ... issued by SAMA as part of its adoption of Basel III post-crisis final reforms."

### [[Pillar 3 Disclosure]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing what a bank must publicly disclose, note that this Framework document is the enforceable instrument that defines and operationalizes the Pillar 3 disclosure concept for KSA. It sets the guiding principles (meaningful, consistent, comparable), board-approval and assurance duties, signposting conditions, and frequency/timing rules that give the concept its concrete obligations — including that the Pillar 3 report be published concurrently with the financial report. Conclude that the abstract 'Pillar 3 disclosure' duty is only definable by reference to this Framework's specific templates and rules; check applicability (domestic banks consolidated and standalone; not foreign bank branches) before relying on any exemption.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Sec 3 Implementation dates):** "Disclosure requirements will be effective on 01 January 2023."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 6 / Principle 3):** "Disclosures should highlight a bank's most significant current and emerging risks and how those risks are managed"

### [[SOPE - Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying which capital standard underpins a bank's operational-risk disclosure, note that the Pillar 3 Framework relies on SOPE — SAMA's Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk issued as part of its Basel III adoption — as the source of the operational-risk RWA and capital figures reported. The Framework expressly lists operational risk among the revised Pillar 3 disclosure areas and defines SOPE in its glossary as the governing calculation standard. Conclude that operational-risk lines in the disclosure templates must be computed under SOPE, not disclosed independently; verify the SOPE methodology before treating a template figure as compliant.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Introduction (a)):** "Credit risk, operational risk, the leverage ratio and credit valuation adjustment (CVA) risk"
