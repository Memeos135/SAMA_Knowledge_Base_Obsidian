# Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks

## Connections

### [[Additional Licensing Guidelines for Digital-Only Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing governance obligations for a digital-only bank, apply the Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks in full — the digital-only guidelines create no lighter regime. Document 1897 states digital-only banks 'are required to follow the same SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia as with conventional banks,' the same instrument the general licensing criteria invoke for internal audit adequacy. The practical conclusion: a digital-only applicant's governance and internal audit arrangements are measured against the identical corporate governance standard as a conventional bank, so a reviewer should not accept a reduced governance framework on grounds of a purely digital operating model.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7, para 26):** "internal audit arrangements and requirements as set out in SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia."

### [[Licensing Guidelines and Minimum Criteria]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a license applicant on internal-control expectations, do not treat the Licensing Guidelines' audit criterion as self-contained — it cross-refers to a separate governance instrument that supplies the substantive standard. The Guidelines require applicants to satisfy SAMA on internal audit arrangements 'as set out in SAMA's Principles of Corporate Governance for Banks Operating in Saudi Arabia,' making that document the operative source for the audit adequacy test. For a compliance decision you would consult the Corporate Governance Principles for the actual internal-audit requirements rather than relying on the Guidelines' brief reference.
