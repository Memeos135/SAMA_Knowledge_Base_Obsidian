# Independence and Objectivity of Audit Unit

## Connections

### [[Internal Audit Principles for Local Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a local bank's internal audit function against SAMA's Internal Audit Principles, treat independence and objectivity as a mandatory core attribute of the audit unit, not an aspirational value. The Principles (issued under the Central Bank Law and the Banking Control Law) list 'Independence and Objectivity' under Principle 4 on the unit's key characteristics, and the covering letter frames the whole regime as ensuring the unit performs an 'independent and objective assessment' of governance, risk management and controls. Conclude that when reviewing audit-unit arrangements you should verify this attribute is embedded in structure and reporting lines, since it is a substantive requirement of the framework rather than a general principle.

### [[Three Lines of Defence Model]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a bank's internal audit function satisfies SAMA's Internal Audit Principles, treat independence/objectivity and the three-lines model as a single obligation set, not separate ideas: the Principles position the audit unit as the third line of defence — distinct from and reviewing the first and second lines — precisely so that its assurance is independent and objective. Because the same instrument defines the unit's independence as flowing from its third-line role (a supervisory tool for the board/management, 'not a substitute for it'), a compliance reviewer should verify that reporting lines, mandate and staffing keep audit structurally separated from operational and risk/control (second-line) functions. Conclude that a firm cannot claim compliance with the independence principle while blurring audit into second-line control activities.
