# Mortgage & Finance Licensing

**Cohesion:** 0.08 - loosely connected
**Members:** 29 nodes

## Why this community

KSA licensing and contractual regime for finance/refinance and real-estate mortgage lending — the statutory laws, licensing rules, and mandated contract/notice templates governing finance companies and mortgage registration.

## How members connect

- Hierarchy runs from primary laws (Finance Companies Control Law, Real Estate Finance Law, Finance Lease Law, Registered Real Estate Mortgage Law) down to licensing guidelines/rules and prescribed contract templates.
- Licensing guidelines reference common gatekeeping instruments (Fit and Proper Form, LEI/GLEIF), tying market entry to fitness and legal-entity identification requirements.
- Murabaha/Ijarah contract templates operationalise the finance laws and mortgage registration into borrower-facing terms — pricing (SAIBOR index, APR), default/delay notices, late-payment and termination provisions.
- Consumer-protection and credit-reporting touchpoints (notice articles, Credit Information Company) attach obligations at the borrower-relationship level within the finance-company regime.
