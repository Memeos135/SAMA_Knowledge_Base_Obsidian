# Early Warning Signals (EWS)

## Connections

### [[Monitoring the Restructuring Plan]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating whether a bank's NPL lifecycle is coherent, connect early identification (EWS) to the restructuring/monitoring stage: the Guidelines state that effective early identification and early restructuring can prevent long-term default and losses. This link tells you the two functions are ends of one continuum — EWS triggers should ultimately support timely restructuring where a borrower is viable and cooperative. Verify that early signals actually drive escalation to restructuring options rather than allowing accounts to drift, since delay is expressly said to lower recovery.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / 2.1):** "To allow the opportunity to develop a corrective action plan at a very early stage"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 83 / Appendix 3 (iii)):** "If banks are effective in identifying early warning signs ... engaging in early restructuring solutions, this could prevent long-term default and losses"
- **Caveat:** The two nodes are thematically joined within the same Guidelines rather than by a direct article-level cross-reference; confirm the intended escalation path in the primary text.

### [[Watch List]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's problem-loan controls under these Guidelines, treat the watch list as the operational output of the EWS process, not a standalone register: the EWS unit interprets signals and decides which borrowers go onto the watch list for corrective action. The link tells you that a compliant EWS framework must feed a properly maintained watch list, with risk-management approval to remove loans and defined maximum tenure before escalation. Check that inclusion criteria, minimum data fields, and monthly reporting to a management committee/Board are documented, since SAMA treats an effective EWS framework as a 'mandatory requirement.'
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / 2.3 Identification):** "determine whether that borrower should be included in the watch list (prepared on the basis of EWS) for further corrective action"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Action & Monitoring):** "A loan is added to the watch list prepared on the basis of EWS ... Risk management approval required to remove the loan from watch list"
