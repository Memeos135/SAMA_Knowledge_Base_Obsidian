# Aggregation Business Continuity

**Cohesion:** 0.06 - loosely connected
**Members:** 47 nodes

## Why this community

Operational resilience regime for data-aggregation providers: business continuity and cyber-security obligations that SAMA imposes on regulated entities, anchored in the Instructions for Practicing Aggregation Activity and the BCM/Cyber frameworks they incorporate.

## How members connect

- The Aggregation Instructions cite the SAMA BCM Framework and Cyber Security Framework, making those framework controls (BCP, DRP, BIA/risk assessment, crisis management, cyber resilience) enforceable requirements on the Aggregation Entity.
- Hierarchy runs from SAMA-owned frameworks (BCM, CSF) down to obligations 'implemented by' each Member Organization; CRFR further decomposes cyber duties into governance, operations/technology and resilience domains.
- Defined-actor chain: Aggregation Entity is the obligated party serving the Consumer and interfacing with Financier via the Aggregation Platform; SAMA is supervisor and framework owner.
- External standards (ISO 22301, NISTIR 7298, PCI-DSS, SWIFT CSCF) are cited as benchmarks the SAMA frameworks map to, not as independent obligations.
