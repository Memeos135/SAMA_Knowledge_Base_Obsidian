# New Banking Products and Services Regulation

## Connections

### [[Notification and Non-Objection Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a bank must do before launching a new product, read the Notification and Non-Objection provisions (Section 7) as the operative gate within the New Products Regulation, and note they are not interchangeable. Notification (7.1) is the default and is only available to banks that satisfy the required maturity level in the Cyber Security, Counter-Fraud, Business Continuity and IT Governance Frameworks; non-objection (7.2) is the exception required for specified products (home loans, financial leases, derivatives, and products not covered by existing SAMA rules) and is mandatory for banks that do not meet that maturity level. Conclude that you must first classify the product and confirm the bank's framework-maturity status to know whether mere notification suffices or prior SAMA non-objection is legally required before launch.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Sec 2):** "regulatory requirements of notifying SAMA prior offering a new product or service, and the required supporting documents to be submitted"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12 / 7.2.1):** "Banks are required to seek SAMA's non-objection for the below products and services prior launching, as an exception to the notification requirements"

### [[Products and Services Risk Assessment]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a bank must do before launching a new product, treat the risk-assessment requirements as an integral component of the overarching New Products Regulation, not a separate exercise. The Regulation states risks from new/modified products 'must be identified, assessed, monitored and managed appropriately by the banks,' and Section 6.3 operationalizes this by mandating a full risk assessment that 'form[s] the basis on whether or not to introduce them to the market.' A compliance reader should conclude that failing to complete and document the 6.3 risk classification and lifecycle review is itself a breach of the Regulation's core obligation, and that the risk assessment output is a prerequisite to any notification or non-objection decision.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Sec 1 Introduction):** "the risks posed by the introduction and/or modification of products and services must be identified, assessed, monitored and managed appropriately by the banks"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Sec 6.3.2):** "Banks must conduct a full risk assessment of new products and services which form the basis on whether or not to introduce them to the market"
