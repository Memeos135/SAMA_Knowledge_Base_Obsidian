# Finance Support Licensing

**Cohesion:** 0.19 - loosely connected
**Members:** 14 nodes

## Why this community

Licensing and supervision of finance-support activities (notably debt collection) and adjacent BNPL/payments and AML governance obligations, sitting under the Finance Companies Control Law framework and its finance-lease dimension.

## How members connect

- Rules for Licensing Finance Support Activities is the anchor; the Amendment and the debt-collection circulars (outsourcing, cancellation of the licensing requirement) amend/reference it, showing an evolving licensing perimeter for collection operations.
- The Implementing Regulations of the Finance Companies Control Law (with Finance Lease Law) is the parent authority cited by most members, including the promissory-note credit-card circular and the Raising BNPL Financing Ceiling circular that adjusts a BNPL product limit.
- Governance and AML linkage: Compliance with Governance Principles and the Business Risk Assessment Guide for ML/TF/PF connect the licensing regime to enterprise-wide risk-assessment obligations.
- Payments cross-regime edges: PSP licensing guidelines and the Payment Systems Oversight Framework relate conceptually via shared AML/governance expectations; note duplicate entries for the BRA Guide and Oversight Framework should be de-duplicated.
