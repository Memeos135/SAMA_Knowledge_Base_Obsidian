# Effective EPE Internal Model

## Connections

### [[Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating a bank's use of an Effective EPE internal model for counterparty exposure (EAD), note that the model rules cross-reference the credit-risk collateral and netting standards: where the EPE model recognizes collateral other than cash it must fall back to the comprehensive-approach standard supervisory haircuts, and collateral must meet the legal-certainty standards set out in chapter 9 of the Minimum Capital Requirements for Credit Risk. This ties EPE modelling approval (subject to SAMA validation, backtesting and alpha requirements at 7.54, 7.58) to the credit-risk framework's eligibility and haircut rules. Conclude that IMM/EPE recognition is not self-contained—collateral and netting effects still have to satisfy the referenced credit-risk provisions before capital benefit is claimed.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 607 / 7.54):** "The EPE model (and modifications made to it) must be subject to an internal model validation process"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 615 / 7.52):** "if it is not able to model collateral jointly with the exposure then it must use the standard supervisory haircuts of the comprehensive approach"

### [[Saudi Central Bank (SAMA)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** If a bank intends to use an internal EPE model (IMM) to calculate counterparty EAD, treat SAMA approval and ongoing supervisory review as prerequisites, not formalities — the text conditions use of alternative conservative measures on 'prior approval of SAMA' and gives SAMA power to require corrections or additional capital when backtesting shows model underperformance. This link establishes SAMA as the gatekeeper and enforcer over the EPE modelling regime. Conclude that reliance on internal EPE outputs is contingent on SAMA's initial approval and continued acceptance, and that supervisory findings can force model fixes or capital add-ons.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 607 / (12)):** "a measure that is more conservative than the metric used to calculate regulatory EAD ... may be used in place of alpha times Effective EPE with the prior approval of SAMA"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4):** "setting out SAMA's expectations on banks' group-wide risk oversight and monitoring practices"
