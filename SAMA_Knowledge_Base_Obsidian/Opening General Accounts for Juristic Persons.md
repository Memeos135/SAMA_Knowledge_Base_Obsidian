# Opening General Accounts for Juristic Persons

## Connections

### [[Appendix B Liquidity Statement|Appendix B: Liquidity Statement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping a DTFC's account-opening duties against its prudential reporting duties, note these are distinct obligation streams that share the same regulated deposit base. The juristic-person account rules (Chapters 8-9) govern who a DTFC may take deposits from and the KYB documentation required, while Appendix B is the monthly liquidity return submitted under Chapter 5 that measures liquid assets against the resulting deposit liabilities. The deposits generated under the account-opening chapters are the very liabilities that feed the 20% liquid-asset and 15x-capital tests in the liquidity regime, so a compliance reader should treat the account book and the liquidity return as connected: onboarding decisions directly affect the reportable deposit-liability figures.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 36 / Chapter 8 / Art 83):** "DTFCs can only open General Accounts and Term Deposit Accounts for juristic Persons in accordance with the rules stipulated in the Section 3 of the regulations."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 10 / Chapter 5 / Art 32):** "Every DTFC shall prepare and submit to SAMA at the end of every month... liquidity information to SAMA as set out in Appendix B to these Regulations."
- **Caveat:** Link is a functional/regime connection between onboarding and liquidity reporting, not an explicit cross-reference; verify how deposit liabilities are aggregated for Appendix B.

### [[General Account]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising on onboarding a juristic customer, treat the General Account definition as the object that the account-opening rules govern: the juristic-person documentation requirements (Chapters 8-9) determine when and for whom a General Account may lawfully be opened. The General Account is defined narrowly as an internal-books account whose sole purpose is to facilitate Term Deposit operations, and the account-opening chapter expressly prohibits opening it for natural persons and permits it only for juristic persons meeting the KYB documentation. A compliance reader should conclude that eligibility and documentation for a General Account are controlled entirely by these account-opening rules, and that funding/withdrawal mechanics come from the separate operating rules.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 36 / Chapter 8 / Arts 82-83):** "DTFCs must NOT open General Accounts and Term Deposit Accounts in the name of Natural Persons... DTFCs can only open General Accounts... for juristic Persons"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 55 / Art 195):** "the sole purpose of the General Account is to facilitate operations of Term Deposit Accounts."
