# Term Deposit Account Rules

## Connections

### [[Accounts Operating Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a Deposit-Taking Finance Company (DTFC) on account operations, treat the General Account and Term Deposit Account rules as one integrated regime rather than separate products, because the operating rules make the General Account exist solely to service Term Deposits and hard-wire funding flows between them. Under the linkage rules a Term Deposit may only be credited from the General Account to which it was tied at initiation, must share that account's name, cannot be de-linked/re-linked, and on maturity principal and profit route back to the originating General Account. For compliance decisions, conclude that you cannot assess Term Deposit obligations (funding source matching, disclosure/blocking on SAMA request, freezing on ID expiry) in isolation — the linked General Account inherits the same customer identity and is captured in any SAMA disclosure or blocking search.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46 / Para 120):** "DTFC must linked Term Deposit Account to a General Account upon its initiation. (Term Deposit Account will only receive credits from the same General Account to which it is linked...)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 55 / Para 195):** "As stated in Section 1.1, the sole purpose of the General Account is to facilitate operations of Term Deposit Accounts."

### [[DTFC General Account Opening Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When structuring a DTFC's deposit products, treat the General Account and Term Deposit Account as strictly dependent, not free-standing: a Term Deposit Account can only be opened where the same customer already holds an active General Account, and it may only be credited from — and repaid to — the General Account to which it was linked at initiation. The opening rules state the General Account's sole purpose is to facilitate Term Deposit operations, and Chapter 10 forbids de-linking and re-linking a Term Deposit Account to a different General Account. You should conclude that funds cannot circulate between unrelated customers or accounts through this pairing, and that the linkage fixed at initiation is legally binding and non-transferable.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 46 / Chapter 10, para 119-122):** "A General Deposit Account of the same customer is already open and active... DTFCs must not de-link the Term Deposit Account with the General Account to which it was linked."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 55 / para 195, 200):** "the sole purpose of the General Account is to facilitate operations of Term Deposit Accounts... DTFC must credit the principle and profit amount separately to the General Account from which Term Deposit Account was funded."
