# BNPL Licensing Provisions

## Connections

### [[Rules for Regulating BNPL Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether an entity may lawfully conduct BNPL activity, rely on this link: the Rules make licensing a hard precondition (no BNPL activity without a SAMA license) and set out the full licensing chain — application, verification visits, five-year term, renewal, revocation, and fees in Articles 3, 7-12. The licensing provisions are subordinate to the Finance Companies Control Law, and the license is activity-specific (no other activity without prior SAMA non-objection). You would conclude that unlicensed BNPL activity is prohibited outright, and that scope creep beyond the licensed activity independently requires SAMA approval.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Art 9(3)):** "The BNPL company shall not engage in any activity other than the activity for which the license has been issued unless it obtains a prior written non-objection from SAMA."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 3 / Art 3(2)):** "BNPL activity shall not be carried out except after obtaining a license from SAMA in accordance with the provisions of the Law"
