# Microfinance Licensing

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 3 nodes

## Why this community

Licensing gateway for consumer microfinance under the finance-companies regime — entry requirements and control-person suitability.

## How members connect

- 'License Application Guidelines for Consumer Microfinance' is subordinate implementing guidance operationalizing the 'Finance Companies Control Law' (primary law → guidance hierarchy).
- The Guidelines reference the 'Fit and Proper Form', making director/senior-manager suitability a mandatory precondition of licensing.
- Together they define who may apply, what suitability must be evidenced, and the statutory basis for refusal/approval.
