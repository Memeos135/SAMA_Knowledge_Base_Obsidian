# Companies Law

## Connections

### [[Bank Accounts for Liquidation and Financial Restructuring]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a company is being wound up outside insolvency, the Companies Law provides the alternative legal basis for a liquidation account — the rules expressly permit the account 'for liquidation under the Companies Law' and tie it to the termination reasons stated in Article 16 of that law, requiring either a court order or a partners'/general assembly decision plus appointment of a liquidator. This link tells you two distinct statutory tracks feed the same account category (Bankruptcy Law vs Companies Law), each with its own trigger documents. You would conclude that voluntary/corporate liquidation is validated by the corporate decision and liquidator appointment, not necessarily a court insolvency order.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 90 / 300.1.7(II)):** "Liquidation for the termination reasons stated in Article 16 of Companies Law ... In case of a voluntary liquidation by partners, the decision issued by the partners or general assembly"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 89 / 300.1.7):** "for liquidation under the Companies Law. The following conditions and documents shall be met and provided"
