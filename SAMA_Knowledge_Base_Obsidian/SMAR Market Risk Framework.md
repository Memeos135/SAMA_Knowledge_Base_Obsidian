# SMAR Market Risk Framework

## Connections

### [[Template MR1 Market risk under standardised approach|Template MR1: Market risk under standardised approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing what MR1 must contain and how figures are computed, the SMAR market-risk framework is the substantive rulebook it draws on: MR1 reports market-risk RWA under the standardised approach, whose content and measures (ES, DRC, capital requirements) are defined in SMAR (e.g. SMAR13, SMAR14). The disclosure template does not itself set the capital calculation — SMAR does — so a compliance reader should verify the underlying SMAR provisions (including which desks/positions are in scope of the standardised versus internal models approach) rather than relying on the template narrative alone to determine the correct charge.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 846 / Table MR3):** "Content: Capital requirement (as defined in SMAR14 of the market risk framework)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 755):** "Definition of standardised approach: The standardised approach for market risk ... SEC-ERBA, SEC-SA or a risk weight of 1,250% must also be used"

### [[Template MR2 Market risk IMA per risk type|Template MR2: Market risk IMA per risk type]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When completing or auditing MR2, treat the SMAR market-risk framework as the governing substantive rulebook, not just a cross-reference: every metric in MR2 (expected shortfall, non-modellable risk factor charges, DRC, amber-desk surcharges) is defined by and calculated 'in accordance with' specific SMAR provisions. The disclosure template merely presents figures that SMAR13 and SMAR10 dictate. Conclude that any question about how an MR2 value should be computed must be resolved against the cited SMAR sections, and the template cannot be filled correctly without applying the underlying framework.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 845):** "Default risk capital (DRC) requirement: in accordance with SMAR13.18, measure of the default risk of trading book positions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 845):** "Expected shortfall (ES) as defined in SMAR13.1 to SMAR13.12... Capital requirement for non-modellable risk factors... calculated in accordance with SMAR13.16 and SMAR13.17"

### [[Template MR3 Market risk simplified standardised approach|Template MR3: Market risk simplified standardised approach]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When a bank uses the simplified standardised approach for market risk, MR3 is the mandatory disclosure and its capital-requirement content is defined by SMAR14 of the market-risk framework. The template presents interest rate, equity, commodity and securitisation components, but the amounts are governed by the SMAR SSA rules, so the framework determines what must be reported. Conclude that MR3 applies only to banks on the SSA and that its figures must be derived from SMAR14; a reviewer should confirm SSA eligibility and read MR3 against that provision rather than the full standardised or IMA methods.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 846):** "Market risk under the simplified standardised approach (SSA)... Capital requirement (as defined in SMAR14 of the market risk framework)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 846):** "The template is mandatory for banks that use the simplified standardised approach... Content: Capital requirement (as defined in SMAR14 of the market risk framework)."
