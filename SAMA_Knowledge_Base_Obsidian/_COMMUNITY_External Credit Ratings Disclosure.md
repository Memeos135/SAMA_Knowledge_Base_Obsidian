# External Credit Ratings Disclosure

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

Pillar 3 qualitative disclosure regime for banks' reliance on External Credit Assessment Institutions (ECAIs) under the standardised approach to credit risk. Governs transparency obligations about how external ratings map to risk weights.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: Table CRD sets the disclosure template banks must complete when using external credit ratings for regulatory capital.
- Subordinate to SAMA's capital adequacy/Pillar 3 disclosure framework; obligation is to explain the use and mapping of nominated ECAIs, not to set risk weights themselves.
