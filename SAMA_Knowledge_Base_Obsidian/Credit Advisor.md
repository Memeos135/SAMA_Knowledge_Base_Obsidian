### [[SAMA Mandatory Instructions on Retail Real Estate Finance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a bank or real estate finance company's pre-contract obligations for retail mortgages, note that this SAMA mandatory circular makes the credit advisor a hard gating requirement, not a courtesy. The instructions require the financier, sufficiently before the offer expires, to assign a qualified credit advisor to explain the nature, risks, terms and repricing mechanism of the proposed financing, and expressly prohibit that advisor from being the same employee who dealt with or presented the offer to the customer; documenting this contact is 'an essential requirement for completing the contract'. Conclude that no retail real estate finance contract may be concluded unless the credit advisor has given the explanation, answered all questions and documented it — a segregation-of-duties and disclosure precondition to a valid contract.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 2, para 4):** "the credit advisor may not be the same employee who contacted the customer before or presented the offer (English paraphrase)"
