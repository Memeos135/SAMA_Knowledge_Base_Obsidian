# SAMA LEI Issuance Instructions & SIMAH Agreement

## Connections

### [[Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping identification obligations for SAMA-supervised financial institutions, read this circular as mandating that every such institution — including its subsidiaries and branches of foreign financial institutions operating in the Kingdom — obtain and maintain a Legal Entity Identifier from a GLEIF-accredited operating unit. The LEI is the defined subject of the instruction, and the circular frames it as an ongoing obligation (the identifier must remain valid and updated throughout the relationship) and as a supporting tool for customer identification and risk assessment. A compliance reader should conclude the LEI requirement is a continuous, not one-off, duty and should confirm the referenced earlier circular (cited by number) that first imposed the LEI obligation before relying on scope.

- **Grounding — related node (Page 1 / item 1):** "أن يكون معرف الكيانات القانونية ساري[اً] ومحدثاً وذلك للمؤسسات المالية الخاضعة لإشراف البنك المركزي"

### [[SIMAH (Saudi Credit Bureau)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying who a supervised institution must contract with to issue LEIs, this circular designates SIMAH (the Saudi Credit Information Company) as the accredited local operating unit and authorises financial institutions to sign the annexed 'issuance, updating and renewal of LEI through a financial institution' agreement with SIMAH. The agreement itself confirms SIMAH is GLEIF-accredited as a local operating unit and is SAMA-licensed and supervised. A compliance reader should conclude that the SIMAH agreement is the sanctioned channel for LEI issuance on behalf of business-sector customers, and should review the annexed agreement's cost, data-protection and Credit Information Law clauses before signing.
