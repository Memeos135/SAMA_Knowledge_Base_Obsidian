# Know Your Customer (KYC) Principle

## Connections

### [[Customer Due Diligence (CDD)]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When applying the Guide's onboarding rules, do not treat KYC and CDD as interchangeable: the Guide appears to present KYC as the identification-and-knowledge principle (knowing each customer's true identity, expected activity and risk) and CDD as the applied procedures by which the exchange house monitors financial dealings and verifies customers and beneficial owners on an ongoing basis. The two are sequential/complementary — KYC sets the objective, CDD is the enforceable procedural layer that delivers it — and the Guide's transaction-tiering (e.g. currency-exchange thresholds for citizens and foreigners) expressly triggers both together. Conclude that satisfying identity capture alone is not enough; you should confirm that risk assessment, beneficial-owner verification and ongoing monitoring (the CDD elements) are also performed, and verify the precise KYC/CDD wording against clean primary text before relying on the distinction.

### [[Exchange Houses Operating Procedures Guide]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping onboarding duties for an exchange house, read the Guide's KYC section as an enforceable requirement, not guidance, because the Guide is SAMA's operating instrument supplementing the AML Law/Bylaws and it mandates ongoing identification and verification of all customers and beneficial owners. The Guide states the purpose of KYC is to enable the exchange house to know each customer's true identity and expected activity, and lists concrete measures (verify permanent and casual customers, assess risk by customer type, keep identification records available to the AML compliance officer). Conclude that KYC applies to occasional/transient customers (Hajj/Umrah visitors, GCC and non-GCC nationals) as well as membership customers, and check that identification and beneficial-owner verification is performed and updated continuously — the Guide does not permit onboarding without it.
