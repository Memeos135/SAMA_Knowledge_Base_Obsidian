# Financial Institution Customer Protection

## Connections

### [[Advertising Controls for Financial Products and Services]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing advertising obligations, treat customer protection as the governing purpose that drives every requirement in these Advertising Controls, not a background aspiration. The Controls are issued expressly under SAMA's Article 4 mandate to 'put in place instructions and procedures to protect the customers of financial institutions,' and their stated objective is protecting customers and reinforcing disclosure and transparency. You should therefore interpret ambiguous advertising provisions in favour of customer/consumer protection and disclosure, since that is the enabling authority and stated aim of the instrument.

### [[Digital Brokerage Activity Instructions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping conduct obligations of a licensed digital-brokerage support entity, note it is not exempt from SAMA's customer-protection regime: the Digital Brokerage Instructions expressly state they do not derogate from related instructions, and list SAMA's Principles and Rules for Protecting Financial Institutions' Customers among the texts to be read alongside them. Both flow from SAMA's statutory mandate to issue instructions protecting customers. For a compliance decision you would apply the customer-protection principles cumulatively to the brokerage's dealings with its 'client' (العميل), in addition to the sector-specific technical, disclosure and conflict-of-interest duties in the Instructions.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Art 4):** "وضع التعليمات والإجراءات الكفيلة بحماية عملاء المؤسسات المالية"

### [[Responsible Retail Financing Principles]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a finance company's or bank's obligations for retail lending, do not treat responsible-financing conduct and advertising/consumer-protection controls as one regime — but recognize they share the same statutory anchor (SAMA's Article 4 powers to protect financial-institution customers) and overlap in scope. Both instruments target protection of the natural-person 'customer/consumer' and both draw on the Banking Control Law and Finance Companies Control Law hierarchy. For a compliance decision you would check the Responsible Retail Financing Principles for affordability/lending conduct duties and the advertising controls for how those products may be marketed, applying both cumulatively rather than assuming one displaces the other.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 4 / Art 4):** "وضع التعليمات والإجراءات الكفيلة بحماية عملاء المؤسسات المالية"

- **Caveat:** The link is thematic (shared customer-protection basis) rather than an express cross-citation between the two texts; confirm any direct reference in the primary documents.
