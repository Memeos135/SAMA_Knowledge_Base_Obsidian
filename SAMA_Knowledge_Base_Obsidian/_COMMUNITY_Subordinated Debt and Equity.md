# Subordinated Debt and Equity

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

Regulatory capital treatment of a bank's holdings of subordinated debt, equity, and other capital instruments — both as investments/exposures and for capital-structure eligibility.

## How members connect

- Single-node community: defines risk-weighting or deduction treatment for holdings of these instruments.
- Interacts with the equity-exposure phase-in regime, which governs transitional risk weights for equity holdings.
- Decision relevance: affects whether holdings are deducted from capital or risk-weighted, a determinant of both capital quality and adequacy.
