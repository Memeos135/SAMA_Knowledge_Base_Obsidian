# Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria (AC)

## Connections

### [[Fit and Proper Forms Reference]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the specific deliverables that make up the Stage 2 gate, note that Fit and Proper forms and approvals for management are the first enumerated component of the Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria, alongside shareholders' approval and the financial model. This tells you the AC is not a general readiness declaration but a defined itemised set, so governance/fit-and-proper clearance for management is a hard precondition to obtaining the No Objection Letter. Conclude that management fitness cannot be deferred to post-go-live and must be evidenced within Stage 2.

### [[Regulatory Sandbox]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing what a FinTech must actually satisfy before it can onboard customers, note that the Operational Readiness Assessment Criteria (AC) is the gating requirement within the Regulatory Sandbox — it is the Stage 2 checkpoint between initial approval and go-live. The AC is expressly a list of minimum compliance requirements that must be met before a No Objection Letter is issued for Stage 3 live operations. Conclude that the Sandbox initial approval letter does not permit commencement of operations, and that only demonstrated AC compliance, using the latest AC version sourced from SAMA, unlocks live testing and customer onboarding.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 11):** "The AC requirements are a list of minimum compliance requirements that FinTechs must meet prior to being permitted to go live with operations and onboarding their clients/customers."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 12):** "Once the AC requirements have been met, the Regulatory Sandbox will issue a No Objection Letter for the Fintech to commence operations, which is Regulatory Sandbox Stage 3."
