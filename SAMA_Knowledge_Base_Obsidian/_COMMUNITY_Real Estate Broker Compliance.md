## Why this community

AML/CTF designation of licensed real estate brokers as a Designated Non-Financial Business or Profession (DNFBP), subjecting them to customer due diligence and reporting duties despite being outside the core financial sector.

## How members connect

- Single-member cluster: real estate brokerage compliance treated as a distinct AML/CTF obligation set applicable to a licensed non-financial profession.
- Legal consequence: obligations mirror CDD/KYC and suspicious-transaction reporting duties, but scoping is limited to brokerage activity thresholds — verify applicability before assuming full financial-institution obligations.
