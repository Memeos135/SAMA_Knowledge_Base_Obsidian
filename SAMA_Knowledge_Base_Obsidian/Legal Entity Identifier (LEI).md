# Legal Entity Identifier (LEI)

## Connections

### [[SAMA LEI Issuance Instructions & SIMAH Agreement]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping identification obligations for SAMA-supervised financial institutions, read this circular as mandating that every such institution — including its subsidiaries and branches of foreign financial institutions operating in the Kingdom — obtain and maintain a Legal Entity Identifier from a GLEIF-accredited operating unit. The LEI is the defined subject of the instruction, and the circular frames it as an ongoing obligation (the identifier must remain valid and updated throughout the relationship) and as a supporting tool for customer identification and risk assessment. A compliance reader should conclude the LEI requirement is a continuous, not one-off, duty and should confirm the referenced earlier circular (cited by number) that first imposed the LEI obligation before relying on scope.

### [[SIMAH (Saudi Credit Bureau)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining how a supervised institution actually obtains an LEI for its business customers, note that the LEI regime and SIMAH are operationally linked: SIMAH acts as the GLEIF-accredited local operating unit authorised to register, update and renew LEIs for legal entities under the ROC framework and the GLEIF–LOU master agreement. This tells a compliance reader that LEI issuance in the Kingdom flows through SIMAH as the designated registrar, and that fees (per the agreement's cost schedule) and data-protection duties under the Credit Information Law and its Implementing Regulation attach to that channel. Verify SIMAH's continued GLEIF accreditation and the current fee schedule in the annexed agreement before relying on this route.
