# Account Opening Rules for Juristic Persons

## Connections

### [[Deposit Taking Finance Companies (DTFCs) Regulations 2020]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping account-onboarding obligations for a DTFC, treat the Account Opening Rules for Juristic Persons as a binding sub-regime of the parent DTFC Regulations rather than a standalone practice — the document expressly prohibits opening General or Term Deposit Accounts for natural persons and confines them to juristic persons under Section 3, so a DTFC cannot accept individual depositors. Note the AML/CTF overlap embedded here: the account-opening acknowledgments require the holder to confirm legitimacy of funds and beneficial ownership, and the DTFC's freeze rights on suspected fraud. Conclude that any DTFC deposit onboarding must document board members, senior managers, authorised persons and beneficial-owner verification, and that remote onboarding for new customers is not permitted (in-person verification of originals/signatures is required).
- **Grounding — this node (Page 36 / paras 82-83):** "DTFCs must NOT open General Accounts and Term Deposit Accounts in the name of Natural Persons... only ... for juristic Persons in accordance with the rules stipulated in the Section 3"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 4 / paras 4-5):** "DTFCs are authorized to mobilize savings and time deposits from non-individual customers... maintain one or more records of specified particulars in the case of every depositor"

### [[Know Your Customer (KYC)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a DTFC on onboarding, do not treat the account-opening documentation rules and the KYC standards as separable checklists — they operate as one gate to opening any account. Chapter 8 fixes the minimum identity data and beneficial-owner acknowledgement required (including that the account holder is the real beneficiary), while Chapter 13 makes clear identity establishment, customer-acceptance policy and purpose/BO verification are mandatory KYC preconditions; if KYC suspicions arise on accuracy or sufficiency, the DTFC must refuse to open, refuse the relationship, refuse transactions, and report to SAMA's FIU. You should conclude that satisfying the account-opening document list alone is insufficient — the opening decision must also clear the KYC acceptance and BO-verification standard, and remote opening for new customers remains prohibited.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 37 / para 85(viii)):** "Acknowledges and verify that Account holder is the real beneficiary of the account."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 50 / Chapter 13, para 157):** "the DTFCs shall not allow to open the account, start the relationship or execute any transactions. DTFCs must information Financial Investigation Unit at SAMA about the suspicions."
