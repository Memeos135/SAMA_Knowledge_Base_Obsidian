# Risk Appetite

## Connections

### [[Risk]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing whether a Member Organization's cyber security governance meets the SAMA Cyber Security Framework, read 'Risk' and 'Risk Appetite' as linked control anchors rather than free-standing glossary terms: the framework requires that the cyber security risk management process (identify/analyze/respond/monitor) be run, and separately that risk appetite and risk tolerance be 'clearly defined and formally approved.' The link is inferred from shared placement in section 3.2 and the glossary, so treat it as a lead. You would conclude that risk-management activity is only compliant if the organization can also point to a board/senior-approved appetite statement against which those risks are measured — verify the primary text of 3.2.1 before relying on this coupling.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 20 / 3.2.1 item 11):** "The Member Organization's cyber security risk appetite and risk tolerance should be clearly defined and formally approved."

### [[Risk Tolerance]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When interpreting the framework's requirement that both 'risk appetite and risk tolerance' be defined and approved, do not conflate the two: the glossary's definition of risk tolerance ('acceptable variation relative to performance to the achievement of objectives') expressly cross-refers to 'risk appetite,' signalling they are distinct but paired concepts drawn from COSO. Because item 11 of section 3.2.1 mandates both, you would check that the organization's governance documents address appetite (the aggregate level of risk it will accept) and tolerance (permitted variation) as separate, formally-approved parameters — evidencing one does not satisfy the requirement for the other.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 20 / 3.2.1 item 11):** "cyber security risk appetite and risk tolerance should be clearly defined and formally approved."
