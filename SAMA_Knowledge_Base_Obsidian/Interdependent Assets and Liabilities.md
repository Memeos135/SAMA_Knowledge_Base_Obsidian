# Interdependent Assets and Liabilities

## Connections

### [[Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When calculating NSFR for asset/liability pairs treated as interdependent, do not assume the standard RSF factors apply automatically: the NSFR framework carves out a distinct treatment where SAMA, in consultation with banks, sets the applicable Required Stable Funding factor. This is a bank-specific, negotiated determination rather than a self-applied table value, so conclude that any interdependent-treatment position needs prior SAMA agreement before being relied on in NSFR reporting. Verify against the primary text that the specific asset/liability pair actually falls within this section before departing from standard RSF factors.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 15 / Section 8):** "Interdependent assets and liabilities... SAMA in consultation with Banks through multilateral and bilateral meetings will provide the necessary Required Stable Funding factor."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Section 4):** "The NSFR is defined as the amount of available stable funding relative to the amount of required stable funding."
