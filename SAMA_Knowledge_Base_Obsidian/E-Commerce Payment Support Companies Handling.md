# E-Commerce Payment Support Companies Handling

## Connections

### [[Protection and Safeguarding of Protected Funds]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** These two circulars appear to connect because both are issued under the Payments and Payment Services Law and its Implementing Regulations and both distinguish who does licensed payment work from who does not. The e-commerce support circular reserves merchant onboarding, KYB/CDD and financial settlement to licensed PSPs (excluding technical support providers), while the protected-funds circular imposes safeguarding, segregation and reconciliation duties squarely on PSPs handling protected funds under Article 93 of the Implementing Regulations. The link is inferential; verify the primary Regulation before relying on it, but for scoping you would conclude that safeguarding-of-funds obligations attach only to the licensed PSP, not to a technical link/support company that neither settles funds nor performs CDD.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1-2):** "إنهاء إجراءات التسوية المالية وإيداع الأموال في الحساب البنكي للتاجر (excluded from support providers)"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1 / Art 93 ref):** "يؤكد البنك المركزي على جميع مقدمي خدمات المدفوعات... بشأن حماية وحفظ الأموال المحمية وفق ما نصت عليه المادة (الثالثة والتسعون)"
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED; both source texts are partly OCR-garbled. The shared basis is the Payments and Payment Services Law/Implementing Regulations — confirm Article 93 scope and the PSP/technical-provider boundary in the primary text before relying on the linkage.

### [[Standards for Systemically Important Payment Systems]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** If you are scoping the payments regime, note these two instruments sit at different layers and should not be conflated. The Standards for Systemically Important Payment Systems classifies core infrastructures (e.g. SARIE, the Saudi Payments network/mada, SADAD) as systemically important and subjects them to SAMA oversight, whereas the e-commerce support circular addresses the opposite end — technical link/support providers who are NOT licensed payment service providers. Both appear anchored in SAMA's payments mandate, but the link is inferential; verify the primary texts before treating them as one regime, because the support circular expressly carves technical connectivity out of licensable 'related payment services' while still requiring mada operational/technical permits.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1):** "خدمات الربط والدعم التقني لعمليات مدفوعات التجارة الإلكترونية تُعد من الخدمات المساندة... لا تستدعي الحصول على ترخيص"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2):** "the Saudi Arabian Payments Network (mada): ATM services and electronic payment via POS; and SADAD as systems important to the financial system (paraphrase)"
- **Caveat:** Relation is INFERRED and cross-layer; node A Arabic is OCR-garbled (paraphrased). Confirm the mada operational/technical requirements referenced in the circular against the primary Standards text before relying on any overlap.
