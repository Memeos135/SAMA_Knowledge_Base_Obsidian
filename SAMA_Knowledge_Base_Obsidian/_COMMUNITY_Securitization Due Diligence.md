# Securitization Due Diligence

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

Prudential due-diligence obligations for banks holding or investing in securitization exposures, within the credit-risk / capital treatment regime.

## How members connect

- Single member: imposes an ongoing requirement that banks understand the risk of securitization positions before and while holding them.
- Non-compliance typically carries a capital consequence (loss of favourable risk-weight treatment), linking it to the capital adequacy framework.
- Cross-references securitization risk-weighting and disclosure rules elsewhere in the prudential rulebook.
