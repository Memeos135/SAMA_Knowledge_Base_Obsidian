# Sanctions List Management

## Connections

### [[Screening Procedures and Controls]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When operating a sanctions program under these Rules, understand that 'Screening Procedures and Controls' (Chapter Three) and 'Sanctions List Management' (Chapter Four) are sequential, interdependent obligations within the same TFS regime: the lists maintained under Chapter Four supply the reference data against which the screening required by Chapter Three is performed. Both derive their binding force from the same TFS Rules and share the defined terms Sanctions Lists and Designated Persons. For a compliance decision, do not assess screening adequacy without confirming that list-management (currency and completeness of the applicable UNSC-derived lists) is also satisfied — a defect in one chapter undermines the obligation in the other.

### [[Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what a financial institution must do with sanctions lists, treat 'Sanctions List Management' (Chapter Four) as a component obligation nested inside the overarching Targeted Financial Sanctions Rules, not a standalone regime. The TFS Rules set the mandatory minimum obligations for applying targeted financial sanctions on all SAMA-supervised financial institutions, and list management is one of the chapters through which those TFS obligations are executed. For a compliance decision this means list-management duties inherit the TFS Rules' binding scope, defined terms (Sanctions Lists, Designated Persons, Funds), and board/senior-management accountability requirements — check the parent Rules for the definitions and enforcement basis before treating list management in isolation.
