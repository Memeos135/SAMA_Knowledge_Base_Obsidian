# Remuneration Disclosure Templates

**Cohesion:** 0.50 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

Remuneration governance disclosure regime: the qualitative remuneration-policy table plus quantitative templates disclosing awards, special payments, and deferred pay.

## How members connect

- Table REMA states the remuneration policy (the qualitative/governance baseline); REM1, REM2 and REM3 each reference REMA as the policy framework their figures must reflect.
- The three REM templates disaggregate a firm's remuneration obligations — annual awards, special payments, deferred amounts — evidencing consistency with the disclosed policy.
- Cross-references run from the quantitative templates up to the policy table, establishing REMA as the interpretive anchor for the numeric disclosures.
