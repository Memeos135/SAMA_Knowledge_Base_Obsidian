# Financed Asset Receipt Certificate

## Connections

### [[Murabaha Real Estate Financing Contract]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When reviewing execution and delivery obligations under a Murabaha real-estate financing, treat the asset receipt/acceptance certificate as an integral annex of the contract, not a separate optional document. The model contract's index lists an acceptance-of-asset certificate among its numbered appendices, so the receipt certificate is one of the mandated attachments that evidences delivery/acceptance of the financed asset within the standard form the financier must use. You should conclude that the certificate's form and use are governed by the same mandatory-contract obligation, and check the appendix's exact wording in the contract text before treating any alternative acknowledgment as sufficient.
