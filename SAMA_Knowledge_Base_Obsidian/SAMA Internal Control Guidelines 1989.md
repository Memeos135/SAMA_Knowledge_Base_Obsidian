# SAMA Internal Control Guidelines 1989

## Connections

### [[SAMA Operational Risk Management Guide]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a bank's operational-risk obligations, read the Operational Risk Management Guide together with the 1989 Internal Control Guidelines rather than in isolation, because the Guide expressly designs itself to work in conjunction with them. The Guide states it 'is designed to be used in conjunction with SAMA's Internal Control Guidelines for Commercial Banks... (1989)' as 'essential for developing an integrated program of operational risk control and management,' treating strong internal controls as a primary means of minimizing operational-risk exposure. A reader should conclude that satisfying operational-risk expectations presupposes compliance with the underlying internal-control standards, and should check both instruments when scoping controls obligations.
