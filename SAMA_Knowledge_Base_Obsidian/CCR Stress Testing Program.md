### [[Wrong-way Risk]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping a bank's counterparty-credit-risk obligations, do not treat the stress-testing program and wrong-way-risk management as independent duties, because the same section (7.46-7.48) requires stress testing and scenario analyses to be designed to identify risk factors positively correlated with counterparty creditworthiness — i.e. to detect wrong-way risk. The stress-testing program is the mechanism through which the wrong-way-risk obligation is discharged. You should conclude that a bank cannot demonstrate adequate wrong-way-risk identification without a stress-testing program that specifically addresses correlated exposure and creditworthiness, and both must be reported to senior management and the board.
