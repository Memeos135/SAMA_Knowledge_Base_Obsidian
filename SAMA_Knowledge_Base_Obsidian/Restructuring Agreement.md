# Restructuring Agreement

## Connections

### [[Key Covenants]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When drafting or reviewing a restructuring agreement, treat covenants as a mandatory component rather than optional add-ons, because the guideline states all restructuring agreements should contain covenants and specifies a minimum set. Covenants function as the bank's early-warning and enforcement lever — their violation gives the bank the right to call the loan, charge fees or raise the rate — so they operationalise the monitoring purpose of the restructuring agreement. For a compliance review, confirm that any restructuring agreement includes at least the stated minimum covenants (financial statement submission, tax payment, restriction on sale of the company without prior bank approval).

### [[Restructuring Proposal Term Sheet]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When advising a bank on how a workout should be documented, treat the term sheet and the restructuring agreement as sequential stages of one process rather than interchangeable documents. Both concepts sit in the same SAMA credit-restructuring guidance (SAMA_EN_3575): the term sheet records the main terms agreed during negotiation before lawyers prepare the legal documents, while the restructuring agreement is the formalized instrument that ultimately regulates all details of the workout. For compliance purposes conclude that a signed term sheet is a negotiating/pre-contractual step and does not by itself replace or amend the original loan agreement — the binding position depends on the restructuring agreement (or amendatory loan agreement) that follows.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 85):** "The restructuring agreement is the main document that regulates all the details of the workout."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 86):** "the term-sheet allows the parties to agree on the main terms of the proposed restructuring transaction before the lawyers are asked to prepare the legal documents."

### [[Standstill Agreement]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** When mapping the sequence of workout documentation, note that the standstill agreement and the restructuring agreement appear as distinct stages of one process: the standstill (Appendix 4) is typically the first step, giving the borrower 'breathing space' while creditors formulate a joint approach, and the restructuring agreement is the later document formalising the agreed workout. For a decision this means the two are not interchangeable — a standstill freezes enforcement pending negotiation, whereas the restructuring agreement sets the binding repayment terms. The relationship is inferred from their placement in the same appendix sequence, so confirm the primary text before treating one as a precondition to the other.
