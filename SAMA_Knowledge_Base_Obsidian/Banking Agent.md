# Banking Agent

## Connections

### [[AMLCFT and KYC Requirements|AML/CFT and KYC Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping AML/CFT obligations, treat agent banking as fully in-scope: the Agent must apply KYC and comply with AML/CFT laws, and the Bank must monitor that compliance and remains responsible to SAMA for it. The Regulation requires the Agent's due-diligence process to include KYC and AML/CFT compliance, mandates contractual commitment to AML/CFT and KYC with the Bank providing SAMA all requested documents and periodic AML/CFT reports, and obliges the Bank to ensure monitoring for ML/TF, embezzlement and fraud is in place. You should conclude that customer-facing CDD performed at Agent premises does not discharge the Bank's own AML/CFT accountability, and that reporting to SAMA on Agent AML/CFT sits with the Bank.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / Art 4):** "Description of processes for customer due diligence, including KYC and compliance with AML/CFT laws and instructions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 9 / Art 6):** "Compliance with AML/CFT and KYC requirements, including providing SAMA, by the Bank, with all requested documents and periodic reports on AML/CFT"

### [[Bank (Agent Banking Principal)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When allocating liability in an agent-banking arrangement, do not treat the Agent as an independent third party absorbing its own regulatory risk — the Bank remains the responsible principal. The Regulation makes the board of each Bank responsible for compliance, holds the Bank solely responsible for selecting Agents and answerable for the Agent's acts or omissions, while retaining overall responsibility for the agency relationship. You should conclude that supervisory and enforcement exposure for Agent conduct sits with the licensed Bank, and that contractual delegation to the Agent does not shift that regulatory accountability.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 5 / Art 1):** "Exclusive Agent: an Agent that entered into an agency agreement with one Bank to exclusively provide banking services and/or products on behalf of the contracting Bank"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Art 3):** "Banks shall be solely responsible for the selection of Agents ... The Bank is responsible for all actions or omissions"

### [[Regulation of Agent Banking in KSA]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When scoping who bears AML/CFT and CDD duties in an agent-banking arrangement, treat the Agent as a defined, SAMA-approved legal entity that carries out KYC and customer due diligence at the point of service, but note the Bank retains overall responsibility for the agency relationship. The Regulation defines the Agent and requires the Agent to hold a commercial register permitting the business, obtain approval from its own supervisor, and comply with AML/CFT laws. You should conclude that outsourcing service delivery to an Agent does not transfer regulatory liability away from the Bank, and verify that the Agent's KYC/AML obligations are captured in the contract and processes.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 7 / para 3):** "The Agent shall apply for and receive necessary approval from the relevant supervisory and regulatory entity before the Bank applies for SAMA's non-objection"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 5 / Article 1 Definitions):** "Agent(s): a legal entity contracted by a licensed commercial Bank and approved by SAMA to engage in Agent Banking activity."
