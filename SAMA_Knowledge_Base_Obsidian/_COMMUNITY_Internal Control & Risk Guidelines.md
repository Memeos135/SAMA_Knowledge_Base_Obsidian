# Internal Control & Risk Guidelines

**Cohesion:** 0.50 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA's internal control, operational risk, and physical security expectations for banks — the governance/risk regime defining minimum control standards and self-assessment.

## How members connect

- The Operational Risk Management Guide is the hub, cross-referencing the older Internal Control Guidelines 1989 and Minimum Physical Security Standards 1995 as component control expectations.
- It also references the Risk Management Evaluation Questionnaire as the self-assessment instrument used to demonstrate control adequacy.
- Hierarchy is guidance-level: standards/guidelines feed into the consolidated operational-risk framework rather than a statutory obligation chain.
