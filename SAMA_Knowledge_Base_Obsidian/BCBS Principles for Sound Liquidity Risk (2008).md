# BCBS Principles for Sound Liquidity Risk (2008)

## Connections

### [[Circular on Sound Liquidity Risk Management]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining what liquidity-risk expectations bind banks in KSA, note that the binding instrument is the SAMA circular, and the BCBS 2008 paper is its source content rather than a directly enforceable standard. SAMA's circular expressly states it 'was based on a BCBS document on this subject issued in September 2008' and instructed banks to integrate those Principles into internal systems and to audit against them. Conclude that compliance is measured against the Principles as adopted through the SAMA circular (including the 2015 internal-audit and reporting requirement), and that the BCBS document informs interpretation but does not itself create the obligation.
