## Connections

### [[Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When interpreting ambiguous NSFR provisions or definitions, treat the SAMA NSFR guidance as the local adoption of the BCBS 'Basel III: The Net Stable Funding Ratio' standard of October 2014, which is its stated basis. This means the BCBS October 2014 document is the authoritative interpretive source for calibration and definitions, while SAMA retains the power to impose more stringent standards on individual banks. For a compliance decision, use the BCBS 2014 text to resolve gaps, but always defer to SAMA's document where it deviates or adds stricter requirements — do not assume the standards are identical.
