# Capital Treatment of Unsettled Transactions and Failed Trades

## Connections

### [[Delivery-versus-Payment (DvP) Transactions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining capital treatment for a settlement-risk exposure, treat the DvP provision as one component of the broader 'unsettled transactions and failed trades' chapter (Chapter 25) rather than a standalone rule. Both nodes reproduce the same numbered text (25.1–25.9), so the DvP concept sits inside the failed-trades framework that also governs non-DvP free deliveries and the five-business-day trigger. A reader should conclude that DvP capital requirements cannot be assessed in isolation from the chapter's scope limits (e.g. exclusion of CCR/CVA instruments) and the SAMA waiver power for system-wide settlement failures.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 332 / para 25.3):** "25. Capital treatment of unsettled transactions and failed trades ... Delivery-versus-payment transactions"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 339 / para 25.3):** "Banks must calculate a capital requirement for such exposures if the payments have not yet taken place five business days after the settlement date"
