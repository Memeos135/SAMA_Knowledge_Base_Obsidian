# Currency Denomination Card Templates (6th Issue)

## Connections

### [[Banknote Inspection Requirement]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **What this link tells you:** These two circulars appear linked only because both concern banknote handling by banks and money-exchange/cash centres, but they impose different duties: 4809 sets the mandatory design/colour templates for the strapping cards used to band sixth-issue currency denominations, while 4826 reinforces the duty to inspect banknotes for authenticity and report suspected counterfeits to security authorities. Treat the connection as thematic (currency operations), not as one instrument governing the other. A reviewer should verify each against its own text: strapping-card compliance and counterfeit-detection/reporting are separate obligations that happen to share the same audience of banks and cash/exchange centres.
