# Contracts Register

## Connections

### [[Implementing Regulation of Finance Lease Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining a lessor's registration duties, read the Contracts Register as a creature of the Implementing Regulation, which both defines it and imposes the operative timelines and enforcement effects. The Regulation defines the Contracts Register and, in Articles 15 and 25, requires the Lessor to register the contract and amendments within 10 business days and makes the registered contract extract an enforcement document under the Enforcement Law. For compliance decisions, check that registration occurred within the window and via the licensed Contracts Registration Company, since the register's contents — owned by SAMA — determine enforceability against third parties.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 6 / Art 15):** "The Lessor shall register the Contract and any amendments thereto at the Contracts Register within (10) business days from the date of concluding the contract"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 2 / Art 1):** "Contracts Register: a register for recording finance lease contracts concluded or implemented in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia... in accordance with the provisions of the Law."
