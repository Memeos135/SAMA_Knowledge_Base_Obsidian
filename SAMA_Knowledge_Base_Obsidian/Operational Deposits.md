# Operational Deposits

## Connections

### [[Unsecured Wholesale Funding Run-off]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When classifying corporate or institutional deposits for LCR run-off purposes, do not treat 'operational deposits' and 'unsecured wholesale funding run-off' as separate silos: operational deposits are a preferential carve-out within the unsecured wholesale funding regime, attracting the 25% factor only for the portion proven to serve clearing, custody or cash-management needs. Any excess balance falls back into the ordinary non-operational wholesale categories (e.g. 40% or 100% run-off), and if the bank cannot isolate the operational portion the entire deposit is treated as non-operational. You would therefore verify the operational-need methodology before applying the favourable factor, defaulting to the higher wholesale run-off rate where it is not demonstrable.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 31, para 96):** "only that part of the deposit balance ... proven to serve a customer's operational needs can qualify as stable. Excess balances should be treated in the appropriate category for non-operational deposits"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 33, para 109):** "deposits and other funding from other institutions ... not specifically held for operational purposes ... The run-off factor for these funds is 100%."
