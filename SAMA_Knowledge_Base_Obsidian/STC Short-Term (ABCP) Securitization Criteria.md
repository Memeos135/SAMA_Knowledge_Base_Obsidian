# STC Short-Term (ABCP) Securitization Criteria

## Connections

### [[ABCP Conduit]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When evaluating STC compliance for an ABCP conduit, recognize that the definition of the conduit itself is embedded in the short-term criteria block (18.96) and that compliance is tested at two levels: paragraph 18.97 requires conduit-level exposures (e.g. investing in the conduit's commercial paper) to satisfy the criteria at both conduit and transaction levels, while 18.98 lets transaction-level exposures qualify if the relevant transaction-level criteria are met. This means an ABCP conduit is not a single STC unit — the sponsor must give representations and warranties per criterion. Conclude that STC relief for a conduit exposure depends on identifying which level your exposure sits at and confirming the correspondingly applicable criteria, not on a global conduit assessment.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 269 / Art 18.96):** "ABCP conduit/conduit – ABCP conduit, being the special purpose vehicle which can issue commercial paper"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 270 / Art 18.97):** "compliance with the short-term STC capital criteria is only achieved if the criteria are satisfied at both the conduit and transaction levels"

### [[Simple, Transparent and Comparable (STC) Framework]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining whether an ABCP conduit exposure qualifies for the more favorable STC alternative capital treatment, apply the short-term criteria set in 18.96-18.165 rather than the term criteria: the STC scope provision (18.67(2)) makes ABCP conduits and conduit-financed transactions STC-compliant only if they meet that dedicated short-term criteria block. The framework further conditions relief on the originator/sponsor disclosing transaction-level information and the investor making its own STC assessment, and STC criteria must be met at all times. Conclude that ABCP STC relief cannot be assumed from the conduit rating alone — verify satisfaction of the 18.96+ short-term criteria at both conduit and transaction levels before applying reduced parameters.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 253 / Art 18.70):** "STC criteria need to be met at all times."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 253 / Art 18.67(2)):** "Exposures to ABCP conduits and/or transactions financed by ABCP conduits, where the conduit and/or transactions financed by it meet the criteria in 18.96 to 18.165"
