# Consumer Protection & Data Confidentiality

## Connections

### [[Rules for Regulating BNPL Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a BNPL company's consumer-protection obligations, treat them as spanning both Article 17 (complaint-handling function and confidentiality of consumer data) and the wider consumer safeguards in Articles 20-22 (fee prohibitions, CDD, KYC, data privacy, credit limits), because the parent Rules link these provisions under one duty regime that also cross-refers out to AML/CTF regulations, the Electronic Transactions Law, and the Responsible Lending Principles. Note the term boundary: the BNPL 'Consumer' (beneficiary of the activity) is the protected subject, distinct from the AML 'Customer', even though Article 21 imports CDD/KYC concepts. You should therefore verify BNPL consumer-protection compliance across all these articles jointly, and not read Article 17 as the sole locus of the obligation.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 10 / Art 21):** "The BNPL company shall develop a CDD program and comply with the AML/CTF regulations and instructions... a. Know Your Customer (KYC)... c. Data privacy and confidentiality."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / Art 17):** "The BNPL company, including all of its employees, shall maintain the confidentiality of consumers’ data and transactions"
