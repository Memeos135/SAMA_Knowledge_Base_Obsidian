# Shariah-Compliant Capital

**Cohesion:** 0.20 - loosely connected
**Members:** 11 nodes

## Why this community

Prudential capital treatment for Shari'ah-compliant banking: how Islamic financing structures map onto SAMA's Basel-based credit and market risk capital charges. Relevant for banks running Islamic windows or fully Shari'ah-compliant balance sheets calculating regulatory capital.

## How members connect

- The 'Additional Capital Adequacy Requirements for Shari'ah Compliant Banking' is the governing instrument and references the SAMA Basel Framework as its parent regime — it supplements, not replaces, the standard capital rules.
- That instrument references each named Islamic contract (Murabahah, Tawarruq, Salam, Istisna, Ijarah, Musharakah, Mudarabah) to specify how each is treated for capital purposes.
- Contract type determines which capital charge applies: debt-like structures (Murabahah) link to Credit Risk Capital Requirements, while asset/inventory-exposed structures (Salam, Ijarah) attract Market Risk Capital Requirements — driving different capital outcomes for economically similar financings.
