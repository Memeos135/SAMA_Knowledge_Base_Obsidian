# Circular on External Auditor Accounting Standards

## Connections

### [[Rules Governing Money Changing Business]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When assessing a money changer's external-audit obligations, treat the SAMA circular as an enforcement of a pre-existing legal duty, not a new requirement: the circular expressly invokes Article 18 of the Rules Governing Money Changing Business (issued by Ministerial Decision) which requires every money changer to appoint an approved licensed external auditor to audit its accounts annually. The circular's operative demand — that firms have their external auditor state which accounting standards they follow — derives its authority from that Article. You would conclude that the auditor-appointment duty is the anchoring obligation and the circular is a supervisory request grounded in it.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 1):** "استناداً إلى المادة الثامنة عشر من القواعد المنظمة لمزاولة أعمال الصرافة ... يجب على كل صراف تعيين مراجع حسابات خارجي مرخص"
- **Grounding — related node (Page 1 / Art 18):** "[Art 18] requires every money changer to appoint a licensed external auditor approved by the Institution to audit and review its accounts annually"
