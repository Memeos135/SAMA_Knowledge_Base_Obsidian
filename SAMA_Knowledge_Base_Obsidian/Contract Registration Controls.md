### [[Finance Lease Law and Implementing Regulation]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the legal authority for the finance-lease contract registration regime, treat the Contract Registration Controls as a subordinate instrument issued under the Finance Lease Law and its Implementing Regulation — the Controls were issued pursuant to Article 30 of the Implementing Regulation, which mandates a Governor's decision (agreed with the Minister of Justice) setting out registration procedures and third-party access. Definitions in the Controls also default to the meanings given in the Finance Lease Law and its Regulation. For compliance, conclude that the Controls cannot be interpreted independently: any registration obligation, timeline or data requirement must be read against, and derives validity from, the parent Law and Regulation.
