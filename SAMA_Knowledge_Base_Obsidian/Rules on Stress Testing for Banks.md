# Rules on Stress Testing for Banks

## Connections

### [[BCBS Principles for Sound Stress Testing]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When determining the full compliance perimeter for bank stress testing, treat the BCBS 'Principles for Sound Stress Testing Practices and Supervision' as binding supplementary guidance, not merely background. Section 1.6 states SAMA circulated the Principles for compliance and requires banks to 'take into account the guidance provided' in addition to these Rules, and to approach SAMA where the two conflict. Conclude that meeting the SAMA Rules alone is insufficient; banks must also address the BCBS Principles and escalate any inconsistency to SAMA rather than self-resolve it.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 40 (Q&A on Para 5)):** "The methodologies... are for the guidance of banks and they can adopt any of these and other appropriate techniques in line with their stress testing frameworks."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 8 / 1.6):** "banks are also required to take into account the guidance provided in the aforesaid Principles... In case of any inconsistency... they should approach SAMA for further guidance."

### [[Stress Testing]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **What this link tells you:** When identifying the core obligation these Rules impose, read Stress Testing as the mandatory, framework-based activity the document defines and makes enforceable, not an optional risk exercise. The Rules require banks to 'design, develop and implement a sound and robust stress testing framework' meeting minimum requirements, and SAMA assesses 'the degree of compliance with these Rules' during supervisory review and on-site examinations. Conclude that the stress-testing concept here is a supervisory deliverable subject to prescribed frequency, board/senior-management involvement, and semi-annual reporting to SAMA.
- **Grounding — this node (Page 35 / 12):** "the Agency shall assess the efforts made by banks in embedding the requirements of these Rules into their risk management frameworks... Assessment of the degree of compliance with these Rules."
- **Grounding — related node (Page 14 / 4.1):** "Banks must adopt a holistic approach to stress testing... all material risks... should be covered in the stress testing process."
