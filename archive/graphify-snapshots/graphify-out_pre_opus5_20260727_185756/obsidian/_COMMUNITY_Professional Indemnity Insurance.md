# Professional Indemnity Insurance

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

This community governs the requirement for regulated entities — particularly payment service providers, finance companies, and intermediaries — to hold Professional Indemnity Insurance (PII) as a condition of authorisation or ongoing licence maintenance. It is a financial-resilience and consumer-redress control sitting within SAMA's prudential and licensing frameworks.

## How members connect

- PII adequacy is a licensing condition; SAMA examiners expect documented evidence of current, sufficient cover at authorisation and renewal
- Minimum coverage thresholds and policy terms must align with the regulated activity type and transaction volumes
- PII interacts with capital-adequacy requirements as a complementary loss-absorption mechanism for operational and liability risks
- Consumer-protection rules rely on PII as a backstop for client redress where entity assets are insufficient
- Lapse or material change in PII coverage constitutes a notifiable event requiring prompt disclosure to SAMA
- Outsourcing and third-party arrangements may affect PII scope; entities must ensure cover extends to relevant operational risks
