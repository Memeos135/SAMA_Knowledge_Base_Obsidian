# Bank Fraud Combating Rules

**Cohesion:** 0.22 - loosely connected
**Members:** 11 nodes

## Why this community

This community covers the fraud prevention and detection control framework applicable to licensed banks under SAMA supervision, anchored by the Financial Fraud Combating Guide and supported by the Banking Control Law and payment-channel-specific obligations. It sits at the intersection of prudential licensing, operational fraud governance, and point-of-sale CDD requirements, creating an obligation chain from institutional licensing through to channel-level controls.

## How members connect

- The Financial Fraud Combating Guide operationalises fraud obligations that derive authority from the Banking Control Law and cross-reference the AML Law, binding all licensed banks as defined under that Law.
- The Financial Fraud Combating Unit is the designated internal governance structure mandated by the Guide, with its charter and responsibilities shaped by the Governance and Responsibilities framework within the same instrument.
- The POS KYC Verification Circular extends fraud-related CDD obligations to the point-of-sale channel, referencing both the Banking Control Law and Payment Service Providers Regulatory Rules, linking merchant-facing controls to the broader fraud regime.
- SAMA as Monetary Authority exercises licensing authority under the Banking Control Law and supervises compliance with both the fraud guide and the POS circular, creating a single supervisory principal across these instruments.
- Banking Licensing Provisions establish the threshold condition (licensed bank status) that determines which entities fall within scope of the fraud guide and POS verification requirements.
