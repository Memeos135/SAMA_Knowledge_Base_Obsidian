# SIFI Resolution & Recovery

**Cohesion:** 0.14 - loosely connected
**Members:** 15 nodes

## Why this community

This community covers the SIFI resolution and recovery regime under KSA financial stability law, centering on the Competent Authority's powers (exercised by SAMA and/or CMA depending on the entity type) to plan for and execute orderly resolution of systemically important institutions. The cluster maps the full lifecycle from pre-failure recovery planning through triggered resolution procedures and post-resolution structural tools (Transitional Entity, Asset Management Entity, Sale), with CEDA involvement for decisions of systemic consequence.

## How members connect

- Competent Authority designation chain: SAMA and CMA are referenced as Competent Authority depending on the SIFI's regulated perimeter, creating a dual-regulator coordination obligation.
- Pre-failure planning obligations: SIFIs (including those within a Financial Group) must maintain Board-approved Recovery Plans; the Competent Authority holds and reviews Resolution Plans, triggering an escalation path from recovery to resolution.
- Resolution Procedures as an action menu: Amendment of Rights, Sale of the SIFI, Transitional Entity, and Asset Management Entity are enumerated tools available once Resolution Procedures are invoked, each with distinct asset/liability transfer implications.
- Valuation dependency: Accredited Valuer is a precondition for exercising Resolution Procedures (particularly Sale and asset transfer tools), linking independent valuation to the legal validity of resolution actions.
- Funding mechanism: Resolution Funds are referenced by the Competent Authority as a financing backstop for resolution execution, tying capital adequacy of the resolution fund to procedural feasibility.
- Governance escalation: Resolution Plans require referral to the Council of Economic and Development Affairs for approval, embedding a sovereign-level decision gate above the Competent Authority for the most systemically sensitive actions.
