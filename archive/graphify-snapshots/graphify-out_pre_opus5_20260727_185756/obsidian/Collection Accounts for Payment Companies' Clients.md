# Collection Accounts for Payment Companies' Clients

## Connections

### [[Protection and Safeguarding of Protected Funds]] — `conceptually_related_to` [INFERRED]
- **Why:** SAMA_EN_1430 (Implementing Regulation, Chapter 7) mandates that licensed PSPs protect and safeguard client funds, while SAMA_EN_1644 (Banking Accounts Rules, Rule 300.1.3.7) operationalises this by specifying the account-opening and management requirements for the dedicated collection accounts in which those protected funds must be held at a licensed bank.
- **This node (Page 51 / Rule 300.1.3.7):** "Collection accounts for depositing and retaining the funds of payment companies' clients shall be opened and managed in accordance with the following requirements: A letter from the Chairperson of the Board of Directors..."
- **Related node (Page 3 / Table of Contents):** "الباب السابع: حماية وحفظ الأموال المحمية"
- **Implication:** A PSP must demonstrate to an examiner that every collection account holding protected client funds has been opened strictly under Rule 300.1.3.7 controls (named-purpose account, board-authorised letter, no commingling), thereby satisfying both the Banking Accounts Rules and the Implementing Regulation's safeguarding chapter simultaneously.
