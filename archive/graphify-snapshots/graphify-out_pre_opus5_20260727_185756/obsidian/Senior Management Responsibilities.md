# Senior Management Responsibilities

## Connections

### [[MLTF Risk Assessment|ML/TF Risk Assessment]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** Senior management is assigned dual responsibility: approving the documented risk assessment at least biennially, and implementing continuous monitoring and compliance with AML/CTF requirements based on its results, creating an accountability chain from the assessment output to operational controls.
- **This node (Page 15 / Section 2: Senior Management):** "The board of directors and senior management in the financial institution shall ensure that the policies, procedures and controls in place related to AML/CTF are based on the results of money laundering and terrorism financing risks."
- **Related node (Page 21 / Section 1: ML/TF Risk Assessment):** "The risk assessment… shall be documented and approved by the senior management. When carrying out the risk assessment process, the financial institution can focus on the following factors: products and services, transactions, channels used for delivering services and products."
- **Implication:** Senior management approval of the risk assessment must be evidenced by a signed, dated document; compliance systems should enforce a workflow that prevents AML/CTF policy updates from taking effect without a corresponding senior-management-approved risk assessment on record.
