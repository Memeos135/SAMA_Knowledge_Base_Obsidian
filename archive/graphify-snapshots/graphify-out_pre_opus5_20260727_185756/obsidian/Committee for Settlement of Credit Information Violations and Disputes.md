# Committee for Settlement of Credit Information Violations and Disputes

## Connections

### [[Objection and Complaint Procedure]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Committee for Settlement of Credit Information Violations and Disputes is the defined escalation body that governs the outer boundary of the Objection and Complaint Procedure; Article (51) explicitly conditions re-inclusion of deleted or modified Negative Information on a Committee decision, establishing the Committee as the mandatory appellate authority once the company-level objection procedure is exhausted.
- **This node (Page 2 / Article (1) definitions):** "Committee: The Committee for Settlement of Credit Information Violations and Disputes."
- **Related node (Page 15-16 / Articles (46)-(47)):** "The consumer may object at any time against any negative decision or any information contained in his/her credit record if it was false, not updated, incomplete, or old... The company, in this case, shall investigate the complaint free of charge within 30 days of the objection s…"
- **Implication:** Compliance workflows must implement a two-tier dispute architecture: a company-level investigation process with defined SLAs (5/10/7/2 working-day cascading deadlines under Article 47) as the first tier, and a Committee referral pathway as the mandatory second tier before any re-inclusion of previously deleted Negative Information is permitted.
