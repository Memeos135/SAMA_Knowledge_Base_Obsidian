# Recruitment and Following-up Criteria

## Connections

### [[Anti-Money Laundering Law]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The AML Law's overarching obligation on financial institutions to maintain an effective AML/CTF program is operationalised through the Guide's enumerated program elements, which explicitly include recruitment and follow-up criteria as a mandatory component of internal policies and procedures.
- **This node (Page 25 / Para 2.3):** "The financial institution shall include, as a minimum, the following elements in its internal policy and procedures for combating ML/TF:…h) Recruitment and follow-up criteria."
- **Related node (Page 4 / Chapter II Purpose):** "The purpose of this Guide is to help financial institutions…to meet the requirements of the Anti-Money Laundering Law, issued by Royal Decree No. (M/20) dated 05/02/1439H, and its Implementing Regulations."
- **Implication:** HR screening and ongoing staff-monitoring controls (background checks, integrity vetting, periodic follow-up) must be codified in board-approved AML/CTF policies and evidence compliance with the Law's program requirements, forming an auditable trail for SAMA examiners.

- **This node (Page 25 / Para 2.3(h)):** "The financial institution shall include, as a minimum, the following elements in its internal policy and procedures for combating ML/TF: ... h) Recruitment and follow-up criteria."

- **Implication:** A SAMA examiner will expect a financial institution's AML/CTF program documentation to include a standalone, board-approved policy section on recruitment and follow-up criteria, evidencing that staff integrity checks are embedded as a minimum program element rather than an informal HR practice.
