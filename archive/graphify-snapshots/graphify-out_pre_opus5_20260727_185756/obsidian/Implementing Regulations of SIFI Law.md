# Implementing Regulations of SIFI Law

## Connections

### [[Law of Systemically Important Financial Institutions]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The SIFI Law repeatedly delegates specific rule-making to the Implementing Regulations (e.g., debt-conversion priority, derivative handling, asset separation governance), making the Implementing Regulations the subordinate instrument that operationalises the Law's framework obligations.
- **This node (Page 14 / Art 19):** "The Implementing Regulations shall regulate the handling of derivatives to which the SIFI under resolution is party."
- **Related node (Page 1 / Art 1):** "Implementing Regulations: Implementing regulations of the Law."
- **Implication:** Systems and policies governing resolution execution (debt-to-equity conversion waterfall, derivative close-out rules, asset-separation governance) must trace each control requirement to a specific Implementing Regulations provision, not merely to the parent Law, for examiner evidence trails.
