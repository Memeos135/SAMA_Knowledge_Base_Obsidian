# Implementing Regulation to the Law of Payments and Payment Services

## Connections

### [[Implementation Rules for Banking Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Banking Control Law Implementation Rules establish the supervisory and governance baseline for banks, while the Payments and Payment Services Implementing Regulation explicitly applies to banks (included in its distribution scope) as licensed payment service providers, creating a layered regulatory relationship where both instruments bind banks operating in the payments sector.
- **This node (Page 1):** "ويؤكد البنك المركزي على مقدمي خدمات المدفوعات ومشغلي نظم المدفوعات الخاضعين للنظام واللائحة الالتزام بكافة الأحكام الواردة في اللائحة التنفيذية [distribution scope includes: البنوك والمصارف العاملة في المملكة]"
- **Related node (Page 1):** "SAMA stresses that banks should strictly adhere to the aforementioned Rules as well as other instructions issued regarding the implementation of the provisions of the Banking Control Law in order to ensure the soundness of the banking system"
- **Implication:** Banks providing payment services must maintain compliance evidence under both the Banking Control Law Implementation Rules (governance, inspection, reporting) and the Payments IR (licensing, outsourcing, customer protection) — a single SAMA examination could test obligations under both instruments simultaneously.
- **Caveat:** No direct textual cross-reference from SAMA_EN_1429 to SAMA_EN_1430 appears in the provided excerpts; the references relation is inferred from the Payments IR's explicit distribution to banks and SAMA's supervisory authority over both; confidence is partially INFERRED.

### [[Law of Payments and Payment Services]] — `implements` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Law of Payments and Payment Services expressly grants SAMA authority to issue implementing regulations, and the Implementing Regulation to that Law (SAMA_EN_1430) was issued pursuant to and explicitly references that primary law, establishing the hierarchical implements relationship.
- **This node (Page 1):** "استنادًا إلى صلاحيات البنك المركزي السعودي بموجب نظام المدفوعات وخدماتها الصادر بالمرسوم الملكي رقم (م/17)... نحيطكم بصدور اللائحة التنفيذية لنظام المدفوعات وخدماتها"
- **Related node (Page 8 / Article 4):** "A person may not operate a payment system or provide payment services in the Kingdom unless licensed by SAMA... Determining the terms and conditions for issuing licenses, and the legal structure of Payment system operators and payment service providers"
- **Implication:** RegTech systems onboarding payment service providers must map licensing conditions, capital requirements, outsourcing rules, and customer protection obligations to specific Implementing Regulation articles, not merely to the parent Law — audit evidence must trace each control to its IR provision.
