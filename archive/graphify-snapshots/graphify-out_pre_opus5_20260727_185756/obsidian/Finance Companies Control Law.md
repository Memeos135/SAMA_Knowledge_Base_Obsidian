# Finance Companies Control Law

## Connections

### [[Rules for Regulating BNPL Companies]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Rules are subordinate legislation issued under the Finance Companies Control Law; the Law is the parent statute that grants SAMA the power to issue the Rules and sets definitions that the Rules expressly incorporate, making the Law a binding superior instrument whose provisions the Rules must not prejudice.
- **This node (Page 1):** "SAMA issued these Rules based on the powers vested in SAMA under the Finance Companies Control Law issued by Royal Decree No. M/51 dated 13/08/1433H."
- **Related node (Page 3 / Article 1 & Article 3):** "The terms and phrases mentioned in these Rules shall have the same meanings stated in the Finance Companies Control Law… These Rules shall not prejudice the provisions of relevant regulations, including… the Finance Companies Control Law issued by Royal Decree No. M/51 dated 13/…"
- **Implication:** A RegTech compliance mapping must layer the Rules on top of the Finance Companies Control Law, flagging any gap or conflict where the Law imposes obligations beyond the minimum standards set in the Rules.
