# Limited Network Provider Registration

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

This community covers the registration regime applicable to Limited Network Service Providers (LNSPs) — entities issuing payment instruments usable only within a restricted network or for a limited range of goods and services. It sits within the payments regulatory perimeter, defining the lighter-touch authorisation pathway that is nonetheless subject to SAMA oversight and defined scope conditions.

## How members connect

- Registration scope conditions (network/product limits) determine whether an entity falls inside or outside full PSP licensing requirements
- Breach of network or value thresholds triggers reclassification and potential full-licence obligation
- AML/CTF applicability is modulated by LNSP status — exemption conditions must be actively monitored and documented
- SAMA registration records must be maintained as evidence of authorised status for counterparty due-diligence purposes
- Consumer-protection obligations attach proportionally; entities must evidence which rules apply given their registered scope
- Ongoing compliance requires monitoring of instrument usage to confirm continued eligibility for the limited-network carve-out
