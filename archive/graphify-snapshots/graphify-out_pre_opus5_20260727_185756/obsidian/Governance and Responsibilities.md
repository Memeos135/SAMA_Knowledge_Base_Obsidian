# Governance and Responsibilities

## Connections

### [[Financial Fraud Combating Guide for Banks]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** Chapter Two of the Guide (الفصل الثاني) is explicitly titled 'Governance and Responsibilities', embedding governance and accountability structures as a mandatory component of the fraud-control framework, thereby linking the Guide as the normative source that defines governance obligations for fraud risk management.
- **This node (Page 4):** "ونظراً للقدرة العالية والمتوقعة من البنوك والمصارف العاملة في المملكة للقيام بمسؤوليتها الرقابية الكاملة والتزامها"
- **Related node (Page 3):** "الفصل الثاني: الحوكمة — الحوكمة والمسئوليات"
- **Implication:** Banks must establish and document board- or senior-management-level accountability for fraud risk, with a governance framework (roles, responsibilities, escalation paths) that can be evidenced to SAMA examiners under Chapter Two of the Guide.

### [[Financial Fraud Combating Unit]] — `conceptually_related_to` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Fraud Guide structures the bank's anti-fraud framework as two distinct but interdependent chapters: Chapter Two (Governance and Responsibilities) sets the accountability structure at board/senior-management level, while Chapter Three (Tasks of the Financial Fraud Combating Unit) operationalises that accountability through a dedicated administrative unit — the unit's mandate derives its authority and reporting lines from the governance layer.
- **This node (Page 3, Table of Contents):** "الفصل الثاني: الحوكمة — الحوكمة والمسئوليات … الفصل الثالث: مهام وحدة مكافحة الاحتيال المالي"
- **Related node (Page 5, Art 1-1 / Page 3, Table of Contents):** "وحدة مكافحة الاحتيال: الوحدة الإدارية في البنك المعنية بمكافحة الاحتيال المالي والتعامل مع الحالات والقضايا ذات العلاقة به"
- **Implication:** An examiner will expect to see a documented governance charter that formally defines the Fraud Combating Unit's mandate, reporting lines (e.g. to the board audit/risk committee), independence from business lines, and escalation protocols — evidencing that the unit is not a standalone function but is embedded within the bank's governance accountability structure.
