# Counter-Fraud Requirements

**Cohesion:** 0.67 - moderately connected
**Members:** 4 nodes

## Why this community

This community consolidates SAMA's counter-fraud mandatory requirements across both banks and finance companies, anchored by the Counter-Fraud Framework (CFF) as the governing standard and implemented through binding circulars. It forms the control-family backbone for fraud risk management obligations applicable to licensed financial institutions.

## How members connect

- Counter-Fraud Framework (CFF) is the primary normative document; Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements (10530) references it directly, establishing the CFF as the interpretive authority for specific fraud controls
- Counter-Fraud Fundamental Requirements Circular (10529) and its substantive counterpart (10530) are semantically paired — the circular is the transmittal instrument and (10530) contains the enforceable requirements text
- Both circulars (10529 and 10530) reference Anti-Fraud Rules for Finance Companies (circular 381000103246), extending counter-fraud obligations explicitly into the finance company regime and flagging a cross-entity-type compliance perimeter
- The cluster implies a unified control family: fraud detection and prevention programs, incident reporting obligations, staff awareness requirements, and governance accountability — applicable to both banks and finance companies with sector-specific calibration
- An examiner would expect evidence of CFF-aligned fraud risk assessments, documented controls mapped to the fundamental requirements, and proof that finance companies have implemented the sector-specific circular obligations
