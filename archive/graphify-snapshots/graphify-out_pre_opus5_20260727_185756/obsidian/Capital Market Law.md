# Capital Market Law

## Connections

### [[Finance Companies Control Law (English Translation)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Finance Companies Control Law explicitly cites the Capital Market Law as a compliance benchmark for founding members and board directors — breach of the Capital Market Law disqualifies a person from holding these roles — establishing a direct cross-regulatory fit-and-proper requirement.
- **This node (Page 4 / Article 5 Second (b)):** "not be in breach of the provisions of the Capital Market Law and its Regulations, or convicted of violating the Banking Control Law, the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law or finance laws"
- **Related node (Page 3 / Article 5 First (3)(b)):** "not be in breach of the provisions of the Capital Market Law and its Regulations, the Banking Control Law, the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law or finance laws"
- **Implication:** Fit-and-proper assessment workflows must query CMA enforcement and conviction records for all founding members and board nominees before submission to SAMA, and this check must be repeatable at each renewal or material ownership change.

### [[Implementing Regulations of Finance Companies Control Law (Arabic)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Finance Companies Control Law (and hence its Implementing Regulations) expressly reference the Capital Market Law as a parallel conduct standard: founding members and board nominees must not be in breach of the Capital Market Law, and lending restrictions carve out listed joint-stock companies on the Saudi Capital Market, creating a cross-regulatory compliance dependency.
- **This node (Page 3 / Article 5 (1)(3)(b)):** "not be in breach of the provisions of the Capital Market Law and its Regulations, the Banking Control Law, the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law or finance laws"
- **Related node (Page 3 / Article 1 (Definitions)):** "أنظمة التمويل: نظام التمويل العقاري ونظام الإيجار التمويلي ونظام مراقبة شركات التمويل"
- **Implication:** Licensing and ongoing fit-and-proper workflows for finance company founders and board nominees must include a screening check against Capital Market Law breach/conviction records, requiring integration with CMA enforcement data or equivalent external source.
- **Caveat:** The Arabic Regulations context provided does not itself cite the Capital Market Law by name; the reference is sourced from the English Law (node_b context). The cross-reference is genuine but arises in the parent Law, not in the Implementing Regulations text shown.
