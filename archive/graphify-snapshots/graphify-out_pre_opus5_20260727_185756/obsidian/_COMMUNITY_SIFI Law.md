# SIFI Law

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Systemically Important Financial Institution (SIFI) designation and enhanced prudential obligations under KSA law. This community covers the primary legislative framework and its implementing regulations governing identification, requirements, and supervisory expectations for institutions whose failure could threaten financial stability. The cluster is tight because the Implementing Regulations derive directly from and operationalise the Law, forming a two-tier primary/secondary instrument pair.

## How members connect

- The Implementing Regulations reference the SIFI Law as their legal basis, creating a direct parent-child instrument relationship
- Designation criteria, thresholds, and methodology flow from the Law into the Implementing Regulations as enforceable detail
- Enhanced capital, liquidity, recovery, and resolution requirements are anchored in the Law and specified in the Implementing Regulations
- Supervisory powers and SAMA intervention triggers are established in the Law and procedurally elaborated in the Implementing Regulations
- Compliance obligations (reporting, governance, systemic risk buffers) are traceable through both instruments as a single control chain
