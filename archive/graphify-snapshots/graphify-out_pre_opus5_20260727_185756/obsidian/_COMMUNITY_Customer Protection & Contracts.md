# Customer Protection & Contracts

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

Consumer-facing disclosure and contractual transparency obligations under SAMA's customer protection and payments regulatory perimeter. This community covers the pre-contractual and contractual information requirements that regulated entities must satisfy, linking general protection principles to specific framework contract disclosure rules relevant to payment services and consumer finance. The cluster reflects the obligation chain from high-level principles to operationally specific document requirements.

## How members connect

- Framework Contract Information Requirements reference Customer Protection Principles as the normative foundation for disclosure obligations
- Both instruments share the defined term 'Customer' and apply to retail-facing regulated entities
- Disclosure timing, format, and content obligations in the Framework Contract rules operationalise the fairness and transparency principles
- Pre-contractual information duties create a document-production and evidence-retention requirement auditable against both instruments
- Cross-regime interaction: 'Consumer' definitions may diverge when AML CDD obligations apply to the same customer relationship
