# Financial Inclusion Principles

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

This community addresses SAMA's policy framework for broadening access to regulated financial products and services among underserved segments. It intersects consumer-protection, payments/PSP licensing, and BNPL/finance-company regimes by setting design principles that obliged entities must embed in product development and delivery strategies.

## How members connect

- Inclusion principles constrain product eligibility criteria, preventing blanket exclusions that conflict with SAMA access mandates
- Simplified or tiered CDD requirements for low-risk segments are a direct RegTech implication of inclusion policy
- PSP and BNPL licensing conditions may reference inclusion commitments as part of authorisation or business-plan approval
- Affordability and suitability assessments under consumer-protection rules operationalise inclusion at the product level
- Reporting obligations may require entities to evidence outreach metrics to underserved demographics for SAMA review
