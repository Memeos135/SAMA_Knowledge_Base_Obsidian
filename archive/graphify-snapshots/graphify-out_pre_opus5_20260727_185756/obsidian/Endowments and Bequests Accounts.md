# Endowments and Bequests Accounts

## Connections

### [[SAMA (Saudi Central Bank)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** Endowments and bequests accounts (Rule 300.1.5.8) are governed within the SAMA Rules for Bank Accounts, and the broader Rules explicitly reserve to SAMA the authority over disclosure, enforcement, and dormant/abandoned account procedures that apply to all account types including these special-purpose accounts.
- **This node (Page 22):** "Procedures taken for the disclosure of and enforcement on bank balances, accounts and relationships at all stages shall be strictly confidential. Such requests shall be received only by SAMA except for cases stated in SAMA's instructions."
- **Related node (Page 1):** "Rules for Bank Accounts Updated March 2022... Please refer to SAMA's website (www.sama.gov.sa) for the last updated and amended version of the Rules."
- **Implication:** Banks must route all disclosure and enforcement requests on endowment/bequest accounts exclusively through SAMA, requiring a documented intake and response workflow that distinguishes SAMA-directed requests from general judicial orders.
- **Caveat:** The node_a context provided is dominated by dormant account rules rather than endowments-specific provisions; the linkage to SAMA authority is drawn from the overarching Rules framework rather than an endowments-specific clause, which is consistent but slightly inferred.
