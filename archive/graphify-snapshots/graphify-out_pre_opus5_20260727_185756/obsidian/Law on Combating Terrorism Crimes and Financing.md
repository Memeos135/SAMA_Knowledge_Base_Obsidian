# Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing

## Connections

### [[AMLCTF Guide|AML/CTF Guide]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The AML/CTF Guide's scope and programme requirements explicitly encompass the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing (Royal Decree No. M/21, 12/02/1439H) alongside the AML Law, with the Guide's AML/CTF programme obligations directly implementing both laws' requirements for financial institutions supervised by SAMA.
- **This node (Page 5 / Chapter IV Definitions):** "Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing: The Law on Terrorism Crimes and Financing issued by Royal Decree No. (M/21) dated 12/02/1439H."
- **Related node (Page 25 / Para 2.1):** "The financial institution shall develop an AML/CTF program that includes internal policies, procedures and controls to mitigate ML/TF risks … approved at the level of the board of directors."
- **Implication:** The board-approved AML/CTF programme document must explicitly reference and address obligations under both Royal Decree M/20 (AML) and Royal Decree M/21 (CTF), with controls mapped to each law separately so that SAMA examiners can verify dual-regime coverage.

### [[Record Keeping]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** Article 65 of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing is directly cited in the AML/CTF Guide's Record Keeping section as one of the two statutory bases defining the manner of record keeping, minimum retention periods, and the authority to extend those periods, making the CTF Law a co-equal legal source for the record-keeping obligation alongside Article 12 of the AML Law.
- **This node (Page 5 / Chapter IV Definitions):** "Paragraph (4) of the Implementing Regulations of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and their Financing stipulates that 'The supervisory authorities… referred to in Article 1/22 of the Law shall include (4) Saudi Central Bank.'"
- **Related node (Page 45 / Section 6):** "Article (12) of the Anti-Money Laundering Law and Article (65) of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing state the financial institution's obligations relating to the manner of record keeping, the minimum record-keeping period, and the possibility for extending that…"
- **Implication:** Record-keeping systems must satisfy dual statutory authority (AML Law Art. 12 and CTF Law Art. 65) simultaneously; a SAMA examiner will check that retention parameters, access controls, and extension procedures are configured to meet both laws, not just the AML Law alone.
