# Reliance on Third Party for Due Diligence

## Connections

### [[Due Diligence Measures]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Due Diligence Measures section directly governs the conditions under which a financial institution may outsource CDD execution to a third party, establishing that responsibility for compliance remains with the originating institution even when reliance is permitted under Articles 7/10, 7/11, and 7/13 of the AML Implementing Regulations.
- **This node (Page 35 / para 3.22):** "The financial institution may rely on a third party, whether it is a financial institution or any of the owners of DNFBPs, to implement the due diligence measures, in accordance with the following…"
- **Related node (Page 36 / para 3.24):** "The financial institution shall periodically verify (at least annually) that the third party has the sufficient capabilities and powers required to fulfill the due diligence requirements in a professional manner."
- **Implication:** The CDD/KYB workflow must include an annual third-party capability assessment control, with documented evidence retained and available to SAMA on request, confirming the delegating institution's non-transferable accountability.
