# Objection and Complaint Procedure

## Connections

### [[Committee for Settlement of Credit Information Violations and Disputes]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Committee for Settlement of Credit Information Violations and Disputes is the defined escalation body that governs the outer boundary of the Objection and Complaint Procedure; Article (51) explicitly conditions re-inclusion of deleted or modified Negative Information on a Committee decision, establishing the Committee as the mandatory appellate authority once the company-level objection procedure is exhausted.
- **This node (Page 15-16 / Articles (46)-(47)):** "The consumer may object at any time against any negative decision or any information contained in his/her credit record if it was false, not updated, incomplete, or old... The company, in this case, shall investigate the complaint free of charge within 30 days of the objection s…"
- **Related node (Page 2 / Article (1) definitions):** "Committee: The Committee for Settlement of Credit Information Violations and Disputes."
- **Implication:** Compliance workflows must implement a two-tier dispute architecture: a company-level investigation process with defined SLAs (5/10/7/2 working-day cascading deadlines under Article 47) as the first tier, and a Committee referral pathway as the mandatory second tier before any re-inclusion of previously deleted Negative Information is permitted.

### [[Consumer]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Consumer concept directly invokes the Objection and Complaint Procedure: Arts 43–45 establish consumer rights to access and contest credit records, and Arts 46–51 operationalise the procedural framework (timeframes, written notifications, record amendment) triggered when a consumer exercises those rights.
- **This node (Page 15 / Art 46):** "The consumer may object at any time against any negative decision or any information contained in his/her credit record if it was false, not updated, incomplete, or old... The company shall investigate the complaint free of charge within 30 days of the objection submission date."
- **Related node (Page 14 / Art 44):** "The consumer has the right to: 1. know the name and address of any party that has enquired about his/her credit record during the past 2 years; and 2. file a complaint if his/her credit record contains false or incomplete information."
- **Implication:** A credit information company's complaints management system must enforce the Arts 46–51 cascading timeframes (5→10→7→2 working-day chain) and generate auditable written notifications to the consumer and originating member at each step, with outcome records retained to evidence examiner review.
