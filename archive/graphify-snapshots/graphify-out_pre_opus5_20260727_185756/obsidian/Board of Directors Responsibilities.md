# Board of Directors Responsibilities

## Connections

### [[MLTF Risk Assessment|ML/TF Risk Assessment]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Board of Directors is explicitly assigned primary accountability for ensuring the ML/TF risk assessment is conducted accurately and covers all risks, making board oversight a governance prerequisite that both triggers and validates the risk assessment process.
- **This node (Page 15 / Section 1: Board of Directors):** "The main responsibilities of the board include, but are not limited to, the following: a) Ensuring that the ML/TF risk assessment in the financial institution is conducted accurately and covers all risks facing the financial institution in order to develop appropriate policies t…"
- **Related node (Page 21 / Section 1: ML/TF Risk Assessment):** "The risk assessment shall be updated regularly (once every two years at a minimum) and shall be documented and approved by the senior management."
- **Implication:** The board must evidence its oversight of the ML/TF risk assessment through documented approvals (e.g., board or committee minutes) at least every two years; a SAMA examiner will expect to see a board-approved risk assessment document with a traceable approval date.
