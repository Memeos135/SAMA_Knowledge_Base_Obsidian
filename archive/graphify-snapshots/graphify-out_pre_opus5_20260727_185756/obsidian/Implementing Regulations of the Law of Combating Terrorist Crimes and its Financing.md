# Implementing Regulations of the Law of Combating Terrorist Crimes and its Financing

## Connections

### [[Business Risk Assessment Guide (AMLCFTPF)|Business Risk Assessment Guide (AML/CFT/PF)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Business Risk Assessment Guide (10912) is issued under authority of, and is bounded by, the Counter-Terrorism Crimes and Financing Law and its Implementing Regulations (132), which impose on financial institutions the substantive CTF obligations—including risk-level determination and internal policies—that the BRA Guide operationalises through minimum assessment factors.
- **This node (Page 11, Article 18(1)):** "organizations shall implement and update, monitor, and enhance, when necessary, internal rules, policies, and procedures for combatting terrorism financing. This shall include determining the risk level and proper measure for the effective management and mitigation of such risk."
- **Related node (Page 1, covering circular):** "ونظام مكافحة جرائم الإرهاب وتمويله. الصادر بالمرسوم الملكي رقم (م/١٧) وتاريخ… والأنظمة واللوائح ذات العلاقة"
- **Implication:** Financial institutions' BRA documentation must demonstrably satisfy Article 18 of the CTF Implementing Regulations, meaning the BRA output must map to documented risk-level determinations and proportionate mitigation measures reviewable by SAMA examiners.

### [[Circular Beneficial Owner Query for NPOs|Circular: Beneficial Owner Query for NPOs]] — `references` [INFERRED]
- **Why:** The NPO beneficial-owner query circular (11005) requires financial institutions to use the NCNP portal to satisfy CDD obligations toward non-profit organization customers, grounding this in the CTF Law and its Implementing Regulations (132), which impose CTF-compliance requirements on non-profit organizations and obligate financial institutions to conduct enhanced due diligence on this sector.
- **This node (Page 10, Article 18(1)):** "financial institutions, designated non-financial businesses and professions, and non-profit organizations shall include therein… provisions relating to the measures provided in the Law and Regulations, including those relating to risk management procedures of business relationsh…"
- **Related node (Page 1):** "ونظام مكافحة جرائم الإرهاب وتمويله الصادر بالمرسوم الملكي رقم (م/١)… وما تضمنته من التزامات على المؤسسات المالية بتطبيق تدابير العناية الواجبة… بالتعرف والتحقق من هوية المستفيد الحقيقي"
- **Implication:** When onboarding or monitoring NPO customers, financial institutions must query the NCNP portal as a documented CDD step, record any undisclosed or discrepant beneficial-owner data as a risk exception, and assess whether an STR is required under the CTF Implementing Regulations—creating a dual evidence trail for both the CDD file and the STR consideration log.
- **Caveat:** The inference that 132 is the specific CTF instrument cited is strong given the circular's reference to the CTF Law; however, the circular's Arabic text references the law by Royal Decree number rather than naming document 132 explicitly, introducing minor locator uncertainty.

- **Implication:** Compliance programs must maintain a unified control framework that satisfies both AML IR and CTF IR simultaneously — an STR policy, internal audit scope, and cross-border subsidiary requirements must be documented and tested against both instruments to satisfy a SAMA examination covering either regime.
