# Rules for Regulating BNPL Companies

## Connections

### [[BNPL Company]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The BNPL Company is the defined regulated entity whose entire existence, licensing, obligations, and conduct are governed by the Rules; the Rules are the primary instrument constituting the BNPL Company as a legal/regulatory subject and imposing enforceable requirements on it.
- **This node (Page 3 / Article 2):** "These Rules apply to companies licensed by SAMA to engage in BNPL activity."
- **Related node (Page 3 / Article 1):** "BNPL Company: A joint stock company licensed by SAMA to engage in BNPL activity."
- **Implication:** Any compliance programme, licensing workflow, or examiner-readiness checklist for a BNPL Company must map every obligation to a specific article in these Rules as the controlling instrument.

### [[Finance Companies Control Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Rules are subordinate legislation issued under the Finance Companies Control Law; the Law is the parent statute that grants SAMA the power to issue the Rules and sets definitions that the Rules expressly incorporate, making the Law a binding superior instrument whose provisions the Rules must not prejudice.
- **This node (Page 3 / Article 1 & Article 3):** "The terms and phrases mentioned in these Rules shall have the same meanings stated in the Finance Companies Control Law… These Rules shall not prejudice the provisions of relevant regulations, including… the Finance Companies Control Law issued by Royal Decree No. M/51 dated 13/…"
- **Related node (Page 1):** "SAMA issued these Rules based on the powers vested in SAMA under the Finance Companies Control Law issued by Royal Decree No. M/51 dated 13/08/1433H."
- **Implication:** A RegTech compliance mapping must layer the Rules on top of the Finance Companies Control Law, flagging any gap or conflict where the Law imposes obligations beyond the minimum standards set in the Rules.
