# Customer Protection Principles

## Connections

### [[Framework Contract Information Requirements]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Implementing Regulation structures customer protection obligations (Chapter 5) as downstream requirements that are operationalised through the framework contract disclosure requirements (Chapter 6, Section 1); the contract is the primary instrument through which pre-service information and consent obligations are discharged to the customer/consumer.
- **This node (Page 3 / Table of Contents):** "الباب الخامس: حماية العملاء والشمول المالي ... الفصل الأول - حماية العملاء"
- **Related node (Page 3 / Table of Contents):** "الباب السادس: خدمات المدفوعات ... الفصل الأول — متطلبات العقد وتقديم المعلومات"
- **Implication:** A PSP's compliance framework must map each customer protection principle to a corresponding contractual disclosure item, so that an examiner can trace the protection obligation to the specific contract clause presented to the customer at onboarding.
