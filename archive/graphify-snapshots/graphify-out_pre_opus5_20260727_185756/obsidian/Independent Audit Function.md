# Independent Audit Function

## Connections

### [[AMLCTF Compliance Function|AML/CTF Compliance Function]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** Both the AML/CTF Compliance Function (Section 9) and the Independent Audit Function (Section 10) are mandated governance layers under the same AML/CTF program, with the compliance function explicitly responsible for the program that the independent auditor must test for effectiveness, creating a principal-reviewer relationship between the two nodes.
- **This node (Page 58 / Section 10, opening paragraph):** "The financial institution shall have the AML/CTF internal controls tested by an independent party to ensure that they are adequate to ML/TF risks and implemented effectively… the internal or external auditors involved shall have sufficient experience to conduct a risk-based test…"
- **Related node (Page 56 / Section 9, Para 9.2):** "The financial institution shall establish an independent and specialized AML/CTF unit and provide it with sufficient resources, including human and technological resources, to carry out its work perfectly."
- **Implication:** The independent auditor's scope must be formally documented to cover the compliance function's program, with findings tracked to management responses, and the auditor must be structurally segregated from the AML/CTF unit to satisfy Para 10.2's independence requirement — evidenced in audit-charter and organisational-chart documentation.

### [[Anti-Money Laundering Law]] — `cites` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** Article 14/1 of the Implementing Regulations of the Anti-Money Laundering Law directly mandates that financial institutions establish an independent audit mechanism to test the effectiveness and adequacy of AML/CTF controls; the Guide's Independent Audit Function section implements this statutory requirement.
- **This node (Page 58 / Section 10):** "Article (14/1) of the Implementing Regulations of the Anti-Money Laundering Law and Article (18) of the Implementing Regulations of the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing state the obligations of the financial institution that include setting up an independent audit…"
- **Related node (Page 4 / Chapter II Purpose):** "The purpose of this Guide is to help financial institutions... to meet the requirements of the Anti-Money Laundering Law, issued by Royal Decree No. (M/20) dated 05/02/1439H, and its Implementing Regulations."
- **Implication:** Financial institutions must maintain a periodic independent audit programme (with risk-based scope documentation, auditor qualification evidence, and remediation tracking records) that a SAMA examiner can verify is operationally segregated from the AML/CTF compliance function itself.
