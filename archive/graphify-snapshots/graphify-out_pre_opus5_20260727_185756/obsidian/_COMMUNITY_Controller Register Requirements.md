# Controller Register Requirements

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 1 nodes

## Why this community

This community covers the governance and transparency obligation requiring regulated entities to maintain a register of controllers — persons or entities with significant ownership or influence. It sits at the intersection of SAMA licensing/authorisation conditions and AML/CTF beneficial-ownership frameworks, ensuring supervisory visibility into control structures.

## How members connect

- Controller identification links directly to UBO determination obligations under AML/CTF rules
- Register maintenance is a licensing condition auditable by SAMA examiners at authorisation and ongoing supervision stages
- Data held in the register feeds CDD/KYB workflows when the regulated entity itself is onboarded by another obliged party
- Changes in control typically trigger prior-approval or notification obligations to SAMA
- Register accuracy underpins fit-and-proper assessments for significant shareholders and senior managers
