# Finance Companies Control Law (English Translation)

## Connections

### [[Capital Market Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Finance Companies Control Law explicitly cites the Capital Market Law as a compliance benchmark for founding members and board directors — breach of the Capital Market Law disqualifies a person from holding these roles — establishing a direct cross-regulatory fit-and-proper requirement.
- **This node (Page 3 / Article 5 First (3)(b)):** "not be in breach of the provisions of the Capital Market Law and its Regulations, the Banking Control Law, the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law or finance laws"
- **Related node (Page 4 / Article 5 Second (b)):** "not be in breach of the provisions of the Capital Market Law and its Regulations, or convicted of violating the Banking Control Law, the Cooperative Insurance Companies Control Law or finance laws"
- **Implication:** Fit-and-proper assessment workflows must query CMA enforcement and conviction records for all founding members and board nominees before submission to SAMA, and this check must be repeatable at each renewal or material ownership change.

### [[Companies Law]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Finance Companies Control Law explicitly sets minimum capital floors by reference to the Companies Law and mandates that post-SAMA-approval incorporation be completed under the Companies Law, making compliance with that statute a precondition for obtaining and maintaining a finance licence.
- **This node (Page 3 / Article 5 First (2)):** "The capital of the company shall not be less than the amount specified by SAMA, and not less than the amount specified under the Companies Law."
- **Related node (Page 3 / Article 5 Third):** "Upon approval, applications shall be referred to the Ministry of Commerce and Industry to complete company incorporation and registration in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Law."
- **Implication:** A finance company's licensing checklist must evidence satisfaction of both the SAMA-specified minimum capital threshold and the Companies Law minimum, and must log the Ministry of Commerce commercial registration number as a prerequisite before SAMA issues the operational licence.

### [[Implementing Regulations of Finance Companies Control Law (Arabic)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The Implementing Regulations (Arabic) were issued expressly pursuant to the Finance Companies Control Law; the Law itself defines 'Regulations' as its own implementing instrument, creating a direct hierarchical dependency whereby the Regulations derive their authority from and must be read alongside the Law.
- **This node (Page 2 / Article 1):** "Regulations: Implementing Regulations of this Law. [...] Finance company: A joint stock company licensed to engage in finance activities."
- **Related node (Page 2):** "أصدر البنك المركزي السعودي هذه اللائحة وفقاً للصلاحيات المخولة له بموجب نظام مراقبة شركات التمويل"
- **Implication:** Any compliance gap analysis for a finance company must be run against both instruments simultaneously; the Arabic Regulations supply operative detail (e.g. activity-specific caps, APR calculation) that the Law delegates but does not itself specify, so a RegTech rule engine must ingest both layers.
