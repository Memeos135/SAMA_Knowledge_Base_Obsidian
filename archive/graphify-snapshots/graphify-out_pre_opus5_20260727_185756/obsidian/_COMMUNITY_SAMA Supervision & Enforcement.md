# SAMA Supervision & Enforcement

**Cohesion:** 1.00 - tightly connected
**Members:** 2 nodes

## Why this community

SAMA's statutory supervisory and enforcement authority over licensed financial institutions in the KSA. This community covers the legal basis, scope of powers, and procedural framework through which SAMA conducts examinations, issues directives, and imposes sanctions. It is the foundational governance cluster underpinning all other regulatory regimes, as every sector-specific rule ultimately derives enforceability from SAMA's supervisory mandate.

## How members connect

- Supervision and Enforcement instruments reference SAMA as the competent authority, grounding all powers in SAMA's enabling legislation
- Examination, investigation, and sanction procedures are defined here and cross-referenced by sector-specific frameworks (AML, payments, consumer finance)
- Licensing conditions and ongoing compliance obligations across regimes are enforceable through the mechanisms established in this cluster
- Reporting and notification obligations to SAMA in other regimes are backed by enforcement consequences articulated here
- Auditors and examiners use this cluster to establish the legal basis for findings, directives, and remediation timelines
