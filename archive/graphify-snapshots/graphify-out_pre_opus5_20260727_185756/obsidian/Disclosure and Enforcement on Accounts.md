# Disclosure and Enforcement on Accounts

## Connections

### [[SAMA (Saudi Central Bank)]] — `references` [EXTRACTED]
- **Why:** The disclosure and enforcement provisions explicitly designate SAMA as the sole authorised recipient of disclosure requests and the setter of the form, manner, and period for enforcement actions, establishing SAMA as the regulatory principal whose instructions govern the entire disclosure-and-enforcement operational regime.
- **This node (Page 22 / Section 10 / 10.1):** "Such requests shall be received only by SAMA except for cases stated in SAMA's instructions. Banks shall carry out requests for disclosure and enforcement according to the form, manner and period specified by SAMA."
- **Related node (Page 1):** "Please refer to SAMA's website (www.sama.gov.sa) for the last updated and amended version of the Rules."
- **Implication:** Banks must maintain a dedicated, confidential SAMA-request-handling procedure with a controlled access channel, ensuring that no disclosure or enforcement action is executed without a SAMA-originated instruction and that response formats and timelines conform to SAMA-specified requirements — evidenced by a secure request log accessible only to authorised personnel.
