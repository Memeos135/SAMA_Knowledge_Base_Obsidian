# Credit Information Regulation

**Cohesion:** 0.15 - loosely connected
**Members:** 14 nodes

## Why this community

This community governs the licensed credit information ecosystem under the Credit Information Law and its Implementing Regulations, with SAMA as the licensing and supervisory authority. It defines the obligation chain among Credit Information Companies, their Members, and Consumers — covering data collection, record maintenance, consent, adverse-decision notification, and the formal dispute resolution pathway.

## How members connect

- Licensing as the entry gate: SAMA issues the Credit Information License; no entity may operate as a Credit Information Company or collect Credit Records without it, making the license the anchor control for the entire regime.
- Membership Agreement as the B2B obligation instrument: Members (lenders, financiers) contract with Credit Information Companies via Membership Agreements that define data submission obligations and access rights, creating an enforceable B2B compliance layer.
- Credit Record as the central data object: Negative Information populates the Credit Record; Negative Decisions are linked to it; Consumer Consent governs certain data-sharing uses — the record is the subject of all downstream obligations.
- Consumer rights and consent chain: Consumer Consent is required for specific disclosures; Consumers have a right to access their Credit Record and invoke the Objection and Complaint Procedure if data is disputed or a Negative Decision is contested.
- Dispute resolution escalation: The Objection and Complaint Procedure routes unresolved disputes to the Committee for Settlement of Credit Information Violations and Disputes, providing a statutory adjudication body outside ordinary courts.
- Regulatory hierarchy: The Implementing Regulations of the Credit Information Law operationalize the Law's framework under SAMA's authority, creating a two-tier primary/secondary legislative structure that governs all member obligations.
