# Counter-Fraud Framework

**Cohesion:** 0.12 - loosely connected
**Members:** 20 nodes

## Why this community

This community covers SAMA's Counter-Fraud Framework and its structured control domains (Govern, Prevent, Detect, Respond), which together define the enterprise-wide fraud risk management obligations for licensed financial institutions. It spans the fraud-specific regulatory instruments — including the POS Fraud Circular, Authentication Standard, and Tahaqaq identity verification service — alongside the Cyber Security Framework as a referenced dependency. The dominant regime is SAMA's counter-fraud supervisory mandate, measured through the Counter-Fraud Maturity Model.

## How members connect

- The Counter-Fraud Framework decomposes into four obligation domains (Governance, Prevent, Detect, Respond), each referencing specific controls or sub-standards that institutions must implement and evidence.
- The Governance Domain requires establishment of a Counter-Fraud Governance Committee (CFGC), creating a named accountability structure that an examiner would expect to see chartered and minuted.
- The Prevent Domain consolidates upstream controls — Fraud Risk Assessment, Fraud Risk Appetite, Due Diligence Standards, Authentication Standard, and Intelligence Monitoring — forming a linked prevention control family.
- Fraud Detection Systems (Detect Domain) draw inputs from both the Fraud Risk Assessment and Intelligence Monitoring, creating a data-flow obligation chain between risk quantification and real-time monitoring capabilities.
- Intelligence Monitoring references the Sectorial Anti-Fraud Committee, establishing an inter-institutional information-sharing obligation and a SAMA-coordinated threat-intelligence channel.
- The Preauthorization/Notification POS Fraud Circular and Tahaqaq Verification Service operationalise specific preventive controls within the Framework, anchoring abstract requirements to concrete transaction-level and identity-verification procedures.
