# AML Due Diligence & Accounts

**Cohesion:** 0.12 - loosely connected
**Members:** 30 nodes

## Why this community

This community covers the full AML/CTF compliance framework applicable to SAMA-supervised financial institutions, spanning the Anti-Money Laundering Law, the Law on Combating Terrorism Crimes and Financing, and FATF standards. It clusters around the risk-based CDD lifecycle — from customer categorisation and risk assessment through enhanced/simplified due diligence, beneficial ownership, PEPs, and correspondent relationships — together with the governance, monitoring, reporting, and audit obligations that operationalise that lifecycle.

## How members connect

- Risk-Based Approach anchors the CDD tier selection (SDD/standard/EDD), with ML/TF Risk Assessment feeding account-type-specific controls for non-resident juristic persons, GCC non-bank companies, foreign investors, and liquidation accounts
- The AML/CTF Guide implements the Anti-Money Laundering Law and CTF Law by translating statutory obligations into institutional procedures, citing FATF Recommendations as the international benchmark
- KYC Principle and Beneficial Owner identification form the shared definitional spine that Due Diligence Measures, EDD, and Third-Party Reliance rules all reference, creating an obligation chain from customer onboarding through ongoing monitoring
- Monitoring of Transactions and Activities and Reporting of Suspicious Transactions to SAFIU constitute the detection-and-disclosure control family, both citing the Anti-Money Laundering Law directly
- Governance obligations (Board of Directors Responsibilities, Senior Management Responsibilities, AML/CTF Compliance Function, Independent Audit Function, Recruitment Criteria, Training) form a four-layer accountability structure that an examiner would trace from policy through to staff-level evidence
- Wire Transfer and Correspondence Relationship rules extend CDD obligations to inter-institutional and cross-border payment chains, with Record Keeping requirements creating the evidence-retention layer across all nodes
